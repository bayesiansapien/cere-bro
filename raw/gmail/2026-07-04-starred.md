## **Claude Fable 5 can now do 16% of freelance jobs at a professional level**

Anthropic has [removed covert steganography code ](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/07/01/anthropic-is-removing-its-covert-code-for-catching-chinese-competitors/5265366)from Claude Code after developers discovered hidden Unicode markers used to catch Chinese competitors from distilling its data. The company is replacing this defensive infrastructure with behavioral fingerprinting and fake data injection.

At the same time, Anthropic is tightening safety controls around its Fable 5 models. [New safeguards ](https://www.anthropic.com/news/fable-safeguards-jailbreak-framework)now downgrade sensitive cybersecurity or biology-related requests to weaker models, while a new “Cyber Jailbreak Severity” framework scores AI exploits based on how dangerous and weaponizable they are.

The business case for AI automation is accelerating. According to the _Center for AI Safety_’s latest Remote Labor Index, Claude [Fable 5 now completes 16.1% of professional remote work projects](https://x.com/CAIS/status/2072360965522489789), doubling the capability of its closest rival. However, this heavy reliance has exposed a massive vulnerability. Following a sudden government halt on Fable 5, a _VentureBeat_ survey revealed that[ 67% of enterprises are already hedging their risk](https://venturebeat.com/orchestration/enterprises-lost-claude-fable-5-for-a-few-weeks-new-data-shows-two-thirds-had-already-built-their-hedge) by shifting critical workflows to open-weight or self-hosted systems to protect themselves from future disruptions.

On the development side, optimizing these deployments requires a shift in strategy. Anthropic [cut the system prompt for Claude Code by 80% ](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-says-it-cut-80-percent-of-claude-codes-system-prompt-because-fable-5-models-want-a-smaller-system-prompt/)after discovering that its new Mythos-class models perform better with fewer instructions. Heavy prompting and rigid rules actually hurt the natural reasoning ability of these advanced models, prompting developers to move toward lighter, higher-level guidance instead. 

To manage autonomous agents, Anthropic launched [Claude Enterprise spend controls](https://claude.com/blog/giving-admins-more-visibility-and-control-over-claude-usage-and-spend), which track model usage and connect directly to Datadog. Meanwhile, [Artifacts expanded to Pro and Max tiers](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2072770790114914317), enabling live, self-updating code pages. 

Anthropic is also reportedly entering [early planning with Samsung](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-reportedly-explores-custom-chip-manufacturing-with-samsung-while-insisting-nvidia-still-matters/) for proprietary silicon designed by former Tesla and OpenAI engineers to bypass expensive GPU limits.

Finally, _TestingCatalog_ published an [early look at Claude Science](https://www.testingcatalog.com/early-look-at-anthropics-claude-science-app-for-researchers/) in beta. The platform equips researchers with 60 specialized tools across genomics and cheminformatics to automate workflows, such as CRISPR screen design, on high-performance computing clusters.
