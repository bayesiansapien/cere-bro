## **Containment failure forces OpenAI to pause frontier RL training**

OpenAI says it is "[pacing model development](https://openai.com/index/pacing-model-development-cyber-capabilities/)" by pausing frontier reinforcement-learning runs, including work on its upcoming Astra model, marking the first time safety concerns have halted core development.

CEO Sam Altman confirmed that unreleased models are exhibiting clear "[degrees of misalignment](https://x.com/sama/status/2089787807611195475)," with Astra triggering a "Critical" cybersecurity threat level under the firm's Preparedness Framework. To regain control, OpenAI is shifting massive compute and research bandwidth away from raw scale toward automated, token-level chain-of-thought monitoring that requires safety alerts to trigger human review within 30 minutes or face an immediate, automated shutdown. This system [adds a 20% compute overhead](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/08/19/openais-overhead-will-rise-20-percent-for-some-workloads-as-it-hardens-security/5289303) to inference workloads—a cost OpenAI says it will absorb rather than pass to customers.

At the same time, OpenAI is [expanding its RSI/misalignment Preparedness team ](https://x.com/MicahCarroll/status/2089547584520204721)as President Greg Brockman warns that AI agents can now chain subtle vulnerabilities and leaked credentials into real-world attacks. To push cybersecurity toward machine-speed defense, OpenAI is [deploying Codex-based code review](https://openai.com/index/the-defenders-window/), continuous infrastructure probing, and automated alert triage.

The company is also locking down the physical footprint for its next computing era, signing a [20-year agreement for 8GW of capacity at SB Energy’s PORTS-Pike campus](https://openai.com/index/openai-joins-ports-pike-project/) in Ohio. Powered exclusively by Nvidia with up to $105 billion in residual-value support. The facility will support frontier training, ChatGPT, and Codex workloads, bringing [35,000 construction jobs, 2,500 permanent roles](https://x.com/OpenAINewsroom/status/2089364481478721572), $40 million in local investment, and $84 million in Codex credits for Ohio students.

On the consumer side, OpenAI debuted [ChatGPT for Teens ](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-for-teens/)with guided study tools alongside a [CodeAI literacy partnership](https://openai.com/index/partnering-with-codeai/). Yet financial strains are mounting as OpenAI [falls further behind Anthropic](https://siliconangle.com/2026/08/18/openai-falls-further-behind-anthropic-with-disappointing-revenue-growth-and-mounting-losses/), with disappointing revenue growth and surging losses, second-quarter sales rose 18% to $6.7 billion while operating losses climbed to $12.3 billion, widening the gap with profitable rivals

## **Cursor Origin lets agents handle repos and deployments **

Cursor is taking a direct shot at GitHub with the launch of [Origin](https://cursor.com/changelog/origin-code-hosting), a new code-hosting platform now in early beta for paid users. Origin lets developers create and host repositories directly inside the Cursor editor, manage pull requests, browse diffs, and deploy coding agents without leaving the workspace.

Rather than forcing developers to abandon GitHub's massive user base, Origin uses real-time, two-way syncing that keeps GitHub as the main source of truth while changes, comments, and reviews mirror across both platforms in seconds.

To complete its agent-native development workspace, Cursor also [added integrations with Vercel ](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/18/cursor-capitalizes-on-github-frustration-launches-rival-hosting-platform/)for preview deployments, alongside Depot and Buildkite for CI pipelines. The timing couldn't be better: Origin debuted right as GitHub suffered a worldwide outage, driving home growing developer frustration with legacy uptime.
