## **A stealth AI model called ‘Ox Alpha’ is winning over developers**

An anonymous reasoning model called[ Ox Alpha](https://openrouter.ai/stealth/ox-alpha) surfaced on OpenRouter, drawing attention for its 1M-token context window, 131K-token max output, tool calling, structured outputs, and multimodal support. Early buzz accelerated when a viral subset test put it at an 80% pass rate across 10 DeepSWE tasks, outperforming Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol, though broader evaluations on the full 113-task suite paint a more modest 63% score that roughly matches GPT-5.6.

A cryptic post from DeepMind researcher Evan Otero sparked rumors that it[ might be a stealth Gemini Pro release](https://x.com/EvanOtero/status/2090998729637511301). But when you look at the actual empirical evidence, tokenizer behavior, error signatures, and system artifacts [align directly with Zhipu AI's GLM-5.3](https://pasqualepillitteri.it/en/news/12473/ox-alpha-mistaken-for-gemini-glm-5-3), making a Chinese lab origin the most parameter-consistent explanation. Nothing has been confirmed.

Google DeepMind is also pushing AI beyond traditional benchmarks, [training agents in an isolated EVE Online server](https://deepmind.google/blog/from-atari-to-eve-online-building-on-15-years-of-ai-research-in-games/) to test long-term memory, continual learning, planning and multi-agent behavior. The research could eventually move into EVE Frontier, where agents would face changing rules and persistent economies. Google says the goal is to develop AI that can enrich games rather than replace players.

Google [Antigravity is adding Remote Control](https://x.com/antigravity/status/2090853908138676507), letting users access active sessions from supported browsers and iOS and Android devices.

Meanwhile, in an **𝕏** [post](https://x.com/ZoubinGhahrama1/status/2091548462001115255) sharing what he called an excellent _Economist _[article](https://www.economist.com/interactive/briefing/2026/08/20/the-search-for-consciousness-inside-llms), DeepMind VP Zoubin Ghahramani argues that AI consciousness may need to be judged [without assuming intelligence has to look human](https://x.com/ZoubinGhahrama1/status/2091548462001115255).

## **Unreleased Claude models ‘Marshmallow’ and ‘Melon’ surface in developer platforms**

Anthropic is [testing two early-access Claude models](https://www.binance.com/en-PH/square/post/08-24-2026-ai-trends-developers-spot-two-early-access-anthropic-claude-models-358999736004615) under the codenames Marshmallow and Melon, according to developer sightings. The models, identified as claude-marshmallow-eap and claude-melon-eap, could eventually become Opus 5.1 and Sonnet 5.1. Marshmallow reportedly beats the current Opus 5, but neither model is said to match Fable 5. Anthropic hasn’t confirmed the models or their release plans.

At the same time, Anthropic is [expanding Claude Mythos 5 for cybersecurity](https://claude.com/blog/bringing-claude-mythos-5-to-more-defenders). Claude Security can now use Mythos 5 to scan Enterprise codebases, identify vulnerabilities and suggest patches for human review. Anthropic is also working with security vendors to integrate Mythos into their tools and has committed $35 million in Claude credits to help open-source projects find and fix vulnerabilities.

Anthropic is also [addressing complaints about Opus 5 being too verbose](https://x.com/bcherny/status/2091591570982371454). Engineer Boris Cherny says the model is strong at optimization tasks such as reducing CPU usage, CI times and latency. For now, Claude Code users can use `claude /config outputStyle=concise` to shorten responses.
