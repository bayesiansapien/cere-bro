## **OpenAI debuts finance tracking on GPT-5.5 Thinking, unifies teams under Greg Brockman **

OpenAI has officially placed cofounder [Greg Brockman in charge of product strategy](https://www.wired.com/story/openai-reorg-greg-brockman-product/)  as the company reorganizes to combine ChatGPT, Codex, and developer APIs into a unified AI platform. Codex chief Thibault Sottiaux will oversee core platform teams, while Nick Turley shifts to enterprise products, anchoring development for a desktop super app that integrates ChatGPT, Codex, and the Atlas browser.

OpenAI has launched a [preview of personal finance tools](https://x.com/ChatGPTapp/status/2055317612687675545) for US-based ChatGPT Pro users. Powered by GPT-5.5 Thinking, the system uses Plaid to securely connect with over 12,000 financial institutions, analyzing spending patterns, subscriptions, and portfolio performance to deliver context-aware budgeting advice via a dedicated dashboard.

Expanding agent capabilities, [Codex](https://www.testingcatalog.com/openai-will-let-codex-control-other-desktop-devices-via-computer-use/) is reportedly developing remote-control computer use features to operate macOS applications even when machines are locked or asleep, enabling automated GUI testing and multi-device remote operation.

The company also quietly [acquired voice cloning startup](https://the-decoder.com/openai-bought-a-voice-cloning-startup-famous-for-celebrity-imitations/) Weights.gg to absorb its synthetic audio engineering talent into ChatGPT and developer APIs. In a [first-of-its-kind deal](https://openai.com/index/malta-chatgpt-plus-partnership/) with Malta, OpenAI has granted one year of free ChatGPT Plus access to all citizens who complete a national AI literacy course. ## **Google Cloud Director outlines prompting strategies to counter developer cognitive surrender**

Google Cloud Director [Addy Osmani ](https://x.com/addyosmani)warned that default AI code generation triggers severe cognitive debt, trading long-term engineering skill for present-day speed. Citing 2026 data from Anthropic, MIT, and CHI, Osmani noted that frictionless automation causes cognitive surrender, dropping developer comprehension below 40% and altering baseline brain connectivity.

To stop this skill decay, he [outlined proactive prompting frameworks](https://x.com/addyosmani/status/2056078124346228860) that reintroduce critical friction, urging engineers to establish problem hypotheses before generating code, prioritize conceptual explanations, and audit model outputs like a junior developer's pull request.

Simultaneously, Google Search Central[ updated ](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide)its official documentation to dismiss specialized Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) and Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) tactics as industry myths. The guidance confirms that generative search features like AI Overviews are powered entirely by core ranking systems via retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and query fan-out models. Read more.

## **Trump state visit to China concludes without anticipated AI semiconductor trade agreement**

Donald Trump’s high-profile state visit to China marked a critical turning point where advanced technology and semiconductor supply chains took center stage as core instruments of national diplomacy.

Tech leaders, including Elon Musk, Tim Cook and Jensen Huang, [joined the delegation ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pqY6as0iJAM)amid intense market anticipation for a structural breakthrough regarding advanced chip exports. However, the summit concluded without a formal technology agreement, triggering a swift sell-off in global chip stocks and underscoring that advanced compute is now treated as highly sensitive national infrastructure rather than ordinary trade.

While traditional industrial sectors secured clear commercial victories, led by a massive Boeing agreement to sell 200 aircraft to Beijing, the standoff over semiconductor sovereignty remains unchanged. Nvidia’s H200 chip clearance stalled as Beijing refused to formally approve the purchases.
