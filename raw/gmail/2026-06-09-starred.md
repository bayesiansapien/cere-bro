## **Google locks down $920M monthly compute deal with SpaceX**

Google has locked down a massive multi-year deal with SpaceX, guaranteeing access to 110,000 Nvidia GPUs and server infrastructure starting October 2026. Worth up to [$920 million a month](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/artificial-intelligence/google-signs-usd920m-monthly-compute-deal-with-spacex-companys-projected-annual-data-center-revenue-to-exceed-its-combined-proceeds-from-starlink-launch-services-and-ai-in-2025) through mid-2029, the compute influx will scale gradually to feed Google’s hungriest next-generation models.

That compute is translating into sharper, highly optimized tools. Google DeepMind just dropped [new Gemma 4 models built on Quantization-Aware Training (QAT)](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/quantization-aware-training-gemma-4/). By simulating quantization during the training phase rather than compressing after the fact, Google managed to shrink memory footprints down to 1GB for its smallest variant without tanking accuracy. It pulls this off via static activations, channel-wise quantization, and selective 2-bit compression, allowing heavy-duty AI to run locally on smartphones and laptops.
