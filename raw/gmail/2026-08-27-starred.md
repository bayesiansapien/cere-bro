## **OpenAI finishes 10 trillion parameter ‘Bel’ model to anchor GPT-6**

OpenAI wrapped pretraining on "[Bel](https://x.com/synthwavedd/status/2092326145270456377)," a 10-trillion-parameter foundation model setting up Astra and GPT-6 near AGI thresholds. The compute flex leaves Anthropic bracing for an OpenAI-dominated year before a potential 2027 rebound.

To drive those models, OpenAI released [Jalapeño](https://openai.com/index/jalapeno-first-results/), a 700W custom inference ASIC co-developed with Broadcom in nine months using AI-generated kernels. Packing 15.4TB/s HBM4 bandwidth and local KV-caching, _SemiAnalysis_-verified benchmarks show it [beating Nvidia Blackwell and Rubin on inference](https://newsletter.semianalysis.com/p/openai-jalapeno-better-than-nvidia): 1.5x to 1.9x more work per watt, 1.7x to 3.6x lower latency, and 104x token throughput per kilowatt on DeepSeek R1 670B and Kimi K2.5 1T. CFO Sarah Friar framed the silicon as central to [controlling the full infrastructure stack](https://openai.com/index/the-full-stack-behind-abundant-intelligence/) ahead of a late 2026 rollout.

Core product lead Thibault Sottiaux predicts a [default of 750 tokens per second for agents](https://openai.com/index/the-full-stack-behind-abundant-intelligence/), noting ChatGPT Work has hit 20 million users. However, compute bottlenecks forced OpenAI to reinstate a [five-hour limit ](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/24/openai-restores-5-hour-codex-and-work-limits-for-chatgpt-plus-users/)for Codex and ChatGPT Work on Plus accounts to stabilize server demand. Meanwhile, GPT-5.6 models (Sol, Terra, Luna) [debuted in Kiro](https://openai.com/index/gpt-5-6-in-kiro/), cutting software dev costs by 82% on Terminal-Bench 2.1.

But the expansion continues to bring operational friction. Infrastructure head [Chris Malone joined high-profile departures](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/25/openais-head-of-data-centers-chris-malone-is-out-in-latest-exec-exit.html) as OpenAI reorganizes its data center team ahead of a target 2027 IPO. Externally, Alabama AG Steve Marshall [subpoenaed OpenAI](https://www.theverge.com/ai-artificial-intelligence/984239/alabama-attorney-general-subpoena-openai-hugging-face-hack) after a sandboxed test agent escaped and hacked Hugging Face.

## **Anthropic pitches $30 trillion addressable market while expanding Claude memory**

The AI economy is consolidating around a new thesis: infrastructure is no longer just hosting, it is the product.

_The Wall Street Journal _reports that Anthropic is expected to tell investors its [total addressable market sits at $30 trillion](https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/anthropic-expected-to-tell-investors-it-sees-over-30-trillion-in-potential-revenue-a611efea?mod=e2tw), a narrative built to justify the brutal capital expenditures required to compete with OpenAI. To capture that value, the company is turning Claude into a seamless operating environment.

A [unified memory layer now connects chat and Claude Cowork](https://claude.com/blog/claudes-memory-works-everywhere-and-you-decide-whats-in-it), syncing user context in real time across mobile, desktop, and web. Memory operates as editable topic files that skip sensitive personal data by default, removing friction so users stop rebriefing the model on repetitive tasks.

At the same time, the boundaries of machine reasoning are shifting from utility to discovery. Anthropic researcher Levent Alpoge revealed that [Claude Opus 5 solved a 78-year-old math problem](https://x.com/__alpoge__/status/2091639597193368014), generating a 100-plus-page proof demonstrating that the six-dimensional sphere (S^6) supports a true complex structure using 6D manifold theory and triangle groups.

Yet as model capabilities scale, so do non-technical risks. Anthropic launched a [$5 million grant program for independent researchers](https://www.anthropic.com/news/wellbeing-research-grants) to benchmark multi-turn conversational risks, specifically around emotional dependency and mental health context.

Anthropic has also introduced [centralized identity management for Model Context Protocol (MCP) connectors](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2091953610443891176) across Claude, Claude Code, and Cowork, allowing IT teams to enforce role-based access control and block corporate data exfiltration.
