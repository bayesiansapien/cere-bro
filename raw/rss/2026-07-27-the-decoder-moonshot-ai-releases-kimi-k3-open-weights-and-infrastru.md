# Moonshot AI releases Kimi K3 open weights and infrastructure after shaking up the frontier model race

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/moonshot_ai_logos.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        Moonshot AI has released Kimi K3's model weights and made parts of its infrastructure open source. The Chinese model nearly matches Western frontier models such as Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol on popular benchmarks, but independent tests found major gaps in cyber and math performance, possibly pointing to distillation.</p>
