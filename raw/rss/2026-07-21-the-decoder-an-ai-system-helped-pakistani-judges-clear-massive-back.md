# An AI system helped Pakistani judges clear massive backlogs at $38.50 return per dollar invested

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/judge_pakistan_study.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        A field experiment with 1,559 Pakistani judges found that the AI assistant JudgeGPT boosted case resolution by 6.3 percent. The catch: only judges who got hands-on training saw gains. Without it, the effect mostly disappeared. The researchers estimate a return of up to $38.50 per dollar invested.</p>
