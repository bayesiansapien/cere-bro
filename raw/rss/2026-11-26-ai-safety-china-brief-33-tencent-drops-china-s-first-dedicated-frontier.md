# Brief #33: Tencent drops China’s first dedicated frontier AI safety system card

<h3>Key Takeaways</h3><ul><li><p>Tencent released an 80-page system card for its latest model, the most detailed safety disclosure we&#8217;ve ever seen from a Chinese AI developer.</p></li><li><p>President Xi Jinping and US President Trump discussed AI safety in Shenzhen and reaffirmed their commitment to human control over AI. But working-level progress on the US-China AI Dialogue is unclear.</p></li><li><p>The founding treaty for the World AI Cooperation Organization (WAICO) is now public. We unpack how it will run in practice.</p></li><li><p>An article in China&#8217;s most important official Party journal warned that frontier AI could escape human control and threaten human survival.</p></li></ul><p class="button-wrapper"><a class="button primary" href="https://aisafetychina.substack.com/subscribe?"><span>Subscribe now</span></a></p><h3>Tencent raises the bar for AI safety disclosure in China</h3><p><strong>What happened: </strong>Tencent published an <a href="https://hy.tencent.ai/Hy4_system_card.pdf">80-page system card</a> focused on frontier safety for its latest model, Hy4. The company also hosted a two-hour Reddit AMA, answering community questions.</p><p><strong>This is a big deal: </strong>The Hy4 system card is the most detailed safety disclosure we&#8217;ve ever seen from a Chinese AI developer. Tencent also tested whether Hy4 takes unsafe actions when using agentic tools, is susceptible to prompt injection attacks, or shows signs of deception or loss of control. The card also covers harmful content, child safety, and mental health.</p><p>Notably, Tencent measured Hy4&#8217;s ability to improve other models, a capability relevant to recursive self-improvement (RSI). In the AMA, the team <a href="https://www.reddit.com/r/LocalLLaMA/s/isZQkk3udP">said</a> its RSI capabilities were &#8220;still at an early stage.&#8221; The card gives no threshold for when faster AI R&amp;D would trigger stronger safeguards.</p><p><strong>How they tested it:</strong> Tencent combined third-party evaluations, public and in-house benchmarks, expert red-teaming. Tencent also drew on internal trials to see how people used Hy4 in practice.</p><p>Tencent drew on its impressive in-house cybersecurity expertise to assess its AI for cyber and agent safety risks.<a class="footnote-anchor" href="#footnote-1" id="footnote-anchor-1" target="_self">1</a> This is not something smaller, standalone AI developers could easily do.</p><p>In addition, it also called on two independent third-party evaluators to test Hy4:<a class="footnote-anchor" href="#footnote-2" id="footnote-anchor-2" target="_self">2</a></p><ul><li><p><strong>Fangcun AI (&#26041;&#23544;&#36291;&#36801;), </strong>the Chinese AI safety startup <a href="https://aisafetychina.substack.com/i/222459852/also-on-our-radar">we covered last month</a>, evaluated loss-of-control and manipulation risks.</p></li><li><p><strong><a href="https://alice.io">ALICE</a>, </strong>an Israeli-American AI safety firm that <a href="https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-25/ai-safety-startup-alice-partner-of-google-anthropic-raises-140-million">recently raised</a> $140 million and has worked with Anthropic, Google, and other Western labs, tested content safety, resistance to prompt injection, and refusals of harmful cyber requests.</p></li></ul><p><strong>This points to a growing role for independent safety testing in China </strong>and shows that Tencent is willing to work with both domestic and international partners.</p><p><strong>How the card compares to those of US labs:</strong> The scope of this system card is broadly similar to recent disclosures from <a href="https://deploymentsafety.openai.com/gpt-6-2-chatgpt/preparedness-assessment?utm_source=chatgpt.com">OpenAI</a> and <a href="https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2?utm_source=chatgpt.com">Anthropic</a>, which also cover misuse, agentic AI risks, and misalignment. The level of detail across domains varies, though. For example, Tencent only included very minimal discussion of chemical risks.
