# Black Forest Labs launches Flux 3 Image with multi-step editing that leaves the rest of your picture alone

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1350" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/flux_3_image.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2214" /></p>
<p>        Black Forest Labs has released Flux 3 Image, the image side of its Flux 3 model family. It supports multi-step edits without changing other parts of the image, lets users compose scenes with bounding boxes and up to ten reference images, and outputs up to 4K. Open weights are coming in the next few weeks.</p>
