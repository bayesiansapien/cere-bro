# Anthropic's bio-weapons filter was down for nearly a year, exposing 133 million requests

<p>        In a safety report, Anthropic reveals that its internal filtering system for biological and chemical weapons risks was inactive for nearly a year. During that time, around 50,000 external feedback contractors ran about 133 million unfiltered interactions with the models.</p>
