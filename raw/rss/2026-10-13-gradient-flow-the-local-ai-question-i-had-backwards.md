Their argument is that compute and models aren&#8217;t the bottleneck, and neither is the sheer volume of data. Language models inherited an internet where text, images, and intent were already linked. Biology has huge datasets, but genetic, molecular, and clinical measurements are rarely collected from the same people, and almost never over time. That brought me back to <a href="https://gradientflow.substack.com/p/your-model-is-easy-to-buy-your-data?utm_source=gradientflow&amp;utm_medium=newsletter">a point I&#8217;ve been making lately</a>: useful AI data often has to be built, not just collected.
