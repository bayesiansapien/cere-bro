# Sequoia Capital Goes All Out on AI Under New Leaders

<p>In 2023, Gavin Uberti, Robert Wachen and Chris Zhu, the co-founders of AI chip startup Etched, were still Harvard University students living in a dorm room. That was when Uberti first met with partners from Sequoia Capital. The venture firm was among the first Etched pitched that year when raising a seed round that was trying to compete in the notoriously difficult semiconductor industry. Sequoia didn’t invest then.</p>

<p>Recently, the Menlo Park, Calif., venture firm got another chance to invest in Etched at its Series C. Before and during the fundraising round, Sequoia mounted an all-out charm offensive. Wachen hopped on a plane with Sequoia co-leader Pat Grady&mdash;who runs the firm alongside Alfred Lin&mdash;former senior Sequoia leader Doug Leone and partner Sonya Huang. The Etched cofounder also visited the Los Angeles home of another Sequoia partner, Shaun Maguire. Lin then traveled with a delegation of partners to Etched’s San Jose, Calif., headquarters to look at its chip designs.</p>
