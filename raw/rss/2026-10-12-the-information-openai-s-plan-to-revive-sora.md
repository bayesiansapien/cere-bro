# OpenAI’s Plan To Revive Sora

<p>Remember <b>OpenAI’s</b> video model <b>Sora</b> and all the great <b>Sam Altman</b> <a href="https://x.com/gabriel1/status/1973120058907041902">memes</a> that came from it? Earlier this year, the ChatGPT-maker <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/openai-ceo-shifts-responsibilities-preps-spud-ai-model">canned</a> both the model and the TikTok-like app it powered as part of broader culling of server-intensive “side projects” even as rivals <b>xAI</b> and <b>Google</b> forged ahead with their own video models.</p><p>Well, there’s a chance that a version of Sora is coming back from the dead.</p><p>A stealthy startup developing AI video for filmmakers, which we <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/jeffrey-katzenberg-teams-former-openai-sora-head-new-ai-video-startup">revealed</a> last month, is in talks with OpenAI to access Sora, tweak it and sell software or other services using it, according to a person with knowledge of the talks. Hollywood mogul <b>Jeffrey Katzenberg</b>, former Sora research lead <b>Bill Peebles</b>, and former <b>Dropbox</b> CFO <b>Sujay Jaswa</b> co-founded the startup in recent months<b>.</b> (We don’t know the startup’s name).</p>
