# California Sends AI Safety Lawmaker to Congress

<p><b>Scott Wiener</b>, the California politician who led the successful effort to pass AI safety legislation in the state’s legislature last year, is headed to Washington. The state senator won Tuesday’s election to succeed long-time House Speaker <b>Nancy Pelosi</b>, after his opponent conceded on Wednesday.&nbsp;</p><p>When Wiener arrives in D.C., he will be among the few members of Congress to have authored significant AI legislation. Last year, Calif. Gov. <b>Gavin Newsom</b> signed Wiener’s <b>SB 53</b>, which requires the largest AI companies to write and comply with their own safety policies for managing severe risks from their technology.</p><p>The bill and the AI companies that supported it could indicate the kind of AI policies that a Democrat-controlled Congress will get behind.&nbsp;</p>
