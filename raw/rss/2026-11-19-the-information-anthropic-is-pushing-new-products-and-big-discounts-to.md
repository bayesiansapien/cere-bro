# Anthropic Is Pushing New Products, and Big Discounts, to Justify Its Huge Valuation

<p>To justify its enormous new valuation as a public company, <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/anthropic">Anthropic</a> needs to show that it can expand quickly in other areas of business the way it did with coding. Its early efforts are showing signs of promise&mdash;and highlighting the steep challenges it faces.</p>

<p>Anthropic is gaining traction with Claude Cowork, an AI tool for nontechnical business users, and persuading some customers to spend in new areas such as cybersecurity. The company is also hoping to embed itself deeper in companies’ core operations with Claude Managed Agents, a set of tools that developers can use to build and run agents without having to manage the underlying servers and other infrastructure.</p>
