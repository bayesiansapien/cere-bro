infamy, &#8220;<a href="https://www.politico.com/news/2026/10/04/sam-altman-decoded-interview-ai-01106217">we believe that the world should accept some bad things happening for the benefits of this technology and people having the agency</a>&#8221;,  New York City&#8217;s City Council will hold <a href="https://council.nyc.gov/press/2026/09/16/3240/">an important hearing</a> on AI policy. <a href="https://legistar.council.nyc.gov/MeetingDetail.aspx?ID=1445976&amp;GUID=325D66DF-B99B-4C9A-8061-6EC9907142F2&amp;Options=info%7C&amp;Search=">A number of AI-relevant proposals are under consideration</a>. </p><p>Because New York City is a large market, I believe that I could help pick up some of the slack where the Federal government is falling short. (A <a href="https://secureaiproject.org/bills-we-support/">number of states</a> are also trying to fill in the slack).</p><p>I plan to testify. A preview of my remarks:</p><p style="text-align: center;"><em><strong>NYC City Council Hearing on AI</strong></em></p><p style="text-align: center;"><em>Gary Marcus</em></p><p style="text-align: center;"><em>Monday, October 5, 2026</em></p><p><em>My name is Gary Marcus. I am a scientist, author, and NYU Professor Emeritus who cofounded an AI startup that was acquired by Uber. My Substack, Marcus on AI, has about 120,000 subscribers; on X, I have 250,000 followers. I am frequently in the media, often described as &#8220;a leading AI expert&#8221; and &#8220;voice of reason&#8221;.</em></p><p><em>We stand at a pivotal moment in history, and New York City has a chance to improve our chances, both for itself, and for the world.</em></p><p><em>Contemporary AI is like a bull in a china shop, powerful and impressive, but difficult to control. Already, the advent of general purpose agents with broad internet access has led to tens of thousands of hacking incidents; other, related technologies, like deep fakes, have led both to a deluge of convincing and realistic propaganda and a sea of nonconsensual deepfaked porn. Chatbots have been implicated in a number of suicides, and <a href="https://www.cbc.ca/news/canada/british-columbia/mother-jones-chatgpt-safeguards-tumbler-ridge-bc-shooter-9.7358031">even mass murder</a>. Not every fear that people talk about strikes me as realistic &#8211; I rather doubt that human extinction is imminent, despite all the headlines &#8211; but it is clear that AI comes with serious risks.</em></p><p><em>It is also clear that the current administration in DC&#8212; deeply tied to the big tech companies &#8212; is unwilling to do anything about it.</em></p><p><em>What we need, above all else, is an FDA-like regulatory regime for AI, in which AI developers bring their software to independent review, much as drug manufacturers bring their new drugs to the FDA for a review. AI developers should be obligated to demonstrate that the benefits for their new products outweigh the risks &#8212; and not have market access otherwise.</em></p><p><em>I said the same to Louisiana&#8217;s Senator Kennedy in May 2023 at the US Senate. It is still true.</em></p><p><em>But the US Senate did nothing.</em></p><p><em>New York City is in a position to do what the US Senate failed to do: demand independent regulatory review for AI used in its borders. <a href="https://legistar.council.nyc.gov/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=8239233&amp;GUID=A927E066-C669-4F66-8E5A-4B610FA321E5&amp;Options=&amp;Search=">The bill on third party validation</a> proposes to do just that. It would protect New York&#8217;s Citizens from some of the harms from AI, and could allow New York to be a global leader, much as California has been a global leader around clean energy standards.</em></p><p><em>In the strongest possible terms, I urge council to support this bill.</em></p><p><em>Finally, events of recent days suggest OpenAI may have fired employees who were whistleblowing. Without strong protections from whistleblowing, <a href="https://legistar.council.nyc.gov/LegislationDetail.aspx?ID=8239234&amp;GUID=75E3ADBA-51CD-4CF8-B8B7-6A1B9A1F22FE&amp;Options=&amp;Search=">like New York City is considering</a>, we are all in trouble.</em></p><p><em>In sum by protecting whistleblowers, and demanding independent validation of new AI models, New York City can help lead the way to a positive, rather than negative, AI future.</em></p><p class="button-wrapper"><a class="button primary" href="https://garymarcus.substack.com/subscribe?"><span>Subscribe now</span></a></p><p>P.S. If you wish to testify, in person or by Zoom, you can sign up <a href="https://council.nyc.gov/testify/">here</a>.</p><p></p>
