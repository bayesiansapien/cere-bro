# Tencent's AI chief says AI bioweapons are coming, but closing off models isn't the answer

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/tencent_Logo.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        Tencent chief AI scientist Vinces Yao Shunyu warns that AI will soon be able to develop bioweapons and argues for a middle ground between open and closed models. While safety filters on open-source systems can be easily stripped through abliteration, closed models like Anthropic's Claude often block too aggressively. For its upcoming open-weight model Hy4, Tencent is filtering dangerous hacking knowledge directly from the training data.</p>
