# Anthropic's Claude can now orchestrate up to 1,000 AI agents in parallel through dynamic workflows

<p>        Anthropic is adding dynamic workflows to Claude Managed Agents, letting a lead agent distribute tasks across up to 1,000 sub-agents at once. In testing, a single agent found at most 27 of 70 hidden bugs in a codebase, while the multi-agent workflow consistently caught 66.</p>
