# Political opposites unite in Washington to rein in AI

<p><img alt="Digital map of the USA" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="816" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Stargate-US-digital-map-title.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1456" /></p>
<p>        From Bernie Sanders to Steve Bannon, political opposites in Washington are jointly demanding hard brakes on artificial intelligence. Sanders wants a construction freeze on data centers, while OpenAI is backing the FRONTIER Act and its mandatory outside safety audits for the first time. Despite Trump's skepticism, bipartisan pressure for binding AI rules keeps growing.</p>
