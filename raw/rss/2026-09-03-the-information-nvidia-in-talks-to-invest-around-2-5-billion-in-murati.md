# Nvidia in Talks to Invest Around $2.5 Billion in Murati’s Thinking Machines Lab

<p>Thinking Machines Lab, the AI developer led by ex-OpenAI Chief Technology Officer Mira Murati, is in talks to raise between $5 billion and $6 billion at a valuation of at least $40 billion before the investment, existing investor Andreessen Horowitz has told other prospective investors.</p>

<p>Nvidia is expected to contribute about half of the amount, said the investment firm, which holds a board seat at Thinking Machines Lab. The move would extend a series of deals Nvidia has struck to expand its influence with open-source AI developers, including buying open-source model provider Hugging Face for $12.9 billion.</p>
