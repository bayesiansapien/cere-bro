# Elon Musk’s AI Group Guts Grok Video Generation Team

<p>SpaceX’s artificial intelligence group has axed most of the team behind Grok Imagine, its AI tool for video and image generation, according to people familiar with the situation.</p>

<p>Around 60 people who worked on Grok Imagine were laid off in the past week from the SpaceX Super Intelligence group, leaving only a handful of employees to keep the product alive. The tool remains available to users, though its future is uncertain without a dedicated team working on it.</p>
