# Google Deepmind's Genie 4 puts a modern spin on agent designs that predate LLMs

<p>        Google Deepmind has introduced Genie 4, a real-time world model that simulates interactive 3D worlds with sound and talking characters. Gemini models help plan in the background to keep the simulation consistent. Genie 4 is still an early research model, and longer sessions lead to visual inconsistencies.</p>
