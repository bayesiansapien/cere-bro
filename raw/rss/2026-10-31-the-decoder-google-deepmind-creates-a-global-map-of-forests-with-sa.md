# Google Deepmind creates a global map of forests with satellite data and AI

<p><img alt="A 3D voxel-style world map with a blurred foreground and a focus on South America, where the forests are depicted in shades of green and the plantations in pink as cubes." class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1012" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/forest-typology-01-hero.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1800" /></p>
<p>        Google Deepmind and the World Resources Institute have released ForTy, a global map that classifies forests and other land into six categories, including primary forest, plantations, and tree crops. The map could help track deforestation under EU rules, but it identifies only about half of planted forests and tree-crop areas, and 60% of plantations.</p>
