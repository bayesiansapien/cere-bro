# AI’s $300 Billion Stock Binge Is Testing Investors

<p>Volatile trading in AI-related stocks this past week has put a spotlight on investors’ appetite for additional share offerings&mdash;and the prices companies could get if they opt to sell.</p>

<p>While tech giants, chip companies and AI cloud providers have sold or priced more than $300 billion of new stock and mandatory convertible securities so far this year, another roughly $100 billion in potential share sales remains on tap. Most would come through at-the-market programs, which give companies the option to sell shares gradually into the open market, including a proposed $60 billion ATM from Meta Platforms that could launch in early 2027.</p>
