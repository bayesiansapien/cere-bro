# How Is Anthropic So Efficient?

<p>When <b>Anthropic</b> on Monday dropped its long-awaited initial public offering prospectus filing, a lot of the details—see our top takeaways <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/anthropics-s-1-takeaways-giant-compute-commitments-complex-lock-ups">here</a>—weren’t exactly new, given how much my colleagues and others <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/anthropic-seeks-palantir-style-voting-control-seven-co-founders-ahead-ipo">had</a> <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/anthropic-considers-letting-shareholders-sell-ipo-departing-spacex-playbook">previously</a> <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/anthropics-big-charity-bill-shareholders">reported</a> about it.</p><p>Two items stood out to me, though: how Anthropic is running its models more efficiently than before, and changes it made to its already-unusual governance structure.&nbsp;</p><p>Anthropic’s ability to generate $3.5 billion in free cash flow in the first half of 2026, from $16.3 billion in revenue, shows just how much it has improved the way it runs AI in the cloud, compared to the same period a year earlier, when it burned $1.6 billion. Specifically, Anthropic said it is generating an 85% gross profit margin from powering existing models, which doesn’t include the cost of developing new models. (If that figure rings a bell, it’s the same gross margin estimate my colleague Ann described in this column about <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/newsletters/ai-infrastructure/attractive-economics-behind-anthropic-data-center-sprint?rc=c48ukx">Anthropic’s data center grab</a>, which elicited sharp questions from some AI skeptics at the time.)&nbsp;</p><p>Anthropic is doing so even as it relies almost entirely on computing it rents from cloud providers, which themselves need to generate a profit. So its IPO filing is a reality check on the AI skeptics’ longtime view that a business model founded on selling models on a pay-as-you-go, consumption basis wouldn’t be profitable.</p>
