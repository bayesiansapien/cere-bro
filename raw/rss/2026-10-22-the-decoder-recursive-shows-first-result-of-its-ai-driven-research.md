# Recursive shows first result of its AI-driven research process with Flint

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1080" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/recursive-flint-header-1.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1920" /></p>
<p>        The AI startup Recursive is unveiling Flint, its first language model, a 40-billion-parameter MoE for local coding developed by former DeepMind researchers. It was created in four months using the company's in-house AI system, "Eureka Machine," which independently tests and optimizes architectures. Recursive has yet to prove whether this approach will actually lead to self-improving AI.</p>
