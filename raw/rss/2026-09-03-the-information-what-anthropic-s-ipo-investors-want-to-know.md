# What Anthropic's IPO Investors Want to Know

<p>We’re getting ready for Anthropic to make its initial public offering prospectus public <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/anthropic-considers-letting-shareholders-sell-ipo-departing-spacex-playbook">sometime after Labor Day</a>. Though the company’s private investors have had a window into its torrid growth and <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/anthropic-hikes-2026-revenue-forecast-20-delays-will-go-cash-flow-positive?rc=zjctrx">projections</a>, this will be the first time public investors are getting a full financial picture of the Claude maker.&nbsp;</p><p>Just how complete a picture remains a big question, though. We’re hearing that investors want a <i>lot</i> more from Anthropic than the financial statements that securities regulators generally require companies to report.&nbsp;</p><p>In a series of meetings, executives at Anthropic have been going back and forth with potential large investors on whether the company can provide other business- or performance-related metrics particular to AI. These include AI tokenomics, including what it makes off every AI token it processes, how much revenue it’s generating per gigawatt of compute, and <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/why-net-retention-rates-are-all-the-rage-for-ai-investors?rc=zjctrx">net revenue retention</a>, a measure of a tech vendor’s ability to keep customers and increase their spending. There’s no guarantee Anthropic will provide any of these.&nbsp;</p>
