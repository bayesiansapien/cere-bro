# How SpaceX’s AI Unit Turned Itself Into an AI Cloud Firm

<p>SpaceX’s AI unit held talks over the summer about leasing computing capacity to Microsoft, say people familiar with the matter, a sign of how fully Elon Musk has embraced the neocloud business after his initial reluctance.</p>

<p>Selling computing power has quickly become one of SpaceX’s most lucrative businesses, generating billions of dollars a month in commitments from compute-constrained AI labs including Anthropic and Google, with more deals still to kick in. The status of the Microsoft talks isn’t clear, the people said. A spokesperson for Microsoft declined to comment.</p>
