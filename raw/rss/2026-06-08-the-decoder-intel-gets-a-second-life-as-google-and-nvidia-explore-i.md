# Intel gets a second life as Google and Nvidia explore it as a TSMC backup for AI chips

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/intel_logo_wall.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Google has ordered more than three million AI chips from Intel for 2028. Nvidia is testing Intel's manufacturing tech for its upcoming Feynman architecture. Both moves come as TSMC can't keep up with AI chip demand. Intel's long-struggling foundry division is getting a rare second chance.</p>
