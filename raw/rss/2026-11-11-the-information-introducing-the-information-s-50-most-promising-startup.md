# Introducing The Information’s 50 Most Promising Startups of 2026

<p>As artificial intelligence changes how companies get hacked, investors are paying up for startups that promise to keep defenders ahead.</p>

<p>That dynamic shaped <strong><a href="https://www.theinformation.com/projects/the-information-50-2026">this year’s The Information 50</a></strong>, our annual list of the most promising startups. For the first time, our list includes a category devoted solely to security, and it holds some of the list’s biggest bets. Two of the four most highly valued companies in this seventh edition of the list are security startups valued at more than $1 billion apiece, and six of the security firms are run by Israelis, which have a long history of breakout hits in this field.</p>

<p>At a time when many people use AI to research new companies, our reporters didn’t use AI to find candidates. They just had good ol’ fashioned conversations with dozens of founders and investors&mdash;including many that pointed us to startups they missed out on&mdash;and collected hard data on revenue and growth rates at the companies.
