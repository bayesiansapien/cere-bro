# OpenAI expands Codex with role-specific plugins to build a general-purpose app for non-developers

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/openai_chatgpt.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI is expanding Codex with role-specific plugins for data analysis, sales, and investment banking. Five million people use the tool each week, and one in five isn't a developer, the company says. That non-developer group is growing three times faster than the developer base, a sign that OpenAI is positioning Codex as an all-purpose work app.</p>
