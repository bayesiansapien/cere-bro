# Google Cloud now offers OpenAI models alongside Gemini and Claude, becoming the only major provider with all three

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/google_logo_wall.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Google Cloud is the first major cloud provider to offer Gemini, Claude, and OpenAI's GPTs on a single platform. At launch, GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol, and GPT-6 Luna are available as a private preview for enterprise customers.</p>
