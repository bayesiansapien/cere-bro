# US Department of Justice backs fair use for AI training in landmark copyright case

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="608" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/token_to_ai_content.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1088" /></p>
<p>        In the class-action lawsuit involving The New York Times, the US Department of Justice argues that training AI models on copyrighted text qualifies as fair use. The filing directly contradicts a report from the US Copyright Office. Its director was fired by the Trump administration shortly after the report was published.</p>
