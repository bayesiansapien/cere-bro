# New open model Inkling 2 Mini thinks in loops to solve problems with fewer words and still runs on a laptop

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="946" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/thinking_machines_ca_illustration.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1527" /></p>
<p>        Thinking Machines has released Inkling 2 Mini, an open looped transformer that runs 12 of its 24 layers three times per token. This lets the model reason internally for longer without generating extra text. On Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index, it ties with Qwen3.8 27B at roughly six times lower cost.</p>
