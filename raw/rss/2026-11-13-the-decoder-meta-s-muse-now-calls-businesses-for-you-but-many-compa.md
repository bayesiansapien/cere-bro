# Meta's Muse now calls businesses for you, but many companies might just hang up on the AI

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="702" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/met_muse_gadget_logo.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1158" /></p>
<p>        Meta's AI agent Muse can now call businesses on behalf of paying US subscribers, handling everything from cable providers to restaurants. The agent already has over 10 million users, according to Meta's AI chief Alexandr Wang. Google Gemini, Instinct, and xAI's Grok Bot offer the same feature, but many businesses hang up as soon as they detect an AI on the line.</p>
