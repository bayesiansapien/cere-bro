# Google's Gemini 3.5 Transcribe turns speech to text in 85 languages while auto-correcting your verbal stumbles

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/google_gemini-1.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        Google's new Gemini 3.5 Transcribe recognizes over 85 languages, strips filler words, and corrects slips of the tongue in real time. It hits a 4.0 percent word error rate in streaming mode, with 70 percent lower latency than its predecessor, Chirp 3. Through function calling, the model can hand off tasks to other Gemini models.</p>
