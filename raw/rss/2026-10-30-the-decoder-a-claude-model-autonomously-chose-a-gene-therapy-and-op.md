# A Claude model autonomously chose a gene therapy and operated on a mouse brain with no human decision-making

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/claude_mouse_op.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        An AI agent performed brain surgery on an epileptic mouse, picking the drug, the brain target, and the injection coordinates on its own. According to lead researcher Ocana-Santero, it's the first time an AI agent has autonomously chosen a treatment and delivered it to a living mammal. But when a calibration error hit mid-surgery, the agent refused to continue and couldn't be talked out of it.</p>
