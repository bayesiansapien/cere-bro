# How Much Is Anthropic Really Worth?

<p>While investors digest the news of <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/nvidia-ceo-jensen-huang-resigns-creating-uncertainty-ai-sector?rc=zjctrx">Nvidia boss Jensen Huang’s exit</a> and its implications on the <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/nvidias-dealmaking-binge-faces-reckoning-jensen-huangs-exit?rc=zjctrx">AI build-out</a>, they are simultaneously mulling another nebulous question: How much is Anthropic worth?</p><p>If everything goes according to plan, the market should give its answer when the company debuts on the Nasdaq exchange next week. Anthropic has set a tentative pricing range of between $1.25 trillion and $1.4 trillion, but several investors we spoke to expect it to price above that range.&nbsp;</p><p>Pricing an initial public offering above the proposed range “is a signal of underlying demand momentum, which is very important to get people excited to participate,” said Samantha Lau, chief investment officer of small- and mid-cap growth equities at AllianceBernstein.&nbsp;</p>
