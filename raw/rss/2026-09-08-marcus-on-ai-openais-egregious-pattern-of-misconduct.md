# OpenAI’s Egregious Pattern of Misconduct

<p>For something like a week, there been a constant stream of reports about OpenAI&#8217;s apparent misconduct: around the hacks, around apparent coverups, possible data fudging &#8212; and maybe even intellectual theft with a hint of extortion. Not to mention a bullshit game in which, in (apparent) concert with Jensen, OpenAI tried to pretend Astra was tantamount to AGI. </p><p>The story that broke this morning&#8212;still waiting for OpenAI&#8217;s full response&#8212;blew my mind.
