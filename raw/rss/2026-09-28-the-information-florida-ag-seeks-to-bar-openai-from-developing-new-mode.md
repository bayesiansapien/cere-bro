# Florida AG Seeks to Bar OpenAI from Developing New Models Without Guardrails

<p>Florida Attorney General James Uthmeier on Monday asked a Florida state court for an injunction that would bar OpenAI from developing new AI models “without third-party approved safety guardrails” and from allowing minors to use ChatGPT. The injunction would be effective throughout Florida.</p> <p> ...</p>
