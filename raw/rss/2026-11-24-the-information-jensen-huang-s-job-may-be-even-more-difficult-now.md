# Jensen Huang’s Job May Be Even More Difficult Now

<p>Even before Jensen Huang’s tumultuous departure two weeks ago, he was grappling with growing competition in AI chip design and intensifying questions about his massive funding commitments to customers, such as neoclouds. Now that he’s back, in a stunning maneuver orchestrated by President Donald Trump, his job may be even harder than before.</p>

<p>He’ll likely need to rebuild any trust lost with suppliers and his own executives, according to industry analysts and customers. (<a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/sex-lies-ai-chips-jensen-huangs-last-days-nvidia-ceo">He had lied</a> to some senior Nvidia executives about his infidelities, which was a key reason for his initial resignation.) And he faces an array of inquiries: the Commerce and Justice departments are still investigating Megaspeed,&nbsp;once&nbsp;a major Nvidia customer, and Supermicro, one of the biggest manufacturers of Nvidia’s AI server racks. The SEC is reportedly also investigating Nvidia, too.</p>
