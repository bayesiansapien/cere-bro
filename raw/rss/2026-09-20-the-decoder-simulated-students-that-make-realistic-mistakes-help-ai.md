# Simulated students that make realistic mistakes help AI tutors learn faster

<p><img alt="Illustration: Four people are solving interactive tasks such as puzzles, mazes, and coding challenges as part of an AI-powered tutoring workflow." class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1047" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/ai-tutors-microsoft-nano-banana-pro.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1920" /></p>
<p>        Microsoft and the University of Illinois built StudentSim to replicate individual students from limited data and give AI tutors fast, low-cost feedback. In tests covering 60 students across chess, English, and math, it outperformed GPT-5.4. A chess tutor trained with StudentSim also earned the highest expert ratings among three versions tested.</p>
