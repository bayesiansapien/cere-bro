# Everything That Led to Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s Fall

<p>Jensen Huang is gone from Nvidia, the chipmaker he co-founded 33 years ago. The origins of that downfall go back to the 2010s&mdash;to when Alice Huang, one of the women with whom he allegedly had an affair, was setting herself up to become a major Nvidia customer. Their relationship went unnoticed for a long time, but then it became just one element in a growing array of scandals around Jensen that eventually led to his resignation on Wednesday. As the drama widened, it came to include two more alleged affairs, a confrontation at Jensen’s home and a fateful board meeting. Below, you’ll find a curated timeline of what happened.</p>
