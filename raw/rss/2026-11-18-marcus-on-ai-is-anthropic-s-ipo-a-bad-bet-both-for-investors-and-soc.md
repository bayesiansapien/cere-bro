when it was trading at over $300 (today&#8217;s price is around $115), here&#8217;s why I (not a professional investor so just giving my own reasoning!) have my doubts about the high valuation for the upcoming Anthropic IPO. </p><p>Either way, as a person who has been warning about risks of AI night and day for the last several years, I would like you to consider not buying Anthropic stock for the sake of humanity.</p><p>I will get to the moral side of the argument at the end. </p><p>But first, the bull case, as I understand it, and then what I think it gets wrong and leaves out.</p><h1>Part I:
