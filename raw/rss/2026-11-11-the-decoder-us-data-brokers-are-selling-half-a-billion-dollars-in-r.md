# US data brokers are selling half a billion dollars in RL training environments to Chinese AI labs

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/china_vs_usa_ai_race.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        US data brokers like Mercor, Surge AI, and AfterQuery are selling roughly half a billion dollars' worth of AI training data to Chinese labs, according to a ChinaTalk investigation. That includes the same RL environments previously built for OpenAI or Anthropic. At least nine firms are involved, and export controls barely apply.</p>
