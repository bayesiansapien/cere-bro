# Reports of AI-generated child sexual abuse material have surged more than 8,400 percent in two years

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/ai_generated_abuse.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Edinburgh's Childlight Institute reports a more than 8,400 percent increase in AI-generated child sexual abuse material over two years, from about 4,700 reports in 2023 to over 400,000 in 2025. Abusers are using tools like nudify apps for blackmail. Childlight CEO Paul Stanfield wants governments to classify the problem as a preventable public health emergency.</p>
