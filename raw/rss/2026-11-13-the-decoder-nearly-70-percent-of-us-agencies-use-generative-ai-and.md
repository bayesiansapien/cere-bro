# Nearly 70 percent of US agencies use generative AI, and unions are finally catching up with guardrails

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/workers_fighting_ai.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        US public-sector unions are increasingly pushing through protections against AI surveillance and automated firings. In Oakland, a strike vote forced retraining programs instead of layoffs, and a transport union in Ohio won veto power over autonomous vehicles. California banned so-called bossware. All of this is happening while nearly 70 percent of US government agencies already use generative AI.</p>
