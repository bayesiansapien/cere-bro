# Delhi High Court hands OpenAI a win by rejecting major Indian news agency's copyright injunction

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/openai_logo_large_right.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        The Delhi High Court has handed OpenAI a major win in its copyright fight with news agency ANI. For the first time, a court has classified AI training as private use. ANI undermined its own case by citing articles published after the models were trained. The main trial is still pending.</p>
