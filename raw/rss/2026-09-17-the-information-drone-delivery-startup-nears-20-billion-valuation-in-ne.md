# Drone Delivery Startup Nears $20 Billion Valuation in New Funding Talks

<p>Zipline, a startup making drones that can deliver small packages like meals and medical supplies, is in talks to raise $1 billion at a valuation of roughly $20 billion, according to three people with knowledge of the fundraise.</p>

<p>The funding round, if it closes, would more than double its valuation from its last funding round, which valued the company at $7.6 billion after the investment. It follows a partnership announced last month with Uber to expand delivery by drones across the U.S. and expectations by investors that the costs of drone delivery are falling, expanding what was once a niche market.</p>
