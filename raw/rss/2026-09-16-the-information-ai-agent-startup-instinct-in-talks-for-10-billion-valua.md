# AI Agent Startup Instinct in Talks for $10 Billion Valuation

<p>The startup behind personal AI assistant Instinct is in talks to raise $1 billion at a valuation of about $10 billion, a person with knowledge of the deal said.&nbsp;Sequoia Capital and Benchmark have been in talks to lead the financing, the person added.</p>

<p>The Information <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/personal-ai-app-instinct-faces-compute-crunch-lead-new-funding">reported earlier this month</a> that the startup is looking to raise new funding as the invitation-only agent faces&nbsp;constraints in compute capacity. As Instinct’s service has become more widely available, the number of users has grown to more than 100,000, according to the person.</p>
