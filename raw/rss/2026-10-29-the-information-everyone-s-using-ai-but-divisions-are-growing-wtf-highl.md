# Everyone’s Using AI But Divisions Are Growing:  WTF Highlights

<p>Artificial intelligence has thoroughly infiltrated business and politics, but it’s also starting to divide companies and investors. That was the takeaway from leaders at The Information’s WTF Summit, a gathering of senior women in the technology and finance industries this week.</p>

<p>Investors are increasingly picking AI winners and losers, and favoring companies that can train models and control the chips and data centers that undergird them, said Goldman Sachs’ global <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/goldman-sachs-investment-banking-co-head-sees-repositioning-amid-ai-boom?rc=zjctrx">co-head of investment banking Kim Posnett</a>. “What you’re seeing is not a change in AI sentiment,” she said, but “you’re seeing a repositioning and differentiation across the AI trade.”</p>
