# UK's job market is splitting in two as AI demand surges while knowledge work postings crater

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/ai_worker.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        AI now appears in 9.4 percent of British job postings, up from about 2 percent in 2023, according to Indeed. Overall hiring is falling in knowledge work fields such as marketing and management, but AI-related listings are rising fast. Indeed calls it a "two-speed labor market."</p>
