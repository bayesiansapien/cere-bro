# Grok-built apps exposed patient data, plaintext passwords, and SpaceXSI's own sales pipeline

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/grok_xai_logo.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Security firm Claroc found more than 35,000 Grok-built apps open to anyone without login, including patient data and confidential SpaceXSI documents. The cause: "Anyone with the link" was preselected as the default access setting. SpaceXSI took only its own 24 employee apps offline. The rest remain accessible.</p>
