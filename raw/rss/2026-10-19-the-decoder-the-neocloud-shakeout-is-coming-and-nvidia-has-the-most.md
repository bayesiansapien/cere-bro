# The neocloud shakeout is coming, and Nvidia has the most to lose

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1080" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/NVIDIA-Vera-Rubin-NVL144-with-Tray.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1920" /></p>
<p>        After stock crashes and Firmus's scrapped IPO, industry executives expect only seven to ten of the hundreds of AI cloud providers to survive. CoreWeave lost a quarter of its market value, and Microsoft pulled out of a deal with Nscale for 23,000 Nvidia chips. Nvidia has a lot riding on the outcome, since customers outside the hyperscalers make up 45 percent of its data center revenue.</p>
