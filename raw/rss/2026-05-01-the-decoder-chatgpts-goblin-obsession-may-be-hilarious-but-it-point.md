# ChatGPT's goblin obsession may be hilarious, but it points to a deeper problem in AI training

<p>        A faulty reward signal during training caused ChatGPT models to start dropping goblins, gremlins, and other mythical creatures into their answers at a surprising rate. OpenAI says it's an example of how small, poorly tuned training incentives can produce unexpected side effects.</p>
