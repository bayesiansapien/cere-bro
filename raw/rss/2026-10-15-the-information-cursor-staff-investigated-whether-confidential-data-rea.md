# Cursor Staff Investigated Whether Confidential Data Reached SpaceX for AI Training

<p>In the weeks before SpaceX’s AI unit in August introduced Grok Bot, its version of a personal AI agent, engineers at Cursor discovered a potential problem.</p>

<p>Cursor, the AI coding startup <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/spacex-xai?rc=ndc4g0&amp;selected_employee=elon-musk">SpaceX</a> was then in the process of acquiring, was developing Grok Bot, and as part of that process, employees across Cursor were using the tool extensively for testing and as a part of their everyday work. In late July, engineers raised concerns that Cursor might have sent data from that internal testing&mdash;potentially including confidential information about its customers, suppliers and business partners&mdash;to SpaceX’s AI unit. One of Cursor’s lawyers warned that if word got out that the company was using the data for training, that could cause a “huge trust problem,” according to internal communications viewed by The Information.</p>
