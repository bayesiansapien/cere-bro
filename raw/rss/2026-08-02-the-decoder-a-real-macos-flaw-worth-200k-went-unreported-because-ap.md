# A real macOS flaw worth $200K went unreported because Apple's bug bounty inbox was full of AI slop

<p>        Apple's bug bounty program is drowning in AI-generated bug reports. The company has capped submissions per researcher because fabricated reports are clogging the review pipeline. As a result, Italian startup Bynario was initially unable to report a serious macOS vulnerability worth up to $200,000 on the black market.</p>
