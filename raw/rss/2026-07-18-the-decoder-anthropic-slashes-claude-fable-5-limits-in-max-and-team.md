# Anthropic slashes Claude Fable 5 limits in Max and Team Premium and pushes Pro users toward API pricing

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/claude_logo_money.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic will include Claude Fable 5 in Max and Team Premium plans starting July 20, but at just 50 percent of regular limits, which themselves drop by a third that same day. Pro users get a one-time $100 credit, then pay API rates. The reversal from Anthropic's original plan to pull Fable from subscriptions entirely likely comes down to competitive pressure from OpenAI's cheaper GPT-5.6 Sol.</p>
