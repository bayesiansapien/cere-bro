# Anthropic study shows AI needs hours, not weeks, to build exploits from security patches

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/glasswing_claude.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic's security team found that its Mythos Preview AI model can turn security patches for Firefox and the Windows kernel into working exploits within hours, for a few thousand dollars and no specialized knowledge. Eight complete attack chains were finished before Microsoft's auto-updates had reached a single device. The old patch rhythm is obsolete, Anthropic argues.</p>
