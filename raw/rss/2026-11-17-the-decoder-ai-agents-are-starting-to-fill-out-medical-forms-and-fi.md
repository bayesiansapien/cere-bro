# AI agents are starting to fill out medical forms and file insurance requests in place of human staff

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/openai_healthcare_agents.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI wants GPT-6 Astra and GPT-6 Luna to automate administrative tasks in US healthcare through computer-use agents that operate existing clinic software and insurance portals the way a human would. Luna handles tasks like prior authorizations for about 8.5 cents each. Companies including CVS Health and Cigna Healthcare are already deploying the technology.</p>
