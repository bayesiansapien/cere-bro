# Exclusive: Salesforce Acquires Startup Cresta for $2.7 Billion

<p>Salesforce extended its recent shopping spree, <a href="https://www.salesforce.com/news/stories/salesforce-signs-definitive-agreement-to-acquire-cresta/">announcing</a> on Monday plans to acquire Cresta, a nine-year-old provider of AI-powered contact center automation software. Salesforce didn’t disclose a price but it agreed to pay $2.7 billion for Cresta, according to a person familiar with the deal.</p> <p> ...</p>
