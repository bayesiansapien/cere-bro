# German AI consortium releases Soofi S, an open 30B model that tops benchmarks in both English and German

<p><img alt="Abstract illustration: Server data streams flow into cubes, symbolizing the LLM Nano Banana Pro against the silhouette of Munich." class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1025" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/soofi-s-german-llm-nano-banana-pro.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1920" /></p>
<p>        The German consortium behind the AI model Soofi S has acknowledged in version 3.0 of its tech report that test questions from the science benchmark GPQA accidentally ended up in the training data. The community caught the error by examining the publicly available data. The team removed the benchmark from its evaluation and recalculated all results.</p>
