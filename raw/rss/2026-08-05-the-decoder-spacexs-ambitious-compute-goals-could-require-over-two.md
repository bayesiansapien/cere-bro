# SpaceX’s ambitious compute goals could require over two million Nvidia Rubin GPUs

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/SpaceX-IPO.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        SpaceX plans to more than 5x its compute capacity by the end of 2027, betting exclusively on Nvidia's Vera Rubin platform. The expansion could require well over a million new GPUs. Meanwhile, the company's AI segment posted $2.56 billion in Q2 revenue, driven mostly by leasing out its own server capacity.</p>
