# Apple’s Slowdown

<p>Apple’s new CEO, John Ternus, has learned from the best. The script for his first earnings call on Monday was so full of marketing palaver about Apple products, and so lacking in information, that Ternus’ predecessor, Tim Cook, must have been glowing with pride. Perhaps after seeing investors freak out last week when other big tech firms reported earnings squeezed by AI investments, Ternus was determined to bore Wall Street into submission.</p><p>Alas! Finance chief Kevan Parekh woke listeners from their Ternus-induced slumber by projecting 7% to 10% growth in revenue for the December quarter, a marked slowdown from the 12% growth in the September period. Meanwhile, he projected a drop in Apple’s gross margin, thanks to rising costs of memory. Apple shares promptly traded down, falling as much as 4% in after-hours trading. Parekh blamed limited component supplies for the expected revenue slowdown: Apple can’t make as many of its devices as it could sell. But that may not be the only explanation.&nbsp;</p>
