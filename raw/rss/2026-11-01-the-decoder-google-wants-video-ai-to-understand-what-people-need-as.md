# Google wants video AI to understand what people need as they work

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1080" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/02-streamvibe-kochassistent-1920x1080-1.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1920" /></p>
<p>        A Google-led research team built StreamVibe, a video agent designed to watch people cook or assemble furniture and speak up only when help is needed. A benchmark shows that none of the tested models reliably pick the right moment in longer videos, as errors compound and response quality drops sharply.</p>
