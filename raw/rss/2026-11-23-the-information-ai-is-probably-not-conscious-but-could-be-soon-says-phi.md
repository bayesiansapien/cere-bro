# AI Is ‘Probably Not’ Conscious but Could Be Soon, Says Philosopher Rob Long

<p>AI models probably aren’t conscious now but “there’s a pretty good chance that they could be conscious in the next few years,” said Rob Long, a leading philosopher studying the subject, on the latest episode of AI Deep Dive. (<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BGXADuA5GTU">The full episode is available here</a>.)</p>

<p>Figuring out whether AI models have the kind of subjective experiences that constitute consciousness is quickly becoming a conundrum for the companies that train them. If they are conscious, and people fail to recognize that, mistreating them would be a moral catastrophe, said Long, who is the executive director of Eleos AI, a non-profit that researches AI consciousness and welfare. On the other hand, if people afford AI models undue moral consideration, that would hamstring developers’ ability to crack down on rogue AI agents, he said.</p>
