# Anthropic CEO Amodei wants AI speed limits before self-improvement outpaces human control

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/anthropic_amodei.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei is calling for a controlled slowdown in AI development. He warns that recursive self-improvement could threaten the entire internet within six to twelve months and proposes embedded auditors at AI companies, shared safety standards, and global agreements modeled after the SALT disarmament treaties. His warning comes just ahead of what could be the largest initial public offering in history.</p>
