# AI-generated influencers flood social media with pro-Trump content ahead of midterms

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/ai_fake_influencer_social_media.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Hundreds of AI avatars are flooding TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube with pro-Trump messaging. Some accounts have pulled in more than 35,000 followers and millions of views, and Trump himself has already shared AI-generated content. It's unclear whether this is the work of individual activists or a coordinated campaign.</p>
