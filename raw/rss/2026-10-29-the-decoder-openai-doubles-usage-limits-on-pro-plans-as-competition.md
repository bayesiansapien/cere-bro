# OpenAI doubles usage limits on Pro plans as competition with Anthropic heats up

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/openai_red_Logo.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI is doubling usage limits across its paid plans, from Pro 100 to Pro 500. A planned cut to the Pro 200 quota was reversed after user pushback. More efficient models like Terra and Sol should push usable capacity even higher. The move comes just ahead of Anthropic's IPO, as both companies fight for B2B dominance in AI.</p>
