# Suno launches v6 music models built with Warner, BMG, and Believe

<p><img alt="Feature image of the Suno v6 model with the &quot;v6&quot; lettering in bright orange and pink on a dark background; the Suno logo is in the upper left corner." class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1080" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/Suno_v6_Main.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1920" /></p>
<p>        Suno has unveiled a new AI music model generation, v6, in three versions, built together with Warner Music Group, BMG, and Believe. All older models are being shut down. Songs can now be partially changed through text commands or generated multimodally from text, audio, and images. The company won't say which catalogs went into training, while Universal and Sony keep suing.</p>
