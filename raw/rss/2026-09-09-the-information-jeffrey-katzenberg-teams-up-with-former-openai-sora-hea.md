# Jeffrey Katzenberg Teams Up with Former OpenAI Sora Head on New AI Video Startup

<p>Hollywood mogul Jeffrey Katzenberg is teaming up with the former head of OpenAI’s Sora app to launch a new AI startup that would train its own video models and try to win over filmmakers, people familiar with the matter said.</p>

<p>The founders have been talking to potential investors including Andreessen Horowitz about raising significant funding for the new company, the people said.</p>
