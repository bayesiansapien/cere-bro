# Qwen-Drive 1.0 tells you why it brakes, just don't expect the explanation to match the maneuver

<p><img alt="A teddy bear mascot steers a mini electric car against a backdrop of segmented street scenes, point clouds, and traffic maps (Qwen-Drive-1.0)." class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="941" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/qwen-drive-1.0-blog_banner.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1672" /></p>
<p>        Alibaba's research arm has released Qwen-Drive 1.0, an AI model that handles environmental perception, traffic Q&#038;A, and route planning in one system. The researchers show that text-image models don't automatically understand three-dimensional space. Spatial awareness has to be trained on purpose. The goal is a single model that runs both the cockpit and the driving system.</p>
