# Google Energy Chief: Space Data Centers Cost Up to 100 Times More Than Those on Earth

<p>Data centers in space could cost up to 100 times as much as those on Earth, Google's head of data center energy said this week, a reality check from a company that itself is testing AI server chips in orbit.</p><p>“Let’s explore all the terrestrial options before we go extraterrestrial,” Amanda Peterson Corio said at <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/everyones-using-ai-divisions-growing-wtf-highlights?rc=zjctrx">The Information’s WTF summit,</a>&nbsp; a gathering of senior women in the technology and finance industries. The comments came as SpaceX’s orbital data center plans underpin part of its roughly $1.8 trillion market capitalization.</p>
