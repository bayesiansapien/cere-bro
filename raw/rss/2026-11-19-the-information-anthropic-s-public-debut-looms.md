# Anthropic’s Public Debut Looms

<p>We all need a break from the Nvidia soap opera, so we’re switching things up today and talking about that other drama percolating in the background—Anthropic’s public debut. The company behind Claude <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/anthropic-prices-ipo-83-share?rc=9byxri">priced its initial public offering</a> on Wednesday night at $83 a share, several dollars above its preliminary pricing range, and at a level that values the whole company at around $1.5 trillion.</p><p>Seeing as this is the first top AI model developer to go public, not to mention the biggest, we’d bet the stock reaches $150 when it opens on Thursday, as everyone and their dog vies for a piece of the action. Of course, if they were smart, they’d hold back. The stock is more than likely to settle lower in the next few days, as the initial burst of exuberance dissipates. As for where the stock will trade in six months, I can’t guess.
