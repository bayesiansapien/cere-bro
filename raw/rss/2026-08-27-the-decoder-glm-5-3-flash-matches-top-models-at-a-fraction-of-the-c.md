# GLM-5.3-Flash matches top models at a fraction of the cost, and runs without Nvidia

<p><img alt="Grid of black squares with white Z logo against a blue color gradient, brand pattern by Zhipu AI." class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/zhipu-ai-logo-wall.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Z.ai releases GLM-5.3-Flash, an open-source model with 320 billion parameters that lands just three points behind the larger GLM-5.3 on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index, at a seventh of the cost. What's notable is that all of the inference traffic ran on Chinese AI chips instead of Nvidia hardware.</p>
