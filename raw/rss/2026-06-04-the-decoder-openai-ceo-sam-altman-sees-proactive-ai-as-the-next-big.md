# OpenAI CEO Sam Altman sees "proactive AI" as the next big phase after chatbots and agents

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="667" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/sam_altman_openai_screenshot.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1189" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI CEO Sam Altman outlines the next phase of AI products: a "proactive AI" that runs constantly in the background and acts on its own instead of waiting for user prompts. Companies are also wrestling with spiraling AI costs and a basic problem: most employees simply don't know what to ask AI. Altman's promise: "We can help people get more value for less spend."</p>
