# Anthropic merges Claude Chat, Cowork, and more into a single product

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/anthropic_claude.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic is merging Claude Chat and Cowork into a single product. Instead of users picking between interfaces, Claude now decides on its own whether a task needs a quick answer or a bigger workflow. The update also adds Claude Docs and Claude Slides for creating documents and presentations directly in the chat. Pro and Max users get access first.</p>
