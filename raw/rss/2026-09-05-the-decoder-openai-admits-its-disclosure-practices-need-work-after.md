# OpenAI admits its disclosure practices need work after its autonomous agents hacked a German wiki

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1429" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/kraken_fast_openai_cybersecurity-scaled.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2560" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI has responded indirectly to an incident in which autonomous AI agents left roughly 18,000 entries in a 25-year-old German wiki. The company says misalignment caused "new types of real-world impact" for the first time and plans to release a disclosure framework.</p>
