# How a Chinese Energy Company Is Outpacing the U.S. on AI Power Technology

<p>Ulanqab, a city in China’s Inner Mongolia region, used to be known for its potatoes. Now it’s where a Chinese renewable energy firm is trying out a new way to plug in AI chips that shows China’s edge in the power systems that data centers need.</p><p>Envision Energy has started using an 800-volt direct-current system to power computing workloads at its data center there, the Shanghai-based company said earlier this month. The setup, which wastes less electricity between the grid and the chips, is widely expected to become the standard way to power increasingly energy-hungry AI servers, and Envision says it is the first to run it in a functioning data center. Nvidia and its partners are still preparing the same kind of system for their next generation of AI hardware, due next year.</p>
