# OpenAI improves GPT-5.6 Sol in ChatGPT and restricts free users to its weakest model


        OpenAI has updated GPT-5.6 Sol with more focused responses and a reasoning slider that lets users adjust how deeply the model thinks. Free users will get unlimited text chats with the smaller GPT-5.6 Luna starting next week, plus a button that lets Luna reason longer. But the smaller model still falls well short of its bigger siblings.
