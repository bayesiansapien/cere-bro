# Nvidia Shares Are Priced For Everything To Go Wrong: That Makes No Sense

<p>Shares of Nvidia don’t trade like those of a company whose revenues are expected to rise 83% this year. Instead, the stock is priced as though everything that <em>could</em> go wrong in the next couple of years <em>will</em> go wrong. That creates an opportunity for investors willing to take a longer view.</p>

<p>Most semiconductor investors are chasing the stocks they think will grow the fastest over the next few years. So while shares of Nvidia have appreciated just 10% so far this year, distant rival Advanced Micro Devices is up 142%, while memory chip maker Micron, whose business has exploded thanks to demand from AI data centers, is up 213% year to date. The Philadelphia semiconductor index is up 71%.</p>
