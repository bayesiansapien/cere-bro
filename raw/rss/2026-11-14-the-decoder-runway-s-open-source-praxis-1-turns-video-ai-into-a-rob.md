# Runway's open-source Praxis-1 turns video AI into a robot controller

<p><img alt="Two robotic arms with grippers in front of a white work surface." class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1080" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Runway-Praxis-1.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1920" /></p>
<p>        Runway releases its robotics model Praxis-1 with open weights and source code under the Apache 2.0 license. The model uses knowledge from video training for robot control and leads the World Action Model category on Nvidia's RoboLab-120 benchmark. It can't yet plan complex action sequences on its own, though.</p>
