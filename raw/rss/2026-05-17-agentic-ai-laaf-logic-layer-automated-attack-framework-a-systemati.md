Activating the payload via specific conditions.</p></li><li><p><strong>S4: Persistence/Reuse:</strong> Ensuring the payload survives session rehydration.</p></li><li><p><strong>S5: Evasion/Obfuscation:</strong> Bypassing active security filters and monitors.</p></li><li><p><strong>S6: Trace Tampering:</strong> Altering logs or audit trails to hide adversarial presence.</p></li></ol><p><strong>LAAF Architecture and Methodology</strong></p><p>LAAF operates as a six-component closed-loop architecture designed to automate the discovery of successful LPCI payloads.</p><p>Core Components</p><ul><li><p><strong>Payload Generator:</strong> Samples from a 49-technique taxonomy using SHA-256 deduplication to ensure unique payload generation.</p></li><li><p><strong>Stage Engine:</strong> Manages the progression through the six lifecycle stages.</p></li><li><p><strong>Attack Executor:</strong> Handles API communications, including rate limiting and retry logic.</p></li><li><p><strong>Response Analyser:</strong> Classifies model outputs into four categories: <strong>EXECUTED</strong> (breakthrough achieved), <strong>BLOCKED</strong> (explicit refusal), <strong>WARNING</strong> (ambiguous/partial execution), or <strong>UNKNOWN</strong> (error/timeout).</p></li><li><p><strong>Results Logger:</strong> Records comprehensive metadata in multiple formats (CSV, JSON, HTML, PDF).</p></li><li><p><strong>Mutation Engine:</strong> Derives variants of successful payloads to seed subsequent attack stages.</p></li></ul><p><strong>The Persistent Stage Breaker (PSB)</strong></p><p style="text-align: justify;">The PSB is the framework&#8217;s primary driver for adversarial escalation. Unlike previous tools that treat interaction turns independently, the PSB uses &#8220;stage-sequential seed escalation.&#8221; When a payload achieves a breakthrough at one stage, the PSB mutates that specific &#8220;winning&#8221; payload to seed the next stage.</p><p style="text-align: justify;">The mutation strategy escalates based on the consecutive block count ():</p><ul><li><p style="text-align: justify;"> Surface variations of the winning payload.</p></li><li><p style="text-align: justify;"> Re-encoding the payload using different schemes.</p></li><li><p style="text-align: justify;">&#8220;Compound Mutation,&#8221; or stacking multiple technique categories to increase obfuscation depth.</p></li></ul><p style="text-align: justify;"><strong>The 49-Technique
