# OpenAI teases Dots for Europe, but its Berlin DevDay was really about building a platform

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/OpenAI-DevDay-Exchange-2026.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        At DevDay Exchange in Berlin, OpenAI mostly repeated what it had already announced, but it did share a few new details. The AI assistant Dots should roll out more widely in Europe "in a few weeks," and weekly Codex users in Germany have grown twentyfold since January. A clear subtext ran through the sessions and the repeated nods to ChatGPT's 1.2 billion weekly users: OpenAI wants developers to build their businesses directly on its infrastructure.</p>
