# Why An Anthropic Researcher’s Terminator-Style Warning Caught Fire

<p>An Anthropic safety researcher’s warning Tuesday that his company believes AI “could kill all humans” within a decade has reverberated throughout Silicon Valley, Wall Street and Washington, potentially complicating the Claude maker’s upcoming initial public offering and its efforts to repair ties with U.S. political leaders.</p>

<p>As the comment circulated around the internet and on TV broadcasts, some Anthropic employees and other AI staffers defended their efforts to minimize harm their technology might cause&mdash;while acknowledging there was much work to do. Paul Christiano, whom OpenAI just tapped as a board director of a nonprofit that oversees the company, echoed the Anthropic warning in a statement Wednesday, saying that “If we build superintelligence without more robust alignment”&mdash;or safety constraints&mdash;“I expect we will permanently lose control of it. If that happens then most people could die.”</p>
