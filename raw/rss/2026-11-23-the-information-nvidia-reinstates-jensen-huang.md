# Nvidia Reinstates Jensen Huang

<p>Nvidia reinstated Jensen Huang as CEO on Monday morning, after “senior levels of the United States government” assured Nvidia’s board that his reinstatement “poses no national security risk.”</p>

<p>The move is an extraordinary reversal by the company’s board, whose chair Harvey Jones just six days ago had ruled out bringing Huang back. But it followed days of pressure from President Donald Trump, employees and some investors, as well as other observers such as Elon Musk. Nvidia’s stock had fallen 48% from a high in early October to as low as $125 on Friday, wiping $2.4 trillion off its market value. The stock jumped 32% in early market trading on Monday.</p>
