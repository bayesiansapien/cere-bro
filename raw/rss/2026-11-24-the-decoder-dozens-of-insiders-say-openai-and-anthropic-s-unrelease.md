# Dozens of insiders say OpenAI and Anthropic's unreleased models have outgrown their safety measures

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/openai_anthropic.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        According to an Axios report, many employees at OpenAI and Anthropic believe the companies' own unreleased frontier models can no longer be safely controlled. Both companies are internally debating whether to halt releases or even stop training altogether, while employee trust in leadership is eroding.</p>
