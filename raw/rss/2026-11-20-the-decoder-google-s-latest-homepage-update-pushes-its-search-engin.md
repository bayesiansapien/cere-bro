# Google's latest homepage update pushes its search engine further toward an AI answer engine

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/google_gemini-1.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        Google is rolling out a collapsible sidebar globally on its Desktop Search homepage. In AI Mode, it lets users reopen recent AI searches, search threads, and create notebooks. Temporary threads that aren't saved to history are also launching worldwide. Google Search co-lead Robby Stein says the feature should make it easier for users to continue longer web research.</p>
