# Nvidia's open Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe combines transcription, translation, and speaker recognition

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/nvidia_logo.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        Nvidia has released Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe, an open speech recognition model that handles transcription, speaker recognition, and translation and takes its instructions as text prompts. It leads several benchmarks and uses only a sixth of the active parameters of its strongest open rival. Nvidia is also shipping a dataset with more than 800,000 hours of speech, along with tools for fine-tuning.</p>
