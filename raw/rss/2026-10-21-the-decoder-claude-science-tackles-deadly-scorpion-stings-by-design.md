# Claude Science tackles deadly scorpion stings by designing protein binders from scratch

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/claude_scorpion_toxines.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic's Claude independently designed protein binders targeting deadly scorpion neurotoxins. Out of more than 318,000 designs, 211 were tested in the lab. Seventeen met the criteria, and the two strongest bind extremely tightly to Cn2, a toxin from a Mexican scorpion. Whether they actually work as antivenom remains an open question.</p>
