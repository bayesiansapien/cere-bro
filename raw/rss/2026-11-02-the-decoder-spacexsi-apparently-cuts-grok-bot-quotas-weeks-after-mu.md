# SpaceXSI apparently cuts Grok Bot quotas weeks after Musk called it his fastest-growing product

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/grok_bot_costs.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        SpaceXSI is apparently cutting usage limits for its AI agent Grok Bot, less than two weeks after Musk called it his fastest-growing product. The operating costs were "unsustainably high relative to revenue," Musk wrote on X. OpenAI and Anthropic are also tightening their flat-rate plans as they grapple with the high costs of AI agents.</p>
