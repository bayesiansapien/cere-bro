# Jensen Huang's ouster likely traces back to Nvidia's risky business practices more than his personal life

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1024" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/nvidia_logo_glitch.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1536" /></p>
<p>        Jensen Huang likely left Nvidia more because of risky business practices than anything else, a Wall Street Journal report suggests. At the very least, those practices made him enemies internally. The company offered cloud providers billions in financial guarantees for chip purchases, including a planned $250 billion deal with OpenAI. Interim CEO and then-CFO Colette Kress tried to contain the risk, and Huang reportedly didn't like that.</p>
