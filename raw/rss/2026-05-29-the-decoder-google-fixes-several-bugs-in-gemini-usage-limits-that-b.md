# Google fixes several bugs in Gemini usage limits that burned through quotas too fast

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/google_gemini_optical_trick.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        A bug in Google's Gemini app caused just one or two Omni videos to eat up the entire usage quota. Google has fixed the bug, Ultra members now get twice as many video generations, and failed requests are no longer charged. Google also plans to add more transparency around other usage.</p>
