# You Have No Idea How Close the AI Bubble Is to Popping

<p>The AI industry has been unusually nervous this week. I&#8217;d like to <a href="https://www.thealgorithmicbridge.com/p/this-wasnt-supposed-to-happen-to">claim credit</a> for that but it was <a href="https://www.ft.com/content/11632d03-358c-454b-af95-d06d4072932b">The Financial Times</a> that got hold of Anthropic&#8217;s quarterly revenue figures from a private investor meeting: the company is making an obscene amount of money, growing at an obscene rate, and yet investors are panicking.</p><p>Anthropic has realized that if you promise the stars the Moon won&#8217;t suffice.</p><p>But let&#8217;s hear the numbers first.</p><p>$16+ billion revenue in a single quarter and 46% QoQ growth. Holy. Both figures are unprecedented for such a young company at this stage. Together, they might be unprecedented <em>in history </em>(maybe Apple). Not to mention the reported ~$74B in run-rate revenue. Anthropic is a beast of a business; not even OpenAI is that high.</p><p>What&#8217;s the problem then? Why are investors complaining about the GOAT? Because everything is unprecedented in AI, and so &#8220;unprecedented revenue&#8221; <em>is</em> <em>not enough.</em></p><p>By the way, <a href="https://blog.tickertrends.io/p/openai-and-anthropic-arr-tracking">TickerTrends nailed it</a> in the chart below. because, like Atlas, the world rests on its shoulders. <em>Isn&#8217;t that what you wanted, Dario? Don&#8217;t you fail us now&#8212;where&#8217;s that country of geniuses in a datacenter, let me see it! </em>In November 2025, I wrote that OpenAI was &#8220;<a href="https://www.thealgorithmicbridge.com/p/you-have-no-idea-how-screwed-openai">too big to fail</a>.&#8221; I meant that 1) the government wouldn&#8217;t let it fail but also that 2) were it to fail, <em>everything would fail with it.</em></p><p>The same rationale applies to Anthropic now, which took the role of industry lynchpin from OpenAI this year. Tragically, it&#8217;s failing to rise to the occasion.</p><p>Naive extrapolations suggest Anthropic will fall short of its $100B run rate revenue target. Q4 must see an unlikely re-acceleration of revenue growth if they want to appease investors. Most likely, though, it will keep plateauing. I don&#8217;t think Fable 5.5&#8212;Anthropic&#8217;s ace up the sleeve, <a href="https://x.com/AndrewCurran_/status/2110753908482281691">coming very soon</a>&#8212;can move the needle; which use cases will it unlock that Opus 5.5 didn&#8217;t? That&#8217;s the key question: is the <em>marginal gain</em> from a new model enough to put the company back into exponential growth?</p><p>I just don&#8217;t see it.</p><p>Suddenly, the <a href="https://www.reuters.com/business/finance/anthropics-q3-revenue-surges-45-nearly-17-billion-2026-10-14/">$2 trillion valuation</a> that some of Anthropic&#8217;s backers are hoping for at IPO seems too much. As do the <a href="https://www.reuters.com/business/anthropics-518-billion-ai-buildout-hinges-largely-deals-that-cannot-be-canceled-2026-09-29/">$518 billion in infrastructure commitments</a> it has made for the next decade (80% non-cancelable). It also seems bad that the AI boom is <a href="https://www.wsj.com/economy/the-ai-build-out-is-becoming-the-biggest-economic-bet-in-u-s-history-c60716dd">the biggest in history</a>, above railroads, dot-com, canals, etc.</p><div class="captioned-image-container"><figure><a class="image-link image2 is-viewable-img" href="https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!5iDZ!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Faf9e22c1-b97f-4735-826f-edb09c32525b_735x525.webp" target="_blank"><div class="image2-inset"><source type="image/webp" /><img alt="A Financial Times chart of BIS data, 'How the AI boom compares with boom-bust cycles of past major innovations', showing investment as a multiple of its pre-boom trough.
