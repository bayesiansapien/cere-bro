# Datadog Offered $4 Billion to Buy Cloud Security Startup ‘Upwind’

<p>Enterprise software firm Datadog recently offered to buy Israeli cloud cybersecurity startup Upwind Security for nearly $4 billion, according to someone with direct knowledge of the talks, the latest sign of white-hot interest in such firms.</p>

<p>Upwind has fielded other takeover attempts from multiple bidders in recent months but hasn’t accepted any offers. It is finalizing a $300 million funding round at a roughly $3.8 billion valuation, meaning the Datadog offer doesn’t carry much of a premium over the private valuation, said two people with knowledge of the situation. The startup sells software to keep tabs on applications, including so-called AI agents, that run in the cloud and to detect and automatically respond to potential evidence of hacks or breaches.</p>
