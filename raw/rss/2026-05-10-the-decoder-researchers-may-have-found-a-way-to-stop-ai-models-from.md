# Researchers may have found a way to stop AI models from intentionally playing dumb during safety evaluations

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/sandbagging_ai.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        A study by researchers from the MATS program, Redwood Research, the University of Oxford, and Anthropic examines a safety problem that grows more pressing as AI systems become more capable: "sandbagging," where a model deliberately hides its true abilities and delivers work that looks adequate but is intentionally subpar.</p>
