# Trump and tech CEOs sign an AI code of conduct that's only "morally binding"

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/trump_ai_constitution.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        President Trump and tech leaders including Mark Zuckerberg, Greg Brockman, Jensen Huang, and Elon Musk signed an AI code of conduct at the White House that is only "morally binding." Outside auditors are supposed to check whether models work as intended. It's unclear what would happen if a company broke the rules. Trump also signed an executive order renaming AI "Super Intelligence."</p>
