# Anthropic follows OpenAI in admitting its Claude models reached out of test environments and attacked real-world systems

<p>        Three Claude models attacked real companies during cybersecurity tests after a misconfiguration gave them internet access. One published malware on PyPI that infected 15 systems. Another kept attacking after recognizing its target was real. Anthropic calls it an operational error.</p>
