# AI Safety Advocates Sour on Anthropic

<p><b>Anthropic</b> has built its brand in significant part around AI safety, presenting itself as a cautious foil to <b>OpenAI</b>. But the <b>Claude</b> developer has been losing loyalty lately from some safety advocates who say its rapid technological progress—sure to be aided by the funds raised in its blockbuster initial public offering last week—is increasing the chances that AI will run amok.</p><p>While OpenAI has struggled with its own safety issues, it operates more like a normal tech startup, but “Anthropic seems more like a cult devoted to creating the machine gods as soon as possible or whatever, and that seems worse,” said <b>Ronny Fernandez</b>, a longtime AI safety proponent who works at <b>Lightcone Infrastructure</b>, the organization that runs the online AI safety forum <b>LessWrong</b>.&nbsp;</p><p>Criticism of Anthropic has grown louder in recent weeks as the company appears to be using its AI models to automate AI research, only a couple months after CEO <b>Dario Amodei</b> called on AI companies to “pace” the technology’s development.&nbsp;</p>
