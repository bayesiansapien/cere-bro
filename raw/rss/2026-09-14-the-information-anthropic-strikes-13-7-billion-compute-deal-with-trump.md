# Anthropic Strikes $13.7 Billion Compute Deal With Trump-Linked Rum Group

<p>Anthropic has signed a computing deal worth $13.7 billion over six years with Rum Group, a company with roots in social media and long-standing ties to the Trump administration, according to a person familiar with the matter.</p>

<p>The deal is the latest in a spate of cloud computing agreements Anthropic struck in the past year, with major cloud providers like Google, tech companies such as SpaceX and <a href="https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-26/anthropic-to-pay-nscale-45-billion-for-ai-computing-power">smaller neoclouds such as Nscale,</a> amid skyrocketing demand for its Claude Code and Cowork products. The agreements total at least 14.8 gigawatts of compute capacity, <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/anthropic-clinched-517-billion-compute-deals-11-months">costing as much as $517 billion over the next decade.</a> The deal with Rum would add to that total.</p>
