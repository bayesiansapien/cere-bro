# Meta's Hatch AI agent could cost up to $200 a month and marks its first paid AI product

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/meta_ai_logo_wall_muse.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Meta is developing a paid AI agent product called "Hatch" that could cost up to $200 per month. Users describe what they need in simple language, and Hatch builds working tools, schedules appointments, or sends emails. CEO Mark Zuckerberg sees the product as a way to open up new revenue streams beyond advertising and refinance the company's massive AI investments.</p>
