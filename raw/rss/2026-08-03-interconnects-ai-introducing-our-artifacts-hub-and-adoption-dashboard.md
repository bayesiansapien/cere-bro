# Introducing our Artifacts Hub and Adoption Dashboard

<h5><em>No voiceover for this quick post, but a quick video version is <a href="https://youtu.be/vSXTiEepGqM">here</a>.</em></h5><p>We&#8217;re expanding our open models coverage into standalone projects that let you go deeper on the state of the open model ecosystem. The new free sources of data are:</p><ol><li><p>The <a href="https://artifactshub.ai/">Artifacts Hub</a> &#8212; a curated view of the models trending on Hugging Face, highlighting inference tokens via <a href="https://openrouter.ai/">Open Router</a>, model intelligence via <a href="https://artificialanalysis.ai/">Artificial Analysis</a>, and our <a href="https://atomproject.ai/relative-adoption-metric">tailored adoption metrics</a> building on top of <a href="https://huggingface.co/">Hugging Face</a>&#8217;s data.</p></li><li><p>Our <a href="https://dashboard.interconnects.ai/">Adoption Dashboard</a> &#8212; a living dashboard of download and derivative model numbers by geography and organization. This highlights the US-China gap and growing players in the open ecosystem.</p></li></ol><p>To date, our primary efforts on Interconnects have been release recaps for popular models like <a href="https://www.interconnects.ai/p/kimi-k3-the-open-weights-escalation">Kimi K3</a>, <a href="https://www.interconnects.ai/p/glm-52-is-the-step-change-for-open">GLM 5.2</a>, <a href="https://www.interconnects.ai/p/deepseek-r1-recipe-for-o1">DeepSeek R1</a>, etc. and monthly round-ups of the open models that matter, <a href="https://www.interconnects.ai/t/artifacts-log">Artifacts Log</a>. We&#8217;re expanding on these, building on the tools and internal data we&#8217;ve collected for other projects like <a href="https://atomproject.ai/">The ATOM Project</a> (and <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07190">report</a>). This allows us to capture our ecosystem view of open models, develop methods for understanding adoption of giant MoE models, and everything in between. We&#8217;re sharing them freely to help the open ecosystem find its strengths and grow.</p><p class="button-wrapper"><a class="button primary" href="https://artifactshub.ai/"><span>Visit the Artifacts Hub</span></a></p><p>The Artifacts Hub right now covers 792 models released in the last two years, across the core text-focused language models and multimodal generative models. At Interconnects we follow the data of every model on Hugging Face, analyze the core few thousand LLMs (this <a href="https://github.com/Interconnects-AI/tracked-models">list</a> is public on GitHub
