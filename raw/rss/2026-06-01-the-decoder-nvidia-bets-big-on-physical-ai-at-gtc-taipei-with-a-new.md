# Nvidia bets big on physical AI at GTC Taipei with a new world model, driving brain, and open humanoid robot

<p>        Nvidia used GTC Taipei to launch a series of models for robots, autonomous vehicles, and video systems. The centerpieces are the new world model Cosmos 3, a significantly scaled-up driving model called Alpamayo 2 Super, and an open reference platform for humanoid robots.</p>
