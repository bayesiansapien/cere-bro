# Zuckerberg's Biohub leads a $1.8 billion push to build AI models that predict cell behavior

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/biohub_investment.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Biohub, the research organization backed by Mark Zuckerberg and Priscilla Chan, is coordinating a $1.8 billion initiative to train AI models that predict cell behavior. Meta, Google DeepMind, Isomorphic Labs, and the US Department of Energy are funding data, lab equipment, and compute. A first dataset should be ready in about a year.</p>
