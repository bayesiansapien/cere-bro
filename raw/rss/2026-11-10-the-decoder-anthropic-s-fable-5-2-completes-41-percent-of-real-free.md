# Anthropic's Fable 5.2 completes 41 percent of real freelance jobs, up from 2.5 percent a year ago

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Remote-labor-index-bench-title.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic's Fable 5.2 completes 41.3 percent of real freelance jobs at professional quality on the Remote Labor Index, more than double the next-best systems Opus 5.5 and GPT 6 Astra. A year ago the top score was 2.5 percent. Areas like product design and animation remain a weak spot.</p>
