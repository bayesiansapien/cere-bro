# Google's Lyria 3.5 music model now lets users edit individual track sections without starting over

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1080" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/google_lyria_35.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1920" /></p>
<p>        Google released Lyria 3.5, its new music generation model, and built it into Google Flow Music. The model generates tracks between 30 seconds and 3 minutes long. A new feature called "Selective Section Painting" lets users edit specific sections of a track. Google still hasn't shared any details about the training data.</p>
