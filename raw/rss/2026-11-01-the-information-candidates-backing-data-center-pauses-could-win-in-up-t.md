# Candidates Backing Data Center Pauses Could Win in Up to 15 Governor’s Races

<p>As data centers have gone from obscurity to the top of voters’ minds, midterm candidates are taking a stance on the issue. The fight is most heated in races for governor, the office that has shown the most power to limit data centers on a broad scale. Whichever candidates win, more doors are likely to close on the industry, leaving it with fewer places to build and higher costs where it still can.</p>

<p>In 21 of the 36 states electing a governor on Tuesday, at least one major-party nominee backs a data center moratorium, according to The Information’s analysis of candidate platforms and public statements. Both nominees back pauses in five states (Michigan, New Hampshire, Ohio, Oregon and Texas), and a candidate promising a pause is likely to win in three (Maine, New Mexico and New York), based on recent polls. In seven&nbsp;more, it could be a close race between a candidate who backs a pause and one who doesn’t (Alaska, Florida, Georgia, Iowa,&nbsp;Kansas,&nbsp;Wisconsin and Vermont). Those 15&nbsp;states are home to more than a third of the U.S. population.</p>
