# OpenAI and the Trump administration are negotiating a government stake in the AI startup

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/openai_anthropic_white_house.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI and the Trump administration are negotiating a direct government stake in the AI startup. The idea is a "Public Wealth Fund" that would pay out directly to American citizens. Senator Bernie Sanders wants to push through a 50 percent tax on AI shares by law. Critics fear the arrangement could create a "too big to fail" dynamic similar to the 2008 financial crisis.</p>
