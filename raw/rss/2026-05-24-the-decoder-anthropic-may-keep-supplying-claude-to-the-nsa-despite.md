# Anthropic may keep supplying Claude to the NSA despite being flagged as a supply chain risk by the Pentagon

<p>        Anthropic will likely keep supplying AI models to the NSA despite being labeled a "supply chain risk." Intelligence agencies lack Nvidia's latest Grace Blackwell chips, and Anthropic's "Mythos" model reportedly runs on older hardware too. The controversial "any lawful use" clause that derailed earlier talks is not part of the deal.</p>
