# ByteDance to Buy Back Shares at $400 Billion Valuation

<p>ByteDance, the Chinese owner of TikTok, is planning to buy back up to $6 billion worth of shares from existing investors at a valuation of about $400 billion, a 20% increase in the valuation used in its last buyback a year ago, according to a letter to shareholders seen by The Information and a person familiar with the plans.</p>

<p>The increase in <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/bytedance">ByteDance</a>’s valuation comes after the company completed a deal with the Trump administration in January to prevent a U.S. ban of TikTok. In the deal, ByteDance sold an 80% stake in TikTok’s U.S. data security operation to an American-owned joint venture, while retaining the app’s revenue-generating operations.</p>
