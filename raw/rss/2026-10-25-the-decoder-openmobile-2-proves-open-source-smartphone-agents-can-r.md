# OpenMobile-2 proves open-source smartphone agents can rival proprietary models

<p><img alt="A smartphone surrounded by icons (camera, shopping, music, video, calendar, cloud, map) on a blue background." class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1047" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/open-mobile-2-smartphone-app-training-nano-banana-pro.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1920" /></p>
<p>        Smartphone agents don't get better by processing more data. They improve by training on as many different apps as possible. The researchers behind OpenMobile-2 built an open training environment with more than 110 applications, including 35 replicas of popular apps. On real Android devices, their model scores highest among open agents, the authors say.</p>
