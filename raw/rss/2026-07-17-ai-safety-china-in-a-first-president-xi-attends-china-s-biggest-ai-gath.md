# In a first, President Xi attends China’s biggest AI gathering

<p><span>China&#8217;s most influential AI conference just kicked off today: the 2026 World AI Conference (WAIC) is taking place in Shanghai from July 17-20. This year&#8217;s event just got even more prestigious. President Xi Jinping </span><a href="https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/S5YCn6Fa2OZvjyFSfkaBcQ"><span>attended in person</span></a><span> for the first time, underscoring the rising prominence of AI on the national agenda.</span></p><p><span>We&#8217;re excited to share that Concordia AI will be hosting the </span><strong><a href="https://www.worldaic.com.cn/events/forum/2070aa321a554018bc75f675dab1479c"><span>Frontier and Agentic AI Safety Forum</span></a><span> as an official part of the 2026 WAIC on July 19</span></strong><span>.</span></p><h2><span>What is WAIC?</span></h2><p><span>The World AI Conference (WAIC) is China&#8217;s largest and most influential AI conference, held annually since 2018. It brings together senior Chinese government leaders, industry executives, and renowned experts from across the globe.</span></p><p><span>WAIC&#8217;s political calibre has consistently risen over the years:</span></p><ul><li><p><span>President Xi sent a congratulatory letter to the inaugural 2018 conference, where Vice Premier LIU He (&#21016;&#40548;) delivered a speech.</span></p></li><li><p><span>From 2019 to 2023, the most senior in-person attendee was the Shanghai Party Secretary.</span></p></li><li><p><span>In 2024 and 2025, Premier LI Qiang (&#26446;&#24378;), China&#8217;s second-ranked official, delivered opening remarks.</span></p></li><li><p><span>This year marks a new milestone: President Xi Jinping attended in person for the first time and </span><a href="https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/S5YCn6Fa2OZvjyFSfkaBcQ"><span>delivered a speech</span></a><span> at the opening ceremony &#8212; significantly raising the conference&#8217;s status.</span></p></li></ul><p><span>For foreign stakeholders, WAIC is a key event to watch, and a critical opportunity to engage with Chinese counterparts.</span></p><h2><span>Join us: Concordia AI&#8217;s Frontier and Agentic AI Safety Forum</span></h2><p><span>This year&#8217;s WAIC features more than 140 thematic forums, typically running a half or full day &#8212; including multiple forums on AI safety and governance. The full agenda is on the </span><a href="https://www.worldaic.com.cn/events/forum"><span>official conference website</span></a><span>.</span></p><p><span>Among them, Concordia AI is proud to host the Frontier and Agentic AI Safety Forum.</span></p><p><span>&#128197; Date: July 19<br />&#128344; Time: 8:45-12:30 (UTC+8)<br />&#128205; Venue: Thompson Shanghai Expo, 3/F Function Room 1 and 2<br />&#128187; Online livestream: In the official </span><a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/hi-waic/id6760387830"><span>WAIC App</span></a><span>. </span></p><p><a href="https://aisafetychina.substack.com/p/concordia-ai-holds-the-ai-safety"><span>Last year</span></a><span>, our forum drew more than 200 in-person attendees and 14,000+ livestream views. This year, we&#8217;re bringing together another outstanding lineup of leading scientists, policy experts, and industry voices from China and around the world to discuss frontier AI safety and global governance &#8212; including Alondra Nelson (former acting director of the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy), Amandeep Singh Gill (United Nations Secretary-General&#8217;s Envoy on Technology), XUE Lan (&#34203;&#28572;) (Tsinghua University), and ZHOU Bowen (&#21608;&#20271;&#25991;) (Shanghai AI Laboratory) alongside former researchers from OpenAI, Google DeepMind, and Anthropic, and senior experts from Alibaba, the Frontier Model Forum, Singapore&#8217;s Infocomm Media Development Authority, Hugging Face, and the China Academy of Information and Communications Technology (CAICT).</span></p><h2><span>Full agenda</span></h2><div class="captioned-image-container"><figure><a class="image-link image2" href="https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!1GO4!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7f534c6d-d199-45bc-82e7-8b6cc32150a7_1406x7500.png" target="_blank"><div class="image2-inset"><source type="image/webp" /><img alt="" class="sizing-normal" src="https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!1GO4!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F7f534c6d-d199-45bc-82e7-8b6cc32150a7_1406x7500.png" /><div></div></div></a></figure></div><p></p><div class="subscription-widget-wrap-editor"><div class="subscription-widget show-subscribe"><div class="preamble"><p class="cta-caption">Thanks for reading AI Safety in China!
