# AI beats Stratego's greatest player, ending one of the last human strongholds in board games

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Stratego-Ataraxos-title.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        The AI system Ataraxos has decisively beaten the best Stratego player of all time. The board game is a tough test for AI because both sides set up their pieces face down. Google Deepmind fell short in 2023 despite a multimillion-dollar budget. Researchers from Carnegie Mellon, NYU, Stanford, and MIT built Ataraxos for less than $8,000.</p>
