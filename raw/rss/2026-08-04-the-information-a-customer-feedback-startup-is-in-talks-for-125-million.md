# A Customer Feedback Startup Is in Talks For $125 Million Financing Led By Anthropic Investor

<p>AI that generates realistic voices has come a long way. It powers applications like <b>ChatGPT</b>, phone-based customer support and increasingly helps businesses solicit verbal feedback from their customers about products or experiences.</p><p>One AI startup in that field is San Francisco-based <b>Listen Labs</b>, which helps businesses automate interviews with existing or potential customers. The startup is raising $125 million of funding at a valuation of around $1.5 billion, according to a person with knowledge of the talks. <b>Menlo Ventures</b>, a large <b>Anthropic</b> backer, is in talks to lead the round, the person said.&nbsp;</p><p>If completed, the new round would triple the startup’s valuation from a financing round it announced just seven months ago. It has already raised $100 million in funding from investors including <b>Ribbit Capital</b>, <b>Sequoia Capital</b> and <b>Conviction Partners</b>.</p>
