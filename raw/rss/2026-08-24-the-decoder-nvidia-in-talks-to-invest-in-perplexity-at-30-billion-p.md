# Nvidia in talks to invest in Perplexity at $30 billion-plus valuation

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/nvidia_logo.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        Nvidia is negotiating an investment in Perplexity at a valuation above $30 billion, more than 50 percent higher than its last funding round, The Information reports. Perplexity's annualized revenue has tripled to over $750 million. Much of Nvidia's investment money tends to flow back as revenue when portfolio companies buy its chips.</p>
