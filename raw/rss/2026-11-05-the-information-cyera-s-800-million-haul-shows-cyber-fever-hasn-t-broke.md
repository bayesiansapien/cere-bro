# Cyera’s $800 Million Haul Shows Cyber Fever Hasn’t Broken

<p>Last week my colleagues Jemima and Valida <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/exclusive-cybersecurity-mania-push-cyera-valuation-20-billion?rc=geicgp">scooped</a> that Cyera, a five-year-old cloud security startup, was mulling a new fundraise at an eye-popping $20 billion valuation, up from $12 billion in June.</p><p>Since then, those talks have gotten more serious, I’ve learned. Cyera is indeed planning to raise around $800 million in a round led by Cyberstarts, which wrote the startup’s first seed check in 2021, according to two people with knowledge of the deal. </p>
