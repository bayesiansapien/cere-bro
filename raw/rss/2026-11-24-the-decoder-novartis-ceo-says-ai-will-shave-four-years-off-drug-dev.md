# Novartis CEO says AI will shave four years off drug development, not cure all disease

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/claude_novartis.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Novartis CEO Vas Narasimhan, who also sits on Anthropic's board, is pushing back on the cure-all promises from Anthropic CEO Amodei and DeepMind founder Hassabis. AI will speed up drug development, he says, but it won't beat cancer in a decade. The real value lies "somewhere in the middle."</p>
