# Nvidia’s Board Should Come Clean on Huang’s Resignation

<p>Memo to the board of Nvidia: Folks, you need to explain why you pressured Jensen Huang to resign. Otherwise, <a href="https://www.wsj.com/tech/the-campaign-to-bring-back-jensen-huang-as-nvidia-ceo-e91b0b2c?mod=hp_lead_pos1">a bring-back-Jensen movement</a> will only grow and could theoretically escalate to the point where it threatens your roles on Nvidia’s board. Even <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/16/us/politics/trump-jensen-huang-nvidia.html">President Donald Trump</a> has weighed in, supporting Huang. The board could use Nvidia’s earnings update on Tuesday to provide everyone with some answers.</p><p>As quixotic as the Jensen loyalists’ effort may seem right now, some of their complaints are on the money. A letter from investors Brad Gerstner and Gavin Baker <a href="https://x.com/GavinSBaker/status/2121401397346521412?a=3NQwtzibzPKzjQOv_wI&amp;s=20">posted on X</a> on Friday, for instance, made the point that there was “massive uncertainty associated with recent events surrounding” Huang’s departure. The pair were correct to say that a “slow, measured and deliberate process regarding the future leadership of Nvidia” was in everyone’s best interests.</p>
