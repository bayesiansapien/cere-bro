# White House reverses course and restores UK AI Safety Institute's access to frontier models

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/aisi_us_access.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        The White House has reversed course and restored the UK's AI Security Institute's early access to AI models from Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and OpenAI. In September, the Trump administration had told the companies to stop sharing their models with the British agency. Diplomatic pressure from AI Minister Kanishka Narayan led to the reversal.</p>
