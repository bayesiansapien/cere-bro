# Nvidia’s Earnings on Deck; Anthropic’s Public Debut

<p>If life was simple, the biggest question this week for people in tech would be where Anthropic shares open when the AI firm goes public. (Above $150 a share? See below.) Instead, we can’t rule out that more news breaks in the Nvidia drama and overwhelms Anthropic’s week in the sun. That news could be anything from reports of other women who had affairs with the now-departed CEO, Jensen Huang to developments in <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/huang-weighs-nvidia-comeback-bessent-pushes-board-look-outside?rc=9byxri">the campaign</a> to <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/thousands-nvidia-staff-sign-petition-bring-back-huang?rc=9byxri">bring him back.</a>&nbsp;Huang, meanwhile, has <a href="https://foreignaffairs.house.gov/news/press-releases/chairmen-mast-moolenaar-and-crawford-request-jensen-huang-brief-congress-on-link-to-chinese-intelligence-asset">agreed to testify</a> before Congress, which could happen this week.</p><p>Of course, even without such developments, Nvidia will be in the news. The AI chip designer on Tuesday is scheduled to report earnings for its third fiscal quarter, ending in late October. Those earnings will remind us all of why it is such a star company: It has projected revenue growth of around 89%, continuing a reacceleration of growth this fiscal year from last year’s “slowdown” (when revenue grew 66%). What we’ll be focused on more than the numbers will be comments from Nvidia’s interim CEO, Colette Kress. While as finance chief she’s a fixture on these calls, on Tuesday she makes her first public appearance in charge of the company since Huang’s exit.</p>
