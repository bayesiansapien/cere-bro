# Chinese Open Models Win Over One in Five of The Information’s Subscribers

<p>Nearly two-thirds of The Information’s subscribers surveyed last month said their organizations develop AI applications, and a third of subscribers said they receive a return from AI that is a “multiple of what we spend.” The result suggests enterprises have made some progress since the Massachusetts Institute of Technology <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/newsletters/applied-ai/ai-native-apps-18-5-billion-annualized-revenues-rebut-mits-skeptical-study?rc=c48ukx">study</a> last year that indicated just 5% of custom-made enterprise AI tools reached production.</p><p>The new result, first shared during <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/open-source-model-price-cuts-keep-ai-costs-control?rc=c48ukx">AI Agenda Live last month</a>, likely reflects recent advancements, including AI models’ ability to access other applications to handle complex tasks, work in the background for hours at a time, and the general improvement of AI-produced code. It could also reflect The Information’s subscriber base, which is weighted toward enterprises that have experience developing software.</p>
