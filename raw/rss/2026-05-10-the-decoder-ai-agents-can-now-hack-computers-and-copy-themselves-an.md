# AI agents can now hack computers and copy themselves, and they're getting better fast

<p>        Palisade Research shows that AI agents can hack remote computers, copy themselves onto them, and form replication chains. In one year, the success rate jumped from 6 to 81 percent. The researchers expect remaining barriers to fall as models get better at hacking.</p>
