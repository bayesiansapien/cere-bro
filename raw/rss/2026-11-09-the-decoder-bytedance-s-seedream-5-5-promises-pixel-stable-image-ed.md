# Bytedance's Seedream 5.5 promises pixel-stable image editing across 100 rounds without color drift

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1287" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/seedance_examples-scaled.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2560" /></p>
<p>        Bytedance Seed has introduced Seedream 5.5, an image model built for professional production workflows. It supports stable multi-pass editing over up to 100 iterations, generates native RGBA images with transparent backgrounds, and, according to Bytedance, outperforms GPT-Image-2.5 Sunburst in realism. A Pro version is planned.</p>
