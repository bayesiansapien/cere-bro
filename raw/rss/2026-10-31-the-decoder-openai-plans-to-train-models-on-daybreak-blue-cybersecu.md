# OpenAI plans to train models on Daybreak Blue cybersecurity session data that was previously off-limits

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/openai.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Starting November 29, OpenAI will use data from Daybreak Blue sessions to train its models, even when users have turned off training. After backlash, a follow-up email promised a separate opt-out. The move revives concerns from the dispute with mathematicians who suspected OpenAI used their unpublished research for training without consent.</p>
