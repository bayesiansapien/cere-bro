# Anthropic says AI is eroding Nvidia's CUDA lead, cutting an AMD chip switch from months to three days

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/nvidia_moat.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic claims it used AI itself to breach Nvidia's CUDA moat. The company's in-house model Mythos reportedly ported Claude to AMD GPUs in just three days, with almost no human intervention. Anthropic now runs Claude on four chip families and aims to cut costs and reduce vendor dependencies.</p>
