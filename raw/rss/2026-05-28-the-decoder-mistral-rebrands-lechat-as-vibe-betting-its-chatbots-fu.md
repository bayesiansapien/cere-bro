# Mistral rebrands LeChat as Vibe, betting its chatbot's future is as a full-blown work agent

<p><img alt="Stylized chat icon as a cat&#039;s head in front of a human silhouette made of cables and circuits in bright colors" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1047" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/le-chat-agent-surf-wave-nano-banana-pro.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1920" /></p>
<p>        Mistral AI is renaming its chatbot Le Chat to Vibe and bundling chat, coding agents and a new Work Mode under one brand. The Work Mode docks onto Google Workspace, Outlook, Slack or GitHub and processes tasks such as emails, reports or pull requests independently. Mistral has not specified any concrete usage limits though. The company is thus positioning itself more directly against the agent-based offerings from OpenAI, Google and Anthropic.</p>
