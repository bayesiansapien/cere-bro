# Jensen Owes His Job to Trump

<p>If Jensen Huang was <a href="https://www.nytimes.com/2026/11/20/technology/jensen-huang-nvidia-culture.html">a tough taskmaster</a> at Nvidia before, just imagine what he’s going to be like now! President Donald Trump <a href="https://truthsocial.com/@realDonaldTrump/117666141823845971">has proclaimed</a> Huang’s reinstatement, <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/nvidia-reinstates-jensen-huang?rc=9byxri">announced Monday morning,</a> was vital to America’s “success and prosperity.” In other words, Huang may feel he’s untouchable. His only problem is that he now <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/trump-put-jensen-huang-back-charge-nvidia?rc=9byxri">owes his job to Trump.</a>&nbsp;Just imagine what the newly restored CEO’s relations with the president will be like in the future.&nbsp;</p><p>What will Trump ask for in return? You know he’ll want his pound of flesh. Will Trump ask Huang to give the government an equity stake in Nvidia? Trump has done that <a href="https://www.cato.org/blog/government-ownership-stakes-companies-becoming-routine-under-trump">with other companies</a> (in addition to asking for a <a href="https://www.politico.com/news/2026/06/05/ai-companies-white-house-profit-sharing-00952167">cut of revenue</a> from transactions in which government approval was needed). Or will the government ask to put people on Nvidia’s board? Seaport analyst Jay Goldberg said on The Information’s <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/titv?rc=9byxri">TITV on Monday</a>: “I could easily see the…administration making the argument that…Nvidia is very important to U.S. national security so maybe we should keep a closer look on it and have a board member.”&nbsp;</p>
