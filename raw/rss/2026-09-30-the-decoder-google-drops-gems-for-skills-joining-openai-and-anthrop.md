# Google drops Gems for Skills, joining OpenAI and Anthropic in the shift to agent-ready prompt formats

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/google_gemini-1.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        Google is replacing Gems with "Skills" in Gemini chat. Skills are detailed, reusable prompts that users invoke by typing "/" or that Gemini runs automatically. The format is based on an open standard from Anthropic. Gems will be phased out starting in November, with existing ones migrating automatically.</p>
