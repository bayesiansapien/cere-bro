# Anthropic plans fund for workers threatened by AI, Musk counters with SpaceX shares for children

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1125" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Anthropic-Public-Benefit-Fund-2000x1125-1.webp" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2000" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic wants to put five percent of its shares into a new public benefit fund that will also look into direct payments to workers at risk of losing their jobs. The fund is a company-owned LLC, so Anthropic keeps control of it as a possible IPO approaches. By offering the option of direct payments, Anthropic goes further than its competitors. Elon Musk says on X the company should just give the shares directly to the public.</p>
