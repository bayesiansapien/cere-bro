# Gemini 4 Carbon takes the MathArena crown but costs 15 times more than GPT-6.1 Sol

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="783" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/gemini-4_carbon_logo.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1397" /></p>
<p>        For the first time in a while, no OpenAI model leads the MathArena AI math benchmark. Google's Gemini 4 Carbon and Anthropic's Fable 5.2 have knocked GPT-6.1 Sol from the top, though both cost significantly more. Gemini ignored the time limit in testing and almost never gave up on a task.</p>
