# Bloomberg analysis of 10 million job postings shows AI-exposed roles in China fell 10 percentage points faster than others

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/china_flag_visual-1.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        A Bloomberg News analysis of 10 million job postings suggests AI is compounding China's already weak labor market. Entry-level workers and the creative industry are feeling the shift most, with more than 90% of Chinese micro-dramas now produced using AI. Former central bank adviser Cai Fang warns that a larger AI shock is still ahead.</p>
