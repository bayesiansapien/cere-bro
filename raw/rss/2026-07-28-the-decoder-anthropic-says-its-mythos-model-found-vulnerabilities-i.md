# Anthropic says its Mythos model found vulnerabilities in cryptographic algorithms that secure the internet

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/anthropic_cybersecurity.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic's Claude Mythos Preview found weaknesses in key cryptographic algorithms, including a better attack on HAWK, a post-quantum signature scheme that human experts had reviewed for more than two years. The model found it in just 60 hours at an API cost of about $100,000. The findings don't affect systems in use today, but they show how AI could challenge core assumptions behind internet security, Anthropic says.</p>
