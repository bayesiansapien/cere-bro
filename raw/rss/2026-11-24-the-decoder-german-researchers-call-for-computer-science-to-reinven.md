# German researchers call for computer science to reinvent itself as "Computer and AI Science"

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/darmstadt_ai_computer_science.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        In a position paper, TU Darmstadt researchers call for redefining computer science as "Computer and AI Science." Their biggest concern is code generated on the fly at runtime, which they say upends today's safety and correctness guarantees.</p>
