# Google launches Gemini for legal work to automate contracts and research

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/gemini_logo.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        With Gemini Enterprise for Legal, Google launches an AI solution for the legal industry that connects to systems like iManage, DocuSign, and Everlaw through MCP connectors. Partners like Deloitte sell ready-made AI agents for tasks like contract review. Anthropic already offers similar solutions. All providers use the same models as in their other products.</p>
