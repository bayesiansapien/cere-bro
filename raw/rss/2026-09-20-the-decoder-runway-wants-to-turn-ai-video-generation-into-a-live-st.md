# Runway wants to turn AI video generation into a live stream you control in real time

<p>        Runway wants to stream AI video as users prompt it, rather than make them wait for finished clips. The approach builds on GWM-1, its world model that generates video frame by frame. Beyond creative tools, Runway sees uses in robotics and autonomous driving.</p>
