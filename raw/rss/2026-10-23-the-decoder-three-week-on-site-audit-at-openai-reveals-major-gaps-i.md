# Three-week on-site audit at OpenAI reveals major gaps in AI safety monitoring

<p><img alt="OpenAI claims to have found a security approach for AGI" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="816" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/12/openai_safety_research_alignment_illustration.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1456" /></p>
<p>        External auditors from METR and Redwood Research spent three weeks on-site at OpenAI examining the company's internal monitoring system for AI agents and found significant gaps, with up to 25 percent of agent traffic from models at the GPT-5.5 class and above going unmonitored. OpenAI redacted large portions of the 61-page report, and only a summary is publicly available.</p>
