# Firm linked to Jensen Huang's ex-lover reportedly brokered nearly $12 billion in Nvidia chip orders for China

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1024" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/nvidia_logo_glitch.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1536" /></p>
<p>        Megaspeed, the firm owned by ousted Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's former lover Alice Huang, arranged nearly $12 billion in Nvidia chip orders for Chinese companies between 2024 and 2025, according to the WSJ. Short seller Culper Research estimates Nvidia's actual China revenue share exceeds 20 percent, far above the officially reported 7 percent.</p>
