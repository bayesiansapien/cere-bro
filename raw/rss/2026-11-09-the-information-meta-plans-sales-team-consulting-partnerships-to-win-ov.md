# Meta Plans Sales Team, Consulting Partnerships to Win Over Big Companies

<p><a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/meta">Meta Platforms</a> is taking the next steps in a plan to build a new business selling its AI products to companies.</p>

<p>The company has begun forming a sales organization to sell those products to small and midsize businesses and also forge&nbsp;relationships with consulting firms and systems integrators, including Accenture, Deloitte and IBM, to help it sell and deploy AI products to corporate customers, according to a person familiar with the matter and job postings reviewed by The Information.</p>
