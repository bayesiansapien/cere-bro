# Jensen Huang’s Right-Hand Man Oversaw Chip Customers—Including Alice Huang

<p>For years, anytime a major <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/nvidia">Nvidia</a> customer wanted to buy chips or otherwise needed attention, they could turn to the company’s longtime former sales chief Jay Puri. That included Alice Huang, the former mistress of Jensen Huang, the Nvidia co-founder and former CEO who was pressured to resign last week by the company’s board, according to a person with knowledge of Nvidia’s operations.</p>

<p>Alice Huang worked with a Chinese firm that secretly imported at least $7.5 billion of banned Nvidia AI chips into China, circumventing U.S. export restrictions, <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/jensen-huangs-yearslong-ties-alice-huang-enabled-nvidia-chip-sales-china?rc=c48ukx">The Information reported</a>. And Puri had a personal connection to another key customer, Supermicro, a server maker whose then-senior executives were criminally indicted in March for allegedly diverting Nvidia chips to China.</p>
