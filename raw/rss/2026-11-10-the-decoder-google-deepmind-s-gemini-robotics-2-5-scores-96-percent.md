# Google DeepMind's Gemini Robotics 2.5 scores 96 percent sorting metal parts in a Mercedes-Benz assembly pilot

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="810" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Google-Deepmind-Gemini-Robotics-2-5.webp" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1440" /></p>
<p>        Google Deepmind introduced Gemini Robotics 2.5, a new control model for robots, and tested it with Apptronik on active Mercedes-Benz production lines in Hungary and Germany. The humanoid robot Apollo 2 sorted metal parts there with a reported success rate of 96 percent.</p>
