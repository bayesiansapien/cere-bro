# Utopai Becomes a Unicorn as Video AI Takes Off

<p>While much of the AI industry has fixated on new personal AI assistants from <b>Meta</b> and <b>OpenAI</b>, video AI is enjoying a quiet renaissance. Hollywood mogul <b>Jeffrey Katzenberg</b> is teaming up with the former head of OpenAI’s <b>Sora</b> app to <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/jeffrey-katzenberg-teams-former-openai-sora-head-new-ai-video-startup">launch a new AI film company</a>. Startup <b>Higgsfield</b> <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/ai-video-startup-talks-quadruple-valuation-5-billion">quadrupled its valuation</a> to <a href="https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/higgsfield-raises-400-million-series-b-financing-at-5-4-billion-valuation-with-annualized-revenue-reaching-700-million-302852430.html">$5.4 billion</a> in August. And <b>Kling AI</b>, the video unit of Chinese social media giant <b>Kuaishou Technology</b>, <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/kuaishou-announces-kling-ai-video-units-fundraising-15-billion-valuation">raised nearly $3 billion</a> at a $15 billion valuation over the summer.</p><p>The latest video AI company to seize this moment is <b>Utopai Studios</b>, a 4-year-old film startup based in Mountain View, Calif. Utopai closed a funding round for $60 million that valued it at $1 billion and has already received term sheets for a subsequent round that would value the company at $2 billion to $3 billion, according to someone close to the company.&nbsp;</p><p>The enthusiasm for video AI startups follows improvements in the quality and length of the clips that video AI models can generate. In September, Higgsfield announced that it had crossed $1 billion in annualized revenue, showing that the technology has serious commercial potential.</p>
