# Rogo, Hebbia Revenue Rises as Claude Finance Threat Looms

<p>AI startups that aim to supplant the grunt work of junior bankers, private equity analysts and corporate finance associates have attracted billions of dollars of funding. They also face nagging doubts about whether <b>Anthropic</b> and <b>OpenAI</b> will annihilate them before long.&nbsp;</p><p>The two most highly valued finance AI startups—<b>Rogo</b> and <b>Hebbia</b>—have been trying to dislodge the naysayers with a blitz of new products, offices and newly announced customers. Both sell software and AI agents that can take written instructions and create a financial model, memo or a hundred-page pitch deck by plugging into the data providers banks pay for and into a firm's own files. Both use models from Anthropic, OpenAI and&nbsp;<b>Google</b>.&nbsp;But their growth has diverged. </p>
