# Interim Nvidia CEO Colette Kress Has Tried to Tame Nvidia’s Riskiest Bets

<p>Nvidia’s appointment Thursday of its longtime chief financial officer Colette Kress as interim CEO will force the finance veteran to guide the business through a fraught storm of uncertainty created by the sudden resignation of former CEO Jensen Huang.</p>

<p>Kress, 59, joined Nvidia in 2013, when the business was a humble supplier of chips for gamers. And while she has played an important role in guiding the company’s ascendance to an all-important place in the AI economy, she wasn’t someone who set the agenda on the company’s major decisions, according to people familiar with Kress.</p>

<p>Huang had run Nvidia with total control&mdash;and never established a clear succession plan. One person familiar with Kress said Huang tended to “steamroll” her whenever the two disagreed.</p>
