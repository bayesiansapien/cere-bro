# Sierra Acquires Agent Startup ‘Takeoff’ to Diversify Its Business

<p>In 2020, 22-year-old <b>Aakash Thumaty</b> was a product manager at <b>Salesforce</b> when he wrote an essay, “Value Driven Innovation at Shopify and the Incumbency Trap at Salesforce,” which argued against Salesforce’s subscription-based business model. He sent it to <b>Bret Taylor</b>, who at the time was Salesforce’s chief operating officer. To Thumaty’s surprise, Taylor shared it with other executives at the company.</p><p>Six years later, Thumaty and Taylor’s paths are crossing again. <b>Sierra</b>, the AI customer support startup Taylor now leads, is acquiring Thumaty’s three-person startup, <b>Takeoff</b>, which develops agents that can work on tasks for long periods of time, the companies told The Information exclusively.</p><p>They wouldn’t disclose details but it’s unlikely to have been a high priced acquisition. Takeoff was founded a year ago and was closing in on $10 million in annualized revenue earlier this month, Thumaty said. (It had previously raised $15 million in funding from <b>Matrix</b> and venture capitalist <b>Lachy Groom</b>.)</p><p>But Sierra executives hope Thumaty’s expertise will come in handy as the company tries to move beyond AI-powered customer support agents into selling agents for other tasks.</p>
