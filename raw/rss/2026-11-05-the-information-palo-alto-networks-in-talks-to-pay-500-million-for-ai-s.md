# Palo Alto Networks in Talks to Pay $500 Million for AI Security Startup

<p>Palo Alto Networks is in talks to buy three-year-old Israeli security startup Clutch Security for around $500 million, according to people familiar with the matter.</p>

<p>If finalized, the acquisition would be the fifth Palo Alto Networks has announced this year, as <a href="http://www.theinformation.com/articles/inside-nikesh-aroras-deals-hunt-palo-alto-networks">CEO Nikesh Arora seeks to bolster</a> the company’s products that counter new AI threats. Clutch develops software that helps companies track passwords and authentication codes used by AI agents.</p>
