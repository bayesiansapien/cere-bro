# Microsoft and Nvidia reportedly team up on AI PCs that run actual agents instead of Copilot

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="453" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/openclaw_microsoft.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="680" /></p>
<p>        Nvidia is pushing into the PC market with its own chips as the main processor. The first Windows computers from Dell and Microsoft's Surface line are set to be unveiled next week at Computex and Build. Microsoft is also planning new software likely based on the OpenClaw framework that lets AI agents handle tasks locally on Windows PCs, a second shot after the Copilot+ PC concept largely flopped.</p>
