# Ideogram 4.0 drops as an open-weight model with native 2K resolution and improved text rendering

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="576" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/ideogram_4_astronaut-2.webp" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1024" /></p>
<p>        Ideogram releases version 4.0 of its text-to-image model as an open-weight model with native 2K resolution, bounding box control, and improved text rendering. On the DesignArena leaderboard, it ranks first among all open models; only closed systems from OpenAI and Google score higher. Commercial use requires a paid license.</p>
