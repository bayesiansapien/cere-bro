# World Action Models give robots the ability to simulate consequences before they move

<p><img alt="Robot camera scans cup, knife, chopping board and kettle on a colorful kitchen counter." class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1047" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/World-Action-Models-Robotics-Nano-Banana-Pro.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1920" /></p>
<p>        World Action Models tackle a basic weakness of today's robotics AI: current models learn which movements match which camera images, but they don't understand how the world actually changes as a result. A new survey organizes about a hundred papers into two architectural lines and shows a key edge: these models can learn from everyday videos that contain no robot action labels. That kind of data was nearly useless for traditional robotics AI.</p>
