# Nvidia researchers argue huge AI context windows are a dead end

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/you-dont-need-long-context-nano-banana-pro.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1408" /></p>
<p>        Nvidia researchers argue that language models often don't need their full context windows—which are slow, expensive, and prone to errors—and propose an alternative. In tests, periodic summaries and targeted keyword search usually matched full-context results at a fraction of the cost. The team even proposes a new class of models that works with short notes instead of long context.</p>
