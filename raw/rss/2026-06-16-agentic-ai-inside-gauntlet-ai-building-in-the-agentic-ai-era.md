coordinate workflows, and complete meaningful work with increasing autonomy. In this world, the most important skill is not memorizing frameworks. It is learning how to build reliable systems around intelligence.</p><p>Gauntlet is not a normal course. It is a fellowship program for people who want to learn by building. The program is a 10-week, full-time AI engineering fellowship where engineers ship real systems under production pressure, with weekly reviews, escalating complexity, and a final capstone. Participants build across web, mobile, and AI systems, and the program is designed around execution rather than passive lectures.</p><p>That matches my experience.</p><p>The work is not abstract. You are building agents, RAG systems, MCP-based workflows, compound orestration, recursive self improvement, agent loop, red teaming, multimodal interfaces, evaluation pipelines, voice agents, infrastructure, and real applications under pressure. You are learning where agentic AI breaks: tool reliability, memory, security, latency, observability, user intent, and evaluation. You are also learning how to recover, redesign, and ship anyway.</p><p>The best part of Gauntlet is not just the technical intensity. It is the people. You are surrounded by builders who are willing to learn quickly, build seriously, take feedback, and keep going when the work gets hard. The networking is not the shallow kind where people exchange LinkedIn profiles and disappear. It is the kind that happens when you are working beside fellow challengers late into the night, solving hard problems together, and seeing how people think under pressure.</p><p>For anyone serious about agentic AI engineering, this matters.</p><p>AI is moving too quickly for theory alone. You need to build. You need to ship. You need to learn how to use agents, RAG systems, harness engineering, MCP, multimodal tools, evaluation pipelines, infrastructure, and real user feedback inside actual projects. More importantly, you need to learn how to turn probabilistic models into dependable products.</p><p>The next cohort starts soon on July 6, 2026, with another cohort on September 14, 2026.</p><p>You can apply here: <a href="https://www.gauntletai.com/apply">https://www.gauntletai.com/apply</a></p><p>If you want to apply and get an interview, please select &#8220;Referral&#8221; when asked how you heard about the program and use my name: <strong>Ken Huang</strong>.</p><p>If you are looking for something comfortable, this is probably not it.</p><p>If you are willing to learn, build, and surround yourself with people who are pushing hard into the future of AI engineering, Gauntlet may fit you nicely.</p><p>Sources: <a href="https://gauntletai.com/">Gauntlet AI</a>, <a href="https://gauntletai.com/apply">Apply to Gauntlet AI</a>, <a href="https://gauntletai.com/about">About Gauntlet</a>.</p>
