# OpenAI’s data suggests that frontier models are hiding their thoughts because developers optimize for capability and compute

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/CoT-Controllability.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI's safety team systematically investigated why the chains of thought in frontier models like GPT-6 Astra are increasingly evading oversight. Four training steps drive the erosion, including architecture choices and reinforcement learning. The analysis shows that the loss of transparency is not a law of nature but also reflects tradeoffs around capability and cost.</p>
