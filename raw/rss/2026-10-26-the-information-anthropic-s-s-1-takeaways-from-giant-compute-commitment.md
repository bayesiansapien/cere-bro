# Anthropic’s S-1 Takeaways: From Giant Compute Commitments to Complex Lock-Ups

<p><a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/anthropic">Anthropic</a> publicly filed a preliminary prospectus for its planning initial public offering, providing a look inside its business for the first half of this year, as well as new details about its governance and more than 90 pages of risk factors.</p>

<p>The S-1’s filing on Monday sets the stage for Anthropic to start its investor road show in about two weeks, leading up to an IPO that Anthropic is expected to target for the week of Nov. 16, ahead of the Thanksgiving holiday. The stock is to be listed on the Nasdaq Stock Market under the ticker symbol ANT.</p>
