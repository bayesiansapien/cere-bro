# The Information’s WTF Summit in Pictures

<p>At The Information’s annual WTF Summit a few days ago, women leaders from Anthropic, Google, Goldman Sachs and dozens of other companies across technology and media gathered to discuss the forces remaking those industries. Conversation topics ranged from on the state of tech M&amp;A to chip financing to the geopolitics between America and China. Across all of those talks, the VIPs had their eyes fixed on what lies ahead&mdash;perhaps no one more so than Amanda Peterson Corio, Google’s global head of data center energy. “I live in 2031,” she told attendees.
