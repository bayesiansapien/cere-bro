# Lumentum Sell-Off After Marvell Offer Raises Chance of Alternate Bid

<p>Lumentum, the optical networking component maker that Marvell Technology said on Monday it would buy, was swept up by Wednesday’s election-driven sell-off of stocks that have benefited most from the AI data center boom. But investors shouldn’t lose sight of the bigger picture&mdash;another bidder for Lumentum could emerge, or Marvell could come back and sweeten its proposal with more cash.</p>

<p>Marvell’s proposed all-stock acquisition of Lumentum&mdash;which as of Tuesday valued the firm at around $80 billion&mdash;shone a spotlight on the latter’s position in a promising corner of the AI infrastructure market. Optical technology, such as the lasers Lumentum makes, moves data within and between data centers faster than the copper wiring traditionally used in these facilities. The pressures of AI computing make faster data transmission vital.</p>
