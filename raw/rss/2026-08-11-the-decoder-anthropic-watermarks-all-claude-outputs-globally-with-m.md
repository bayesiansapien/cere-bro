# Anthropic watermarks all Claude outputs globally with marks that "may persist through some editing"

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="608" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/anthropic_logo_wall-1.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1088" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic will embed invisible watermarks in all Claude-generated text and sign files using the C2PA standard. New models shipping from August 2026 onward will have labeling built in from day one. The policy applies worldwide, and Anthropic plans to provide detection tools for third-party verification.</p>
