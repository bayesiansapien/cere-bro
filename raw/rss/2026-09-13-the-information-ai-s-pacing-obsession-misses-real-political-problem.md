# AI’s ‘Pacing’ Obsession Misses Real Political Problem

<p>Dario Amodei typically isn’t considered some camera-ready media maestro. His interview quotes aren’t particularly pithy, and he struggles to control his strained facial expressions. But his habit of sending his most high-stakes messages via essays posted on his own website, <a href="http://darioamodei.com/">darioamodei.com</a>, has proven to be a brilliant media strategy. “The medium is the message,” the ubiquitous half-century-old wisdom from scholar Marshall McLuhan, still resonates. The approach conveys authority and diligence, putting Amodei above the fray.&nbsp;</p><p>Amodei’s <a href="https://darioamodei.com/post/we-must-pace-the-frontier">latest essay, on Saturday</a>—suggesting that the industry slow down the pace of AI development—set off a weekend full of takes on the tech industry’s preferred medium, X—well-meaning, conspiratorial, annoying, breathless, thought-provoking takes, including nods of agreement from Amodei’s rivals, <a href="https://x.com/sama/status/2098811563415150910?s=20">Sam Altman</a> and <a href="https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2098789109980332057?s=20">Elon Musk</a>. Timing is important here, too, of course. AI doom is dominating the headlines, not just in Washington and Silicon Valley but around the world.
