# Microsoft Nears $500 Million Deal to Sell AI to ByteDance, Irking OpenAI

<p>ByteDance is in advanced talks to spend an additional roughly $500 million on cloud services from Microsoft, primarily to use OpenAI models from Microsoft’s Azure cloud, according to two people with knowledge of the situation.</p>

<p>The move to expand the Chinese firm’s already large contract with Microsoft has drawn the ire of OpenAI. The AI company has complained to Microsoft in recent months that it believes Chinese firms are “distilling” its AI, or running OpenAI models to create their own competing AI models, according to the people with knowledge of the discussions. OpenAI itself prevents most Chinese firms from using its AI, but Microsoft&mdash;which has the rights to sell OpenAI’s technology&mdash;doesn’t impose the same restrictions.</p>
