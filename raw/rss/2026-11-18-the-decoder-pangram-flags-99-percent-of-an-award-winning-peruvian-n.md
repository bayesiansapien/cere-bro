# Pangram flags 99 percent of an award-winning Peruvian novel as AI-generated, but the publisher stands by its author

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/pangram_literature.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        The AI detection tool Pangram flags 99 percent of a prize-winning Peruvian novel as AI-generated, but Mexican publisher Almadía is standing by the award after its own review. Pangram also classified over 80 percent of the author's personal texts as machine-written. French publisher Gallimard plans to start screening manuscripts with the tool.</p>
