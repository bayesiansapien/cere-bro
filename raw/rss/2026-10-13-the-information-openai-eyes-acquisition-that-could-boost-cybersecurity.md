# OpenAI Eyes Acquisition That Could Boost Cybersecurity Business

<p>OpenAI, which is trying to develop a substantial cybersecurity business, is in early talks to buy Trail of Bits, a firm that finds and fixes vulnerabilities in open-source software, according to people familiar with the matter.</p>

<p>The size of the potential deal couldn’t be learned. OpenAI has&nbsp;sometimes paid in the <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/openai-agrees-buy-ai-healthcare-app-100-million?rc=zjctrx">low hundreds of millions of dollars</a> in the form of stock to acquire small startups, though it sometimes pays less.&nbsp;Discussions are ongoing and the deal could fall apart, said the people.</p>
