# AI's energy appetite drives Nvidia and Amazon to pour billions into massive power infrastructure

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/energy_pollution-2.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        The AI industry's hunger for power keeps growing. Nvidia is investing up to $3 billion in Lancium, a power infrastructure developer that already has four gigawatts under contract in Texas. Amazon, meanwhile, is building a gas-fired power plant in the state with a capacity of up to 7.65 gigawatts that could emit 33 million tons of CO₂ per year, making it the dirtiest in the country.</p>
