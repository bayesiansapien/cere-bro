# How Google’s New AI Chip Software Helps It Battle Nvidia

<p>A software launch last month that got little attention in the wider world could have big implications for Google’s burgeoning AI chip business.&nbsp;</p><p>Google late last month <a href="https://cloud.google.com/blog/topics/ai-infrastructure/torchtpu-is-now-open-source-run-native-pytorch-on-tpus?e=48754805">announced</a> an open-source version of TorchTPU, a software layer for its tensor processing units that represents the biggest leap on the software side of the Google chip business in years.</p>
