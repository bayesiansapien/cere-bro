# Researchers stretch LeCun's JEPA AI into a universal world model that works from physics to biology

<p><img alt="Abstract visualization: Four modular sensors feed colored data streams into a central neural world model." class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1047" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/jepa-anything-world-model-generated-image-nano-banana-pro.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1920" /></p>
<p>        Researchers at PhAI Labs have expanded Yann LeCun's JEPA architecture to work across seven fields, from robotics to biomedicine. The effort also produced a liver cancer treatment candidate that showed promise in lab tests, though the study doesn't establish whether it could become an actual therapy.</p>
