# AI Stocks’ Big Worry: a Chip Glut

<p>If anyone needs therapy nowadays, it may be tech investors <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/nvidia-hosts-first-all-hands-jensen-huangs-return?rc=9byxri">more than Jensen Huang</a>. Let’s face it: The stock market has been a little manic-depressive lately. On Tuesday, for instance, the Jensen-reinstatement rally reversed itself, sending Nvidia stock down nearly 6%, while Anthropic closed below its IPO price. Neoclouds such as CoreWeave and Nebius both dropped 11%.</p><p>That’s perhaps a sign that there are some things more important to the market than the fate of any one man, even our “geniuses,” as President Trump calls Huang. Among those things are supply and demand for computing capacity, and what that means both for heavily indebted neoclouds and growth expectations for chip suppliers. Worries that we could be headed for a glut of chips have percolated on Wall Street for months, without much evidence of such a surplus. But on Tuesday, SemiAnalysis published a report saying “we are starting to see signs of a broader pricing cooldown” in the market for Nvidia’s AI chips, citing price cuts by SpaceX. Uh oh.</p>
