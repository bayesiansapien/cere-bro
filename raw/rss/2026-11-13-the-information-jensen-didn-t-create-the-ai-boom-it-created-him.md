# Jensen Didn’t Create the AI Boom. It Created Him.

<p>If you follow investors and reporters on X, you would get the sense that the resignation of Jensen Huang amounts to the end of the AI boom. A summary of my X feed Wednesday morning would go something like “I hope the Nvidia board knew what they were doing before ending the world as we know it.”</p><p>But if you believe that to be true, you haven’t been paying attention. Because as much as Jensen created the AI boom (and clearly Nvidia’s GPU—the brains that trains AI models—did), the AI boom created Jensen. And that’s key to understanding the world that comes after his reign.&nbsp;</p>
