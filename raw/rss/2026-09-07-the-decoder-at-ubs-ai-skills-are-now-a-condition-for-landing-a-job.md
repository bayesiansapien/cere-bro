# At UBS, AI skills are now a condition for landing a job

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/ai_jobs_young_people_cognitive_decline.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Swiss banking giant UBS is making AI skills a required qualification from 2027 for graduates and interns in Global Banking and Markets, with applicants having to show in interviews how they use AI to improve outcomes and efficiency. Santander is already looking for advanced AI users too, while Morgan Stanley predicts more than 200,000 banking jobs in Europe will disappear within five years.</p>
