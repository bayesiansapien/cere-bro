# US technology stocks suffer their worst weekly loss since the pandemic as AI doubts pile up

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1440" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/tech_crash_synth.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2560" /></p>
<p>        The Nasdaq 100 has slipped into a bear market after its worst weekly loss since the Covid-19 crisis. Nvidia plunged 14 percent as the SEC reviews its accounting, and Anthropic lost nearly all of its gains a day after its record IPO. AI doubts, scandals, rising interest rates, and political headwinds are hitting overheated valuations all at once.</p>
