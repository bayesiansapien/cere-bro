# TSMC Weighs Making Advanced Memory Chips to Capture More AI Spending

<p>Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Co. is considering expanding into the advanced memory chip manufacturing sector, a move that would give the world’s largest chipmaker an even bigger share of the AI hardware supply chain, according to two people with direct knowledge of the matter.</p>

<p>The Taiwanese chipmaking giant recently started drawing up plans for making a specific type of advanced memory chips, 3D DRAM, at the request of Nvidia, its biggest customer, the two people said. An intense shortage of memory chips, driven by the AI hardware boom, has sent prices soaring and prompted chip designers such as <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/nvidia">Nvidia</a> and <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/apple">Apple</a> to look for sources of additional supply. Apple earlier this year hiked prices for its devices, citing rising memory costs.</p>
