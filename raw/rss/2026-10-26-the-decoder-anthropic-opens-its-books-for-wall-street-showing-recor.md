# Anthropic opens its books for Wall Street, showing record revenue and mounting obligations

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/anthropic_claude_bull_.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic is the first major AI lab to file an S-1 with the SEC, planning to list on the Nasdaq under the ticker "ANT." The numbers are strong, with $11.6 billion in quarterly revenue, a first-ever operating profit, and existing customers spending nine times more year over year. But the company also carries roughly $800 billion in long-term compute commitments.</p>
