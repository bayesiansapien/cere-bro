# Anthropic's Q3 revenue hits $16.8B but growth slows ahead of planned November IPO

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/anthropic_amodei.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic hit $16.8 billion in third-quarter revenue, a 45 percent gain that still falls far short of Q2's 140 percent surge. With a November IPO approaching, some investors now expect the company's valuation to drop below $1.5 trillion. Slowing growth and a tough market are cooling expectations for the AI boom's biggest Wall Street test yet.</p>
