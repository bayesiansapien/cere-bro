# OpenAI will watermark ChatGPT text in the EU but makes it optional for API users worldwide

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/openai_chatgpt_text_detection-2.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI is adding invisible textGrain watermarks to ChatGPT in the EU, but unlike Anthropic, it will let API customers worldwide opt out. Tests show detection rates as high as 95 percent, dropping to 17 percent when a quarter of the words are replaced.</p>
