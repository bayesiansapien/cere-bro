# Anthropic turns Claude into a full workspace with Plugins, spreadsheets, whiteboards, and soon Meeting notes

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="941" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/claude_code_logo.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1672" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic is expanding Claude with Sheets, Whiteboards, and Interactive Connectors, letting partners like Adobe and Atlassian embed their apps directly inside Claude. Anthropic says Claude Meetings, which can join calls and take notes, is coming soon. Step by step, Anthropic is turning Claude into a central work platform for teams that brings many other apps together in one place.</p>
