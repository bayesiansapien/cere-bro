# Sakana AI bets AI that improves itself can break the compute arms race of frontier labs

<p><img alt="Illustration with stylized fish made of circuits, arrows and gears, suggesting circular movement and technical evolution." class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Sakana-AI.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Sakana AI has launched a dedicated research lab for recursive self-improvement: AI that iteratively improves itself. The Japanese startup, co-founded by Transformer co-author Llion Jones, sees RSI as an alternative to the raw compute arms race among big US labs. Anthropic, meanwhile, warns about the control risks of this very technology.</p>
