# Suno Studio 2.0's new chat feature lets you talk to your DAW like it's a bandmate

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/suno_ai_logo.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        With Studio 2.0, Suno turns its AI music platform into a full production tool for Premier subscribers. A chat feature creates instruments and plugins via text, while MIDI import and 32-bit export come without limits. That unlimited export clashes with Suno's own recently announced download caps designed to combat AI music spam on streaming platforms.</p>
