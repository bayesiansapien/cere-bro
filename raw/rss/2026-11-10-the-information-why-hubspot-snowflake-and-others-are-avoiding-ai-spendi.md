# Why HubSpot, Snowflake and Others Are Avoiding AI Spending Caps

<p>While some companies are curbing employees’ AI use to contain soaring costs, many others in the tech industry are pushing ahead with full-throttle spending.</p>

<p>As the chart below shows, large cloud providers including Amazon Web Services and software firms such as Palantir, Snowflake and HubSpot haven’t put firm caps on engineers’ spending as employees use AI coding tools like Anthropic’s Claude Code and OpenAI’s Codex to build apps and features. Neither have the frontier labs themselves. And venture capitalists say many of their portfolio companies are still urging employees to use as much AI as possible.</p>
