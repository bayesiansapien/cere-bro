# Quoting Daniel Stenberg

We actually improved curl during these a little more than three weeks. A little over 200 bugfixes actually. 200 bugfixes that none of those rust rewrites have. We also fixed a few vulnerabilities. In another month we have done another 200 and maybe fixed a few more vulnerabilities. Will these clones keep up?</p></blockquote>
