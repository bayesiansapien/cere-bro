# Google's WeatherNext 3 ditches physics simulations and learns weather directly from live satellite data

<p><img alt="A graphically enhanced satellite image of cloud vortices in blue and yellow, with the text &quot;WeatherNext 3&quot; superimposed on top" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="675" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/weathernext-3-01-hero.webp" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1200" /></p>
<p>        Google Research and DeepMind are releasing WeatherNext 3, a weather model that skips traditional physics simulations and learns directly from real-time satellite data. It produces hourly forecasts at up to five-kilometer resolution, five times more detailed than its predecessor. Google says regions in Africa, Latin America, and the Asia-Pacific that have lacked accurate forecasts should see the biggest gains.</p>
