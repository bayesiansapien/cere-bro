# Anthropic’s IPO Triumph

<p>Anthropic pulled it off. The biggest pure-play AI company out there, maker of the Claude line of AI models, went public on Thursday, with its shares jumping as much as 39% above its IPO price. The stock settled back a bit to close at $97.17, up 17% from its IPO price. That gave it a market capitalization of $1.76 trillion, assuming you include all options, restricted stock units and convertible notes that might one day become shares. Not bad for a company that’s just five years old.</p><p>What makes the IPO even more notable is that it occurred despite months of investor jitters about the cost of AI development. Partly as a result of those jitters, we’ve had a very choppy market that has stalled IPO plans by a slew of other companies—including data center developers SB Energy and Nscale that filed publicly over the summer. Notably, SB Energy on Thursday updated its IPO filing for the first time since late September, suggesting it was still hoping to go public, while Nscale updated its filing a week ago also for the first time since September. But Anthropic’s success doesn’t mean the IPO market has opened up. “I don't think this opens the floodgates unless you look like Anthropic,” Lise Buyer, managing partner of Class V Group, said on a subscriber video discussion convened by The Information.</p>
