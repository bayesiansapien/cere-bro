# OpenAI’s Latest Hire Burnishes Its Cybersecurity Credentials

<p>It may be the dog days of summer, but <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/newsletters/the-briefing/googles-ai-shake-means-pichai-succession?rc=zjctrx">the biggest AI labs</a> are breaking up the monotony with a few executive changes. The latest is OpenAI Chief Revenue Officer Denise Dresser. <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/openai-replaces-chief-revenue-officer-wiz-president?rc=zjctrx">The ChatGPT maker said Thursday</a> she would leave the firm after just eight months on the job.&nbsp;</p><p>In her place, OpenAI has hired Dali Rajic, formerly president and chief operating officer of Wiz, the Israel-founded cybersecurity company Google bought earlier this year for $32 billion. The hiring underscores just how much cybersecurity has emerged as a key selling point for AI companies.&nbsp;</p>
