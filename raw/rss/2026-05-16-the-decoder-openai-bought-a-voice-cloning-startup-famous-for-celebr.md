# OpenAI bought a voice cloning startup famous for celebrity imitations

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/openai_audio-1.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI has acquired Weights.gg, a small startup that let users create and share AI voice clones of celebrities like Taylor Swift and Donald Trump. The team of around six now works at OpenAI, but the company doesn't plan to release a standalone cloning product.</p>
