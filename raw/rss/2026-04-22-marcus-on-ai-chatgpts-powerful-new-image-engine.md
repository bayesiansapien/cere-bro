powerful new image engine</a>&#8221;, but as ever, its functional understanding of the world seems limited. </p><p>I first learned about the new system when some some smart aleck on X sent me an are several errors, and those errors are revealing. For example, the rear center-pull (?) brake is mislabeled as the seat stay, and the big gear on the back is mislabeled as the rear brake.  There is a label for a spoke that is pointing to blank space.</p><p>In many modern bikes, of course, a rear brake <em>can </em>be found back there, but not in this diagram. Instead this system has combined a typical <em>position</em> for a modern disc brake system with a <em>diagram</em> of an older (though still in use) caliper (or similar) system. The system doesn&#8217;t actually understand how the various parts <em>function.</em></p><p>And of course there are literally hundreds of labeled bikes on the internet as a quick Google Image Search would reveal. (Which is why my usual test here has been a tandem bike, to make things a little more challenging.)</p><p></p><p>To up the degree of difficulty, I asked ChatGPT to &#8220;please draw a taller than
