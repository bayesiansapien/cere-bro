# Nvidia to Invest in Chip Rival d-Matrix as Its Challengers Choose Partnership

<p>Nvidia plans to invest in d-Matrix, a seven-year-old developer of server chips for AI that aims to compete with Nvidia, according to three people with knowledge of the deal.</p>

<p>The planned investment is part of Nvidia’s effort to make its technology compatible with rival chip designers and bring them into its dominant hardware ecosystem, even as it has <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/newsletters/ai-infrastructure/nvidias-share-ai-inference-chip-market-appears-rising?rc=c48ukx">made recent market-share gains against nearly all of them</a>.</p>
