# GPT-6 Astra appears to show a "step change" in spatial reasoning based on early benchmarks

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/openai_robot_arms.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        In a new robotics benchmark, GPT-6 Astra shows major gains in spatial understanding. On StationeryBench, the model completed 7 out of 100 tasks with dual-arm robots, while competitor MolmoAct2 couldn't finish a single one. A researcher calls it a "step change in spatial reasoning."</p>
