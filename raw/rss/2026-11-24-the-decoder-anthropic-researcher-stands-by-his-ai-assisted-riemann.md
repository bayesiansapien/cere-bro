# Anthropic researcher stands by his AI-assisted Riemann proof but owns up to "stupid things" in the rollout

<p>        Anthropic researcher Levent Alpöge is walking back parts of his announcement of an AI-assisted proof of the generalized Riemann hypothesis. The claim that it ran to more than 250,000 pages was a joke, he says, and the published Lean code is "full of garbage." He still stands by the math.</p>
