# Chinese AI models are politically complex, not just government mouthpieces, study finds

<p><img alt="Collage illustrating the political orientation of Chinese AI models: Chinese flags, propaganda symbols, and a megaphone are on the left; chat bubbles and search icons are on the right." class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/KI-Bias-Propaganda.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        A study by Vals AI tested 25 AI models for alignment with Chinese government positions and found surprising results. Chinese models respond to political questions less uniformly than expected, and Western models occasionally adopt official Beijing positions too. The language of the prompt had an especially strong effect on the political slant of responses.</p>
