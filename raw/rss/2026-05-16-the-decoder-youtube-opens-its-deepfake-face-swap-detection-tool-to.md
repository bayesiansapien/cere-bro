# YouTube opens its deepfake face-swap detection tool to all adult creators

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1080" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/youtube_likeness_feature.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1920" /></p>
<p>        YouTube is opening its Likeness Detection tool to all creators 18 and older. The system spots AI-generated face fakes in other users' videos and lets creators file removal requests directly through YouTube Studio. Until now, the feature was limited to partner program members; now it's meant to protect smaller channels too.</p>
