# Historic UN Security Council Briefing on AI

This afternoon at the UN Security Council, Yoshua Bengio, Sam Altman, Dario Amodei, and Hugging Face CEO and Cofounder Clement Delangue gave back-to-back speeches. I absolutely loved Bengio’s, agreeing with virtually every well-crafted word, and, with his permission, am reprinting his remarks in full, below. The more surprising thing is that I agreed with almost all of what the other three said as well (and most of what followed, as many other speakers from around the world gave their own remarks). 1 Pretty much everyone converged on exactly the same things I keep harping on: The need for preflight safety testing. The need for transparency auditing. The need for liability. The need for global dialogue and international cooperation. The need for immediate action. At this point, with leading scientists (like Bengio) and major CEOs all reaching consensus, there is no excuse not to start implementing them.
