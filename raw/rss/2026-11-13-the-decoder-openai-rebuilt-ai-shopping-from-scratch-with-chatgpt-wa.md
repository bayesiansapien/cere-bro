# OpenAI rebuilt AI shopping from scratch with ChatGPT Wallet

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1439" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/ChatGPT-Wallet-title-scaled.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2560" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI is taking a second shot at AI-powered shopping with ChatGPT Wallet after scrapping its first approach, Instant Checkout. Users register a card once and confirm each purchase through Face ID or a fingerprint, while actual card details stay hidden behind payment tokens. Rivals such as Anthropic, Meta, and xAI already have their agents making purchases.</p>
