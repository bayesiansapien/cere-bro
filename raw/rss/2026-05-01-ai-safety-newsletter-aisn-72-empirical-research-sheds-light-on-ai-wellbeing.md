<p>Welcome to the AI Safety Newsletter by the<a href="https://safe.ai/"> Center for AI Safety</a>. We discuss developments in AI and AI safety. No technical background required.</p><p>In this edition, we discuss a research paper on AI Wellbeing and which AI models are the happiest. They coincide with the American public&#8217;s declining sentiments toward AI.</p><p><strong>Targeted anti-AI violence.</strong> On April 10, a man <a href="https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/man-accused-molotov-cocktail-attack-sam-altmans-home-opposed-ai-court-rcna331610">threw</a> a Molotov cocktail at the San Francisco home of OpenAI CEO Sam Altman. He then went to OpenAI&#8217;s headquarters and threatened to burn it down. No one was injured, but the suspect was arrested carrying a jug of kerosene and an anti-AI manifesto. Days earlier, an Indianapolis city councilman&#8212;who had supported a local data center project&#8212;had his home <a href="https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/politics-news/indianapolis-councilman-says-shots-fired-house-no-data-centers-note-rcna267023">shot</a> at thirteen times, with a note left on his doorstep that read &#8220;No Data Centers.&#8221; And last year in November, a man <a href="https://www.wired.com/story/openai-office-lockdown-threat-san-francisco/">threatened</a> to murder people at OpenAI&#8217;s San Francisco offices, prompting a shelter-in-place order for employees. However, such violence actually harms social movements, and AI safety groups have <a href="https://www.cnn.com/2026/04/17/tech/anti-ai-attack-sam-altman">made clear</a> they do not condone violence in any form.</p><p><strong>Public sentiment about AI has been deteriorating for some time. </strong>The attacks coincide with falling public confidence in AI. An<a href="https://www.nbcnews.com/politics/politics-news/poll-majority-voters-say-risks-ai-outweigh-benefits-rcna262196"> NBC News survey</a> in March found that only 26% of Americans view AI positively, while 46% have negative opinions. An April Gallup poll found that Gen Z&#8217;s feelings about AI have also <a href="https://news.gallup.com/poll/708224/gen-adoption-steady-skepticism-climbs.aspx">worsened</a> over the last year, despite a majority of them using AI tools weekly. A popular <a href="https://x.com/bayeslord/status/2045966479338901898">post</a> on X summed up the sentiment around society&#8217;s waning AI optimism.</p><p>Furthermore, Princeton University&#8217;s Bridging Divides Initiative, a research group that tracks political violence, says it has been seeing &#8220;an <a href="https://thehill.com/policy/technology/5832941-ai-openai-sam-altman-violent-attacks/">uptick</a> in cases of harassment and threats&#8221; around AI and data centers.
