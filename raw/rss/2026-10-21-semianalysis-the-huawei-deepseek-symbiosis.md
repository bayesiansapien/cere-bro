# The Huawei-DeepSeek Symbiosis

<p>Right before China&#8217;s national day holidays, DeepSeek released Ascend support for <a href="https://github.com/tile-ai/tilelang">TileLang</a>, <a href="https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepGEMM-Ascend">DeepGEMM</a>, <a href="https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepEP-Ascend">DeepEP</a>, <a href="https://github.com/deepseek-ai/TileKernels">TileKernels</a>, <a href="https://github.com/deepseek-ai/FlashMLA">FlashMLA</a>, and <a href="https://github.com/deepseek-ai/DeepSelect">DeepSelect</a>.</p><p>Neither can do this alone. Although they have Pangu, Huawei has no frontier lab to prove their chip can train or infer models, DeepSeek has models but cannot buy the compute it needs. Each supplies exactly what the other lacks: Huawei provides the hardware and native software stack; DeepSeek brings the workloads and engineering needed to make that hardware useful for its models. September 30&#8217;s release of Ascend support in DeepSeek repositories is an artifact of this trade, where the exchange runs through the kernel-authoring language.</p><div
