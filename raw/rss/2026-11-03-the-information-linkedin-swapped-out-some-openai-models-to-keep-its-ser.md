# LinkedIn Swapped Out Some OpenAI Models to Keep Its Server Costs Flat

<p>The more people use AI features in apps, the more the companies selling those apps <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/inside-cognitions-booming-growth-high-cash-burn?rc=geicgp">have to spend on servers to run them</a>, potentially squeezing their profit margins.</p><p>But some firms have found ways to keep costs in check. For instance, Microsoft-owned LinkedIn says it has flattened the cost of running its AI-powered Hiring Assistant, which uses large language models to automate candidate search and outreach for recruiters, even as usage of the tool has steadily increased in the past year.&nbsp;</p>
