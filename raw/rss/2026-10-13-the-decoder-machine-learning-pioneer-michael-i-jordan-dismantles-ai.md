# Machine learning pioneer Michael I. Jordan dismantles AI doom rhetoric as "grand moralizing performance"

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/paris_AI_doomer.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Michael I. Jordan, described by Science as "the most influential computer scientist," considers warnings about superintelligence pure science fiction. He calls the resignations of young AI researchers a "grand moralizing performance" and companies' demands for regulation "theater." On Hinton's thesis that the brain can be reproduced in silicon and scaled into superintelligence, he's blunt: "None of the steps in this argument is credible. Here, we're in the land of science fiction."</p>
