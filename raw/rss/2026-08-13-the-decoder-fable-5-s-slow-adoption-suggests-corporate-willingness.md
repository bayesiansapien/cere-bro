# Fable 5's slow adoption suggests corporate willingness to pay for frontier AI has hit a ceiling

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/openai_anthropic-2.jpeg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic's Fable 5 is considered the most powerful AI model on the market, but U.S. companies are barely buying it. According to Ramp data, Fable 5 accounts for only six percent of Anthropic tokens sold. The model's steep price tag suggests corporate AI spending may have hit a ceiling, at least as long as performance gains don't translate into measurable everyday value.</p>
