# OpenAI opens its cybersecurity tool Daybreak to NATO and European governments

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/russian_influence_campaigns.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI is opening its cybersecurity tool Daybreak to NATO and several European governments, including Germany, France, and Poland. The EU's cybersecurity agency ENISA has already used it to find real vulnerabilities in EU software. The NATO Communications and Information Agency (NCIA) says it's already making heavy use of the underlying models.</p>
