# Amazon Acquires Chip Software Business from AI Startup Decart

<p>Amazon said it is acquiring key technology and top staff from Israeli AI startup Decart, in a move designed to help Amazon run compute for AI more efficiently, including with its homegrown Trainium AI chips.</p> <p>Decart’s software is designed to get more out of chips, and helps the chips run faster. ...</p>
