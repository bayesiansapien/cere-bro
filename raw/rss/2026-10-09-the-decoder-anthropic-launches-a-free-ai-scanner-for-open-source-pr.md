# Anthropic launches a free AI scanner for open-source projects

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="936" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Anthropic-Cybersecurity.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1664" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic has launched "Cyber Mission," a program to protect critical infrastructure and open-source software from cyberattacks. Partners like CrowdStrike and Palo Alto Networks will help secure power grids and water systems, while a free AI scanner automatically checks open-source projects for vulnerabilities with an expected accuracy above 90 percent.</p>
