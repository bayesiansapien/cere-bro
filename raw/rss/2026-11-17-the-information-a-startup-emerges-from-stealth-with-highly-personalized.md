# A Startup Emerges From Stealth With Highly Personalized AI

<p><b>OpenAI’s ChatGPT</b> remembers details of its users and what they look up, while <b>Anthropic’s Claude Code</b> uses its “auto memory” to take notes on users’ preferences and feedback. A new startup thinks AI models can get even more personalized.</p><p><b>First Person Labs</b>, founded seven months ago by two PhD students in the <b>Stanford Artificial Intelligence Laboratory</b>, a research institute at the university, is developing tech to make <b>“user models,”&nbsp;</b>the term for more personalized AI models and agents trained on an individual’s data. The startup is coming out of stealth Tuesday and has raised around $10 million in a funding round led by <b>Andreessen Horowitz’s</b> infrastructure team.&nbsp;</p><p>“Any interface you talk to knows you,” <b>Ken Liu</b>, the company’s co-founder and CEO, said, adding that the company’s tech is “like a biographer—someone that follows you around and sees everything.”</p>
