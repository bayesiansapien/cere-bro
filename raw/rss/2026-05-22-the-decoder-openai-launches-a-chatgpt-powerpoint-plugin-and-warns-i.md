# OpenAI launches a ChatGPT Powerpoint plugin and warns it might accidentally delete your content

<p>        OpenAI brings ChatGPT directly into PowerPoint. A new beta plugin creates presentations from notes, documents, or images and edits existing slides. The add-in is available worldwide across all tiers. OpenAI recommends saving important decks before using it.</p>
