# ByteDance’s Founder Rules Out Distillation on AI Models

<p>ByteDance won’t resort to distillation as a shortcut to advancing its AI model capabilities, even if that means the company lags behind domestic rivals for now, founder Zhang Yiming told employees during a meeting last month. One reason, say people close to the company, is ByteDance’s&nbsp;complicated history with the U.S. government over the company’s&nbsp;TikTok video app.</p>

<p>The previously unreported comments, heard by two employees present at the meeting, offer a rare glimpse into the ByteDance founder’s thinking on the company’s AI development strategy. The meeting, an all-hands for ByteDance’s AI team internally known as Seed, took place just after Moonshot AI’s Kimi K3 model sparked alarms in the U.S. that Chinese models were narrowing the gap at a pace quicker than previously expected.</p>
