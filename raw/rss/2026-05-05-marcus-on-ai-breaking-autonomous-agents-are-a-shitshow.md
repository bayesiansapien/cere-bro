# Breaking: Autonomous Agents are a Shitshow

not wrong.</p><p>A <a href="http://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.19959807">new study</a> from researchers from Stanford, MIT CSAIL, Carnegie Mellon, ITU Copenhagen, and NVIDIA and Elloe AI Labs, examining 847 autonomous agent deployments drawn from healthcare, finance, customer service and code-generation, showed that 91% were vulnerable to subtle but dangerous <a
