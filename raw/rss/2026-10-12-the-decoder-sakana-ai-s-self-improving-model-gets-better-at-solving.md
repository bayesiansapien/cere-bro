# Sakana AI's self-improving model gets better at solving and judging, but can't catch its own blind spots

<p><img alt="Robot stations equipped with conveyor belts sort colorful shapes and assemble them into complex cubes in a closed-loop process." class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1047" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/mass-recursive-self-improvement-optimization-nano-banana-pro.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1920" /></p>
<p>        Sakana AI's MASS method lets one language model improve itself by solving tasks, judging results, and refining workflows, reaching a win rate of nearly 70 percent against the base model after two rounds. But when the model misses the same errors across all three roles, it can mistake them for progress and reinforce them during training.</p>
