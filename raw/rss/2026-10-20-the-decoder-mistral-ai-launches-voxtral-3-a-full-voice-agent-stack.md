# Mistral AI launches Voxtral 3, a full voice agent stack that listens, thinks, and talks in real time

<p><img alt="Stylized Mistral logos in yellow and orange on a wall symbolize the branding of the AI platform Nano Banana Pro." class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1429" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/mistral-logo-wall-nano-banana-pro-scaled.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2560" /></p>
<p>        Mistral AI launched Voxtral 3, a new family of language models built for real-time voice agents. The system combines speech-to-text, text-to-speech, and a voice agent SDK, supports more than 50 languages, and lets agents tap into external tools during live conversations. It can run on your own infrastructure or through EU-hosted APIs to keep data under your control.</p>
