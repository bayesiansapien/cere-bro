# Isomorphic Labs raises $5 billion in its second mega-round within six months

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1080" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Isomorphic-Labs-Funding.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1920" /></p>
<p>        Isomorphic Labs has raised $5 billion, just six months after a $2.1 billion round. Alphabet's AI drug discovery company, led by Demis Hassabis, plans to use the funding to push its drug pipeline toward clinical trials. The company has no approved drugs yet.</p>
