# Nvidia halts server shipments to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Indonesia after tighter US export controls

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1024" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/nvidia_logo_glitch.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1536" /></p>
<p>        Nvidia has told partners such as Dell and Supermicro to stop shipping servers to Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Indonesia. Following new US export controls targeting Chinese cloud access, dealers are already seeking buyers for thousands of B300 chips, while one cloud executive describes "chaos", Loans and major deals are now on the line for Southeast Asia's data centers.</p>
