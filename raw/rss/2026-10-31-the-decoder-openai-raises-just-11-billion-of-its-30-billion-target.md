# OpenAI raises just $11 billion of its $30 billion target as big investors lose their AI appetite

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/openai_anthropic.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI raised only about $11 billion in its latest funding round, less than half of the $30 billion it was targeting. At $1.25 trillion, the valuation falls short of expectations, and major investors appear to be pulling back amid the recent tech sell-off. Meanwhile, rival Anthropic is pushing to go public as early as November.</p>
