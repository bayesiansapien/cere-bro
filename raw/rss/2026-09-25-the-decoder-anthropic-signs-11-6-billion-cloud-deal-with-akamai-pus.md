# Anthropic signs $11.6 billion cloud deal with Akamai, pushing its compute spending past $500 billion in under a year

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/antropic_logo_chat_cowork-2.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic has reportedly signed a seven-year, $11.6 billion cloud deal with Akamai Technologies and will receive a warrant for up to 5 percent of Akamai's shares. Its compute deals have reportedly totaled $517 billion in 11 months. CEO Dario Amodei has warned that Anthropic could go bankrupt if its revenue forecasts are even slightly off.</p>
