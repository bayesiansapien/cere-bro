# Anthropic says it cut Claude reasoning token theft by up to 99% in fight against industrial-scale distillation

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/anthropic_distillation_defense.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic says it has cut the extraction of Claude's internal reasoning tokens by 90 to 99 percent. The company links the large-scale distillation attacks to Chinese companies Zhipu and Moonshot, and has already banned tens of thousands of accounts.</p>
