# Nvidia open-sources Flora to make robot video labeling 125x cheaper than GPT-6 Astra

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1080" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/nvidia-flora-hero-robot-hands.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1920" /></p>
<p>        Nvidia's new data engine Flora labels one hour of robot video for 72 cents instead of $88 with GPT-6 Astra, while scoring slightly higher on quality. The open system, built on three fine-tuned Qwen3.5 models, catches errors in robot actions far more reliably than any competitor but sometimes falls short on descriptive accuracy. Nvidia is positioning Flora as a key piece of its strategy to control the entire robotics value chain.</p>
