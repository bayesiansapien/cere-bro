# Amazon and Meta are in talks to end the block on Meta's AI shopping agent Muse in Amazon's online store.

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="702" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/met_muse_gadget_logo.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1158" /></p>
<p>        Amazon and Meta are in talks to bring back Muse, Meta's AI shopping agent that Amazon kicked off its platform in September. The two sides are stuck on ad revenue and who gets to control search results. That matters because Amazon's ad business generates $68.6 billion a year, and Muse already commands 51 percent of all agentic shopping orders.</p>
