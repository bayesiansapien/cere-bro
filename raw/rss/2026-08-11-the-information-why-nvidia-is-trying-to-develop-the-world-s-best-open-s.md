# Why Nvidia Is Trying To Develop The World’s Best Open-Source AI Models

<p><a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/nvidia">Nvidia</a> is doubling down on its open-source efforts, pouring investment into an ambitious new in-house AI model that it hopes will drive demand for its hardware&mdash;but that could also compete with customers and partners.</p>

<p>Nvidia is aiming for the new model, the biggest of a family that it is calling Nemotron 4, to be on par in its performance with the best open-source AI models in the world, according to several people who work on Nemotron. The research paper detailing Nvidia’s last big model had 570 authors, and employees said Nemotron 4 would involve even more people. “Everyone just wants to be involved at this point,” one former employee said.</p>
