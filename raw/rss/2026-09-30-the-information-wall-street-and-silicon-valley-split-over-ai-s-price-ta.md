# Wall Street and Silicon Valley Split Over AI’s Price Tag

<p>Shifting economic forces are driving a widening divide between the financial views of Silicon Valley and Wall Street, clouding the outlook for trillions of dollars invested in AI.</p>

<p>Stock market angst has already caused delays for several medium-sized initial public offerings, and it is starting to cool once-red hot sentiment about <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/anthropic">Anthropic</a>’s giant IPO, which is coming later in the year than investors expected. Two large public investors said Tuesday that they thought bankers should value Anthropic closer to $1.5 trillion, rather than the $2 trillion that some bankers have floated in early conversations. The AI firm is still likely weeks away from an official IPO roadshow.</p>
