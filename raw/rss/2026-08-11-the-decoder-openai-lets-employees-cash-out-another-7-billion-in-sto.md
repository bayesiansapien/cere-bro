# OpenAI lets employees cash out another $7 billion in stock

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/openai_logo_large_right.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI wrapped up a $7 billion stock buyback, letting current and former employees sell shares at the company's $852 billion valuation. The move is meant to ease pressure on employees waiting for liquidity ahead of a potential IPO. OpenAI ran a similar $6.6 billion sale in October 2025.</p>
