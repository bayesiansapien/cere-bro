# New Israeli Fund Gets an Early Exit; $1 Billion for Anton Levy’s New Fund

<p><a href="https://www.theinformation.com/newsletters/dealmaker/region-software-m-hopping">Software dealmaking is so hot in Israel</a> right now that an Israeli VC fund notched its first exit before it had even wrapped up fundraising.</p><p>Team8, which just raised $365 million to back seed and Series A companies, co-led a $48 million fundraise for Tel Aviv-based Koi late last year out of a new $265 million fund for seed and Series A startups. In April Koi, whose software aims to prevent risks from AI agents, sold to Palo Alto Networks for about $400 million.</p><p>Exits like Koi helped the firm’s managing partners, who include Sarit Firon and Liran Grinberg, raise the firm’s latest fund alongside a new $100 million vehicle for follow-on investments.&nbsp;</p>
