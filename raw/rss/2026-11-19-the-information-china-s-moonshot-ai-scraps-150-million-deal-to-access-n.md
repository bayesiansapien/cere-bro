# China’s Moonshot AI Scraps $150 Million Deal to Access Nvidia Chips in Indonesia

<p>Moonshot AI, the Beijing-based developer of the Kimi models, has scrapped a $150 million deal to access <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/nvidia">Nvidia</a> Blackwell chips through a data center in Indonesia, after the U.S. this week announced a rule to restrict Chinese companies from accessing advanced chips remotely, according to two people with direct knowledge of the matter.</p>

<p>Moonshot signed the contract with a Southeast Asian neocloud to rent access to a sizable cluster of Blackwell Ultra B300 chips, starting from as early as next year, the people said. The B300 is one of Nvidia’s most powerful AI chips, and is barred by the U.S. government from sale to China. Chinese companies were able to access restricted Nvidia chips from data centers outside of the country, a loophole that was plugged by the rule released by the U.S. Commerce Department this week.</p>
