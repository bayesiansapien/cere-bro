# Evaluating Recent Model Releases on GDPx

<p><a href="https://gdpx.semianalysis.com/">Earlier this month</a> we released <a href="https://gdpx.semianalysis.com/">GDPx</a>, a benchmark designed to measure the domains most valuable for frontier intelligence. Most agentic benchmarks only measure a single domain like coding or legal. The few that try to be comprehensive, like <a href="https://openai.com/index/gdpval/">GDPVal</a> or <a href="https://agents-last-exam.org/">Agent&#8217;s Last Exam</a>, make the mistake of selecting domains based on percentage of GDP or the O*NET taxonomy.</p><p>AI&#8217;s impact is nowhere near evenly distributed across the economy today, and we expect this trend to intensify in the future. Many tasks already can&#8217;t justify paying for Fable 5.2, much less Fable 7. Thus, <strong>if your goal is to track the progress of frontier models, you should focus on the domains where they will be most impactful.</strong></p><p>It&#8217;s for this reason that GDPx intentionally forgoes industries like government and real estate, and instead covers the following domains:</p><ul><li><p>Chip design</p></li><li><p>Cybersecurity</p></li><li><p>Biology</p></li><li><p>Finance</p></li><li><p>Legal</p></li><li><p>Quant trading</p></li><li><p>Recursive self-improvement (RSI)</p></li><li><p>Hardware design</p></li><li><p>Materials science</p></li><li><p>Alignment</p></li><li><p>Software engineering</p></li></ul><p>Our methodology is also unique. Astra and Sol</h1><p>GPT-6.2 Astra and 6.2 Sol both do extremely well on GDPx, scoring 19.3% and 11.5%, respectively.
