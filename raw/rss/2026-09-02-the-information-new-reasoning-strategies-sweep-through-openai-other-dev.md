<p>Before we get to today’s column, we’re just three weeks away from <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/events/ai-agenda-live-2026">AI Agenda Live</a>, our flagship conference on all things artificial intelligence (a.k.a. my personal Super Bowl).&nbsp;</p><p>We’ll be joined in San Francisco by top researchers, founders, operators and investors who will be answering all of our burning questions about what’s coming up in bleeding-edge AI research, the next breakthrough AI use cases, and how AI startups are thinking about competing with their biggest model suppliers.&nbsp;</p><p>Speakers include <b>Google DeepMind</b>&nbsp;SVP &amp; Chief AI Architect <b>Koray Kavukcuoglu</b>, who will be doing one of his first public interviews following the recent Google leadership shakeup, and <b>OpenAI’s</b> head of core products <b>Thibault Sottiaux</b>, who will be chatting about the ChatGPT maker’s product roadmap.
