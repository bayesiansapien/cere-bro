# Exit polls show AI ranked last among voter priorities in the 2026 U.S. midterms

<p><img alt="illustration of the US flag in an abstract data stream" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="816" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/usa_flag_data_stream.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1456" /></p>
<p>        In the 2026 U.S. midterm elections, AI ranked last among campaign issues: only 56 percent of respondents cited AI and data centers as an important factor. According to NBC exit polls, corruption (90 percent) and the cost of living (89 percent) clearly dominated, despite growing public attention to the issue.</p>
