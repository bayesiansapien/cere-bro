# Another Google Deepmind researcher quits, says building superintelligent AI soon is "inherently irresponsible"

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1429" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/google_deepmind-scaled.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2560" /></p>
<p>        Google Deepmind researcher Robert O'Callahan has quit, saying AI's "current rate of change is far too high." He worked on chip design tools that helped make AI cheaper and faster, a contribution he can no longer justify. Many colleagues share his concerns but rarely speak out, he says.</p>
