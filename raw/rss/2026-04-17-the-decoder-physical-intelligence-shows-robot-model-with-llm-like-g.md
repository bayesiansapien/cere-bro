# Physical Intelligence shows robot model with LLM-like generalization, flaws included

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1440" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Pi07-title-scaled.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2560" /></p>
<p>        US start-up Physical Intelligence has introduced π0.7, a new robot foundation model designed to recombine skills learned during training, similar to how a language model reassembles text fragments from its training data. The researchers describe this as early signs of "compositional generalization" in robotics.</p>
