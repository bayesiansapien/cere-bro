# China’s New AI Gold Rush: World Models

<p>When Hongyuan Lu finished his doctorate program in AI and engineering a year ago, he reached out to some investors to see if he could raise money for a startup to build world models, AI systems that can understand and simulate the physical world. But the investors, who were focusing on AI agent apps at the time, weren’t interested.</p>

<p>This year, the environment is completely different, he said. “World model” has become a new buzz phrase in China’s startup sector, and Lu, who has co-authored academic papers exploring how to make AI understand a virtual spatial environment, started receiving friend requests on the WeChat messaging app from venture capitalists. His startup&mdash;FaceMind, which is based in Shanghai and Hong Kong&mdash;has raised $20 million in seed money this year from investors including Chinese cybersecurity firm Qihoo 360’s venture fund.</p>
