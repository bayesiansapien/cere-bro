# Deepseek open-sources the inference engine that powers its fastest and cheapest AI model

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/deepseek_deepseek_logo.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Deepseek is open-sourcing DeepInfer, the inference framework behind its fast V4.1-Flash model. The vLLM-based system cuts compute costs and energy use when running AI models. The release adds more pricing pressure on Western providers like OpenAI and Anthropic.</p>
