# AI agents now control part of Deepmind's compute for Gemini experiments, but recursive self-improvement remains distant

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="893" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/gemini-4-neon__blog-header__sm.width-1600.format-webp.webp" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1600" /></p>
<p>        Google Deepmind uses AI agents to run experiments on their own for upcoming Gemini models. Research chief Koray Kavukcuoglu says the lab is still far from recursive self-improvement. For oversight, Deepmind intentionally leaves the agents' internal chains of thought untrained so they can be reviewed. Kavukcuoglu measures AGI by everyday user trust rather than benchmarks.</p>
