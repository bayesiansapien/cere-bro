# Hot take on GPT-6 Astra

<div class="twitter-embed"></div><p>Hot take on OpenAI GPT-6 Astra*<a class="footnote-anchor" href="#footnote-1" id="footnote-anchor-1" target="_self">1</a>, with a challenge to Greg Brockman&#8217;s claims about it being AGI toward the end:</p><ul><li><p>Looks to be pretty impressive. Multiple reports suggest it is a genuine advance. </p></li><li><p>As someone who has campaigned for nearly a decade for (neuro)symbolic world models, often to exceptional hostility, it is <em>extraordinarily</em> vindicating to see that a product from OpenAI explicitly creates and manipulate symbolic world models in the course of some of its most impressive computations.</p><div class="twitter-embed"></div></li><li><p>What we don&#8217;t know is how robust that capability is. That is THE key question.</p></li><li><p>Success on ARC-AGI is great and impressive, but not &#8212;despite the name of the task&#8212;proof of AGI; I suspect we will see loads of problems with open-ended real world tasks. As with other recent models I would suspect best performance in verifiable domains.</p></li><li><p>And as a scientist, it&#8217;s disappointing that we don&#8217;t (yet?) know much about how the system actually works.</p></li><li><p>Without a clearer sense of what&#8217;s under the hood, I feel less confident about both what it can and can&#8217;t do, and what new risks we may encounter. I doubt the world is ready. </p></li><li><p>As ever, enthusiasts got an advance look; skeptics did not. That&#8217;s a sound marketing strategy, but it often turns out to be misleading. What we have often seen is initial enthusiasm that gets tempered over time. I suspect we will see that here as well.</p></li><li><p>The new system appears to be <em><a href="https://x.com/tomekkorbak/status/2095596839886274689?s=61">less</a></em><a href="https://x.com/tomekkorbak/status/2095596839886274689?s=61"> monitorable</a> than prior systems, which is not great from a safety perspective.  One really doesn&#8217;t want more capability in conjunction with less monitorability.  But also more <a href="https://x.com/tomekkorbak/status/2095596839886274689?s=61">alignable</a>, not sure why.</p></li><li><p>Would be great to see whether Astra can make progress on any of the ten tasks that Miles Brundage and I bet on at the end of 2024. (No AI to date has succeeded on any, AFAIK.)</p></li></ul><p class="button-wrapper"><a class="button primary" href="https://garymarcus.substack.com/subscribe?"><span>Subscribe now</span></a></p><div class="footnote"><a class="footnote-number" contenteditable="false" href="#footnote-anchor-1" id="footnote-1" target="_self">1</a><div class="footnote-content"><p>This hot take is VERY tentative, pending more information about how the systems works and more detailed examination of what its limitations are.</p><p class="button-wrapper"><a class="button primary button-wrapper" href="https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/hot-take-on-gpt-6-astra?utm_source=substack&amp;utm_medium=email&amp;utm_content=share&amp;action=share"><span>Share</span></a></p></div></div>
