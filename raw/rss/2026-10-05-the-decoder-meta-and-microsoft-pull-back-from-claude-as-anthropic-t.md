# Meta and Microsoft pull back from Claude as Anthropic transforms from partner into competitor

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/anthropic_claude.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        Meta and Microsoft, two of Anthropic's biggest enterprise customers, are sharply cutting their use of Claude. Microsoft slashed the monthly per-employee budget in its cloud division from $100,000 to $10,000, while Meta halved its Claude Code users to 30,000. Both companies are pushing their own AI tools instead. For Anthropic, that reliance on a few major clients is turning into a strategic risk.</p>
