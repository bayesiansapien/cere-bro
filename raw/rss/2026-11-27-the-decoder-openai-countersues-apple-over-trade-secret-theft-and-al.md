# OpenAI countersues Apple over trade secret theft and alleged coercion of shared suppliers

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/openai_apple.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI is suing Apple in a California court, accusing it of trade secret theft and of coercing shared suppliers. The countersuit follows Apple's own lawsuit from the summer. Just two years ago, the two companies teamed up to bring ChatGPT to the iPhone. Now OpenAI is building its own devices to compete directly with Apple.</p>
