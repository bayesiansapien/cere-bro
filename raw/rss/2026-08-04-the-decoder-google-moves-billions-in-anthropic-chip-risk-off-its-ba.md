# Google moves billions in Anthropic chip risk off its balance sheet

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/google_anthropic_finance_illustration.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Google is working with Broadcom, Apollo, Blackstone, and Morgan Stanley on a multibillion-dollar financing structure that supplies Anthropic with AI chips and data centers while keeping most of the risk off Google's balance sheet. The setup leaves roughly $200 billion in contracts dependent on Anthropic's continued growth and ability to make its lease payments.</p>
