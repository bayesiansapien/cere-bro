# How Anthropic Upstaged OpenAI on Road to Blockbuster IPO

<p>On a sunny day in San Francisco last month, Anthropic Chief Financial Officer Krishna Rao and other executives huddled in the company’s downtown headquarters to brief major investors on its latest financials&mdash;and assuage concerns.</p>

<p>Revenue growth in the third quarter had fallen from its torrid pace. Privately, some investors doubted that the company could go public at the $1.5 trillion valuation bandied about for months, especially as AI-related stocks took a beating.</p>

<p>Throughout the month, Rao and his squad countered those concerns by highlighting growth opportunities in fields from life sciences to chip design, say people familiar with the conversations.</p>

<p>As for the broader market’s jitters? Ignore it, they told investors. That turned out to be decent advice: By Thursday’s close, Anthropic was worth $1.76 trillion on a fully diluted basis.</p>
