# AI Safety Push Sparks Demand for Watchdog Groups. Critics Doubt Their Independence.

<p>Rising alarm about AI’s perils is thrusting into the spotlight a collection of little-known research groups focused on the technology’s safety&mdash;and fueling questions about their ability to effectively monitor the industry’s biggest companies.</p>

<p>Both <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/anthropic">Anthropic</a> Chief Executive Dario Amodei and <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/openai">OpenAI</a> CEO Sam Altman promised in recent days to embed evaluators from such outside research organizations inside their companies to help ensure they develop AI at a safe pace. Other executives including&nbsp;<a href="https://x.com/satyanadella/status/2099220712024408084">Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella</a> also endorsed the idea of embedding independent evaluators in companies.</p>
