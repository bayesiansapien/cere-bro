# Nvidia Moves to Backstop a Cloud Provider’s $45 Billion Anthropic Project

<p>Nvidia is in talks to provide Nscale, a young AI cloud provider, with billions of dollars in credit guarantees for debt to develop a 460-megawatt data center that Anthropic plans to use, according to people with knowledge of the deal discussions.</p>

<p>The deal is not yet signed, and terms could change. But it is another example of how Nvidia has continued to signal its financial support of so-called neoclouds like Nscale, CoreWeave and Firmus, which have faced pressure from investors because of the debt those companies have raised will&nbsp;still need to raise&nbsp;to develop specialized data centers for AI.</p>
