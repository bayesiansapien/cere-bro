# Why Google, Amazon and Microsoft Need Chinese Data Center Suppliers

<p>U.S. data center operators’ reliance on Chinese manufacturers for batteries, cooling systems, optical transceivers and other equipment has come under scrutiny over national security concerns.</p><p>But Chinese equipment makers have an edge over their American counterparts, which have been criticised for long lead times amid supply chain shortages. So despite <a href="https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/08/declaring-a-national-emergency-to-secure-the-united-states-bulk-power-system/">rising hostility</a> from the Trump administration and a <a href="https://www.mccormick.senate.gov/news/press-releases/senators-mccormick-gallego-cornyn-fetterman-introduce-bill-to-keep-chinese-transceivers-out-of-u-s-national-security-systems/">bipartisan group of U.S. senators</a> to the Chinese suppliers, American data center builders like Amazon, Google, Microsoft and Oracle can’t avoid them.</p>
