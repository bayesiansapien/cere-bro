# AI Inference Startups Are Quietly Buying Their Own Chips

<p>The fast-growing crop of startups that lease computing capacity from cloud firms and then resell it, without spending a nickel on their own AI chips, are at a crossroads. Finding it harder to line up enough computing capacity to serve their customers, some are starting to buy their own chips, which is likely to drastically change the economics of their business.</p>

<p>The difficulty in getting enough compute also has pushed some of these startups to look for computational resources from dozens of smaller neoclouds, which tend to have more available capacity than larger hyperscalers such as Microsoft Azure, Amazon Web Services and Google Cloud Platform. <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/new-fireworks-hack-threatens-1-billion-business?rc=9byxri">News this week</a> of a hack of Fireworks, one of the best known of these startups, highlights the risk of that approach.</p>
