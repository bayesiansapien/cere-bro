would grow so much that it would be a major factor in the 2028 Presidential elections.</p><div class="twitter-embed"></div><p>That prediction very seems to be on track. Frustration with AI may even factor into the midterms. It&#8217;s already splitting both parties.</p><p>&#167;</p><p>Here&#8217;s my theory about what&#8217;s going on, slightly edited from a post which <a href="https://x.com/garymarcus/status/2050750631342907725?s=61">has gone viral on X</a>.</p><p>Outside of coding (where there is clear value), and a handful of other domains (e.g. brainstorming), Generative AI has been a net negative for society.</p><p>GenAI has been undermining secondary and college education, opening up mass surveillance, increasing disinformation, delusions, impersonation, phishing, and other forms of cybercrime, nonconsensual deep fake porn, bias in employment and other domains, and economic disparity, drowning the world in slop and unwanted, over-leveraged environment-damaging data centers that risk causing a recession.</p><p>Simultaneously it has empowered a bunch of people who want to privatize almost all the gains while leave all the downsides to society, taking almost zero responsibility. </p><p>I don&#8217;t think we are better off than we were four years ago.</p><p>Some of this is technical (LLMs aren&#8217;t reliable), some of it is political/economic (such as the utter lack of responsible regulation). </p><p>Most of these downsides were predictable.  (I warned of most of it in my <a href="https://www.techpolicy.press/transcript-senate-judiciary-subcommittee-hearing-on-oversight-of-ai/">2023 Senate testimony</a>, in fact, and so did many other folks working in AI ethics over the years. But <a href="https://cdn.openai.com/GPT_2_August_Report.pdf">even more striking is that OpenAI did too, even earlier, in 2019</a>.)</p><p>On net, things don&#8217;t look good.</p><p>&#167;</p><p><strong>All that said, I honestly believe some future form of AI might be great.</strong> But Generative AI has hurt more than it has helped, and been managed irresponsibly.</p><p>It&#8217;s no wonder many people have had enough.</p><p class="button-wrapper"><a class="button primary" href="https://garymarcus.substack.com/subscribe"><span>Subscribe now</span></a></p><p></p><p class="button-wrapper"><a class="button primary" href="https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/the-growing-ai-backlash?utm_source=substack&amp;utm_medium=email&amp;utm_content=share&amp;action=share"><span>Share</span></a></p><p></p><p></p>
