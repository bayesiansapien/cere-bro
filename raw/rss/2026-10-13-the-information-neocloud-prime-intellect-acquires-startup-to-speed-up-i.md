# Neocloud Prime Intellect Acquires Startup To Speed Up Inference

<p>Neoclouds and so-called inference providers that help app developers run open source models are trying to differentiate themselves in a very crowded market.&nbsp;</p><p>For instance, one such neocloud, <b>Prime Intellect</b>, which was valued at $1 billion in an equity financing in July, is buying a startup to help it make open models run faster and more cheaply on its <b>Nvidia</b> servers, compared to its rivals.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>Prime Intellect, based in San Francisco, is acquiring <b>Moondream</b>, a young developer of open-source models that has fewer than 10 employees, according to Prime Intellect co-founder and CEO <b>Vincent Weisser</b>. (Weisser wouldn’t share the terms of the deal, but Moondream has raised $4.5 million in funding.)&nbsp;</p><p>San Francisco- and Seattle-based Moondream is best known for an open-source vision-language model for analyzing and understanding images, like identifying boxes in an image of a warehouse or ads in a livestream. But Prime Intellect is largely acquiring the startup for its employees’ skills in writing software to make models run more efficiently, Weisser said.</p>
