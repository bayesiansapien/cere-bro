# Why DoorDash Needs Its Own AI Assistant

<p>Tech companies may have already perfected the art of separating consumers from their money—but AI promises to take that capability to a whole new level. The latest demonstration of that is DoorDash’s Wednesday <a href="https://about.doordash.com/en-us/news/text-doordash">announcement</a> that customers will soon be able to order restaurant meals and groceries by text. The new service is a result of DoorDash integrating the capabilities of its AI assistant, which is already in its app, into iMessage.&nbsp;</p><p>So if you’re like me and you like to reorder the same restaurant meal every Friday night, now you can do so with a quick text. To be honest, the convenience of this seems illusory, as DoorDash’s mobile app already makes reordering a meal as easy as pie. But the AI assistant, whether it’s in the app or outside it, is an advance. If its a chore to figure out where to order food for a special dinner from, it can suggest places. The assistant is even more handy for groceries: It can list the ingredients you need for a meal and order them.&nbsp;</p>
