# Why Big Tech is Suddenly Paying Up for Startups

<p>When <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/startup-openrouter-fields-multi-billion-dollar-takeover-interest?rc=ndc4g0">The Information broke the news</a> that OpenRouter was in talks to sell the company last month, a shock wave rippled across Silicon Valley.</p>

<p>My phone lit up with bankers asking me if I had also heard that there had been a record nine suitors for the company, which helps customers switch between different AI models and centralize billing. I got the sense that at least two people I called about the deal turned around and placed their own bids as soon as I hung up.</p>
