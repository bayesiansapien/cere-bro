# Microsoft offers a quarter of its workforce voluntary severance while shielding AI-critical teams

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/microsoft_workforce_cuts.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Microsoft is offering voluntary severance packages to about 60,000 employees, roughly a quarter of its workforce, in the second such program this year. The company's headcount has held flat at around 223,000 since 2022 even as it plans to spend about $170 billion on AI infrastructure in 2026 alone.</p>
