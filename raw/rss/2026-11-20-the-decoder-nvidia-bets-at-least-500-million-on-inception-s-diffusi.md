# Nvidia bets at least $500 million on Inception's diffusion models to strengthen its open-source Nemotron lineup

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/inception.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Nvidia is close to acquiring AI startup Inception for at least $500 million, according to The Information. The company is just two years old and builds language models based on diffusion processes, an alternative approach to the token-by-token generation used by models like ChatGPT. The deal would bolster Nvidia's own open-source models and its push into robotics.</p>
