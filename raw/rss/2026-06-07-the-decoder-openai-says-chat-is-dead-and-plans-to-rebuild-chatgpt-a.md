# OpenAI says "chat is dead" and plans to rebuild ChatGPT as a full-blown agent app

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/chatgpt_dead.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI is planning the biggest overhaul of ChatGPT since its launch. The chatbot will become a "superapp" bundling coding tools, AI agents, and partner apps like Canva and Booking.com. "Chat is dead," the company says internally. The future supposedly belongs to agents that handle tasks on their own.</p>
