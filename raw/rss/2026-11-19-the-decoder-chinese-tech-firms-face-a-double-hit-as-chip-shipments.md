# Chinese tech firms face a double hit as chip shipments stop and overseas cloud access disappears

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/us_vs_china_open_models.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Stricter US export controls on AI chips are hitting fast. China's Moonshot AI walked away from a $150 million deal in Indonesia, while TSMC and Samsung stopped shipping to Chinese customers. A New York Times investigation found that Alibaba had routed Nvidia chips to Chinese military researchers through a web of front companies.</p>
