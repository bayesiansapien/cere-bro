# Could this be the Frontier Labs’ WeWork moment?

<p>In May, at the peak of tokenmaxxing, people were talking about OpenAI IPO&#8217;ing at a trillion dollars, Anthropic at two trillion dollars. Maybe that will still happen, but it probably shouldn&#8217;t. </p><p>Tokenmaxxing died, Chinese open-weight models forced price wars, there was some  <a href="https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/openais-revenue-is-reportedly-20-billion-less-than-previously-projected/">hinkiness around some of projections OpenAI&#8217;s  revenue</a> and the so-called &#8220;rogue AI&#8221; (really just bad software) fiasco hadn&#8217;t yet made the news. With the errant, and possibly criminal, nature of poorly controlled agents now front and center, potential liability is clearly becoming a major new issue. LLM&#8217;s might well someday be renamed Large Liability Modules.</p><p>A significant new copyright decision by a U.S. appeals court also <a
