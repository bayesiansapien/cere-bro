# "But marinade" and leaked passwords are what researchers found in ChatGPT's hidden reasoning

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/marinade.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Security researchers found a vulnerability in the APIs of OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google that lets them extract encrypted reasoning traces and move them between models. A scan of public sessions turned up dozens of passwords and API keys. The traces also show that the reasoning summaries users see often hide what the models are actually doing.</p>
