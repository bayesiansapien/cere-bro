# Applied Intuition Fields Offers at $30 Billion Valuation

<p>For the last three years, AI labs like OpenAI, Anthropic and xAI have sucked up a lot of investor attention—as well as nosebleed valuations. Now a growing number of startups that make apps, agents or other software powered by AI are fetching valuations of $20 billion or higher.&nbsp;</p><p>Applied Intuition, a maker of software to train self-driving vehicles, is slated to join this club. Applied Intuition has received investment offers that would value it at least $30 billion, according to people who have spoken to company leaders. The offers are likely to encourage the company to raise at least $1 billion in the next few months, they said.&nbsp;</p>
