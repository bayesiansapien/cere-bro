# The revenge of Claude Mythos

<p>Remember two months ago, when the world was panicking over Claude Mythos, sparked in part by a tweet from Axios?</p><div class="twitter-embed"></div><p>and how Tom Friedman freaked out about it at the New York Times?</p><div class="captioned-image-container"><figure><a class="image-link image2" href="https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0wGR!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F81ef7b03-f51c-4c17-8678-40741a888a80_1268x293.webp" target="_blank"><div class="image2-inset"><source type="image/webp" /><img alt="" class="sizing-normal" height="293" src="https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!0wGR!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F81ef7b03-f51c-4c17-8678-40741a888a80_1268x293.webp" width="1268" /><div></div></div></a></figure></div><p>And how Anthopic said Mythos was too dangerous to release, and held hushed meetings in Washington? </p><p>And how Anthropic&#8217;s valuation leapt up after that? </p><p>Well here, we are, two months later. And guess what&#8230;.</p><p> (A) the world has not ended (remember  <a href="https://open.substack.com/pub/garymarcus/p/three-reasons-to-think-that-the-claude?r=8tdk6&amp;utm_campaign=post-expanded-share&amp;utm_medium=web">how I told you not to panic?</a>) and (B) suddenly, two months later&#8212; just as tokenmaxxing has come to end and companies have begun to limit their AI token budgets &#8212; Mythos is <em>no longer too dangerous to release.</em></p><p>Nope.</p><p>Instead, Anthropic tacked some guardrails on, and pushed it out the door.  </p><p>Anyone can now use it, for the right price. A press release I just got in my email gives up the game:</p><div class="captioned-image-container"><figure><a class="image-link image2" href="https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!XsM0!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F03fe0a0a-debc-4fca-a011-dda5a5fe38f2_1543x261.png" target="_blank"><div class="image2-inset"><source type="image/webp" /><img alt="" class="sizing-normal" height="246" src="https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!XsM0!,w_1456,c_limit,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fsubstack-post-media.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2F03fe0a0a-debc-4fca-a011-dda5a5fe38f2_1543x261.png" width="1456" /><div></div></div></a></figure></div><p>Yep, Anthropic &#8212; that once said they wouldn&#8217;t work on frontier models because they were dangerous, and that called for a pause &#8220;option&#8221; that would probably never in a million years invoke unilaterally or so long as China exists &#8212; just introduced their new frontier model, hellbent on racing everyone on all the benchmarks.</p><p>Anthropic once again played the media like a fiddle.  And so many folks ate it all up.</p><p>&#167;&nbsp;</p><p>And worse, this routine is not even new.
