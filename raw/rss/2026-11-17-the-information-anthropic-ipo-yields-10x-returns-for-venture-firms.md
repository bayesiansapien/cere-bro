# Anthropic IPO Yields 10X Returns for Venture Firms

<p>For the first two years of its existence, Anthropic was shunned by most venture capitalists, drawing instead on a mix of wealthy individuals such as former Google CEO Eric Schmidt and cloud computing companies <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/newsletters/the-briefing/amazon-google-win-big-anthropic-ipo">like Google</a>.</p>

<p>But even after VC firms made relatively late entries into Anthropic, a handful of them, including Spark Capital, Factorial Funds, Sound Ventures and Menlo Ventures, will hold stakes worth over 10 times their investment when Anthropic prices its IPO&nbsp;Wednesday at an expected $1.4 trillion valuation, according to people with knowledge of their investments.</p>

<p>Slightly later investors, including Bessemer Venture Partners and Lightspeed Venture Partners, are slated to see billions of dollars in returns.</p>
