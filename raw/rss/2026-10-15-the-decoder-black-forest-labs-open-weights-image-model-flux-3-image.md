# Black Forest Labs' open-weights image model FLUX 3 Image Klein runs on consumer GPUs with just 8 GB of VRAM

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1024" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/bfl_black_orest_labs_logo-1.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1536" /></p>
<p>        Black Forest Labs has released FLUX 3 Image Klein, a 5.6-billion-parameter open-weights image model that supports native 4K generation, pixel-precise editing, and generation with up to ten reference images. With 38 percent fewer parameters than its predecessor, it still outperforms proprietary competitors in internal tests and runs on GPUs with just 8 GB of VRAM.</p>
