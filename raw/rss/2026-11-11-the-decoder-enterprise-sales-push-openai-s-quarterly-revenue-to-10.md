# Enterprise sales push OpenAI's quarterly revenue to $10 billion, though it still trails Anthropic

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/openai_logo_astra-1.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI grew its quarterly revenue 50 percent to $10 billion but is still burning more cash than it brings in. Adjusted operating losses climbed to $10.9 billion, while rival Anthropic operates far more efficiently on $17 billion in revenue. To close the gap, OpenAI is now fueling a price war for enterprise customers.</p>
