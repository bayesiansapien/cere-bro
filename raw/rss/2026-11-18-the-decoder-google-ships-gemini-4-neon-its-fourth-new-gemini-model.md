# Google ships Gemini 4 Neon, its fourth new Gemini model in a month

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/gemini_4_argon.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Google released Gemini 4 Neon, a new frontier model built for software development, knowledge work, and science. It handles text, images, video, audio, and PDFs with a context window of one million tokens. At $3 per million input tokens and $15 per million output tokens, Neon is priced above previous Gemini 4 models.</p>
