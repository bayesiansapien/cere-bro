# Airbnb Founder Chesky Is Self-Funding His New AI Startup

<p>Airbnb on Thursday shed new light on a secretive AI startup that founder and CEO Brian Chesky&nbsp;controls, saying the company has reached an agreement that could allow it to invest in the startup and license its technology. Chesky has so far self-funded the AI lab and plans to continue financing it, according to a person with knowledge of his plans.</p>

<p>The new details, some of which were disclosed in a securities filing after Airbnb’s quarterly report, involve a startup Chesky has kept in stealth mode for much of the last year as he continues to run the short-term rental site he founded nearly two decades ago. Airbnb Chief Technology Officer Ahmad Al-Dahle, who joined Airbnb in January after running aspects of Meta’s AI organization, has been advising the startup.</p>
