# Anthropic's Claude Cowork AI agent is now available on mobile and web

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/antropic_logo_chat_cowork-2.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic is rolling out its AI agent Claude Cowork to mobile and web. Until now, the feature was limited to the desktop app. The agent keeps working in the background even when the laptop is closed and pings users on their phone when it needs a decision. The move blurs the line between Chat and Cowork even further.</p>
