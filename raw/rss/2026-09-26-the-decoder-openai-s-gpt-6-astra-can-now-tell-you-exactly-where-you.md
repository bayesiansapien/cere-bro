# OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra can now tell you exactly where you screwed up your IKEA shelf

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/furniture_assembly_benchmark.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra can look at a photo and tell whether an IKEA furniture piece was assembled incorrectly, hitting an 80 percent accuracy rate. Back in November 2025, the best model managed just 28 percent. According to Epoch AI, the speed isn't quite fast enough yet for real-time assembly guidance, but the gap is closing quickly.</p>
