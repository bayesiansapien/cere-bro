# Uber’s Jill Hazelbaker to Join OpenAI as Chief Corporate Affairs Officer

<p><a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/uber">Uber</a>’s President Jill Hazelbaker is joining <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/openai">OpenAI</a> as its chief corporate affairs officer, filling a role that has been empty since the start of the year.</p>

<p>Hazelbaker, who is also a veteran of Google and Snap, will be responsible for global communications and brand strategy. She will attempt to stabilize a team that has had significant turnover in its senior management in recent years and a time when market jitters ahead of a potential IPO are mounting.</p>
