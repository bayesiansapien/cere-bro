# Signal blocks screenshots on iOS 27 and Android to stop AI agents from reading private chats

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/signal_ai_agents.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Signal is blocking screenshots to stop AI agents like Apple's Siri AI, Google's Gemini Intelligence, and Meta's Muse from reading private chats. The app calls it an "existential threat" to privacy, but admits its protections remain incomplete, including on macOS. Apple, Google, and Microsoft should follow suit.</p>
