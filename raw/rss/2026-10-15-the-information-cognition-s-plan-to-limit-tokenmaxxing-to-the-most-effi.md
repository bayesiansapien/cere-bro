# Cognition’s Plan to Limit Tokenmaxxing to the Most Efficient Engineers

<p>To rein in AI spending, large companies are capping employees’ usage, turning to cheaper and smaller models and using routers to send only the hardest requests to the most expensive models.</p><p><b>Cognition</b>, the startup behind the AI coding agent <b>Devin</b>, is adding a new trick to the book: measuring how efficiently different engineers use AI so companies can allocate more resources toward their most efficient employees, Cognition told The Information exclusively.</p><p>“The limits that a user should have should not be static. They should be dynamic based on the efficiency of your usage and the value that you're getting out of it,” said <b>Calvin Giroud</b>, a vice president of customer engineering at Cognition.</p><p>By looking at an engineer’s coding session using Devin, Cognition can estimate the equivalent number of hours it would have taken a human to do the same amount of work. Dividing by the costs of that session, Cognition gets a measure of each engineer’s efficiency.&nbsp;</p>
