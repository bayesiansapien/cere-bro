# OpenAI offers discounts up to 90 percent to poach Anthropic's biggest customers

<p>        OpenAI is poaching Anthropic's customers with steep discounts. Companies like Rippling, Replit, and DataCamp say they've cut AI costs by up to 90 percent after switching.
