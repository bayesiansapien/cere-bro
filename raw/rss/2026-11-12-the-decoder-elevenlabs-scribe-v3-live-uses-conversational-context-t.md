# ElevenLabs Scribe v3 Live uses conversational context to stop voice agents from cutting off callers mid-sentence

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/elevenlabs.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        ElevenLabs has released Scribe v3 and Scribe v3 Live, two new speech recognition models designed to transcribe accurately through background noise and crosstalk. Benchmarks from Artificial Analysis confirm that Scribe v3 narrowly beats competitors like OpenAI, Google, and Microsoft on accuracy. That precision comes at a cost, though. Rivals like Microsoft and xAI run many times faster and charge roughly half the price.</p>
