# OpenAIs GPT-6 Cyber can both break into systems and propose how to fix them

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/openai_cybersecurity.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        With GPT-6 Cyber, OpenAI is introducing an AI model built to find security vulnerabilities in software, develop exploits, and suggest patches. According to OpenAI, it has already found 81 previously unknown vulnerabilities in Firefox and developed 948 working exploits in the Linux kernel, significantly more than its predecessor.</p>
