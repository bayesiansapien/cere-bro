# Bret Taylor’s Sierra in Talks to Raise at $30 Billion Valuation

<p>Sierra, the AI customer support startup founded by former Salesforce co-CEO Bret Taylor, has been in talks to raise new funding at around a $30 billion valuation, according to two people with knowledge of the fundraise. That price would nearly double the $15 billion-plus valuation it received in its last round announced in May.</p>

<p>The three-year old startup is discussing raising around $1.5 billion in new funding, one of the people said.&nbsp; Lightspeed Venture Partners, Greenoaks and Benchmark are in talks to invest, the person said. The talks are still early and terms could change.</p>
