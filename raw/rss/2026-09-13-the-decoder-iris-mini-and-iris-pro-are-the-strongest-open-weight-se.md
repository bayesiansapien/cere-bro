# Iris-mini and Iris-pro are the strongest open-weight search agents in their class

<p><img alt="Colorful browser windows and speech bubbles, connected by arrows to a glowing network, symbolize AI search agents." class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1047" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/search-agents-allspark-nano-banana-pro.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1920" /></p>
<p>        The AllSpark team has released Iris-mini and Iris-pro, two open-source search agents built on Qwen models that lead benchmarks among open-weight models in their size classes. According to the paper, the training data and models also improved performance on tasks they were never trained for, including general tool use and office work.</p>
