# Apple brings a fully revamped Siri built on Google's Gemini, but not to the EU

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/apple_logo_neon_green.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        Apple is shipping its rebuilt "Siri AI" after years of delay, built on Google's Gemini models and running partly on the device, partly through Private Cloud Compute. Early testers praise multi-step requests and screen context, but report hallucinations and gaps with personal context. In the EU, the assistant stays unavailable for now.</p>
