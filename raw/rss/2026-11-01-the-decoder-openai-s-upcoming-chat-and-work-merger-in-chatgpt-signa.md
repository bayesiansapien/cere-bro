# OpenAI's upcoming chat-and-work merger in ChatGPT signals the end of all-you-can-prompt pricing

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/openai_chatgpt_colors.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI combines the Chat and Work modes into a single interface. Starting in December, Plus, Pro, and Business plans will share a common, token-based quota for ChatGPT and Codex. More complex models consume more of this quota, while simple instant chats do not count toward it. In practice, this is likely to result in a decline in ChatGPT’s quality for heavy users on lower-cost plans.</p>
