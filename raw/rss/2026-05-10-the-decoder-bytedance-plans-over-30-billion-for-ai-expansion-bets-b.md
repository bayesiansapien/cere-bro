# ByteDance plans over $30 billion for AI expansion, bets big on Chinese chips

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/china_neural_network.jpeg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1365" /></p>
<p>        ByteDance is raising its planned AI spending for 2026 to over 200 billion yuan (roughly $30 billion), at least a 25 percent jump from earlier plans. The TikTok parent is increasingly turning to Chinese chips. Still, the figure looks modest next to the $725 billion that Google, Amazon, Microsoft, and Meta are planning to spend combined.</p>
