# Nvidia in Talks to Invest $3 Billion in SB Energy as Part of OpenAI Data Center Deal

<p>Nvidia is in talks to invest as much as $3 billion in SB Energy, the Softbank Group-backed developer of a massive planned Ohio data center project for OpenAI, according to people familiar with the discussions.</p>

<p>The investment is being discussed as part of Nvidia’s negotiations with OpenAI and SB Energy over the chip giant providing <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/nvidia-nears-deal-guarantee-100-billion-financing-massive-data-center">around $100 billion</a> in credit support for the planned Ohio data center campus, one of the people said. Those deals would add to a growing number cases where Nvidia is using its financial heft to support AI-related companies and projects&nbsp;to make it easier for firms to buy and use Nvidia hardware.</p>
