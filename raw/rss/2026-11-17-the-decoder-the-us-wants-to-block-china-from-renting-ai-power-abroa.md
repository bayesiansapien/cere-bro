# The US wants to block China from renting AI power abroad but China already built a parallel supply chain from US AI output

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Datacenter-Remote.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        The US is tightening its chip export controls. Data centers in third countries like Singapore and Malaysia will soon need a US license to buy Nvidia chips, closing the loophole that let Chinese firms rent cloud compute abroad. A Reuters investigation also reveals how China offsets its hardware shortage through industrial-scale distillation of US AI models.</p>
