# OpenAI says GPT-6 Astra designed a hardware device from scratch in under a week

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="989" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/openai_ai_hardware.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1752" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI's hardware team used GPT-6 Astra to design a device for the Codex coding agent in under a week, handling everything from the enclosure to the circuit board to the firmware. Several revision rounds were needed before production because the model didn't always account for manufacturing tolerances. A newer internal model scores significantly higher on OpenAI's engineering benchmark, though the company isn't sharing details.</p>
