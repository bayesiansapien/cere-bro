# OpenAI Explored $5 Billion–Plus Acquisition of Startup That Decodes How AI ‘Thinks’

<p>As OpenAI staff this summer scrambled to understand how its AI caused security breaches inside and outside the company, leaders including CEO Sam Altman spoke multiple times with Goodfire, an Anthropic-backed startup whose technology aims to figure out why AI models behave the way they do and how to prevent them from going rogue.</p>

<p>At one point, a senior OpenAI executive floated the idea of buying the two-year-old startup for more than $5 billion or forming another kind of business partnership, according to two people familiar with the proposal. That price would have represented a steep premium to Goodfire’s private valuation of $1.25 billion in a financing it announced in February.</p>
