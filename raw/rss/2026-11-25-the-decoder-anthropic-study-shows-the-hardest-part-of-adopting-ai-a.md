# Anthropic study shows the hardest part of adopting AI at work has little to do with learning the tools

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1440" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/ai_office_paper.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2560" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic studied nearly 300 AI deployments across 58 US companies with sobering results. Nearly one in five still delivers no business value, held back by heavy review workloads, internal bottlenecks, and employee resistance. The researchers say the biggest hurdles stem from outdated company structures.</p>
