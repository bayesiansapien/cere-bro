# Anthropics' Claude cracks a 25-year-old NSA cryptography mystery in under 30 minutes

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/anthropic_unintended_actions.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic's AI model Claude has solved a 25-year-old cryptography puzzle. The mysterious seeds behind NIST's standard curves for digital signatures were provided by the NSA without explanation, raising long-standing suspicion about built-in backdoors. Claude figured out in under 30 minutes that the seeds came from "lrand48," a simple C random number generator, making deliberate backdoors far less likely.</p>
