# Google Deepmind's Project Genie update brings Genie 4, chainable worlds, and a community gallery for remixing

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1080" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Genie-4-Google-title.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1920" /></p>
<p>        With Genie 4, Google DeepMind is launching its most realistic world model to date for Google AI Ultra subscribers. It generates interactive 3D worlds that can be modified in real time via text input and, for the first time, includes native sound. A community gallery allows users to share and remix the worlds they've created.</p>
