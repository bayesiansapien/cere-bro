# Elevenlabs makes Music v2.5 available via app and API with free and pro tier options

<p>        ElevenLabs has released Music v2.5 for its AI music generator. In a blind test with nearly 48,000 comparison pairs, listeners preferred the new version over its predecessor. The company says the model was trained only on licensed music.</p>
