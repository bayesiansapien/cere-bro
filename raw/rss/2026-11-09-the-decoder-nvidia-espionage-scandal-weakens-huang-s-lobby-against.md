# Nvidia espionage scandal weakens Huang's lobby against tighter AI chip export controls

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/nvidia_logo.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has admitted to an extramarital affair with a woman US intelligence agencies classify as a suspected Chinese informant. The board has launched an independent investigation. The affair weakens Huang's lobbying efforts against stricter AI chip export controls, with a newly elected Democratic Congress poised to ramp up scrutiny of AI chip smuggling to China and push for tighter restrictions.</p>
