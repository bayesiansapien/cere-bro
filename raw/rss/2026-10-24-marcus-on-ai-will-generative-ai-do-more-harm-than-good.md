# Will Generative AI do more harm than good?

<p>In early March 2023, I had a debate with Keith Teare, hosted by the organization Open to Debate, on the question of whether ChatGPT would do more harm than good. I think about that question a lot, nearly every day. </p><div class="youtube-wrap" id="youtube2-xK35NH3UL1c"><div class="youtube-inner"></div></div><p>Right now, the <em>negative</em> argument against generative AI&#8212;the ledger of bad things&#8212;is quite serious. We have nonconsensual deep fake porn, a rise in phishing scams, wholesale disinformation, cognitive surrender, threats to democracy from the subtle influence of governments on LLMs, not to mention the use of generative AI as a surveillance tool. And the effects of all that IP theft on artists and writers, and potential negative effects more broadly across the employment market (though, mercifully, these have been more modest than some people
