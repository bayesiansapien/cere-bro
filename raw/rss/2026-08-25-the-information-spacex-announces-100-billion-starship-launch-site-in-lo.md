# SpaceX Announces $100 Billion Starship Launch Site in Louisiana

<p>SpaceX announced on Tuesday plans to build a $100 billion launch site for its Starship rocket on the coast of Louisiana.</p> <p>SpaceX plans to begin construction of the site, which the company has named “Starbase, Louisiana,” in 2027 and is targeting 2029 for the first Starship launch from the site. ...</p>
