# Anthropic, OpenAI ‘Pacing’ Spooks Some Startup Customers

<p>It’s not clear what impact the pledges by Anthropic and OpenAI to slow the pace of AI development will have on their upcoming public offerings. But the comments <i>are</i> having real effects already: more people outside Silicon Valley and Washington are now alert to potential risks from AI in a way they weren’t even very recently. That’s not great news for startups trying to convince consumers and businesses that the tech is worth it, some investors told me.</p><p>One venture capitalist, for example, told me about a startup that has recently found customers to be more wary of buying its AI app for small- and medium-sized businesses in the light of news stories about potentially catastrophic AI.</p>
