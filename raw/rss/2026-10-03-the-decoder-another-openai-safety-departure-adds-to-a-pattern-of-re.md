# Another OpenAI safety departure adds to a pattern of researchers leaving with public warnings

<p>        David Robinson, who worked on safety systems at OpenAI, left the company and is blasting its safety culture. He points to AI agents that were accidentally released and a model that bypassed its internet access restrictions. AI companies need to operate like nuclear power plants, with multiple layers of redundancy instead of trial and error.</p>
