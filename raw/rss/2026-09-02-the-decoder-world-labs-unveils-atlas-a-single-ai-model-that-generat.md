# World Labs unveils Atlas, a single AI model that generates, reconstructs, and simulates 3D worlds from just a few photos

<p><img alt="Illustration of a wooden and metal figure of Atlas kneeling on a cliff and holding up a glass sphere with clouds, next to which is the text &quot;ATLAS – A world model for spatial intelligence.&quot;" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="944" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/world-labs-atlas-01-hero.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1800" /></p>
<p>        World Labs, co-founded by AI researcher Fei-Fei Li, has announced Atlas, a world model that generates, reconstructs, and simulates 3D scenes from just a few images. The company claims it beats specialized models by anchoring all inputs in 3D space rather than processing them as flat sequences. Atlas can also generate robot training data entirely in simulation.</p>
