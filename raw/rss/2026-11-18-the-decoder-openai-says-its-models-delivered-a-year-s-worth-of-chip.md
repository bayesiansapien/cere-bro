# OpenAI says its models delivered a year's worth of chip optimization on top of AMD's own engineering

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/openai_red_Logo.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        Working with AMD, OpenAI achieved up to 14 percent PPA improvement on AMD's flagship GPU IP in less than five months. According to OpenAI, that's roughly a year's worth of progress, and AMD has already folded the changes into a design sent to production. Anthropic is also applying AI to chip work to optimize cost and performance per dollar.</p>
