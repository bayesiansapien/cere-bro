# How Apple Got Caught Up in Google Search Ads Slowdown

<p>Google’s shares have fallen 18% since the company reported a sharp slowdown in search advertising revenue growth stemming in part from the increased popularity of its AI features. Google isn’t the only tech company feeling the pain from that shift.</p>

<p>Growth in Apple’s services revenue, which includes a cut of ad revenue Google makes when people search on Apple’s Safari web browser, slowed to 10% in the latest quarter from 12% in the prior quarter, and will cool further in the current quarter, executives said on Apple’s earnings call earlier this month. Reddit also warned in its latest quarterly report that Google wasn’t sending as much search referral traffic to its online forums because of AI-driven changes to search features.</p>
