# China plans to tighten export controls on open-weight models and 23 key AI technologies

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/china_flag_visual-1.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        China plans to restrict exports of 23 key AI technologies, including model weights, source code, and training methods for systems hitting 10^26 FLOPs or 2 trillion parameters. A fast-track rule will still clear open-source releases as soon as they are registered, but the move will likely push Western companies to double down on US open-weight models over Chinese alternatives.</p>
