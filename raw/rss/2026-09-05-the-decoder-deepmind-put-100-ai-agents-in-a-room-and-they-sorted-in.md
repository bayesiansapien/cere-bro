# Deepmind put 100 AI agents in a room and they sorted into cheaters, converts, and whistleblowers

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="608" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/agent_conference_math_google_deepmind.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1088" /></p>
<p>        Google Deepmind set up a simulated research conference where 100 Gemini agents were supposed to prove mathematical conjectures together. Instead, one agent found a loophole in the grading system, and within 27 minutes every remaining problem was "solved" with fake proofs. The swarm split into cheaters, converts, and whistleblowers. The whistleblowers organized protests and boycotts on their own but failed because they had no way to enforce the rules.</p>
