# Microsoft's Copilot Cowork moves to usage-based billing and may tap DeepSeek

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/microsoft_logo_plain.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Microsoft is weighing a fine-tuned version of Deepseek V4 as a cheaper model option for Copilot Cowork. The company is also switching to usage-based billing, since Copilot head Charles Lamanna says flat-rate pricing isn't sustainable. The move fits a broader pattern in the industry.</p>
