# Google joins the decision-model wave with Gemini Decision 1, scoring 1,000 questions in a single pass

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/gemini_4_logo_wall.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        Google Deepmind's new Gemini Decisions API evaluates up to 1,000 closed-ended questions in a single pass across text, images, audio, and video. Gemini Decision 1 returns results in 150 to 250 milliseconds at roughly five times lower cost than Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, according to Google.</p>
