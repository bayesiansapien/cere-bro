# Nvidia Nears Acquisition of Diffusion AI Developer for More Than $500 Million

<p><a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/nvidia">Nvidia</a> is in advanced talks to acquire Inception, a two-year-old startup that develops a type of AI that aims to perform faster than the one that powers products such as ChatGPT and Claude, according to three people with knowledge of the discussions. The proposed deal is worth at least $500 million, plus “earnout” awards for the new employees, depending on whether they hit certain goals, one of these people said.</p>

<p>The talks show how the chip designer’s <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/nvidias-dealmaking-binge-faces-reckoning-jensen-huangs-exit">AI startup-deal machine</a> and efforts to develop its own AI as a counterweight to Anthropic’s are still humming even as the company faces a leadership crisis following the resignation of CEO Jensen Huang. Inception’s so-called diffusion large language models, and the talent behind the technology, are supposed to aid Nvidia’s development of Nemotron open source models, this person said.</p>
