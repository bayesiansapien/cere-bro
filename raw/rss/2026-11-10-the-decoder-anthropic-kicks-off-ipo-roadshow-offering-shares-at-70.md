# Anthropic kicks off IPO roadshow, offering shares at $70 to $78 and targeting a trillion-dollar valuation

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/anthropic_claude.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic is kicking off its IPO roadshow, targeting a valuation of up to $1.02 trillion. Beyond the public offering of up to $64.4 billion, the company is securing $26 billion through a private placement with Google, Nvidia, Microsoft, and other major tech firms as anchor investors. The IPO comes at a time when investors are growing increasingly skeptical of AI investments.</p>
