# Why Anthropic’s IPO Is Making Philanthropist Craig Newmark Hold Back on AI Safety Donations

<p><b>Craigslist</b> founder <b>Craig Newmark</b> says he’s recently become worried about AI’s potential to cause serious harm to critical infrastructure and human society. He’s considered directing some of his philanthropic funding towards organizations trying to make sure powerful AI doesn’t harm humans.</p><p>But Newmark told me he is holding back for now because he expects a tsunami of donations from newly wealthy <b>Anthropic</b> employees to such organizations, following Anthropic’s blockbuster IPO this week. These potentially outweigh what he could give, and he feels his money could have more utility elsewhere.</p><p>“Lots of people are paying lots of attention to it, so I can focus in other areas,” Newmark said in a video interview earlier this month.&nbsp;</p><p>“Now, maybe I'm being naive thinking that the people developing AI will be the most concerned about its safety,” Newmark said. If AI employees don’t donate to such groups, Newmark says he may have to call “very publicly and very loudly” for philanthropic support for AI safety in addition to his own.</p>
