# The Pentagon's new AI playbook treats slow adoption as a bigger risk than imperfect alignment

<p><img alt="Illustration of a U.S. Navy destroyer with a binary data stream and target markers, symbolizing AI-based weapons integration." class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/US-Navy-Weaponize-AI.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        The US Department of the Navy has signed a strategy to "weaponize" data and AI and build an "AI-first" fleet. Large language models would run directly on warships, and an AI war council would prioritize mission scenarios. The core message is that moving too slowly carries greater risks than "imperfect alignment."</p>
