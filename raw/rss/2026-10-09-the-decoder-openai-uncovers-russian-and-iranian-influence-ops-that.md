# OpenAI uncovers Russian and Iranian influence ops that planted fake stories in real news outlets

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/russian_influence_campaigns.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI exposed a Russian and an Iranian influence operation and banned the accounts involved. The Russian "Dark Clark" campaign spread disinformation across Latin America and drew reactions from politicians, earning the first category 5 out of 6 rating in OpenAI's reporting history. The Iranian "Bogus Bylines" operation used seven fake journalists to place nearly 100 articles in online outlets worldwide.</p>
