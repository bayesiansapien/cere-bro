# Google warns that fully automated cyberattacks have arrived and will get worse fast

<p>Google's Threat Intelligence Group has for the first time caught attackers finding and exploiting security vulnerabilities with no human involvement at all. An AI framework called "exploit_agent," found on an exposed server, automatically compared code before and after patches, built exploits, and ran them on its own.</p>
