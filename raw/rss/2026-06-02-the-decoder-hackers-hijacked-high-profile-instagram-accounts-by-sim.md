# Hackers hijacked high-profile Instagram accounts by simply asking Meta's AI chatbot to change the email

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Meta-AI-Instagram-Vault.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Hackers took over prominent Instagram accounts, including the Obama White House page, by simply asking Meta's AI support chatbot to change the email address on file. Two-factor authentication was bypassed entirely. Meta has patched the flaw, but security researchers say another exploit is already circulating on Telegram.</p>
