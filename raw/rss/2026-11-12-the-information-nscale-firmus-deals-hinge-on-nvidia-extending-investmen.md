# Nscale, Firmus Deals Hinge on Nvidia Extending Investment Sprint

<p>In the last three years, Nvidia has emerged as <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/nvidia-accelerates-ai-startup-investments-nears-deal-with-cloud-provider-lambda-labs?rc=zjctrx">one of the most active backers</a> of AI startups, small cloud providers and frontier AI labs&mdash;largely thanks to CEO Jensen Huang’s unrelenting focus on ensuring continued, strong demand for its specialized AI chips. With his sudden resignation late Wednesday, that source of cash now looks at risk.</p>

<p>Venture capitalists, startup founders and semiconductor executives say they expect Nvidia to pull back on its frenetic dealmaking pace, <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/nvidias-100-billion-dealmaking-juggernaut-will-go-next?rc=zjctrx">which totaled $140 billion</a>, including massive credit guarantees, in just two months this year. That activity includes showering cash on big chip users&mdash;Nvidia plans to put $10 billion into Anthropic’s initial public offering next week. But the most vulnerable could be the companies in the middle of funding rounds in which Nvidia is expected to invest.</p>
