# GPT-6.2 Astra users now hit limits 40% faster after OpenAI patched a pricing error

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/openai.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI says a billing bug gave GPT-6.2 users incorrect discounts. After the fix, Astra and Sol users hit their limits 40 and 25 percent faster than last week. As an apology, subscribers get an extra reset, and business users get free Fast Mode through year-end. OpenAI is also testing a Chat and Work mode merger.</p>
