# Why Anthropic’s VC Investors Aren’t Letting Go

<p>Earlier this week I reported that at least <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/anthropics-vc-investors-hit-10x-returns-ipo">four venture firms</a> were in line to make at least 10 times their investment in Anthropic, whose shares closed up 17% on the first day of trading Thursday. For many venture capitalists and the endowments and foundations that invest in VC funds, the offering is the first step to turning private shares into cash.&nbsp;</p><p>But some VCs want even more of the company: Sequoia Capital and Iconiq had planned to buy more shares at the IPO, a person familiar with the firms’ thinking said. Lightspeed Venture Partners and Accel intended to do the same, Bloomberg News reported Tuesday. And Altimeter CEO Brad Gerstner told CNBC his firm aimed to invest $5 billion in the IPO. Many of these investors already stand to reap billions from Anthropic’s IPO: Iconiq and Lightspeed hold some of the largest VC stakes, valued at over $27 billion each based on the size of their stakes before the IPO.</p>
