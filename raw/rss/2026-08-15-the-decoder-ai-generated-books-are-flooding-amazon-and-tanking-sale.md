# AI-generated books are flooding Amazon and tanking sales for human authors

<p><img alt="Mass production of AI-generated books on an assembly line ahead of the launch of Amazon&#039;s Kindle website." class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1047" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/AI-generated-books-dilute-Amazon-Kindle-Nano-Banana-Pro.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1920" /></p>
<p>        AI-generated books make up 20 percent of Amazon's self-published catalog but bring in only 12 percent of sales. A new study finds that revenue per book is dropping for human-written titles too, in seven of eight genres. The findings could give copyright plaintiffs the market-harm data their cases against AI companies have been missing.</p>
