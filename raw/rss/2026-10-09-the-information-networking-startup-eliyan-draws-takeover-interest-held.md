# Networking Startup Eliyan Draws Takeover Interest, Held Early Talks With Arm

<p>Semiconductor M&amp;A has been humming. One of the next deals could be for Eliyan, whose technology allows separate chips inside an AI system to talk to one another more quickly and efficiently.&nbsp;</p><p>The five-year-old startup, valued at $1 billion in July, has notified some of its investors in the last few days that it received a takeover bid, according to people familiar with the matter. It has been working with an investment bank since the summer and is likely to aim for a valuation of $3 billion, according to some of the people.&nbsp;</p><p>Softbank-owned Arm, an existing investor, held early talks about potentially buying Eliyan, but it didn’t make a bid and would be unlikely to do so at that valuation, said a person familiar with the matter.</p>
