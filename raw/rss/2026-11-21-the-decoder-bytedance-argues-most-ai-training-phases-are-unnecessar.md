# Bytedance argues most AI training phases are unnecessary and backs it up with a competitive open model

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1047" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/bytedance-axio-training-pipeline-nano-banana-pro.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1920" /></p>
<p>        Bytedance and several US universities introduce Axion 1.0, an AI agent that rivals top commercial models from OpenAI and Anthropic on benchmarks after just three training phases. The researchers attribute the gains to carefully curated training data rather than ever more training steps. The model, data, and code are freely available.</p>
