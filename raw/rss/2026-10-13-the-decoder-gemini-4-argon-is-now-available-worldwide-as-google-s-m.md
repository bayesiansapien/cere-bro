# Gemini 4 Argon is now available worldwide as Google's most capable frontier model

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="964" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/gemini_4_argon.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1681" /></p>
<p>        Google is making Gemini 4 Argon available worldwide through its API, the Gemini app, and Google Antigravity. The frontier model is aggressively priced but trails Anthropic's Claude Opus 5.5 and Meta's Muse Wonder Full in independent benchmarks. Internally, Google says Argon already cut coding tasks from months to weeks.</p>
