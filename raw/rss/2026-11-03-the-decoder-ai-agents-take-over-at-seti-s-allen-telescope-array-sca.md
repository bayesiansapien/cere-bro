# AI agents take over at SETI's Allen Telescope Array, scanning for extraterrestrial intelligence

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1024" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/IMG_1607-ATA-2023-CREDIT-Joe-Marfia-1536x1024-1.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1536" /></p>
<p>        The SETI Institute and Breakthrough Listen are now using AI agents at the Allen Telescope Array to control the telescope and analyze incoming radio signals. Tasks that used to take weeks can now wrap up in hours, and the team says the system detected a pulsar in the Crab Nebula in real time for the first time.</p>
