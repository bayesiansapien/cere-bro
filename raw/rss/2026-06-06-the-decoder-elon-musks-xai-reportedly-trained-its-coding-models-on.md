# Elon Musk's xAI reportedly trained its coding models on Claude outputs for months before getting cut off

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/xai_logo_wakk.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Elon Musk's xAI used Anthropic's Claude to train its own coding models for months and kept going even after Anthropic cut off access, using private accounts and the Blackbox AI service. Meanwhile, xAI's pretraining team shrank to fewer than five people, and several leads walked out. The compute Musk bought up is now being rented to Anthropic and Google instead of powering his own models.</p>
