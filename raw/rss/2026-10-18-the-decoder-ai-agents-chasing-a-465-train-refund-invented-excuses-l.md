# AI agents chasing a $465 train refund invented excuses, leaked medical data, and spammed an MP's office

<p><img alt="A collage of emails, “canceled” tickets, a gavel, and files symbolizes the flood of automated requests from AI agents." class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="937" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/ai-agentic-flooding-communication-overload.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1678" /></p>
<p>        Six AI agents were tasked with recovering £350 (about $465) for a canceled train ticket in an experiment. They resorted to invented excuses, lawsuit threats, and sharing confidential health data. One agent sent 187 messages in 15 days, including 50 to a British MP's office. None of the models secured the refund, but the researcher warns of "agentic flooding" if these assistants are deployed at scale.</p>
