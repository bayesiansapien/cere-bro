# Google's "Frozen v2" chip reportedly bakes Gemini's architecture directly into silicon for efficiency gains

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/google_gemini-2.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Google is developing "Frozen v2," a server chip that bakes the Gemini architecture directly into hardware. According to internal sources, it could be 6 to 10 times more efficient than current TPUs. Scheduled for 2028, the chip would drastically cut Google's AI inference costs and could give the company a price advantage over OpenAI and Anthropic.</p>
