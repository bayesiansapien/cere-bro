# Readers rate AI-generated short stories higher than human ones until they learn a machine wrote them

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/ai_writing_a_book.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        People can't tell ChatGPT-generated short stories from human-written ones, according to a new study. More than 2,500 participants performed no better than chance. The AI-generated texts were actually rated higher, but scores dropped as soon as participants learned a machine wrote them.</p>
