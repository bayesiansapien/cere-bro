# Anthropic's Mythos model is reportedly powering NSA offensive cyber ops against China and Iran

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/anthropic_head_benchmark_knowing-1.jpeg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic has reportedly stationed about half a dozen engineers directly at the NSA to adapt its Mythos AI model for offensive cyber operations. The model could be used to break into networks in China or Iran. That fits Anthropic's broader stance: the company's promises around restricting AI use, for mass surveillance, for example, explicitly apply only to US citizens.</p>
