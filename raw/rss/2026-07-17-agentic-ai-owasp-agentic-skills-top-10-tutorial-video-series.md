# OWASP Agentic Skills Top 10 - Tutorial Video Series

<p>Hello, subscribers. We have just published a 10-part video series on the OWASP Agentic Skills Top 10.</p><p>You can watch the full series on my Substack podcast page here:</p><p><a href="https://kenhuangus.substack.com/podcast">Watch the full video series on Substack</a></p><p>You can also find the OWASP project here:</p><p><a href="https://owasp.org/www-project-agentic-skills-top-10/">OWASP Agentic Skills Top 10</a></p><h2>About this Series</h2><p>Agentic skills are becoming first-class citizens of AI workflows.</p><p>They let AI systems call tools, retrieve data, modify files, trigger actions, use external services, and complete tasks across platforms. That power is useful, but it also changes the risk model. A skill is no longer just content. It can become an execution path.</p><p>The OWASP Agentic Skills Top 10 was created to help developers, security teams, platform operators, and AI builders understand where these risks show up and how to reduce them before they become production incidents.</p><p>This video series walks through all 10 risks in a practical way. Each episode explains the issue, gives examples, and points toward the controls teams should consider when building or reviewing agentic skill ecosystems.</p><h2>The Full Video Series</h2><p><a
