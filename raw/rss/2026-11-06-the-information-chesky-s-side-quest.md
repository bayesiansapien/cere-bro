# Chesky’s Side Quest

<p>Once upon a time, being CEO of a public company was a full-time job. In fact, for most CEOs, it still is—and <a href="https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-15/why-more-executives-are-deciding-not-to-become-ceo">an exhausting one at that</a>. So it’s a little puzzling to see that a growing number of tech CEOs are embarking on their own side quests, including most recently Airbnb’s Brian Chesky, who has started an AI lab, as <a href="https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-06-04/airbnb-ceo-brian-chesky-plans-to-start-a-new-ai-company">Bloomberg reported</a> earlier this year.</p><p>Airbnb disclosed Thursday, as it reported third-quarter earnings, that it had sold some intellectual property to Chesky’s new venture for $7.1 million in cash. Airbnb also got the right to invest in any future equity financings of the venture and to license “certain” IP of the lab for internal use in Airbnb’s business. (Hopefully, the IP Airbnb will license isn’t the same IP it just sold to the lab.) Get this: It’s not just Chesky who is involved. Airbnb Chief Technology Officer Ahmad Al-Dahle also has equity in the venture and, like Chesky, “performs services” for it.</p>
