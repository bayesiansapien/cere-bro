# To Strengthen OpenAI, Brockman Goes ‘Full Management Mode’

<p>In August, OpenAI President Greg Brockman called up Denise Dresser, then the company’s chief revenue officer, with a tough message: Her time with OpenAI was coming to an end.</p>

<p>Dresser, a former Slack CEO, had been in the job for only eight months, and while OpenAI had seen promising growth selling its AI to businesses, Brockman had someone else in mind for the position, who he hoped would run the sales team in a more data-driven way, said a person close to the company: Dali Rajic, president and chief operating officer of Wiz, an Israeli cybersecurity company.</p>

<p>OpenAI had initially considered approaching Rajic before hiring Dresser&mdash;but had been told by a headhunter that he wanted to see Wiz through its $32 billion sale to Google, according to that person. To woo Rajic, Brockman flew to see him at his Austin, Texas, home. In the end, Brockman’s persistence paid off. On Aug. 13, the company made a dual announcement: Dresser was out, and Rajic was in. (OpenAI declined to comment on Dresser’s exit; Dresser did not respond to a request for comment.)</p>
