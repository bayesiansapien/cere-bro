# Australia's proposed AI safety bill would force frontier model makers to open up to independent testing

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/australia_ai_safety.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Australia's government, led by Prime Minister Anthony Albanese, wants to enshrine mandatory safety rules for frontier AI companies in law for the first time. A new regulator would enforce independent model audits and impose steep penalties for violations. With this move, Canberra is deliberately prioritizing domestic regulation before entering international talks.</p>
