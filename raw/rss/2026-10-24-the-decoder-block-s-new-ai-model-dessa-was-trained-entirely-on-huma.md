# Block's new AI model Dessa was trained entirely on human speech to catch emotions text-based models miss

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/dessa_block.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        Block, the company behind Square and Cash App, has introduced Dessa, a speech model that processes audio directly instead of converting it to text first. Dessa is designed to recognize emotions, sarcasm, and pauses in real time. The model runs locally on a MacBook Pro M4 Max but isn't publicly available yet.</p>
