# Goldman Sachs pulls AI coding tools from traders and bankers

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="941" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/goldman_sachs_logo.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1672" /></p>
<p>        Goldman Sachs is pulling AI coding tools like Claude Code and GitHub Copilot from traders and bankers, limiting access to engineers only. As recently as July, CIO Marco Argenti publicly praised "vibe coding" by non-technical staff. Now the bank cites "productivity and risk management" as the reason for the reversal.</p>
