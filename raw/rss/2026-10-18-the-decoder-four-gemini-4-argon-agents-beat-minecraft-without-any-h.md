# Four Gemini 4 Argon agents beat Minecraft without any human help

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="941" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/minecraft_gemini.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1672" /></p>
<p>        Four AI agents running on Google's Gemini 4 Argon have beaten Minecraft for the first time without human help, slaying the Ender Dragon in a fully autonomous run. With no predefined roles or strategies, they coordinated on their own, wrote 2,783 programs, exchanged 229 chat messages, and died 605 times. The session cost $1,620.</p>
