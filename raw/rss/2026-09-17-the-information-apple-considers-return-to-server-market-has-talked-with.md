# Apple Considers Return to Server Market, Has Talked With Nvidia to Use Network Tech

<p><a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/apple">Apple</a> has been working on plans for an enterprise server using its own chips that could incorporate networking equipment from <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/nvidia">Nvidia</a>, people familiar with the project said, as the Mac maker looks to capitalize on surging interest in its computers for AI.</p>

<p>Apple has been developing the server with plans to sell it to AI developers, other businesses and governments, the people said. The product under consideration would come in two versions&mdash;a smaller one with two of Apple’s planned M8 Ultra chips clustered together and a bigger one with four M8 Ultra chips. The M-series Ultra chips are Apple’s highest-performance chips and power products including the Mac Studio.</p>
