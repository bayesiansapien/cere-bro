# Quoting Jack Despain Zhou

<blockquote cite="https://www.tracingwoodgrains.com/p/spitting-in-the-face-of-glaude"><p>Among the many already existing effects of AI has been a great de-individualization. I open a YouTube video, and I hear Claude. I sit in a conference presentation, and I hear Claude. I open my email inbox, and I find pitches and articles from ostensibly serious people with ostensibly serious credentials attached to ostensibly serious institutions who want to publish things attached to their reputations and mine, <em>and I see Claude</em>.</p></blockquote>
