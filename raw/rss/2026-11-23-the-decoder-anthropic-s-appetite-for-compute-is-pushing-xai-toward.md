# Anthropic's appetite for compute is pushing xAI toward a rent-over-build strategy

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="608" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/xai_logo_wall-2.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1088" /></p>
<p>        Since May, SpaceXSI has been renting compute to competitors like Anthropic and Google while scaling back its own AI projects. Anthropic pays about $3.1 billion a month, Google $800 million, the New York Times reports. After its June IPO, SpaceXSI disbanded the 60-person Grok Imagine team, among other cuts.</p>
