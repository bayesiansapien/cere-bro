# Top three ways Dario Amodei has blown his credibility in seven days

I hate to say it, but President Trump is right: Dario has tried to position himself as a " perfect little angel ". But actions speak louder than words. In the week since Dario dramatically called on the AI industry to "pace the frontier"&#8230; Dario has pointed to two organizations for what one might want to call independent monitoring of his own company. One is METR, deeply enmeshed in the same Bay Area ideology and social circles as Anthropic, and the other is Accenture, which Anthropic is already in business with. Choosing them as external voices speaks volumes about Amodei's insincerity. It also came out (not mentioned if I recall in Dario's essay) that Anthropic is gearing up to build a wet biology lab , presumably without the usual institutional review boards overseeing. A tone-deaf and arrogant company building a wet lab with unreliable AI with too little oversight isn't just a horror movie, it's Jurassic Park waiting to happen. To refresh your memory, here is Jeff Goldblum shredding the bad judgment of a company that may as well be Anthropic. Relisten to the whole clip; the last line is especially relevant here. "Your scientists were so preoccupied with whether they could, they didn't stop to think if they should", indeed. Slowdown? What slowdown? Dario ain't gonna pace anything.
