# Lean and Mathlib community officially confirms Levent Alpöge's proof of the Riemann hypothesis is correct

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="608" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/ai_and_math.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1088" /></p>
<p>        The Lean and Mathlib community has officially confirmed Levent Alpöge's proof of the Riemann hypothesis as correct. Dozens of independent verifiers checked the 17 million lines of computer code. The solution arrived years earlier than expected: Terence Tao had predicted it wouldn't happen until around 2050 and didn't expect AI to solve it alone.</p>
