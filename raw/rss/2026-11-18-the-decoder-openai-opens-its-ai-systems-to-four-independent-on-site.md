# OpenAI opens its AI systems to four independent on-site auditors for the first time

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/openai_logo-1.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI is giving four independent audit organizations, including Apollo Research and METR, direct access to unreleased models, source code, and training data for the first time. The embedded reviews are designed to uncover hidden misbehavior and safety gaps. OpenAI can redact or delay reports, so how independent this oversight actually turns out to be remains an open question.</p>
