# Visa Says Its AI ‘Harness’ Makes Anthropic Cheaper to Use For Cyber Defense

<p>Anthropic’s AI is pricey, but customers are finding they can use it more efficiently by adding software known as a harness to control the way AI models operate.</p><p>Visa in April developed a harness that reduces the cost of and time it takes for Anthropic’s Mythos AI to find and fix cybersecurity vulnerabilities in its codebases significantly, the company’s president of technology Rajat Taneja told me.</p><p>“Using the model through this harness, we have found, is more effective than using the model by itself,” he said.&nbsp;</p>
