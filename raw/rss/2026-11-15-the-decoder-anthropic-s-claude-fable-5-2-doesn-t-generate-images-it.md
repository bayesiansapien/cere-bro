# Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 doesn't generate images, it paints them

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/claude_fable_5.2_painting.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 doesn't generate images the way other AI models do. Instead of computing pixels, the language model writes Python code that places hundreds of thousands of simulated brushstrokes on a virtual canvas, complete with bristle texture and paint application. A single photorealistic parking garage scene took roughly 700,000 strokes, according to Anthropic employee Jake Eaton.</p>
