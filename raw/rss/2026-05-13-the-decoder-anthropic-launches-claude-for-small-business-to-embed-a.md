# Anthropic launches Claude for Small Business to embed AI into the tools you forgot you pay for

<p>        Anthropic is launching "Claude for Small Business," a package of 15 agent-based workflows and integrations for tools like QuickBooks, PayPal, and HubSpot. The company is also rolling out free training courses and a workshop tour across ten US cities as it pushes to bring AI directly to small businesses.</p>
