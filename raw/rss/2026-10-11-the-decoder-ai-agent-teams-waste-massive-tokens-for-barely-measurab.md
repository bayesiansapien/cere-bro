# AI agent teams waste massive tokens for barely measurable quality gains, research finds

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/agent_swarm.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Teams of AI agents barely outperform solo agents but cost up to 5.1x more, according to Vals AI. Only one out of four tests with GPT-6 Sol and Claude Opus 5.5 showed a measurable gain. Anthropic's own data backs this up: beyond ten agents, quality plateaus while token costs keep climbing.</p>
