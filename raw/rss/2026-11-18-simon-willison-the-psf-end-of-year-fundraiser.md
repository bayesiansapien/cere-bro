# The PSF end-of-year fundraiser

<p><strong><a href="https://donate.python.org">The PSF end-of-year fundraiser</a></strong></p>
With my Python Software Foundation board director hat on: we've launched our end-of-year fundraiser for the non-profit that supports Python development and the Python community.</p>
<p>If your company or your work benefits from Python, please consider donating this year!</p>
<p>The PSF looks after the IP behind Python, operates PyPI, sponsors core developer sprints, supports community events (including running PyCon US every year), and employs a small and excellent staff team to keep everything running.</p>
<p>We also operate the <a href="https://www.python.org/psf/developersinresidence/">Developers in Residence program</a>, which now includes three security developers (welcome, Brett Smith) focused on helping secure Python, PyPI, and the wider ecosystem.
