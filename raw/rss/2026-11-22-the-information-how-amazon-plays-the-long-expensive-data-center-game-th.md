# How Amazon Plays the Long, Expensive Data Center Game That Neoclouds Can’t

<p>INDIANA, Penn.—A visit this week to an Amazon data center project in Pennsylvania coal country gave me a glimpse of the era we all knew was coming: when tech giants lay claim to some of the hardest-to-secure AI infrastructure while smaller cloud providers, which Nvidia has propped up financially, are trying to hang on.</p><p>The 4.4-gigawatt campus in Pennsylvania, once home to a 2-GW coal-fired power plant near the economically depressed borough of Homer City, could become one of the largest AI power parks in the country.</p><p>Few companies can marshal the resources as readily as Amazon to transform the site from what it was—a Rust Belt relic that once employed thousands of people but released coal dust and arsenic—into an Appalachian hilltop of gas-fired electrons flowing into 36 buildings powering the AI of Anthropic or others.</p>
