# Back-to-back cyber incidents at US financial investigators and spy agencies follow a string of federal hacks and outages

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/cyberkraken_4.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        The US Financial Crimes Enforcement Network took systems offline as a precaution after unauthorized access. Hours earlier, reports emerged of a separate breach at the CIA, NGA, and ODNI, in which investigators suspect attackers used the AI defense tool Artex. Both incidents land on a US public that is already on edge.</p>
