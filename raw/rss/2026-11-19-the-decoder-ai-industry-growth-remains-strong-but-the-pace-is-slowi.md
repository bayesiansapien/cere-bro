# AI industry growth remains strong, but the pace is slowing

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="816" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Throwing-money-at-AI.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1456" /></p>
<p>        Token prices for AI models fell 21 percent in six weeks while usage volume hit a record high, according to the Ramp AI Index. Anthropic dominates with nearly 59 percent of token spending. Data from Ramp, Stripe and OpenRouter independently show the same pattern: the AI industry is still growing, but revenue momentum is cooling.</p>
