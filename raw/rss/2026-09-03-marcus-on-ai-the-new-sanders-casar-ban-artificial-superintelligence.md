is too broad, a guarantee of leaving the US behind, and not the right approach. </p><p>It&#8217;s also naive about the complexities in benchmarking, and would unfortunately leave room for authoritarians to play games with the rules.</p><p>Furthermore, it focuses too much on hypothetical future risks (such as AI that &#8220;plan[s] ... to disempower humanity) to the exclusion of current future. But this is not the way.</p><p>I salute the Sanders-Casar effort to get something down on paper for discussion, but let&#8217;s figure out a better approach. </p><p>(In my postscript below I give one hint; my book <em>Taming Silicon Valley</em> gives many more. An upcoming article with Cal Newport will give still more.)</p><p class="button-wrapper"><a class="button primary" href="https://garymarcus.substack.com/subscribe?"><span>Subscribe now</span></a></p><p>P.S. On the pausing point&#8212;separate from regulating other aspects of the more immediate risks of AI&#8212;I like the language here (boldface added), from <a href="https://x.com/anthonynaguirre/status/2095172581716607211?s=61">Anthony Aguirre</a>. Perhaps Sanders and Casar might borrow it in a revised proposal:</p><p>&#8220;Development of narrowly-scoped AI tools, and those that can be reasonably expected to be significantly below the current frontier in software development, cyber, and autonomous capabilities, may continue. <strong>Systems of greater combined intelligence, generality, and autonomy may be developed only if and when their developers can demonstrate with high assurance to a capable independent authority that sufficient alignment, control, and oversight exist.&#8221;</strong></p><p>Rather than calling for permanent ban that could be undone only by an act of Congress, this framing sets an important high bar &#8211; and hence an incentive &#8212; for the companies to meet. That&#8217;s a good model for a better, less overbearing approach that stills leaves room for future discovery, while encouraging critical research.</p><p class="button-wrapper"><a class="button primary" href="https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/the-new-sanders-casar-ban-artificial?utm_source=substack&amp;utm_medium=email&amp;utm_content=share&amp;action=share"><span>Share</span></a></p><p></p>
