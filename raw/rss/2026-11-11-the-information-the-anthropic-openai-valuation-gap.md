# The Anthropic-OpenAI Valuation Gap

<p>If you want proof of the widening gap in sentiment between public and private investors, take a look at the valuations of Anthropic and OpenAI.</p><p>On Tuesday, Anthropic disclosed it was targeting a valuation of between <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/anthropic-sets-lower-expected-valuation-seeks-raise-64-billion-ipo?rc=zjctrx">$1.26 trillion and $1.4 trillion</a> in its upcoming initial public offering. The prospectus update comes on the heels of OpenAI’s most recent fundraising, which <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/openai-secured-11-billion-targets-9-billion-fundraising?rc=zjctrx">valued the company at $1.25 trillion</a>.&nbsp;</p><p>That means Anthropic expects public investors to value it at only 12% more than OpenAI, assuming it achieves the valuation at the high end of its range. That is in spite of the fact that Anthropic generated 70% revenue than OpenAI in the third quarter and has been growing far faster for much of this year.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p><p>Still, Anthropic’s advisers may be guiding the valuation downward now to make sure there’s plenty of demand for the stock on the first day of trading. There’s a good chance Anthropic can increase the IPO price or sell even more shares if investors’ demand is strong, as some Anthropic investors are expecting.&nbsp;</p>
