# Anthropic CEO Amodei admits "there's a huge uncertainty in what's going to happen next year" days before a record IPO

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/anthropic_logos-5.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Days before what could be the largest IPO in history, Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei called buying compute 'the deepest business dilemma' his company faces. Anthropic's commitments now total nearly $901 billion, and it's still far from clear whether future revenue will justify those costs. The planned IPO is largely meant to serve as a financial buffer against that uncertainty.</p>
