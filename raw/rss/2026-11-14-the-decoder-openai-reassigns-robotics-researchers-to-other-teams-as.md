# OpenAI reassigns robotics researchers to other teams as general-purpose models might make specialized robotics AI unnecessary

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/openai_robot_arms.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI is dissolving its standalone robotics team for the second time, but not because it lacks data. General-purpose models like GPT-6 Astra can apparently control robots directly, making a dedicated unit unnecessary. The researchers are being redistributed across other teams in what the company frames as a way to accelerate its robotics efforts.</p>
