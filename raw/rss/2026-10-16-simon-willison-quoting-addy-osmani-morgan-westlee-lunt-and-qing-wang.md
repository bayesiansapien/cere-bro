# Quoting Addy Osmani, Morgan Westlee Lunt, and Qing Wang

<blockquote cite="https://claude.dev/blog/solving-code-review-at-anthropic/"><p>We think "did a human read the code?" is an imprecise proxy for "what could go wrong with this change, and which check—automated or human—is the right one to catch it?"</p>
<p>Asking that question also changed <em>when</em> review happens. Before, review was a gate at the end of the road: write the code, open a PR, wait for your colleague.</p>
<p>Now, review runs continuously, more like spellcheck while you write. Most changes have been reviewed and fixed by Claude at least once before a human is asked to look. This catches most routine mistakes, and saves team attention for higher-level questions and scrutiny.</p></blockquote>
<p class="cite">&mdash; <a href="https://claude.dev/blog/solving-code-review-at-anthropic/">Addy Osmani, Morgan Westlee Lunt, and Qing Wang</a>, How the Claude Code team does code review</p>
