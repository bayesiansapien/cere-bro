# OpenAI asks outside experts, including a Nobel laureate, to check proofs its AI produced in math, computer science, and physics

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/openai_navier.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI says its internal AI models have cracked three major open questions in math, computer science, and physics. One of them is part of a famous Millennium Prize Problem, each of which carries a $1 million prize. Outside experts, including a Nobel laureate, are now checking whether the proofs hold up. If the computer science proof holds on its own, hundreds of research papers lose their foundation.</p>
