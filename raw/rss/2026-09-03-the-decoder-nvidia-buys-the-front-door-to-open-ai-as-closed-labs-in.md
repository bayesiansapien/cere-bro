# Nvidia buys the front door to open AI as closed labs increasingly design their own silicon

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="864" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/hf-nvidia-partner_hf-nvidia-partner-press-1920x1080-2-1536x864-1.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1536" /></p>
<p>        Nvidia plans to acquire Hugging Face for about $12.9 billion, securing the central platform for open AI models. More than 18 million developers and 200,000 companies use the hub. CEO Jensen Huang promises to keep the platform open and hardware-neutral, but the deal also hands him a powerful distribution channel for compute.</p>
