# OpenAI o1 co-creator Noam Brown urges promising students to drop out as AI enters a "very steep rate of progress"

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/openai_driscoll.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI researcher Noam Brown is urging promising students to drop out, saying it's "not a great time to sit on the sidelines." The o1 co-creator says AI is still in a "centaur period" and struggles with planning and self-correction, though he expects a "very steep rate of progress" soon. According to Brown, language models are already being used to build the next generation of models.</p>
