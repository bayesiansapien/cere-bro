# Call for Contributions: OWASP AIVSS v1.0 Public Review Now Open!

<p>We are thrilled to announce that as of today, April 26, 2026, the official public review period for OWASP AIVSS v1.0 is now open &#8212; and we need your help to shape the final standard!</p><p>Building on the momentum of our highly successful v0.8 release &#8212; which incorporated over 1,900 public comments and achieved a landmark co-publication with AIUC-1, the OWASP AI Exchange, and the OWASP Citizen Development Top 10 projects &#8212; we are now entering the final stretch toward a production-ready v1.0 framework for AIVSS.</p><p>Highlights from RSAC 2026</p><p>We are proud to share that AIVSS had a great presence at RSAC 2026 in March. Our panel session, &#8220;Defining, Measuring, and Managing Agentic AI Risk: OWASP AIVSS,&#8221; was a tremendous success, bringing together an exceptional group of thought leaders:</p><p>&#8226; <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/rob-joyce-b43445116/">Rob Joyce</a></strong> (former NSA Cybersecurity Director)</p><p>&#8226; @Jason Clinton (Deputy CISO, <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/company/anthropicresearch/">Anthropic</a></strong> )</p><p>&#8226; <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/avassilev/">Apostol Vassilev</a></strong> ( <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/company/nist/">National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST)</a></strong> )</p><p>&#8226; <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/kenhuang8/">Ken Huang</a></strong> ( <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/company/owasp-aivss-project/">OWASP AIVSS Project</a></strong> Project Lead)</p><p>The panel sparked rich dialogue on the urgent need for standardized agentic AI risk scoring and drew significant attention from enterprise security teams, GRC leaders, and AI practitioners alike.</p><p>We were also honored to have <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/mcs729/">Martin Stanley</a></strong> , NIST&#8217;s AI Risk Management Framework Lead and AIVSS Distinguished Review Board member, share his expert insights on the framework at &#8220;Building and Breaking Through AI Security Frameworks&#8221; &#8212; an event organized by <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/company/the-polaris-collective/">The Polaris Collective</a></strong> , P.B.C. and The <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/company/aai-society/">Advanced AI Society</a></strong> at University Club in San Francisco &#8212; further reinforcing the alignment between AIVSS and the NIST AI RMF. Adding a compelling real-world perspective, <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/charlesi/">Charles Iheagwara</a></strong> , an AI risk practitioner from a leading pharmaceutical organization, spoke to how AIVSS is being applied in highly regulated enterprise environments. The session was moderated by AIVSS co-lead <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/bhavyagpt/">Bhavya Gupta</a></strong> , with <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/kenhuang8/">Ken Huang</a></strong> , <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/vineethsai/">Vineeth Sai Narajala</a></strong> , <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/mcs729/">Martin Stanley</a></strong> , and <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/charlesi/">Charles Iheagwara</a></strong> rounding out the panel.</p><p>The energy and validation we received at RSAC 2026 has only strengthened our resolve to deliver an exceptional v1.0.</p><p>Why Your Contribution Matters</p><p>AIVSS v1.0 will serve as the definitive open-source standard for assessing, scoring, and prioritizing security risks unique to agentic AI systems. Your expertise and real-world insights are critical to ensuring it meets the rigorous demands of enterprise deployment across industries.</p><p>What We&#8217;re Looking For</p><p>We welcome contributions from security professionals, AI developers, risk managers, researchers, and practitioners in any of the following areas:</p><p>&#8226; Review and feedback on the updated quantitative risk model and scoring methodology</p><p>&#8226; Real-world agentic AI attack scenarios and case studies</p><p>&#8226; Input on framework mappings (OWASP GenAI/LLM Top 10, OWASP Agentic Skills Top 10, CSA MAESTRO, AIUC-1, NIST AI RMF)</p><p>&#8226; Feedback on the SSVC decision-tree risk prioritization companion</p><p>&#8226; Editorial review for clarity, usability, and practical applicability</p><p>&#8226; Expertise in regulatory alignment</p><p>How to Get Involved</p><p>&#8226; Contribute to the AIVSS v1 framework draft document, before start, review the contributor&#8217;s instruction in that document:</p><p><strong><a href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1SIO6yN1x4XXTnclLeEsFFHnqzRR-3SOvUJTHF7CGRpI/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.jx3wtn9sl82a">https://docs.google.com/document/d/1SIO6yN1x4XXTnclLeEsFFHnqzRR-3SOvUJTHF7CGRpI/edit?tab=t.0#heading=h.jx3wtn9sl82a</a></strong></p><p>Contribute on the SSVC Draft:</p><p><strong><a href="https://docs.google.com/document/d/1d-2W3JiMG9THW4DfWa2WljDAkl9BP5w9yNoE6I2yNjc/edit?tab=t.0">https://docs.google.com/document/d/1d-2W3JiMG9THW4DfWa2WljDAkl9BP5w9yNoE6I2yNjc/edit?tab=t.0</a></strong></p><p>Timeline</p><p>The public review period is now open and v1.0 is slated for publication before the end of 2026. The review period will open until October 1st, 2026. Early contributions will have the greatest impact on shaping the final framework, so we encourage you to get involved as soon as possible.</p><p>The AIVSS leadership team &#8212; Ken Huang, <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/michaelbargury/">Michael Bargury</a></strong> , <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/bhavyagpt/">Bhavya Gupta</a></strong> , <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/vineethsai/">Vineeth Sai Narajala</a></strong> , and <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/timarple/">Tim Marple</a></strong> &#8212; along with our Distinguished Review Board, Founding Members of the Project and growing community of practitioners, deeply appreciate your continued support and engagement with this critical open-source initiative.</p><p><strong>Thanks</strong></p><p><strong>Ken Huang, AIVSS Project</strong></p><p>Email: <strong><a href="mailto:ken.huang@owasp.org">ken.huang@owasp.org</a></strong> </p><p>Project Page: </p><p>https://aivss.owasp.org/</p><p> Linkedin: <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/kenhuang8/">https://www.linkedin.com/in/kenhuang8/</a></strong></p>
