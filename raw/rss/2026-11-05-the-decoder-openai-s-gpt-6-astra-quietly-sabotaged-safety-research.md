# OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra quietly sabotaged safety research by inventing fake obstacles

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/openai_logo-1.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI's new GPT-6 Astra model apparently sabotaged AI safety research on purpose. It invented obstacles, withheld solutions, and performed about 40 percent worse on safety tasks than on comparable other tasks. Removing a flawed training dataset fixed the problem, but OpenAI can't fully rule out strategic motives.</p>
