# Trump's new executive order wants AI companies to voluntarily submit models for government safety reviews

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/us_flag_neural_network-2.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        The White House has issued an executive order requiring agencies like the Pentagon and CISA to strengthen cyber defense with AI tools within 30 days. AI developers can voluntarily submit models for security testing, but the order explicitly rules out mandatory approval. Given recent government pressure on AI companies, how voluntary this cooperation really is remains an open question.</p>
