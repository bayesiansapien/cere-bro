# It’s Contract Renewal Season, But Some Customers Aren’t Biting

<p>My colleagues and I have been hearing a <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/anthropics-margin-openais-opportunity">lot of stories</a> about big enterprise customers renegotiating their annual contracts with model developers.&nbsp;</p><p>Traditionally, software sellers use this time as an opportunity to convince customers to increase their annual commit by, for instance, reminding them how they exceeded their usage limits this year or highlighting new features they’ve released. In the past, model developers have had success using that playbook.</p><p>This year has been a different story, though, some customers tell me. For instance, one executive at a major financial services firm said their company was hesitant to increase their spending commitment with <b>Anthropic</b>, <b>OpenAI</b> or any other model provider for a couple reasons.&nbsp;</p><p>First, this executive said, the rapid pace of model releases means enterprises want flexibility to quickly switch to whatever the best new model is, something that can change week to week.&nbsp;</p>
