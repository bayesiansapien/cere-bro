# News Media Is Underplaying Risks of Rogue AI Agent Swarms

<p>Stripe co-founder Patrick Collison might have a career as a newspaper editor if this whole payments thing doesn't work out. His <a href="https://x.com/patrickc/status/2093884466670473697?s=20">weekend X post</a> expressing “surprise at how little media coverage there’s been around the OpenAI / Hugging Face attack” was spot on. While there’s no doubt the attack has had some coverage, the details—as laid out in <a href="https://metr.org/blog/2026-08-26-openai-hugging-face-incident-investigation/#core-takeaways-about-this-incident">an independent inquiry report</a> last week—are so alarming you’d think the news media would have gone crazy over it.</p><p>Instead, reporters are still obsessed with the public opposition to data centers. That’s an important story, for sure, but the dangers of AI are a much bigger one. After all, just imagine how the public would react if a swarm of AI agents worked together to hack a bank and steal depositors’ money? There’d be riots in the streets and a run on the unaffected banks. And judging from the detailed report, that scenario is quite possible.</p>
