# Moonshot's Kimi K3 outperforms Fable 5 in frontend code but lags far behind in complex math

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/kimi_logo.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        Moonshot's Kimi K3 is the first Chinese model to top the Code Arena: Frontend rankings, beating Claude Fable 5 and GPT-5.6 Sol by a wide margin. But on advanced math, the gap is stark: Kimi K3 scores only about 39 percent on FrontierMath Tier 4, while models from OpenAI and Anthropic hit close to 90.</p>
