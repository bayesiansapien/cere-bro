# Anthropic’s IPO Marketing Meets Extinction Risk

<p>Are we sleepwalking our way into AI-caused extinction? It feels a little like that, <a href="https://x.com/EvanHub/status/2097497037956891126?s=20">given an Anthropic researcher’s</a> X post on Tuesday night that there’s a greater than 10% chance that “AI could kill all humans” within the next decade! Despite that rather gruesome note, for tech executives posting on X <a href="https://x.com/elonmusk/status/2097753657714573749?s=20">today</a>, it was<a href="https://x.com/sama/status/2097695001341829212?s=20"> business as usual</a>. (See <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/anthropic-researchers-terminator-style-warning-caught-fire?rc=9byxri">our detailed report</a> on the episode here).</p><p>Plenty of <a href="https://x.com/typesfast/status/2097728838457589800?s=20">dark humor</a> circulated about the researcher’s post, however—<a href="https://x.com/buccocapital/status/2097657305273610438?s=20">this one</a> joking about a Wall Street analyst asking Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei about the “extinction number” on an earnings call is possibly the best. That joke also highlighted the reality that Anthropic will shortly be marketing its IPO. How will its executives handle the warning on the Wall Street investor road show?&nbsp;</p>
