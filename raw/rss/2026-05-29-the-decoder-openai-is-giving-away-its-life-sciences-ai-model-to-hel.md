# OpenAI is giving away its life sciences AI model to help governments prepare for the next pandemic

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/openai_gpt_rosalind_bio.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI is offering its life sciences model GPT-Rosalind for free through the new Rosalind Biodefense program, aimed at pandemic preparedness and biodefense. Early partners include Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory, Johns Hopkins, and vaccine initiative CEPI. Applications are open worldwide.</p>
