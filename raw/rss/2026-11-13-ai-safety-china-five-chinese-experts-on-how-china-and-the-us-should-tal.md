They also agreed to set up a bilateral communication channel for AI incidents.</p><p>According to <a href="https://www.scmp.com/news/china/diplomacy/article/3370926/us-and-china-race-shape-ai-agenda-ahead-xi-trump-talks-shenzhen-sources-say">media reports</a>, AI cooperation is a potential deliverable to be announced when presidents Xi Jinping and Donald Trump meet on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit in Shenzhen next week.</p><p
