# What the Anthropic IPO Could Mean for Shrimp Welfare and Other Obscure Causes

<p>The coming initial public offerings of AI giants <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/anthropic">Anthropic</a> and <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/openai">OpenAI</a> could spell a windfall for San Francisco real estate agents, <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/private-jets-scarce-blame-gusher-ai-wealth">private jet companies</a>&mdash;and wild animals.</p>

<p>As AI employees with fast-appreciating stock look to start cashing out, philanthropies are talking to potential donors and preparing for a possible influx of cash. And because many AI researchers have ties to the effective altruism movement, which takes a particular interest in causes that mainstream philanthropies overlook, the organizations prepping for a windfall might look very different from previous charitable waves.</p>
