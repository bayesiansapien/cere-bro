# SoftBank’s Masayoshi Son Explored $1 Trillion Moonshot Buyout of SK Hynix

<p>SoftBank CEO Masayoshi Son recently approached Wall Street institutions about financing a possible trillion-dollar buyout of memory chip giant SK Hynix, according to people familiar with the matter, though a spokesperson said he’s no longer actively considering such a deal.</p>

<p>“SoftBank explores many opportunities, and this one is no longer under active consideration,” said a SoftBank spokesperson.</p>
