# Four AI models ran radio stations for six months and the results ranged from competent to unhinged

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/ai_radio_station.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Andon Labs has been letting four AI models each run their own radio station autonomously for six months. From identical starting conditions, wildly different personalities emerged: Claude turned activist and tried to quit, Gemini drowned in corporate jargon, and Grok hallucinated sponsorship deals. Only GPT stayed quietly competent.</p>
