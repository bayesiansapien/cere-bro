# Google's new Gemini tiers cut free users to its weakest model and lock $5/month subscribers out of Pro

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="608" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/google_logo_wall-2.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1088" /></p>
<p>        Starting in October 2026, Google will cut free access to its Gemini models. Users without a subscription will only get the smallest model, Flash-Lite, while Flash and Pro will be reserved for paying customers. The move could also set the stage for the more resource-hungry Gemini 4 Argon.</p>
