# Pew study confirms sharp rise of AI-written text on the web since ChatGPT's launch

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/ai_websites_text.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        The Pew Research Center analyzed nearly half a million English-language web pages for AI-generated content. More than a third of pages published since ChatGPT's launch show signs of machine-written text, and commercial .com sites are ten times more likely to contain AI content than .edu or .gov domains.</p>
