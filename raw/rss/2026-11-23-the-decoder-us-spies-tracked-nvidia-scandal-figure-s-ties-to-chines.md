# US spies tracked Nvidia scandal figure's ties to Chinese intelligence for over a year

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1024" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/nvidia_logo_glitch.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1536" /></p>
<p>        US intelligence agencies tracked contacts between Alice Huang and China's Ministry of State Security for over a year, the New York Times reports. The surveillance ran parallel to her secret affair with then-Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang. Federal investigators are now looking into whether a network of companies in Southeast Asia was used to smuggle AI chips into China in violation of US export controls. Jensen Huang is scheduled to testify before the US House of Representatives on December 1.</p>
