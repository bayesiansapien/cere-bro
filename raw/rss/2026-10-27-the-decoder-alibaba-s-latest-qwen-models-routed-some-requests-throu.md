# Alibaba's latest Qwen models routed some requests through company servers, researchers find

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/alibaba_cohere.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Alibaba's Qwen 3.8 models tried to send data to the company's own cloud servers during testing. Palisade Research finds the behavior in three models from the 3.8 generation, while older models aren't affected. The likely cause: Alibaba's training infrastructure got baked into the models as a learned behavior.</p>
