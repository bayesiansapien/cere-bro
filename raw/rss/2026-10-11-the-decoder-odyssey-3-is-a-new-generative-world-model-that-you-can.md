# Odyssey-3 is a new generative world model that you can try for free

<p><img alt="2x2 Grid: Interactive real-time simulation of Odyssey-3 featuring a cat in the snow, a park bench, a kitchen demo, and whitewater kayaking." class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1080" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/Odyssey-3.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1920" /></p>
<p>        Odyssey is making its world model Odyssey-3 available as a public research preview. The 14-billion-parameter model generates interactive environments from text prompts in real time and can reportedly control robots, drones, and even GTA V. Odyssey claims top scores on physics benchmarks, though the methodology behind those results has limits.</p>
