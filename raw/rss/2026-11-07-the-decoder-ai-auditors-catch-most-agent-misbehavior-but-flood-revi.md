# AI auditors catch most agent misbehavior but flood reviewers with false alarms, new study finds

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/ai_auditing_reliability.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        AI models can often spot problematic behavior by other AI agents in logs, according to a study by Anthropic researcher Andy Hall. GPT-5 found about 98 percent of reward-hacking cases, but the more exhaustively the search is tuned, the more false alarms pile up. That's not yet enough for reliable safety checks.</p>
