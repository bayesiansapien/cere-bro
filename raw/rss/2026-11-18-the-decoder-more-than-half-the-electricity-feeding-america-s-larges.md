# More than half the electricity feeding America's largest data centers comes from gas and coal

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/data_center_climate_impact2.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        A Harvard study published in Nature Sustainability found that 403 US hyperscale data centers produced roughly 25.7 million metric tons of CO2 between May 2024 and April 2025, more than all of Denmark. Over half of their electricity came from fossil fuels. With 2,000 additional facilities planned, the researchers warn of a major climate setback.</p>
