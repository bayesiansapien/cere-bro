# How Trump Put Jensen Huang Back in Charge of Nvidia

<p>For days, Nvidia shareholders clamored for Jensen Huang to return to the company, as did thousands of its employees and a broad array of Silicon Valley luminaries. In the end, though, it was President Donald Trump who set the stage for one of the most remarkable CEO comebacks in tech history.</p>

<p>The president played a critical role in the Nvidia board’s head-spinning announcement Monday morning that it had unanimously reinstated Huang as CEO, according to a person familiar with the matter and Trump himself. Over the weekend, U.S. Attorney General Todd Blanche told the president that the department had no evidence of any wrongdoing by Huang, according to a Justice Department official. At Trump’s direction, Vice President JD Vance communicated that fact directly to Nvidia’s board to reassure directors, the person familiar with the matter said.</p>
