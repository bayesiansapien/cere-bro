# Google Deepmind argues video generators already contain the world models computer vision has been missing

<p><img alt="Digital artwork: an urban street scene featuring buildings and a sidewalk, overlaid with colorful geometric AI overlays." class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1047" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/google-deepmind-genception-generated-image-nano-banana-pro.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1920" /></p>
<p>        Google Deepmind's GenCeption repurposes a video generator for classic vision tasks such as depth estimation and segmentation, matching state-of-the-art systems with far less training data. The model trained almost entirely on synthetic videos. Its results add to the debate over whether video generators already contain a kind of universal world model.</p>
