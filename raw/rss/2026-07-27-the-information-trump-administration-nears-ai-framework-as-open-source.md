# Trump Administration Nears AI Framework as Open-Source Questions Loom

<p>The Trump administration is close to finalizing its voluntary framework for AI companies to submit their most advanced models to the government before releasing them to the public, according to three people familiar with the matter.</p>

<p>The White House’s Office of the National Cyber Director circulated a draft of the framework to <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/openai?rc=ndc4g0&amp;selected_employee=sam-altman">OpenAI</a>, <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/anthropic?rc=ndc4g0&amp;selected_employee=dario-amodei">Anthropic</a> and <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/google?rc=ndc4g0&amp;selected_employee=sundar-pichai">Google</a> around two weeks ago, said the people, adding that the three companies jointly submitted their own edits. An <a href="https://www.whitehouse.gov/presidential-actions/2026/06/promoting-advanced-artificial-intelligence-innovation-and-security/">executive order</a> on AI signed by President Donald Trump in early June set a deadline of Aug. 1 for the creation of the framework, which aims to establish a more formal process for powerful new forms of AI capable of swiftly discovering cybervulnerabilities.</p>
