# Google ships four TPU variants in one year as it ramps up competition with Nvidia

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/google_tpu_chips.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Google is expanding its TPU 8 family with two specialized variants: the TPU 8tx keeps sensitive data encrypted even during processing, while the TPU 8i Max doubles compute power and memory by placing two chips on a single package. With nearly 100 external customers now on board, Google is increasingly positioning its in-house chips as an alternative to Nvidia's GPUs.</p>
