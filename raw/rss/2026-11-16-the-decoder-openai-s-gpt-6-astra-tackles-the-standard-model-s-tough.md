# OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra tackles the Standard Model's toughest math problem on a lattice

<p>        OpenAI physicist Nathaniel Craig used GPT-6 Astra to tackle a problem that had stumped particle physicists for over 25 years. The AI model took Craig's strategy for defining the full Standard Model on a computational lattice and turned it into a working proof. Craig says a lot of work remains before realistic simulations are possible.</p>
