# OpenAI shares new prompting tips for GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol to fix brief answers and unnecessary questions

<p>        OpenAI has released new prompt templates for GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol meant to fix common model behaviors such as overly brief answers, unnecessary follow-up questions, and excessive testing when coding.
