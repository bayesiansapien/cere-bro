# Banned Nvidia Blackwell chips turn up in a Shanghai data center linked to Alice Huang, Bloomberg reports

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/jensen_huang_china.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Two Chinese companies installed Nvidia's powerful Blackwell chips at a Shanghai data center linked to Alice Huang, according to a Bloomberg investigation. A serial number led Bloomberg to Taiwanese manufacturer Gigabyte. Exporting these chips to China without a US license is illegal.</p>
