# Nvidia’s Board Rebuffs Jensen

<p>Well, that was embarrassing. A few hours after Nvidia’s recently departed CEO Jensen Huang issued a <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/jensen-huang-would-honored-return-ceo-board-asks?rc=9byxri">plaintive statement</a> saying he’d love his job back, company chair Harvey Jones sent back a message: no chance, buddy. In an unusual appearance on the company’s earnings call Tuesday afternoon, Jones stated unequivocally that the board “does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO.”</p><p>Jones also added something the board didn’t say when it announced Huang’s exit last Thursday: that the exit came “after disclosure of personal behavior inconsistent with the company’s code of conduct, standards and cultural norms.” So now it’s clear: it <i>was </i>Huang’s affairs (with a subordinate and a business partner) that caused his exit, and not, for instance, <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/jensen-huangs-yearslong-ties-alice-huang-enabled-nvidia-chip-sales-china?rc=9byxri">concerns about Nvidia’s possible involvement</a> in smuggling of its chips into China. </p>
