# Leading mathematicians fear AI is making their field dumber, and warn the rest of us is next

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/millenium_math.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        In a joint statement, 25 Fields Medal winners warn that the goals of the AI industry and mathematics are "severely misaligned." They argue that mass-producing solved problems with AI undermines the discipline's true goal: understanding. The mathematicians see this as a symptom of a broader threat to intellectual work.</p>
