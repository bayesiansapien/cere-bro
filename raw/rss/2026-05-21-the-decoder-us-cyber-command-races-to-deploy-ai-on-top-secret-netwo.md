# US Cyber Command races to deploy AI on top-secret networks

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/pentagon_AI_strategy.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        US Cyber Command has launched a task force to run AI models from OpenAI, Google, and others on the most classified Pentagon and NSA networks. The trigger: AI systems like Anthropic's Claude Mythos can find security vulnerabilities faster than the best human hackers - and according to Anthropic, comparable tools could be widely available within six to 24 months.</p>
