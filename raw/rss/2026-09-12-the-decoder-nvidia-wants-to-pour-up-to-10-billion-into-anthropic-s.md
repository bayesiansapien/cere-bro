# Nvidia wants to pour up to $10 billion into Anthropic's record-breaking IPO

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/nvidia_logo.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        Nvidia is in talks to invest up to $10 billion in Anthropic's planned IPO, Reuters reports. At a target valuation of $2 trillion, it would be the largest IPO in history. Most of that money will likely end up right back at Nvidia in chip orders.</p>
