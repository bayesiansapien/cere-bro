# Why Investors Shouldn’t Panic About Nvidia’s Commitments

<p>Talk about a nervous market! Shares of Nvidia fell 5% on Monday on news of a $500 billion partnership the AI chip giant struck with South Korean conglomerate SK Group, along with <a href="https://www.wsj.com/tech/ai/nvidia-in-talks-with-openai-to-guarantee-250-billion-financing-for-data-center-3dd6eae3?mod=tech_feat1_ai_pos4">a Wall Street Journal report </a>that Nvidia was separately in talks to help OpenAI finance a $500 billion data center campus in Ohio. Investors should take a chill pill: Neither of these deals was actually news. Nor were they fully fleshed out.</p><p>For instance, Nvidia and SK have only signed letters of intent to formalize a partnership, <a href="https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/sk-group-and-nvidia-expand-strategic-partnership-across-ai-factories-and-next-generation-memory">they said on Friday</a>. Letters of intent are not worth the paper they’re written on. Remember, Nvidia and OpenAI <a href="https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/openai-and-nvidia-announce-strategic-partnership-to-deploy-10gw-of-nvidia-systems">signed a letter of intent</a> last September for what they called a “landmark strategic partnership,” only <a href="https://www.ft.com/content/dea24046-0a73-40b2-8246-5ac7b7a54323?syn-25a6b1a6=1">to abandon the idea</a> a few months later.&nbsp;</p>
