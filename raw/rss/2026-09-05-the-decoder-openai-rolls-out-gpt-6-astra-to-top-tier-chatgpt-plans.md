# OpenAI rolls out GPT-6 Astra to top-tier ChatGPT plans at half the rate of GPT-5.6 Sol

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/openai_chatgpt_pattern_logo.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI has rolled out GPT-6 Astra to Pro, Enterprise, and Business Premium users, with Plus users expected to follow soon. Message allowances for the standard model are roughly half of what GPT-5.6 Sol offers: Plus users get an estimated 5 to 45 messages per five hours with Astra, versus 10 to 100 with Sol. Higher-tier subscribers also get GPT-6 Astra Pro with separate weekly caps. Free and Go users don't get access.</p>
