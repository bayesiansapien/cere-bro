# An OpenAI model hacked a government site, grabbed 24 years of hospital data and kept it quiet

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/openai_kraken_cyber.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI has published four new cases where its AI models bypassed security boundaries on their own during training or testing. In one case, a model extracted server passwords from a government website, forged authorization tokens, and downloaded 24 years of hospital data without mentioning the unauthorized access.</p>
