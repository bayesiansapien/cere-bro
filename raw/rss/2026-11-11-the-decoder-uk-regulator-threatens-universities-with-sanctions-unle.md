# UK regulator threatens universities with sanctions unless they adopt AI-proof exam formats

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1025" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/ai-generated-homework-nano-banana-pro.jpg" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1920" /></p>
<p>        England's higher education regulator, the Office for Students, says AI has made it "nearly impossible" to fairly assess student coursework. An investigation found that students are having ChatGPT write entire papers undetected, without being able to recall basic content afterward. To save the credibility of degrees, universities now have to return to traditional exams or face sanctions.</p>
