# "How much beauty have we lost?" Mathematicians react with shock and disgust as OpenAI bulldozes their field

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/human_ai_math.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        OpenAI published more than 700 AI-generated manuscripts claiming solutions to open math problems. A math blog then collected over 100 responses from researchers, ranging from fascination with new ideas to existential fears and grief over a lost way of working. Fields Medalist Hugo Duminil-Copin wrote, "I am paralysed."</p>
