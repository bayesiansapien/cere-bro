# GPT-6.2 Astra solved a math theorem no other AI could crack, then invented its own proof strategy

<p>        OpenAI's GPT-6.2 Astra independently produced a machine-verifiable Lean proof of Smoktunowicz's gap theorem, the first of its kind according to Vals AI.
