# Panache — RTS Belle Époque pour mobile

<p><strong><a href="https://github.com/menufactory43/panache/">Panache — RTS Belle Époque pour mobile</a></strong></p>
Well this is delightful: I <a href="https://simonwillison.net/2026/Oct/20/battledrones/">released Battle Drones</a> yesterday under an Apache 2.0 License and there's already a French language fork which reskins the game to take place in steampunk 1900s France and adds an animated intro!</p>
<blockquote>
<p><strong>Panache</strong> est un jeu de stratégie en temps réel pour mobile et navigateur, dans la France de la Belle Époque (vers 1900) : dirigeables, Fardiers à vapeur, Creusot cuirassés, automates en képi, Gargantua la forteresse marchante — et beaucoup de panache.</p>
</blockquote>
<p>You can <a href="https://menufactory43.github.io/panache/">play Panache here</a> - it's worth clicking that link just to experience the new intro video.</p>
<p>So far it's mostly just aesthetic updates as opposed to gameplay changes. The music has had a French twist added to it as well.</p>
<p>The new steampunk models are so cool!</p>
<p><img alt="The new buildings are gold and red, there are dirigibles and biplanes, and the Goliath stomping mech has had a total makeover." src="https://static.simonwillison.net/static/2026/battledrones-french.jpg" /></p>
<p>Here's a page with <a href="https://simonw.github.io/battledrones/models.html">the original Battle Drones models</a> and that same page <a href="https://menufactory43.github.io/panache/models.html">with the new Panache alternatives</a>.</p>
