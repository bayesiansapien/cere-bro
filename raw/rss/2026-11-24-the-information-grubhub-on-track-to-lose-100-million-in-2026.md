# Grubhub on Track to Lose $100 Million in 2026

<p>Marc Lore’s Wonder is not pursuing a sale of Grubhub, Wonder says, after preliminary sales talks with Amazon and Walmart. One factor potentially making Grubhub tough to sell: the U.S. meal-delivery business is on track to lose around $100 million in 2026, according to internal documents viewed by The Information.</p>

<p>That level of red ink might have turned off potential buyers, in addition to the fact that Grubhub is a small player in an intensely competitive market dominated by DoorDash and Uber. The documents showed that Grubhub this year is on track to generate close to $6.4 billion in gross transaction volume. By comparison, DoorDash&mdash;which unlike Grubhub has extensive international operations&mdash;generated $34 billion in gross order volume just in the third quarter.</p>
