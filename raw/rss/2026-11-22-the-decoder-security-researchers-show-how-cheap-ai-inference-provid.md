# Security researchers show how cheap AI inference providers steal keys and snoop on users

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/cybersecurity_data_leak.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Security researchers at Dragos used two million fake AI requests to uncover serious vulnerabilities in the AI supply chain, finding at least three compromised providers, hundreds of stolen AWS keys, and marketplace resellers who were simply reading customer requests. OpenRouter responded by removing three providers and 125 endpoints.</p>
