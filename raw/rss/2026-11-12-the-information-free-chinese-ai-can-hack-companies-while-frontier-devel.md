# Free Chinese AI Can Hack Companies While Frontier Developers Face Diminishing Returns

<p>Chinese AI that anyone can download for free has become good enough to break into corporate networks, said Ariel Zeitlin, co-founder and chief technology officer of Tenzai, an Israeli startup that sells hacking software for testing corporate defenses, known as continuous penetration testing.&nbsp;</p><p>At the same time, the most advanced AI that had an early lead in cybersecurity capabilities is getting harder and costlier to improve, said Micah Hill-Smith, CEO of AI benchmarking firm Artificial Analysis. That means Chinese models won’t be far behind and makes it unlikely that any one developer pulls far ahead of its rivals.</p>
