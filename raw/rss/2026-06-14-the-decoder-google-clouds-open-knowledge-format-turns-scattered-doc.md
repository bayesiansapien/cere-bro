# Google Cloud's Open Knowledge Format turns scattered docs into Markdown files for AI agents

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="608" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/google_logo_wall-2.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1088" /></p>
<p>        Google Cloud's new Open Knowledge Format (OKF) standardizes scattered organizational knowledge as Markdown files with YAML frontmatter, making it portable and usable for AI agents. The minimalist spec formalizes a pattern Andrej Karpathy recently popularized as the "LLM Wiki."</p>
