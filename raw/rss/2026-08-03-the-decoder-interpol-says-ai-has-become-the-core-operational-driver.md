# Interpol says AI has become the "core operational driver of cybercrime" across Africa

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/cybersecurity_illu-1.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        AI is involved in 55 percent of reported cybercrimes in Africa, according to a new Interpol report. Financial losses more than doubled from $192 million to $484 million, and about 600,000 cases of digital extortion involving deepfakes were recorded.</p>
