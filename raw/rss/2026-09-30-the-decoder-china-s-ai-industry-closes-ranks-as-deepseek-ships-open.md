# China's AI industry closes ranks as Deepseek ships open-source software for Huawei's Ascend chips

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/deepseek_deepseek_logo.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Deepseek and Huawei have built open-source programming tools for Huawei's Ascend AI chips. At the center is TileLang, a language designed to offer a simpler programming model than Nvidia's CUDA. The partnership targets the biggest obstacle for China's AI industry, which is software that gets the most out of domestic chips.</p>
