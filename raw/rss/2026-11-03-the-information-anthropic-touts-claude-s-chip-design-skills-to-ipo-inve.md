# Anthropic Touts Claude’s Chip Design Skills to IPO Investors

<p>Anthropic’s AI models have helped uncover serious cyber vulnerabilities and transform the software coding profession. Now the company is seeing just how much of an advantage its models can give it in unfamiliar terrain: designing its own AI chips.</p>

<p>The company, which is barreling toward an initial public offering later this month that could value it around $1.5 trillion, has told investors in recent meetings that its new 15-person chip design team is already using its own Claude models for engineering and design work on its custom chips, according to four people familiar with the discussions. Its engineers have found Claude cuts down the time it takes to design chips compared to their estimates of traditional workflows at large chip designers such as Nvidia and Advanced Micro Devices, said some of the people.</p>
