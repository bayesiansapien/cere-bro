# Deepmind researchers propose "Artificial Symbiotic Intelligence" as an alternative to the singularity

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/symbiotic_intelligence.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        According to researchers at the Deepmind Institute, general AI won't emerge as a single supermodel but as a network of cooperating agents and humans. What will matter most, they argue, is not model size but the rules and institutions that govern how they all work together.</p>
