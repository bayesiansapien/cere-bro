# Hours after Anthropic claims a Claude proof of the Riemann hypothesis, Stanford releases an all-human competing draft

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/anthropic_riemann.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge says he used Claude to prove the Riemann hypothesis, math's most famous open problem for 167 years, with a proof formalized in Lean that spans over 250,000 pages. Just hours later, Stanford researchers published an independent, entirely human competing proof, and mathematicians are now checking whether either one holds up.</p>
