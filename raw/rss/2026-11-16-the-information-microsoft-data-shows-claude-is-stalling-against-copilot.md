# Microsoft Data Shows Claude Is Stalling Against Copilot in Office

<p>Anthropic’s latest push into enterprise apps rattled Microsoft investors last week. Microsoft’s own internal data paints a less threatening picture, at least so far.</p>

<p>Usage of Anthropic features that <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=F6dzjaBCBtU">allow people to do work inside Microsoft’s Office apps with Claude</a>, such as sorting email or generating slide decks, has been falling further behind usage of Microsoft’s Copilot&nbsp;in recent months, according to internal Microsoft data described by an employee with knowledge of it. The number of weekly users of those features, known as Claude for Microsoft 365, is less than one-tenth the number of weekly users of the paid version of Copilot, Microsoft’s own AI tools, in Office, which has been growing at a much faster rate, the person said.</p>
