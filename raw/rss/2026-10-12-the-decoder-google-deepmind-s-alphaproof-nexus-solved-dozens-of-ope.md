# Google Deepmind's AlphaProof Nexus solved dozens of open math problems and actually got peer reviewed

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/google_deepmin_proofs.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Google Deepmind's AlphaProof Nexus solved dozens of open math problems, including nine Erdős problems two of which were unsolved for 56 years, per a study in Science. The publication comes as OpenAI faces a boycott for dumping AI proofs on GitHub without peer review. Deepmind had its results reviewed before publishing.</p>
