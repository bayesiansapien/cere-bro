# Google is paying almost no publishers almost nothing for content used in AI answers

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/google_open_web.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Google pays about 100 digital publishers for content used in AI Overviews, AI Mode, and Gemini, according to The Information. Payments range from under $1,000 over several months to more than $1 million a year. Some participants don't know how Google calculates payments, while some larger publishers are holding out for higher rates.</p>
