# AI is getting better faster than experts can reliably test whether new systems are safe, METR warns

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1152" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/metr_logo.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2048" /></p>
<p>        METR reports that leading AI models like Opus 5.5 and GPT-6 Astra are improving rapidly at research tasks but getting harder to measure. Current data can't rule out a dangerous acceleration in AI development, the organization warns, though it also says common tests may overestimate what the models can actually do.</p>
