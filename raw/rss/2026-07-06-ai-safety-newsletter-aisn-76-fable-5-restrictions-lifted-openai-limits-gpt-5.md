US Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick also </span><a href="https://x.com/howardlutnick/status/2072100729603452965"><span>said</span></a><span> in an X post (above) that the government had worked with Anthropic to &#8220;analyze and approve Fable 5 to ensure alignment across the US Government and strengthen America&#8217;s leadership in AI.&#8221;</span></p><p><strong><span>Anthropic called for a more standardized approach to approving model releases.</span></strong><span> In Anthropic&#8217;s June 12 statement announcing the restrictions, the company described the jailbreak the government raised concerns about as &#8220;narrow&#8221; and said it did not provide any greater assistance with malicious activities than other publicly available models.
