# Everything That Led to Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s Return

<p>Jensen Huang is back at Nvidia, with the board announcing his restatement as CEO on Monday morning. He was gone for just 11 days, initially resigning as CEO on Nov. 12&nbsp;after acknowledging an affair with a woman who U.S. authorities think might’ve been a Chinese spy. His relationship with the&nbsp;woman goes back years, part of Nvidia’s deep network in Asia that has allowed its chips to end up in the hands of Chinese companies. Huang has advocated against America’s chip export ban and struck a deep relationship with President Donald Trump, who was a major force in convincing Nvidia’s board to rehire Huang. The intertwined scandals and drama around Huang and Nvidia has roiled the broader stock market, exacerbating fears about the AI economy.&nbsp;&nbsp;At one point, Nvidia’s stock had been nearly halved from its recent high.</p>
