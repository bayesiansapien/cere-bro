# How JPMorgan, Citi Missed Megaspeed Risks in a $2.8 Billion Data Center Loan

<p>A Southeast Asian data center boom has attracted billions in financing from major U.S. banks. JPMorgan and Citigroup, for instance, lent money in 2025 to a data center operator that did business with Megaspeed, a company now at the center of the scandal surrounding Nvidia and former CEO Jensen Huang.</p>

<p>Now, as scrutiny of the region’s role in national security intensifies, those loans highlight an emerging risk for banks: indirect exposure to potentially problematic companies through their borrowers. Banks lending to data center operators are typically expected to vet the operators’ major customers, and the financing arrangement raises questions about how thoroughly banks examined complex ownership structures and other potential red flags in Southeast Asia.</p>
