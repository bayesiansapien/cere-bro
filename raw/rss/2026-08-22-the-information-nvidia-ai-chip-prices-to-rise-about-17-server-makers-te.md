# Nvidia AI Chip Prices to Rise About 17%, Server Makers Tell Customers

<p>Prices for some of <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/nvidia">Nvidia’s</a> flagship AI server chip systems are set to rise about 17%, according to two people whose firms were notified by server makers that provide such systems.</p>

<p>The price hikes generally apply to Grace Blackwell 300 and Vera Rubin 200 chip systems that are due to be delivered next year, these people said. The changes could increase the cost of a 1 gigawatt data center by at least $5 billion, based on the current price of chip systems for such a facility.</p>
