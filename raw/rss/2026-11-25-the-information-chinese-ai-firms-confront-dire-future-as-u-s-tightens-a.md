# Chinese AI Firms Confront Dire Future as U.S. Tightens Access to Chips and Tokens

<p>China’s AI model developers are suddenly confronting a dire future, as both the U.S. government and American AI rivals aggressively clamp down on tactics the Chinese companies have long relied on to develop their models.</p>

<p>The U.S. Commerce Department this month introduced a rule aimed at throttling Chinese companies’ access to advanced <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/org-charts/nvidia">Nvidia</a> chips in data centers outside China. The new rule, which plugs a yearslong loophole in Washington’s chip export controls on China, was released after the ouster of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, who has favored greater Chinese access to Nvidia’s chips. It remains in effect for now despite Huang’s reinstatement Monday.</p>
