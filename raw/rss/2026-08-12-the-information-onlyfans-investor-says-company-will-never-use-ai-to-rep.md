# OnlyFans Investor Says Company Will ‘Never’ Use AI to Replace Creators

<p>Every AI executive I talk to these days wants to tell me what their models can now do. <b>James Sagan</b>, the low-profile investor who just bought 16% of <b>OnlyFans</b> at a $3 billion valuation, spent most of our interview telling me what his platform is betting AI can't do.</p><p>In a wide-ranging exclusive interview about the deal, and how he plans to evolve the company, I asked him the obvious question: Does AI disrupt OnlyFans? (You can see the full transcript of the interview <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/onlyfans-new-investor-reveals-financials-ipo-ambitions-rare-interview">here</a>. As a disclosure, my husband invested alongside Sagan in the deal.)</p><p>AI has been upending the pornography industry. AI-generated adult content has been flooding all sorts of services, including the <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/xai-bets-groks-racy-side">leading AI platforms</a>.&nbsp;</p><p>But Sagan doesn’t see it as a threat to his new investment. He went even further and told me OnlyFans is “probably the least likely platform to be disrupted by AI.”&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>
