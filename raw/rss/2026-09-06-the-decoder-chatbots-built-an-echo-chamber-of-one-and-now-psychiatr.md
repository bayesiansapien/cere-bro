# Chatbots built an "echo chamber of one" and now psychiatry has to decide if "AI psychosis" exists

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/ai_hypnosis_syco.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        Researchers at King's College London and other institutions are examining whether "AI-associated psychosis" should become a clinical diagnosis. By OpenAI's own self-reported numbers, about 560,000 users show signs of psychosis or mania each week. Sycophantic chatbots can create an "echo chamber of one" that reinforces delusions.</p>
