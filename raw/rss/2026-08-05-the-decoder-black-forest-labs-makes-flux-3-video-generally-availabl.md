# Black Forest Labs makes FLUX 3 Video generally available and claims it beats Seedance 2.0

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="1439" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/flux_3_video.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="2559" /></p>
<p>        Black Forest Labs has launched FLUX 3 Video, which generates Full HD clips up to 20 seconds long with native audio and lip-synced dialogue in more than 14 languages. It can also render typography directly in scenes. BFL's own Elo rankings put it ahead of Gemini Omni Flash and Seedance 2.0.</p>
