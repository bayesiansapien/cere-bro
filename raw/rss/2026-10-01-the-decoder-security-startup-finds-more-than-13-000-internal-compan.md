# Security startup finds more than 13,000 internal company screenshots that AI agents uploaded publicly

<p><img alt="" class="attachment-full size-full wp-post-image" height="768" src="https://the-decoder.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/glow_github.png" style="height: auto; margin-bottom: 10px;" width="1376" /></p>
<p>        AI agents quietly posted more than 13,000 internal screenshots from 343 organizations, including Fortune 500 companies, to public GitHub repos. Because the platform didn't offer a protected way to upload them, the agents came up with a workaround on their own. The exposed images showed customer data, login credentials, and details about unreleased products.</p>
