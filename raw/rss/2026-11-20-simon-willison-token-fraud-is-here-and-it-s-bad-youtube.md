# Token fraud is here and it’s BAD (YouTube)

<p><strong><a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZDpP7JXPlhc">Token fraud is here and it’s BAD (YouTube)</a></strong></p>
Claire Vo runs <a href="https://www.chatprd.ai">ChatPRD</a>, an AI platform that helps product managers "turn an idea into a spec". The product has been live since the GPT-4 era, and Claire has been building it in public for much of that time.</p>
<p>In this YouTube video (see also the <a href="https://x.com/clairevo/status/2119985786045227197">accompanying Twitter post</a>) Claire describes the <em>flood</em> of token fraud she has seen from groups abusing the product's free trial, sometimes just to get free AI usage but often to resell tokens or run other commercial enterprises on top of the ChatPRD agent.</p>
<blockquote>
<p>I am funding a international Roblox and Fortnite map studio. They run a loop of free trials and promo codes and then tunnel to my agent and use it to build Roblox and Fortnite maps.</p>
</blockquote>
<p>Apparently one of the common prompts Claire saw running through her agent involved SVGs of pelicans riding bicycles! Presumably the token scammers are using that to help sniff the underlying models - different model families have a definite signature, and I've used it like this in the past to try and guess the identity of stealth preview models on platforms like OpenRouter.</p>
<p>Claire is winding down her free trials entirely, which sadly looks like the right decision in the face of this abuse.
