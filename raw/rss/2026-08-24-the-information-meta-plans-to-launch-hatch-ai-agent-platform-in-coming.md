# Meta Plans to Launch ‘Hatch’ AI Agent Platform in Coming Weeks

<p>Meta Platforms plans to launch its consumer version of the OpenClaw AI agent, <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/meta-building-ai-agent-called-hatch-agentic-shopping-tool-instagram">dubbed Hatch</a> internally, as soon as the next several weeks and is targeting October for its latest AI model, called Watermelon, according to internal documents reviewed by The Information.</p>

<p>Hatch is part of Meta Chief Executive Mark Zuckerberg’s ambitions to monetize the company’s enormous AI investments and diversify its revenue streams beyond advertising. Meta has considered a tiered pricing system, as The Information <a href="https://www.theinformation.com/articles/meta-looks-charge-200-month-planned-hatch-ai-agent">previously reported</a>, including charging up to $199.99 for a premium monthly subscription for the product that would include higher usage limits.</p>
