## Architecture & Optimization Mechanics
- **Bonsai is your field's headline this week.** A 27B model in 5.9GB is about **1.75 bits per weight**, roughly ternary. The claimed SWE-bench *improvement* over its predecessor indicates **quantization-aware post-training (QAT plus agentic RL at low bit)**, not post-hoc PTQ. If agentic tool-calling holds near FP16 parity at this bit-width, edge deployment of 27B coding agents on 8GB devices works. What to check: whether the gains come from post-training on SWE-style data (benchmark-targeted) rather than from the compression method.
- **Cerebras at 600 tok/s vs a GPU frontier model at 50 tok/s** shows wafer-scale SRAM removing the memory-bandwidth wall for decode. For routing: a small RL-specialized model on fast silicon can beat a frontier model on *wall-clock to merged PR* even at lower pass@1, so latency-aware routing objectives matter.
- **GPT-6.2 Instant ≈ GPT-6 xhigh** is another data point that reasoning gets distilled into the no-think tier within one generation. Routers should be re-calibrated every release, because last generation's "needs reasoning" query class migrates to the instant tier.
- **MCP file uploads outside the context window** and lazily loaded skills are context-budget optimizations, the protocol-level counterpart of prefix caching.
