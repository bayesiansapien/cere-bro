# How to claim your business in ChatGPT with Shopify

**Channel:** OpenAI
**Published:** 2026-11-20
**Source:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mX7wHNnsghs

## TL;DR
The transcript has no spoken content (music and "hey" only); it is a visual walkthrough. From OpenAI's help docs: merchants can claim their listing in ChatGPT's "Businesses in ChatGPT" (Business Hub), and connecting a Shopify store is one of the automatic verification routes. Once verified, you control your profile, see insights and can create a business agent.

## Key Takeaways
- **Verification options:** phone code, business email code, DNS record, or a Shopify store connection (automatic verification).
- **Persona ID check** is required before publishing profile edits, viewing insights or creating a business agent.
- **Listings exist whether or not you claim them.** OpenAI builds them from other sources, and claiming lets you correct them. Already-claimed listings can be disputed. "Contact support" means the listing is locked.
- **Free for now** (per reporting to Digiday). Local storefronts and restaurants can only claim existing US listings. Status: beta (Nov 2026).
- **Caveat:** Shopify's agentic storefront knowledge base is not connected to the ChatGPT Business Hub profile or business agent.

## Architecture & Optimization Mechanics
Nothing technical in the video. The product implication: ChatGPT is becoming a discovery and commerce surface with merchant-verified entities. That pushes LLM answers from web-scraped facts toward first-party structured data, the same "verified listing" layer Google Business Profile provides for search.
