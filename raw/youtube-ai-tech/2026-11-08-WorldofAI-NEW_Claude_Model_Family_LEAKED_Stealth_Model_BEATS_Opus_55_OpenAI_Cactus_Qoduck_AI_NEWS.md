## Architecture & Optimization Mechanics
- **Five-tier effort ladders are a routing primitive.** When every frontier family ships {low, medium, high, xhigh, max}, a router's real action space is (model × effort), not model alone. Per-request effort selection is probably where most of the cost variance sits.
- **The Qwen 4 preview's hidden multi-size routing is a cautionary tale.** If an evaluation endpoint silently routes between a 27B model and a Max model, any benchmark run against it is measuring a mixture. That applies to your own router evals too: log which backend served every sample.
- **Fingerprinting stealth models** by refusal style, reasoning-trace structure and API response format is an inference-side attribution technique. The 70 to 75% confidence figure is vibes, not a calibrated classifier.
- **The robotics result is a perception-channel failure, not a policy failure.** The models' text safety rules don't carry over once the threat arrives through pixels. Any compression or distillation of a vision-language-action (VLA) model needs safety evals on adversarial visual inputs, not just text red-teaming. Quantization can plausibly make this worse, and that is not tested.

## Real-World Application / Actionable Step
- **Routing:** make reasoning effort a first-class action in your router's policy (model × effort grid). Benchmark the cost-quality Pareto front across effort tiers within one model before adding more models.
- **Eval hygiene:** if you test unreleased or preview endpoints, check for backend routing first (latency distribution, output-length variance, response headers). Throw out results from mixed backends.
- **Compression safety:** if any of your pruning or quantization work touches VLMs or VLAs, add a perceptual-perturbation safety eval (Gaussian blur, generative edits) to your before/after comparison. "Accuracy preserved" is not "safety preserved."
- **Finance:** the political backlash against data centers is now a measurable risk that hasn't stopped the capex. Watch permitting delays and local ballot measures as leading indicators for power and DC-REIT exposure, rather than hyperscaler capex guidance.
- Ignore the Silicon/Obsidian identity debate until there's a release or official benchmark.
