# How Preply combines AI and human tutors to personalize learning

**Channel:** OpenAI  
**Published:** 2026-06-12  
**Source:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-Jm-Bf3g9r8  

## TL;DR
Preply, the world's largest language learning marketplace, has integrated OpenAI’s models to create an AI-human hybrid learning experience. By automating repetitive administrative tasks such as lesson planning and note-taking through "Lesson Insights," Preply has cut tutor prep time by over 50%, allowing human teachers to focus on motivation and engagement—areas where AI currently lacks high-fidelity human essence.

## Key Takeaways
- **Augmentation over Replacement:** AI acts as a co-pilot, handling summaries, feedback, and personalized improvement suggestions, while humans provide energy and emotional motivation.
- **High Adoption Rates:** Over 70% of tutors and 75% of learners have opted into AI-driven features like Lesson Insights, with a 70% long-term retention rate.
- **Engineering Efficiency:** Preply’s engineering team uses Codex models to shift focus from syntax and debugging to high-level system architecture and customer problem-solving.

## AI Tech: Architecture & Optimization Mechanics
Preply prioritizes **Accuracy** as its primary metric, comparing multiple models against an internal framework before selecting OpenAI for its superior performance in grammar and linguistic nuance. The architectural shift involves moving from "general practice" to "highly targeted" learning loops. For an AI researcher, this highlights the value of **Fine-Tuning on Transcripts** to generate high-fidelity, personalized recaps that drive user retention.
