## TL;DR
The team at Lovable reports a significant capability leap with OpenAI's GPT-5.5 (codename "Spud"), particularly in long-horizon planning and context retention. The model has enabled a 31% increase in intent understanding and a 22% reduction in "context amnesia," allowing users to build complex features in "one shot" without Iterative prompting.

## Key Takeaways
- **Planning Dominance:** GPT-5.5 is significantly better at mapping out multi-step engineering tasks before executing.
- **Reduced Amnesia:** The model maintains a much tighter grip on information deep into large sessions, solving the "forgetting" issues of previous generations.
- **Vibe Coding Success:** For Lovable’s non-technical users, this model reduces the "friction points" where they would previously get stuck and abandon projects.
- **Efficiency Gains:** Success in "one shot" significantly reduces the cost and time of development cycles.

## Core Architecture & Research Claims
OpenAI’s GPT-5.5 represents the first fully retrained base model in the "Agentic Sledgehammer" class. It introduces **parallel test-time compute**, which allows the model to dedicate more internal processing to complex reasoning tasks without a linear increase in user-facing latency. Lovable’s internal benchmarks confirm that GPT-5.5 is currently the most consistent model for agentic software engineering.
