# Startup School Europe 2027!

**Channel:** Y Combinator
**Published:** 2026-11-25
**Source:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8gOkTwEHTP8

## TL;DR
YC announced Startup School Europe for **February 6, 2027 at Central Hall Westminster, London**. Paul Graham, Garry Tan and YC companies StarCloud, PostHog and GoCardless will speak. You have to apply for a place.

## Key Takeaways
- **Date and venue:** Feb 6, 2027, Central Hall Westminster. This is the same venue as the November 2024 London edition.
- **Speakers:** Paul Graham, Garry Tan, StarCloud (orbital data centers), PostHog (product analytics) and GoCardless (payments).
- **Access:** Apply on the event page. Admission is selective.

## Architecture & Optimization Mechanics
- Not applicable. This is an event announcement. StarCloud is the one relevant name, because it pitches space-based GPU compute, which is a power and cooling play on inference capex.

## Grounded Context (Web Enrichment)
YC ran Startup School Europe at Westminster Central Hall on November 23, 2024, and the first London edition goes back to 2014. I found no independent press coverage of the 2027 date yet, so the YC video is the only source for now. The lineup leans toward builders over investors, which fits YC's push to recruit European founders into US batches.

## Real-World Application / Actionable Step
- If you're in or near London in early February 2027 and considering a spin-out (for example compression or routing tooling), apply. The real value is the hallway access to YC partners, not the talks, which get posted online afterward.
