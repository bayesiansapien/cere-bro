# Nemotron 3 Nano Omni: Efficient and Open Multimodal Intelligence

The paper introduces Nemotron 3 Nano Omni, a multimodal model that represents the first in its series to natively handle audio alongside text, images, and video. The system demonstrates consistent accuracy improvements over its predecessor, Nemotron Nano V2 VL, across all modalities, enabled by advances in architecture, training data and recipes.

Key capabilities include strong performance in document understanding, extended audio-video comprehension, and computer interaction tasks. Built on a 30B-parameter backbone with efficiency optimizations, the model employs innovative multimodal token-reduction techniques to deliver substantially lower inference latency and higher throughput than other models of similar size.

The team released model versions in multiple precision formats (BF16, FP8, and FP4) along with training data and code to support future research development.
