# ROSE: Retrieval-Oriented Segmentation Enhancement

**Authors:** Song Tang, Guangquan Jie, Henghui Ding
**arXiv:** [2604.14147](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14147)
**HuggingFace:** [hf.co/papers/2604.14147](https://huggingface.co/papers/2604.14147)

## Abstract

Existing segmentation models based on multimodal large language models (MLLMs), such as LISA, often struggle with novel or emerging entities due to their inability to incorporate up-to-date knowledge. To address this challenge, we introduce the Novel Emerging Segmentation Task (NEST), which focuses on segmenting novel entities that MLLMs fail to recognize due to their absence from training data. We propose ROSE: Retrieval-Oriented Segmentation Enhancement, a plug-and-play framework designed to augment any MLLM-based segmentation model.
