# CCFinance: Reconstructing Financial News Samples from Web Archives

Proprietary news archives assign stories to firms, so reproducing a financial study often requires access to both the articles and the vendor's assignments. CCFinance lets researchers reconstruct firm coverage under different selection rules by recording which version of a story was captured, which issuers it names and how often each appears. From 14.8 million English-language records in Fine News, derived from public Common Crawl News, we identify 5.22 million distinct, consistently dated articles with issuer links. Exact reconstruction requires the restricted snapshot and derived artifacts. We compare GLiNER and Stanza on 250 model-labeled article leads and assess issuer links and story focus against blinded model judgments. Estimated precision among 475 accepted links is 96.8%. Among held-out articles with a detected company, requiring three mentions raises subject precision from 31.9% to 66.1%. Across the 2017-2025 panel, the same rule reduces the number of issuer-years with at least 50 qualifying stories from 13,918 to 8,641. Three reimplementations examine volatility tracking, bank networks and overnight returns. In the network study, bank pairs appearing together rose from 177 to 242 across publishers between January and April 2020, but fell from 50 to 35 within Reuters.
