# OceanPile: A Large-Scale Multimodal Ocean Corpus for Foundation Models

The vast ocean remains underexplored by artificial intelligence due to a critical data shortage. Ocean information is scattered across multiple sources and characterized by multimodal, noisy, and weakly labeled data lacking unified formats and semantic alignment. While Multimodal Large Language Models excel in general contexts, their marine science applications are severely limited by the absence of large-scale, well-aligned multimodal datasets for ocean environments.

To address this gap, researchers introduce OceanPile, a comprehensive multimodal corpus for ocean foundation models with three core elements: OceanCorpus integrating sonar data, underwater imagery, marine visuals, and scientific text from authoritative sources; OceanInstruction, a refined instruction dataset created through a novel pipeline directed by a hierarchical Ocean Concept Knowledge Graph; and OceanBenchmark, a manually evaluated testing framework for assessment.

The project implements a multi-stage quality control procedure to maintain scientific accuracy and cross-modal consistency. Experimental results show substantial performance gains for models trained using this dataset. The researchers plan to publicly release all datasets to advance marine artificial intelligence and enable specialized MLLMs.
