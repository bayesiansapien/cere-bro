# Safety Drift After Fine-Tuning: Evidence from High-Stakes Domains

The research examines whether safety properties of foundation models remain stable during domain-specific fine-tuning. The authors analyzed 100 models, including deployed versions in medical and legal domains, and discovered that benign fine-tuning induces large, heterogeneous, and often contradictory changes in measured safety. Their findings demonstrate that models frequently show improvements on some safety benchmarks while simultaneously degrading on others. The analysis reveals substantial inconsistency across different evaluation methods. The work concludes that relying exclusively on base-model safety assessments is inadequate for managing risk in high-stakes applications, as downstream adaptations can introduce unforeseen harms that current evaluation and accountability practices fail to capture.
