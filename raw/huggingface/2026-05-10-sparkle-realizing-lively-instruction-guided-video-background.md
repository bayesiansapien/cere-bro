# Sparkle: Realizing Lively Instruction-Guided Video Background Replacement via Decoupled Guidance

In recent years, open-source efforts like Senorita-2M have propelled video editing toward natural language instruction. We introduce Sparkle, a dataset of ~140K video pairs spanning five common background-change themes, alongside Sparkle-Bench, the largest evaluation benchmark tailored for background replacement to date. We trace quality degradation to a lack of precise background guidance during data synthesis and design a scalable pipeline that generates foreground and background guidance in a decoupled manner with strict quality filtering.
