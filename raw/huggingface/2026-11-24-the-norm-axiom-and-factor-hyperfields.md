# The norm axiom and factor hyperfields

Touchard and the author asked whether every valued hyperfield, in Krasner's sense with the norm axiom, is of the form H_ρ(K): a valued field K read up to relative error ρ. The question turns on the norm axiom. If a valued hyperfield is a quotient K^times/Tcup{0} of a field, the norm axiom forces T=1+m_ρ for a valuation on K. This completes the proof of a published proposition, and it makes the question equivalent to asking whether every Krasner valued hyperfield is such a quotient. For value group Z we prove directly that it is. Without the norm axiom the answer is negative. Layering a hyperfield over a non-trivial ordered abelian group gives a factor hyperfield exactly when the hyperfield itself is one, so Massouros's hyperfields yield hyperfields with a non-trivial valuation, satisfying all the other axioms, that are not factor hyperfields. The general question remains open.
