# RadAgent: A tool-using AI agent for stepwise interpretation of chest computed tomography

RadAgent is an AI system that interprets chest CT scans through a transparent, step-by-step process. It generates CT reports through a stepwise and interpretable process where each resulting report is accompanied by a fully inspectable trace of intermediate decisions and tool interactions. The research demonstrates significant improvements over existing vision-language models, with gains of 6.0 points in macro-F1 and 5.4 points in micro-F1. The system also showed enhanced robustness under challenging conditions and achieved 37.0% faithfulness — a capability entirely absent in comparable models. By structuring CT interpretation as an explicit, tool-augmented and iterative reasoning trace, RadAgent enables clinicians to examine and validate the AI's reasoning rather than passively accepting final outputs, advancing transparency in medical AI applications.
