# Sparkle: Realizing Lively Instruction-Guided Video Background Replacement via Decoupled Guidance

Background Replacement, a task central to creative applications such as film production and advertising, requires synthesizing entirely new, temporally consistent scenes while maintaining accurate foreground-background interactions. This paper designs a scalable pipeline that generates foreground and background guidance in a decoupled manner with strict quality filtering. The paper introduces the Sparkle Dataset (~140K high-quality video pairs), Sparkle-Bench (the largest evaluation benchmark tailored for background replacement), and Kiwi-Sparkle (a model trained on the Sparkle dataset). Experiments demonstrate substantially better performance than all existing baselines on both OpenVE-Bench and Sparkle-Bench.
