# OccuBench: Evaluating AI Agents on Real-World Professional Tasks via Language World Models

**Authors:** Xiaomeng Hu, Yinger Zhang, Fei Huang
**arXiv:** [2604.10866](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10866)
**HuggingFace:** [hf.co/papers/2604.10866](https://huggingface.co/papers/2604.10866)

## Abstract

AI agents are expected to perform professional work across hundreds of occupational domains, yet existing benchmarks can only evaluate agents in the few domains where public environments exist. We introduce OccuBench, a benchmark covering 100 real-world professional task scenarios across 10 industry categories and 65 specialized domains, enabled by Language World Models (LWMs) that simulate domain-specific environments through LLM-driven tool response generation.
