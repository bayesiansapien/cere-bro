# Seedance 2.0: Advancing Video Generation for World Complexity

**Authors:** Team Seedance, De Chen, Liyang Chen
**arXiv:** [2604.14148](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14148)
**HuggingFace:** [hf.co/papers/2604.14148](https://huggingface.co/papers/2604.14148)

## Abstract

Seedance 2.0 is a new native multi-modal audio-video generation model. Compared with its predecessors, Seedance 2.0 adopts a unified, highly efficient, and large-scale architecture for multi-modal audio-video joint generation. This allows it to support four input modalities: text, image, audio, and video, by integrating one of the most comprehensive suites of multi-modal content reference and editing capabilities available in the industry to date.
