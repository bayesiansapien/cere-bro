# TREX: Automating LLM Fine-tuning via Agent-Driven Tree-based Exploration

**Authors:** Zerun Ma, Guoqiang Wang, Xinchen Xie
**arXiv:** [2604.14116](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14116)
**HuggingFace:** [hf.co/papers/2604.14116](https://huggingface.co/papers/2604.14116)

## Abstract

While Large Language Models (LLMs) have empowered AI research agents to perform isolated scientific tasks, automating complex, real-world workflows, such as LLM training, remains a significant challenge. In this paper, we introduce TREX, a multi-agent system that automates the entire LLM training life-cycle. By orchestrating collaboration between two core modules-the Researcher and the Executor-the system seamlessly performs requirement analysis, open-domain literature and data research, formulation of training strategies, preparation of data recipes, and model training and evaluation. The multi-round experimental process is modeled as a search tree, enabling the system to efficiently plan exploration paths, reuse historical results, and distill high-level insights from iterative trials.
