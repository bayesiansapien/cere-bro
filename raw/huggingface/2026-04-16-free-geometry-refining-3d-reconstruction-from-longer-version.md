# Free Geometry: Refining 3D Reconstruction from Longer Versions of Itself

**Authors:** Yuhang Dai, Xingyi Yang
**arXiv:** [2604.14048](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.14048)
**HuggingFace:** [hf.co/papers/2604.14048](https://huggingface.co/papers/2604.14048)

## Abstract

Feed-forward 3D reconstruction models are efficient but rigid: once trained, they perform inference in a zero-shot manner and cannot adapt to the test scene. As a result, visually plausible reconstructions often contain errors, particularly under occlusions, specularities, and ambiguous cues. We introduce Free Geometry, a framework that enables feed-forward 3D reconstruction models to self-evolve at test time without any 3D ground truth.
