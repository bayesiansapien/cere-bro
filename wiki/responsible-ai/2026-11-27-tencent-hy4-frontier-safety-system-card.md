# Tencent's Hy4 system card

**TL;DR.** Tencent published an 80-page frontier-safety system card for Hy4, which AI Safety in China calls the most detailed safety disclosure from a Chinese developer. Scope is close to recent OpenAI and Anthropic cards: cyber and bio misuse, unsafe agentic actions, prompt injection, deception and loss of control, plus content and child safety. It measures Hy4's ability to improve other models (a recursive self-improvement proxy) and says that is "still at an early stage", but gives no threshold that would trigger stronger safeguards.

## Key points

- **Third-party testing.** Fangcun AI (Chinese safety startup) evaluated loss-of-control and manipulation; ALICE (Israeli-American, recently raised $140M, has worked with Anthropic and Google) tested content safety, prompt injection and cyber refusals.
- **Weak spots.** Minimal chemical-risk discussion. Most comparisons are against Tencent's own Hy4-preview, so cross-lab comparison is hard.
- **Context.** Tencent's chief AI scientist Yao Shunyu has publicly called for pre-release safety testing. The same brief notes Xi and Trump discussed AI safety at APEC Shenzhen (11-19) and reaffirmed human control, the WAICO founding treaty is now public, and a Party journal article warned frontier AI could escape human control.
