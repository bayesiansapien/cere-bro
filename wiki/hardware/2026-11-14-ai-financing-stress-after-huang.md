# AI compute financing under stress (11-14)

## TL;DR

The day after Jensen Huang's resignation, the story moved from the man to the balance sheet.

- **Fed Financial Stability Report (via @rohanpaul_ai):** AI is now the top financial risk named by market professionals (87%, up from 50% in spring). Debt raised for AI infrastructure passed **$680B** by October, about 5x all of 2023 and roughly 31% of investment-grade corporate borrowing. Big tech and chipmakers carry over **$3T in future leases and guarantees**. The report names GPU makers guaranteeing the value of chips they sell to buyers they also invest in as "circular financing". The largest US banks hold close to $1T of exposure to big tech, data-center builders and their financing vehicles. Fed Governor Michelle Bowman separately warned AI debt and valuations could trigger a correction.
- **Nvidia's $500B GPU-debt plan** with Apollo, BlackRock, Blackstone, Brookfield, Goldman Sachs and KKR (to turn AI chips into tradable debt) has produced no deals; partners had pushed Huang to raise Nvidia's 25% residual-value backstop on collateral GPUs (CNBC via AI Weekly). The WSJ (via The Decoder) reports Huang's exit traces more to these risky guarantees than to his personal life; Kress had tried to contain them.
- **Neoclouds:** Nscale's updated IPO filing shows a **$1.69B Q3 loss on $307.5M revenue** (up 1,344%), widening from $1.02B in Q2, as Microsoft walked away from an English data center. CoreWeave revenue rose 168% to $3.66B; it is seeking **$9B in customer prepayments** and shorter contracts. Neocloud stocks fell 9-13% on the day.
- **Counter-move:** Riot Platforms named **Anthropic as the tenant of a 15-year, 910 MW powered-land lease** for its whole Corsicana, Texas campus, about **$28B** over the initial term and up to $54B with extensions. Anthropic's IPO is reported six times oversubscribed, pricing 11-18 and trading 11-19 as ANT. SpaceX will spend over $74B on AI supercompute campuses in Texas and Arizona. SoftBank took a $3.7B charge for over-ordering power equipment.
- **Megaspeed:** Ed Zitron's long piece, built on Culper Research's May short report, alleges an Nvidia-adjacent smuggling network that may account for over 20% (over $43B) of Nvidia's FY2026 revenue. Allegations, not findings. Separately, Will DePue says six Chinese models carry numerical fingerprints of Blackwell GPUs, and companies tied to Alice Huang reportedly invested in DeepSeek.

## Relation to prior wiki pages

- **Confirms the 11-13 SemiAnalysis read** ([summary](2026-11-13-nvidia-without-jensen.md)) that Nvidia's $530B backstop book was personal to Huang. The Fed now names that structure as a systemic risk, and the debt partners have stalled.
- **Updates [compute-economics](compute-economics.md):** the 11-13 Looking Ahead predicted the backstop book would grow more slowly under Kress within 30 days. The $500B debt plan stalling is early evidence in that direction; the 11-17 earnings call is the real test.
- **Tension with the research side:** the same week's papers cut serving cost 2-10x (decision models at 1/70th per request, 4-bit training, KV budgeters). Falling unit cost and rising financing stress can coexist if demand does not grow faster than price falls (the Jevons bet the bull case relies on).
