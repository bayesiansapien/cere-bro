# Benchmarks as difficulty measures, synthetic work environments, and a Platonic theorem (2026-11-01)

**Sources:** HF Daily Papers 2026-10-30 · [2610.29927](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29927) · [2610.37161](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37161) · [2610.37786](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37786) · [2610.38174](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.38174) · [2610.39302](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.39302) · psychiatric-intake RL via [@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2116551519034048861) · raw: `raw/huggingface/2026-10-30-*.md`

## Shorts

- **Your Benchmark Is an Empirical Measure Over Difficulty (2610.29927, 140 upvotes).** A benchmark induces a distribution over item difficulty. Two failure modes: *measure mismatch* (its difficulty mix differs from the one you care about) and *coverage gaps* (empty regions of the difficulty axis, so local progress is invisible). Two diagnostic metrics with guarantees; 25 benchmarks across 7 domains analysed; many overweight some difficulty regions. BenchFill rewrites items to fill gaps. Ties to the 10-30 "Sharpening Tax" and "what outcome RL buys" findings: if RL mostly improves finishing on mid-difficulty items, a benchmark heavy in that band overstates progress.
- **WorkFactory (2610.37161).** Builds executable professional tasks from intents via an information-support graph, then probe-executes to find and repair missing information. 112,686 tasks across 612 occupations; fine-tuned WorkPilot models at 4B, 27B and 35B-A3B; WorkPilot-35B-A3B goes 879 to 1565 Elo on GDPval-AA v2.1. Diminishing returns are model-dependent.
- **WebAnything (2610.37786).** Reconstructs resettable website environments from real URLs with an Explorer-Builder and an independent verifier. 244 sites, 12,984 accepted tasks; beats human-assisted WebHarbor on fidelity (12 wins, 2 ties, 0 losses). Fine-tuning Qwen3.5-9B on 5,856 trajectories: +9.9 REAL success, +13.33 WebArena-Verified. Extends [agent-training-environments](agent-training-environments.md) (Trajectory2Gym, 10-28).
- **EasyProver / Aegis (2610.38174).** Recursive Lean agents with isolated contexts and parallel tactic probing, an off-the-shelf frontier model, no training: 669/672 PutnamBench, all six IMO 2026 formalizations, 597/600 NTP4VC. Aegis turns competitive-programming Rust into Lean verification tasks; EasyProver proves 52.7% of 2,000 within 30 minutes. Harness, not training, again carries the result.
- **Platonic Representation Theorem (2610.39302, 60 upvotes).** Proves JEPAs (joint-embedding predictive architectures) trained on separate modalities learn the same representation up to a rotation when positive pairs are two views of one scene. Next-token prediction is a JEPA with "bad views" (parts of one document), which explains why language models align with vision only at scale. Good views need 42x fewer samples to reach the same alignment; a 109M text JEPA aligns with DINOv2 and CLIP better than every evaluated LLM. Sample-efficiency angle: objective choice, not scale, buys alignment.
- **Psychiatric intake RL (Google DeepMind, 2610.40476).** Multi-turn RL against simulated patients who reveal facts only after rapport. Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite trained this way beats its base in 76.7% of 60 matched pairs; missed acute-risk escalations fall 30.5% to 12.9% on 256 adversarial cases. About 1 in 8 crises still go unescalated.
