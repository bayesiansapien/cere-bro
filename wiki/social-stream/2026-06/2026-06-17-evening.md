## Summary

The strongest AI signal this evening is distribution, not research: Grok 4.3 landed on Amazon Bedrock, which xAI staffers framed as completing the set of all four major clouds (AWS, Vertex, Azure, Oracle), a claim no other lab can make yet. Kilo Code paired two cost-and-sovereignty plays in one slot, an Inceptron partnership for EU-hosted open-weight inference (Kimi K2.6, GLM 5.1, MiniMax M2.5, GDPR/ISO compliant) and a "4 spend levers" post arguing most agent work runs fine on a cheaper model, the same routing-as-cost thesis the wiki tracked on 06-07. Claude Build Day produced a cluster of five tweets crowning three winners, all of them world-model and 3D-reconstruction demos, which rhymes with Odyssey's fresh $310M Series B for real-time world models. The rest of the slot is heavy off-topic noise: a flood of French political posts from one account, several US political/crime accounts, and an Elon "full movies by end of year" repost. Signal is thin but the cloud-distribution and EU-sovereignty threads are worth the click.
