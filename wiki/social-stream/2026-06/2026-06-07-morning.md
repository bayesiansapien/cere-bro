- **Google CVPR 2026 booth: 3DCodeBench, D4RT, TIPSv2** (cluster of 3, [@GoogleResearch](https://x.com/GoogleResearch/status/2063376914090250554)). 3DCodeBench benchmarks agentic procedural 3D modeling: a VLM-driven agent turns nested Infinigen procedural factories into standalone Blender Python scripts, with a skills library (every successful API migration becomes a reusable skill) and an experience library indexing recurring patterns, across 212 object classes and 12 frontier VLMs under multi-turn refinement. [D4RT](https://x.com/GoogleResearch/status/2063368807041892423) (a CVPR Best Paper) is a feedforward model that jointly infers depth, spatio-temporal correspondence, and camera parameters from one video via a unified transformer, with a querying mechanism that probes any point in space-time without dense per-frame decoding. [TIPSv2](https://x.com/GoogleResearch/status/2063310829437809017) reports that distillation unlocks superior patch-text alignment, so distilled student encoders significantly surpass their much larger teachers, a datapoint for the wiki's recurring "distillation is not merely lossy" thread ([knowledge-distillation](../../inference-efficiency/knowledge-distillation.md)).
