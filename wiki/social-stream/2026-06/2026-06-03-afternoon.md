## Summary

The slot is dominated by one thing: Hugging Face's Elie Bakouch live-tweeting a ~47-post teardown of Microsoft AI's new MAI tech report, which we already have a wiki page on (the MAI Thinking-1 hill-climbing model trained with zero synthetic data and zero distillation from prior models). - **Microsoft MAI tech report teardown (cluster of 17)** ([@eliebakouch](https://nitter.net/eliebakouch/status/2062022507062841423#m) · [report PDF](https://microsoft.ai/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/main_20260602_2.pdf)). Bakouch calls it one of the most transparent reports at this scale and walks through the post-training stack: no cold start (zero synthetic data), difficulty filtering via two-stage pass rate, top-p masking like DeepSeek, and a GRPO variant with length penalty, entropy-based outer clip (holding entropy near 0.3), no KL term, and global rather than per-response normalization. He flags rounds of RL then self-distillation SFT to recover, attributed to train/inference mismatch and reduced once they switch to bf16. Infra notes: SGLang serving, good final-run numbers, and 40% higher throughput per Watt as bullish for Microsoft's chips. Critiques: unsure why they use SFT over on-policy distillation in consolidation, and the model/perf co-design seems not to weigh inference much.
