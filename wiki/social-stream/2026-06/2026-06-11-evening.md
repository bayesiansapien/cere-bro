- **Grok Build 0.1 solves git-leak-recovery** (cluster of 6, [@kilocode](https://nitter.net/kilocode/status/2065063735803162686#m) · [writeup](https://blog.kilo.ai/p/we-asked-grok-build-01-to-recover)). On a Terminal-Bench task (recover a secret committed then hidden by a branch rewind), Grok ran git fsck, found the orphaned commit, expired the reflog, re-checked, and ran git gc --prune=now. 27 steps, 41 seconds, $0.09, versus a ~30-minute human estimate. The notable bit is mid-task verification instead of declaring victory early.
