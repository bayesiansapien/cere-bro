## Summary

The day's strongest cluster was the agentic PC: NVIDIA and Microsoft used GTC Taipei and Build to push agents onto the Windows device, with eight morning posts spanning OpenShell (a secure local-to-cloud agent runtime with smart query routing), the Vera "CPU for agents," Nemotron 3 Ultra, DGX Station and RTX Spark hardware, and Claude running natively on GB300 Blackwell in Azure. Nous Research's Hermes Desktop preview and Scoble's "Hermes vs OpenClaw is the new Mac vs Windows" framing made the on-device agent the contested surface of the week. The single strongest standout was an afternoon teardown: Hugging Face's Elie Bakouch live-tweeted a ~47-post walkthrough of Microsoft's MAI tech report, full of concrete RL recipe detail (a GRPO variant with length penalty, entropy-based outer clipping, no KL term, global normalization, SGLang serving, 40% higher throughput per Watt). Coding-agent tooling was a third thread: Anthropic's "ant" CLI and revamped /fork, Cursor's year-of-cloud-agents lessons, and Kilo's argument that human review bandwidth, not agent count, is the real limit. Evening was thin but carried two useful items: Kilo's debunking of the "Opus 4.8 is secretly Qwen" rumor as a data-contamination identity bug, and ICE/NYSE joining Anthropic's Project Glasswing. Everything else was political, promotional, or aggregator noise, heavily concentrated in a long run of French political chatter.
