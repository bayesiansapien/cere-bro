## Summary

The day's strongest cross-slot cluster is xAI distribution: Grok 4.3 landing on Amazon Bedrock surfaced in both morning (AWS CEO Matt Garman announcing it) and evening (xAI staff framing it as completing all four major clouds, a set no other lab can claim), while a separate Grok Imagine Video 1.5 launch wave filled the afternoon. The single strongest standout is GLM-5.2 in the morning: Z.ai shipped a near-Opus open-weight coding model under MIT, and @eliebakouch pulled out the one piece of real engineering, an IndexShare and KVShare trick on the multi-token-prediction head that lifts speculative-decoding acceptance length 20%. Hardware was the other real signal, with NVIDIA Blackwell sweeping MLPerf Training 6.0 and CoreWeave training DeepSeek-V3 671B in two minutes. Cursor ran across all three slots as a noisy thread: the claimed $60B SpaceX all-stock acquisition (CEO @mntruell confirming he is joining), a from-scratch frontier model tease, and the Origin code-hosting launch. A quiet world-model thread tied Claude Build Day's three 3D-reconstruction winners to Odyssey's fresh $310M Series B. Everything else was business hype and a heavy flood of off-topic French and US political posts.
