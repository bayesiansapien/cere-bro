- **Anthropic Red measures Mythos Preview's N-day exploit uplift** (cluster of 2: [@logangraham](https://nitter.net/logangraham/status/2064220844168253500#m), [@logangraham](https://nitter.net/logangraham/status/2064220908810887213#m) · [red.anthropic.com/2026/n-days](https://red.anthropic.com/2026/n-days/)). The team found Mythos Preview is meaningfully better at developing N-days, vulnerabilities already publicly disclosed and patched on some systems, where attackers exploit the "patch gap" on machines that have not updated. Once a patch ships, "patch diffing" the pre- and post-patch binary reveals exactly what was fixed; historically that reverse-engineering was slow (WannaCry took 59 days after its patch, Citrix Bleed ~2 weeks), buying defenders time. Mythos collapses that to hours and a few thousand dollars. Anthropic publishes deliberately so the world can prepare, and @logangraham promises more defensive/blue-team work. See [responsible-ai summary](../../responsible-ai/2026-06-09-anthropic-n-days-mythos-cyber.md).
