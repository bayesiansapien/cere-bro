## Posts

- **Google CVPR 2026 demo blitz (cluster of 7)** ([@GoogleResearch](https://nitter.net/GoogleResearch/status/2062952310196879823#m)). Google ran its CVPR booth as a research showcase, and several items are substantive. **BlazeEdit** ([arxiv 2605.28067](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.28067)) is the efficiency highlight: a generalist image-to-image diffusion model that consolidates object removal, outpainting, relighting, tone correction and sticker generation into a single 195M-parameter model by eliminating text-conditioning, completing a full pass in 290ms on a Pixel 10 for private on-device editing. **D4RT** ([d4rt-paper.github.io](https://d4rt-paper.github.io/), CVPR Best Paper) is a single feedforward transformer that jointly infers depth, spatio-temporal correspondence, and camera parameters from one video, with a querying mechanism that probes any 3D point in space-time instead of dense per-frame decoding. **TIPSv2** ([gdm-tipsv2.github.io](https://gdm-tipsv2.github.io/)) is a vision-language encoder whose headline finding is that distillation unlocks better patch-text alignment than standard pretraining, so distilled students beat their larger teachers at dense alignment, a distillation result that echoes the wiki's [knowledge-distillation](../../inference-efficiency/knowledge-distillation.md) thread. **Ego2Web** is a benchmark requiring web agents to ground evidence from egocentric video before acting online. Plus an enterprise agentic-RAG framework (iterative multi-agent search before generation) and Android XR / Gemini-on-glasses demos. The vision/3D items are largely Tier 3 here; BlazeEdit and the TIPSv2 distillation finding are the parts worth keeping.

- **Cursor ships Design Mode** ([@cursor_ai](https://nitter.net/cursor_ai/status/2062950345928749525#m), [blog](https://cursor.com/blog/design-mode)). Cursor's Design Mode lets you click an element, draw on the page, or describe a change by voice from the Cursor browser, and the agent gets the selected element, its code, and the surrounding layout as context for the edit, tightening the notice-to-edit loop for UI work. Co-founder @mntruell framed the broader bet: working with agents should feel like working with a colleague, gesturing at a shared screen and talking live, not just typing chat. A concrete instance of multimodal, spatial agent interfaces.

- **The Copilot bill came due, and Nemotron 3 Ultra tops Kilo's leaderboard (cluster of 2)** ([@kilocode](https://nitter.net/kilocode/status/2063040702020280581#m), [blog](https://blog.kilo.ai/p/the-github-copilot-bill-came-due)).
