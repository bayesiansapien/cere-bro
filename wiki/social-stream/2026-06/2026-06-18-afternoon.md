The dominant signal this slot is talent: Noam Shazeer, the Transformer co-inventor Google paid billions to bring back, announced he is leaving for OpenAI, and the timeline lit up with reactions (a cluster of seven posts from @eliebakouch, @ns123abc, and @MillionInt). Running second is a wave of open-weight model drops, GLM-5.2 (called Opus 4.6 / GPT 5.4 tier) and DeepSeek V4 Vision, both landing within hours. The sharpest research item is Google's AMIE longitudinal-care paper in Nature. Everything else is heavy noise: a large block of off-topic French and US politics from @brivael and others, plus Kilo Code star-count promo. Signal-to-noise is poor this slot, but the talent and open-weights threads are real.
