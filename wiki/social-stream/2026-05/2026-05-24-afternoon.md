## Posts

- **Life-Harness: adapt the interface, not the model (cluster of 2)** ([@rohanpaul_ai](https://nitter.net/rohanpaul_ai/status/2058177628188623212#m) and [@omarsar0](https://nitter.net/omarsar0/status/2058208914148389083#m) via @bayesiansapien retweets, [arxiv:2605.22166](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.22166)). Life-Harness is a lifecycle-aware runtime harness that evolves from training trajectories by converting recurring interaction failures into reusable interventions across environment contracts, procedural skills, action realization, and trajectory regulation, then stays fixed during held-out evaluation. The headline numbers: 116 out of 126 model-environment settings improved across 18 backbones on tau-bench, tau-squared-bench, and AgentBench, with an average relative improvement of 88.5%. A harness evolved only from Qwen3-4B-Instruct trajectories transfers to 17 other models, which is the load-bearing claim that the harness captures environment-side structure, not model-specific behavior. This is the second concrete instance this week of "the model is frozen, the harness does the lifting", after yesterday's [code-as-agent-harness](../../agentic-systems/2026-05-23-code-as-agent-harness.md) survey. Worth a wiki summary page when ingest runs.
