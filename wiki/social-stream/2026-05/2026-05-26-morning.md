- **ZEDA: skip the experts that aren't needed** ([@rohanpaul_ai](https://nitter.net/rohanpaul_ai/status/2058620038693999012#m), curated repost). Rohan Paul's writeup of Zero-Expert Self-Distillation Adaptation, a post-training method that converts already-trained static MoE models (Qwen3, GLM) into dynamic ones where tokens that do not need an expert simply skip them. The headline claim is roughly 50 percent of expert compute removed with almost no accuracy loss. Mechanism: a low-cost self-distillation framework that teaches the model to recognize which tokens are "too easy to need them" and route them around the expert pool. The paper reframes MoE inference economics: half the expert FLOPs in production MoE deployments are wasted on tokens that do not benefit from expert specialization. This is the second MoE-efficiency result this week sitting in the same frame as the 2026-05-24 cs.LG #14 Kurate paper on muP MoE scaling (covered in yesterday's Worth Watching). If ZEDA composes with the muP MoE parameterization and BitCPM-CANN 1.58-bit quantization, the production economics of MoE inference flip materially. No direct wiki page yet; pencil this in as a possible future summary if the original arXiv ID surfaces.
