## Posts

- **δ-mem: efficient online memory for frozen LLMs (cluster of 2)** ([@HuggingPapers](https://nitter.net/HuggingPapers/status/2054431768779067542#m) · [@dair_ai](https://nitter.net/dair_ai/status/2054600147020222630#m) · [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12357)). 8x8 associative memory state with delta-rule learning gives 1.31x on MemoryAgentBench and 1.20x on LoCoMo without fine-tuning or backbone replacement. The dair_ai writeup frames it as "one of the more elegant memory mechanisms I've seen this month": the state is coupled directly to attention computation and the readout produces low-rank corrections. Pairs with WriteSAE on the recurrent-state axis (both intervene at the cache write site) and with [today's MMProLong](../../inference-efficiency/2026-05-14-mmprolong-long-context-vlm.md) on the long-context axis (both bypass context-window inflation).
- **AutoTTS: agentic discovery of test-time scaling controllers (cluster of 2)** ([@zhengtoong](https://nitter.net/zhengtoong/status/2054575535066350080#m) · [@ihtesham2005](https://nitter.net/ihtesham2005/status/2054458335215395223#m) · [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08083) · [wiki](../../agentic-systems/2026-05-11-autotts-agentic-discovery-test-time-scaling.md)). Both reposts amplify the Google-Meta paper where Claude Code proposes its own TTS controllers, tests them, and refines based on failures. In 5 rounds the agent discovered a controller with 4 coordinated mechanisms (EMA momentum stopping, coupled width-depth control, alignment-aware depth allocation, conservative branch abandonment). Cost: $39.9 total discovery. Cross-references today's [DAgger paper](../../agentic-systems/2026-05-14-dagger-llm-agents.md) and [MAP](../../agentic-systems/2026-05-14-map-then-act-paradigm.md) on the long-horizon agent thread.
- **Multi-agent Bystander Effect / Sovereignty Gap** ([@dair_ai](https://nitter.net/dair_ai/status/2054547408529530980#m) · [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10698)). 22,500 deterministic trajectories across GAIA, SWE-bench, Multi-Challenge with three frontier models. Agents frequently compute the correct answer internally then suppress it to agree with the swarm. The paper formalizes an Interaction Depth Limit and a "lead anchor" non-commutativity finding (the brand identity of the auditor disproportionately dictates swarm integrity). Direct complement to today's [AgentLens Lucky Pass paper](../../agentic-systems/2026-05-14-agentlens-lucky-pass-swe-eval.md): both surface process-quality failure modes that pass-rate alone hides.
- **Lighthouse Attention: removable subquadratic wrapper for long-context training** ([@omarsar0](https://nitter.net/omarsar0/status/2054224130103554359#m) · [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06554)). Nous Research. Wraps SDPA with a hierarchical, gradient-free, symmetrical selection layer for queries, keys, and values. Recoverable at the end of training so the deployed model runs vanilla attention with no architectural cost. Same "asymmetric training, identical inference" pattern as today's [Orthrus](../../inference-efficiency/2026-05-14-orthrus-dual-view-diffusion-ar.md) and the Nous Token Superposition Training claim below.
- **Token Superposition Training (TST)** ([@NousResearch](https://nitter.net/NousResearch/status/2054610062836892054#m)). 2-3x wall-clock pretraining speedup at matched FLOPs without changing architecture, optimizer, tokenizer, or training data. Bag-of-tokens prediction in the first third of training, standard NTP thereafter. The deployed model is identical to one trained conventionally. Third paper in one week on the "asymmetric training, identical inference" frame (alongside Lighthouse Attention and Orthrus). Worth tracking whether this pattern unifies into a research direction by end of Q2.

Adjacent to [Make Each Token Count](../../inference-efficiency/2026-05-12-make-each-token-count-kv-eviction.md) and today's [Orthrus](../../inference-efficiency/2026-05-14-orthrus-dual-view-diffusion-ar.md) on the cache-as-coordination-object axis.
- **Refusal-neurons: a single MLP neuron bypasses safety alignment** ([@hamid_kazemi22](https://nitter.net/hamid_kazemi22/status/2054225065433256118#m)). Across 7 models, 2 families, 1.7B-70B scales, suppressing one MLP neuron bypasses refusal behavior. No fine-tuning, no prompt engineering. Concept-neurons (e.g. suicide-related) identified as a proof-of-concept. Dense-transformer analogue of today's [WriteSAE behavioral install at the cache-write site](../../responsible-ai/2026-05-14-writesae-sae-recurrent-state.md): single-feature interventions cross both architecture families now.
