## Summary

The day's dominant cluster is morning: three curated retweets converge on the same architectural argument that the agent's environment (harness, memory, skill registry) is now the bottleneck rather than the model. Code-as-Agent-Harness (@burkov), Harness Engineering (@_vmlops), and ReasoningBank (@DanKornas) all argue that next-gen agent capability comes from structured, persistent, executable environments around the LLM. The strongest non-cluster signals are the SKILL.md supply-chain attack paper from @FeiziSoheil, which shows adversarial natural-language metadata can manipulate skill marketplaces at 86% pairwise win rate, and the GRAM 10M-parameter generative recursive reasoning paper (@rohanpaul_ai) that beats deterministic recursive AI three times its size. On industry economics, two morning reposts amplify the Microsoft-cancels-Claude-Code thread and an evening @ns123abc note ties the RAM oligopoly to Anthropic's reported billion-a-month SpaceX compute bill, which together form the day's clearest signal that token and compute economics are now the binding enterprise-AI constraint. Anthropic's Project Glasswing crossing 10,000 critical vulnerabilities in one month is the strongest standalone signal that offensive AI security capability has outrun defensive deployment. Evening was thin on original AI signal beyond the RAM note.
