- **Lighthouse Attention release from Nous Research** ([@NousResearch](https://nitter.net/NousResearch/status/2055337939270332862#m)). Today's HF Tier 1 paper announced directly. Symmetric pyramid pooling of Q, K, V with gradient-free top-k cascade, 1.4-1.7x speedup at 98K context, ~17x at 512K on a single B200, no custom kernel and no auxiliary loss. → [Lighthouse Attention deep dive](../../inference-efficiency/2026-05-16-lighthouse-attention-long-context-pretraining.md)
- **Mechanistic interpretability cluster: circuits are non-unique (cluster of 2)** ([@DakingRai data-driven circuit discovery](https://nitter.net/DakingRai/status/2055317587882283188#m) · [@fnruji316625 "All Circuits Lead to Rome" ICML 2026](https://nitter.net/fnruji316625/status/2054760064511033571#m) · [arXiv 2605.12671](http://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12671)). Two papers in 24 hours arguing the Functional Anisotropy Hypothesis is wrong: a single LLM task is supported by multiple structurally distinct circuits/sheaves, all faithful and sparse, with low overlap. DakingRai's paper finds existing methods discover dataset-specific or mixed-mechanism circuits rather than general task circuits. The ICML paper introduces Overlap-Aware Sheaf Repulsion to systematically uncover competing mechanisms and proposes a Distributive Dense Circuit Hypothesis. Combined claim: mechanistic interpretability has been over-trusting circuit uniqueness. Falsifies a key prior assumption in the wiki's responsible-ai concept map.
