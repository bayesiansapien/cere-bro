## Posts

- **End-to-end 4-bit training of Inkling 2 Small (cluster of 2)** ([@soumithchintala RT of @thinkymachines](https://x.com/soumithchintala/status/2121361082740777173), [@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2121385644169769258), [post](https://thinkingmachines.ai/blog/end-to-end-4-bit-training/)). Thinking Machines explains how its 594B MoE (mixture-of-experts, where each token passes through a few specialist sub-networks) was trained with NVFP4 (NVIDIA's 4-bit block-scaled format) in the expert forward passes and MXFP8 in the backward passes. The key fix is the transpose. The backward pass regroups values under new block scales, so they de-quantize, transpose, and re-quantize with stochastic rounding, which keeps gradients unbiased on average. They also quantized expert-parallel communication, because once the math got about 3x faster the NVLink wire became the bottleneck. The result is an 8% faster pretraining wall clock and 20% faster RL over full MXFP8, for about a 2% efficiency loss, and the shipped 4-bit checkpoint needs no calibration. Serving needs at least ~500 GB in NVFP4. See [the wiki summary](../../inference-efficiency/2026-11-14-end-to-end-4bit-training-inkling.md).

Cmpnd's dspy-wsp has GPT-6 Astra edit a decision model's weights for about $5-10, improving all five open models tested by 1.9-16.1 points.
