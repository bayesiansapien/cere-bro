## Summary

Only the morning slot had content. The evening slot was empty, and no night or afternoon slot was written, so the morning slot is effectively the whole day. The dominant story was Jensen Huang's resignation as Nvidia CEO. About 12 posts split on whether the affair or a GPU smuggling ring around Megaspeed and Supermicro was the real cause, while the Commerce rule barring Chinese firms from renting Blackwell chips abroad kept circulating. The second strong thread was agent memory and self-improving harnesses: four papers (SoMem, ReHarness, JoMoHa, Trustworthy RSI) all treat the harness and its notes as something to edit, train or statistically gate. The best single item was "No Second Run", the first recursive self-improvement loop run over a full frontier pre-training job, which produced an open 30B MoE base model. PyTorch's Helion autotuning-free kernels and Gemini 4 Hydrogen's $1.6-per-task agent pricing were the strongest efficiency signals. Signal-to-noise was good. The noise was succession gossip, stock bait and agent-infra promotion.
