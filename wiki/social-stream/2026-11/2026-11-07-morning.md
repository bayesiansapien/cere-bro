## Summary

The strongest signal in this window was agent cost cut without touching the model. Three papers landed the same US afternoon and all were picked up by DAIR's curators and amplified by omarsar0 and rohanpaul_ai. EnvOpt rewrites the shared workspace an agent reads. Amazon's weight-memory paper distills user facts into the model. Nace AI's Drex answers bounded choices without generating text. Drex was its own cluster of 4 posts, the most of any item. A second cluster, 3 posts, covered memory supply and the chip bounce: NVIDIA reportedly asked TSMC to plan 3D DRAM while chip stocks recovered part of Thursday's rout. The standout single items were Avi Chawla's explainer on seven MoE routing strategies and HKU's AgentPilot, which lets a 9B model steer a frontier coder. The scheduled slot captures returned nothing and no saved posts arrived, so everything here comes from the Following feed.

## Posts

- **Drex, open decision models (cluster of 4)** ([@omarsar0](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2118693892300439722), [@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2118712420248207829), [@rohanpaul_ai on calibration](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2118713297453437249), [@dair_ai](https://x.com/dair_ai/status/2118694937776509411), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07854)). Nace AI released four open decision models: 8B, 9B, 35B with 3B active, and a 27B Max. A decision model reads a state (a document, record or agent context) and a question with fixed options, then returns one probability per option in a single forward pass, with no text to parse. Max trails Claude Opus 5.5 by 1.6 points on Decision Bench v4 and ties GPT-6 Sol. It answers in 183 ms for about $0.06 per 1K decisions, against $10.17 and 3.5 s for Opus. Training adds RLAF (reinforcement learning from adjusted feedback): a teacher proposes per-option probabilities, the verified answer adjusts them, and the model trains on these soft targets. The calibration chart shows 70% confidence is right about 70% of the time, which makes the number usable as a gate between acting alone and escalating. The paper also reports 5 to 9 points of over-confidence in four of five domains. See the [summary](../../ai-routing/2026-11-07-drex-open-decision-model-family.md).
- **EnvOpt: optimize the agent's environment** ([@dair_ai](https://x.com/dair_ai/status/2118711759792144408), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.07372)). Berkeley's Sky lab, Chicago and Bespoke Labs argue an agent's cost depends on three things: the model, the harness and the environment. Only the first two get optimized. EnvOpt has a coding agent rewrite what a shared workspace (a Slack, a document store, tickets) holds and how it is presented, with no graders, stopping on a validation set. On AutomationBench, Claude Haiku 4.5 gets 72.7% cheaper and passes 14.34% more tasks. The same environment cuts Opus 5's cost 68.1%. On top of Meta-Harness's tuned harness it saves another 83.4%. See the [summary](../../agentic-systems/2026-11-09-envopt-and-agent-cost-shorts.md).
- **Weight memory beats retrieval on user facts** ([@omarsar0](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2118715786152001580), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.05636)). Amazon stores stable user facts in the weights with on-policy self-distillation: the teacher is the same model reading the user's memory, and the student must answer without it. Plain fine-tuning recalls 36.9% of facts. Self-distillation reaches 59.9%, above retrieval at 47.8% and the raw dialogue at 51.3%, at 4B, 9B and 27B. Weight memory forgets older facts and cannot say a fact was never recorded, so a verify-then-regenerate router checks answers against the text store and adds 6.2 points. See the [summary](../../inference-efficiency/2026-11-09-opd-vs-grpo-compute-and-weight-memory.md).

- **Seven MoE routing strategies** ([@_avichawla](https://x.com/_avichawla/status/2118619540213121450)). An explainer of token-choice, expert-choice, dynamic, balanced, hash, soft and group-limited routing in mixture-of-experts models. The key point: an even token split does not make an MoE fast. Small per-expert batches and cross-GPU transfers still cost time, and group-limited routing that follows hardware placement cuts that traffic. See the [summary](../../inference-efficiency/2026-11-07-moe-routing-strategies-for-serving.md).
- **AgentPilot: a 9B strategist for frontier coders** ([@huang_chao4969](https://x.com/huang_chao4969/status/2118571357323808779), [repo](https://github.com/HKUDS/AgentPilot)). HKU splits the agent loop. A 9B model decides what to try next, how to spend the trial budget and when to stop. DeepSeek V4 writes the code. The small model learns from each trial's outcome. The repo plugs into Codex, Claude Code and DeepSeek Harness but shows no independent benchmark yet.
- **Training efficiency on B300s** ([@StockSavvyShay](https://x.com/StockSavvyShay/status/2118722192536195313)). Nebius reports 1.13% more training throughput on 192 B300 GPUs running DeepSeek V3 from one software change. Small, but it supports the claim that the next margin comes from existing hardware.
