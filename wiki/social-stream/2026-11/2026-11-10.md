## Summary

All of today's X signal came in the morning slot. The afternoon and evening slots were empty, since they fall in the quiet US overnight, and there was no night slot. The strongest cluster is Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 launch, with six posts on benchmarks, Arena rankings, GitHub Copilot availability and the safety split between the two models. Meta's Muse Ember is a close second: a small agent-workhorse model at about a third of Haiku 5.5's cost per task, which is a direct routing play. The best technical items are two agents-optimizing-code papers. Evolving Agent Teams wrote an MLA decode kernel 1.2x faster than FlashInfer, and BOXBOX lets an agent hot-reload its own harness mid-run. The NVIDIA-led "intrusion heads" paper is the quiet high-signal find, while the Nvidia chip-diversion affair was all noise with no technical artifact.
