- **How to loop your transformer** ([@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2117223679264473544) · [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40325)). Meta and MIT train dense models up to 1B on 155B tokens. Looping the whole stack gives the best quality at fixed size, and looping middle layers wins when training compute is tight. Per-loop adapters add 1.1 to 4.3% parameters versus 51.4% for doubling depth, and a repaired parallel-loop method runs loops at once during decoding. See [looped transformers](../../llms-foundation-models/looped-transformers.md).
- **Context as infrastructure for agentic RAG** ([@dair_ai](https://x.com/dair_ai/status/2117274289154252866) · [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.41172)). RAGFlow's harness with only BM25 and grep on MiniMax M3 hits 85% on BrowseComp-Plus and 91% on FRAMES. Trees, outlines, wikis and graphs added no accuracy. A claim-and-evidence navigation tree cut tokens 38% on average, but tripled latency until a decision gate cut it from 140s to 30s. Judge indexes on tokens and latency, not accuracy. Pairs with [naive RAG harness cost](../../agentic-systems/2026-10-24-naive-rag-harness-cost-fold-subagents.md).

Prefilling one token, or banning common openers, raises compliance by 34 to 41 points. Good for steering, and a warning for CoT monitoring, which partly relies on that inability.
