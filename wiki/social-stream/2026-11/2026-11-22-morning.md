## Posts

- **G-MTP, Google's Gemma 4 drafter** ([@dair_ai](https://x.com/dair_ai/status/2124246507125903841), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.25813)). The paper adds one shared multi-token-prediction head to Gemma 4 instead of a separate draft model. The head reads the backbone's activations, cross-attends to the backbone's existing KV cache without copying it (so no drafter prefill), and scores vocabulary clusters instead of the full vocabulary. Up to 3.1x on large server models, 2.3x for small models on an H100 and 2.2x on a phone GPU. When the backbone is fine-tuned for a new domain, distilling the drafter with it needs up to 1.83x fewer target passes. Elvis Saravia re-shared it. See the [summary](../../inference-efficiency/2026-11-22-g-mtp-gemma-4-multi-token-drafter.md).

- **Frontier models can unmask anonymous writers** ([@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2124261335982862679)). A University of Maryland paper finds zero-shot frontier models detect AI-written text as well as trained detectors like Pangram. Fable 5.2 named the reviewer behind 56% of 398 private peer reviews and the account behind 69% of comments from heavy Reddit users.
- **JEPA world models and SVReg** ([@TheTuringPost](https://x.com/TheTuringPost/status/2124303722671153346)). A LeCun-group paper says a JEPA can learn a usable latent map for planning only if it avoids collapse differently: SVReg checks embedding spread along random directions and steps in only when it shrinks. The authors then document a tradeoff where slowly changing, hard-to-predict content (a block sitting still) gets erased.
- **Unlimited-OCR from Baidu** ([@byteonai](https://x.com/byteonai/status/2124163291157008857), [repo](https://github.com/baidu/Unlimited-OCR)). A 3B OCR model that parses long PDFs in one 32K-context pass, 93% on OmniDocBench v1.5, MIT licensed. The "RAG is cooked" framing is hype.
