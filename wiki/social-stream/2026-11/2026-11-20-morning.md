## Posts

- **QDA: agents design quantization recipes** ([@omarsar0](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2123517934974955558), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23388)). Post-training quantization recipes differ for every model and bit width, so experts rebuild them by hand. QDA gives that loop to an agent. Each candidate is graded in minutes by KL divergence from the original model on reasoning traces the agent never sees, and only promising ones get long generations to catch repetition loops. It beats the strongest public recipes by 0.9, 1.4 and 2.2 points at W4A16, W4A8 and W3A16 using 64 GPU-hours, and recovers 98.3% of FP8 accuracy at NVFP4 W4A4 on the 320B GLM-5.3-Flash. See the [QDA summary](../../inference-efficiency/2026-11-20-qda-agent-quantization-design.md).

- **Self-improvement that knows what it broke (cluster of 5)** ([@omarsar0 on GUARD](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2123602524221612071), [@rohanpaul_ai on stale skills](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2123492665929118028), [@dair_ai on Safe Personal RSI](https://x.com/dair_ai/status/2123511637584036078), [@rohanpaul_ai on gated harness edits](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2123523115498312010), [@rohanpaul_ai paper link](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2123523118723740085)). GUARD (Google, [2611.23476](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23476)) accepts a harness edit only if its gains outweigh the behaviors it newly breaks, and beats Meta-Harness by 6.3 points at 54.5% fewer tokens. Amazon's stale-skills paper ([2611.22525](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22525)) shows a saved skill worth +4.7 points for Opus fell 11.4 points below no skill after an app changed, and selective repair lifted Haiku's tax-form accuracy from 30.5% to 97.5%. Safe Personal RSI ([2611.23651](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.23651)) tests each self-proposed instruction update for a health assistant and rejected 20 of 21 risky ones. Google's gated-harness paper ([2611.22299](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.22299)) found an unchecked full rewrite matched accuracy but cost nearly 10x the tokens of one-module edits. Read with the [Resolution Hypothesis summary](../../agentic-systems/2026-11-20-resolution-hypothesis-and-gated-self-edits.md), which shows selection in agent evolution is often no better than random.

- **Infrastructure that cuts idle GPU time (cluster of 3)** ([@NVIDIAAI](https://x.com/NVIDIAAI/status/2123530225741078969), [@rohanpaul_ai on OpenAI](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2123577231389934075), [@rohanpaul_ai on Positron](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2123486091353456802)).
