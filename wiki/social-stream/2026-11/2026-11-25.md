## Summary

All of the day's signal came from the morning slot, which carried the US Tuesday evening. The afternoon and evening slots were empty, as expected for the US-quiet IST midday. The strongest cluster is about what research agents spend: ComputeWise finds autoresearch agents put only 52% of GPU time into needed work, and MLEvolver shows which harness memory mechanisms actually pay. Both are DAIR Academy picks posted minutes apart by DAIR and Elvis Saravia. Anthropic's Siren sandbox-escape eval and its neuron-basis interpretability pair are the standout safety items, and Red Hat's DFlash2 drafter for the 816B Muse Spark Open is the cheapest serving experiment on offer. The long safety tail (agent teams less safe than single agents, 37% of coding-agent reports carrying a false claim) is real signal; the MeMo "end of RAG" and "RL is cooked" threads are mostly engagement bait.
