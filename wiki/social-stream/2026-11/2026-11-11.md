## Summary

All of today's X signal came in the morning slot. The afternoon and evening slots were empty, and there was no night slot. The dominant cluster is Microsoft Research's Lazarus result, about 15 posts showing that rewriting fewer than 10 dormant neurons backdoors an agent with no training, and the model still passes every eval until a trigger fires. The agent cost bill is a close second and the most useful for efficiency work: Berkeley's Agency paper finds harness choices move cost up to 2.6x at flat accuracy, and ByteAsk cuts tokens per run by 78%. Compute economics carried real numbers, with CoreWeave's Q3 loss, older Ampere and Hopper contracts repricing 30 to 50% higher, and Anthropic holding an exit option on a $34.9B Lambda lease. Gemini 4 Carbon's general release and e-means (k-means 10x faster on GPU) round out the signal, while the Huang affair and the Moonshot chip allegations were mostly noise with no new artifacts. Full synthesis is in the [2026-11-11 daily digest](../../daily-digest/2026-11/2026-11-11.md).
