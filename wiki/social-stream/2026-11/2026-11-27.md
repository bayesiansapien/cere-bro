## Summary

Thanksgiving made this a one-slot day: the morning Following-feed capture carried the US holiday afternoon and evening, while the afternoon and evening slots were empty and no night slot ran. The strongest cluster is three papers showing agent harnesses improve less than claimed: Amazon's self-revised ARC-AGI-3 harness gained only 2.80 points (just picking the best snapshot did better), Microsoft found agent patches are bigger mainly because they add tests and docs, and UW and Microsoft showed failure memories widen tool search while success memories speed it up. NVIDIA's model-driven context paper came up twice, now with its headline number: GPT 6.1 Sol nearly tripled at a 32K budget when allowed to compact its own context. The best single post is the Scale AI and CMU critic study, where PPO beats GRPO at small RL batches and costs about a quarter of the GPU-hours. Deedy Das's $50 agent-built chess engine is the most useful practitioner story, while the "leaked prompt" and jailbreak threads were pure noise.
