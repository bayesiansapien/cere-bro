## Summary

All of today's signal came in the morning slot. The afternoon and evening slots were empty, so there are no cross-slot clusters. The strongest thread is agent infrastructure: Skill++ shows a growing skill library quietly breaks routing to older skills, Google uses agent swarms to fix silent XLA:TPU miscompilations, and Alibaba's SPAR evolves a sales playbook in a live A/B test across 200,000+ merchants. A second cluster treats harnesses as products. HarnessRouter reports 25-64% cost cuts from swapping only the harness at a fixed model, and OpenVLHarness beats Codex at 63% lower token cost. The standout for efficiency is the $0.28 LongCat-2.5 agent run, where 4.7M of 4.8M input tokens were cache hits, which shows that caching sets the agent bill. The weight-space programming teardown is the best skeptical read of the day, and the "RAG is cooked" and Karpathy-misattribution threads are noise.
