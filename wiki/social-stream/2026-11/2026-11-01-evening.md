## Summary

No new curated reposts this evening, so the slot is the US Sunday-morning Following feed, and it skews toward efficiency. The strongest single item is an explainer of NVIDIA's point that KV cache eviction on a paged server can drop 90% of tokens and free almost no memory: retained tokens stay scattered across nearly every block. A cluster of 4 posts on rack power and capex says 2027 racks jump to 200kW to 1MW, so cooling and power, not chips, become the gate. A second cluster of 3 covers DeepSeek's open-sourced Super-agent, a Router-plus-Workers harness built on the idea that human attention, not the model, is the bottleneck. Standout papers are SDPO (self-distillation from your own failed attempts, about 6x faster than GRPO), CMU's single-algorithm training, LeCun and Balestriero's JEPA proof of the Platonic Representation Hypothesis, and Google's FlowAgent for CI test repair. The usual "paste this prompt into Opus 5.5" harness bait is loud and empty.

- **KV cache compression frees tokens, not memory** ([@_avichawla](https://x.com/_avichawla/status/2116861920682860761) · [NVIDIA blog](https://research.nvidia.com/labs/eai/blogs/kv-cache-compression-and-its-infra-problems/)). vLLM's PagedAttention only reclaims a 16-slot block when all 16 slots are empty, so importance-based eviction can cut the cache 90% logically and 0% physically. Eviction scores also need attention weights that FlashAttention never writes to memory. NVIDIA's TriAttention ranks tokens from pre-RoPE query/key geometry and compacts every 128 tokens, reporting 10.7x less KV memory and 2.5x faster decoding. Track blocks freed, not tokens evicted. An older PyTorch post resurfacing: reordering how the fused MoE (mixture-of-experts) GEMM tiles get scheduled improves cache reuse, giving up to 4x on A100 and 4.4x on H100 for vLLM's Triton kernel. - **SDPO: learn from your own failures** ([@Pyuyi2333](https://x.com/Pyuyi2333/status/2116622177805980065) · [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.20802)). The model re-reads its failed attempt along with the error feedback, then distills that feedback-informed next-token distribution back into itself. No teacher, dense credit assignment. Reaches GRPO's accuracy about 6x faster in wall-clock on chemistry with Olmo3-7B, and needs up to 3x fewer attempts at test time on hard LiveCodeBench problems.
- **One algorithm for every training stage** ([@omarsar0](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2116629539623473617) · [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.40564)). CMU recasts RL as maximum-likelihood fitting to model-generated targets, so pretraining, midtraining, SFT and RL share one loss and one trainer. Unified runs won on continual learning, and a 5% midtraining mix gave the best coding retention after math RL. [Summary](../../llms-foundation-models/2026-11-01-air-schedule-unified-training-benchmark-measure.md)
- **JEPAs prove the Platonic Representation Hypothesis** ([@askalphaxiv](https://x.com/askalphaxiv/status/2116779287390896418) · [paper](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2610.39302)). The paper proves that JEPAs trained separately on images and on text learn the same representation, up to one rotation, when positive pairs are two views of one scene. It matches image-text alignment with 42x fewer samples than next-token prediction, and a 109M text JEPA beats LLMs up to 72B on cross-modal alignment. [Summary](../../agentic-systems/2026-11-01-benchmarks-environments-and-representation-shorts.md)
