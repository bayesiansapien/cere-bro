- **Self-Harness: harnesses that improve themselves** ([@HoodieFade_](https://x.com/HoodieFade_/status/2121634833524724079)). The agent groups its own failures by root cause, proposes harness edits, and keeps an edit only if it helps on one task set and hurts nothing on a second. Weights never change. Qwen3.5 goes from 18% to 36.7% on Terminal-Bench 2.0, and GLM-5 from 44.4% to 85% on AppWorld. The keep-only-if-no-regression gate is the real idea. [Harness engineering](../../agentic-systems/agent-harness-engineering.md)
- **Anthropic: Building always-on agents** ([@Xudong07452910](https://x.com/Xudong07452910/status/2121775188639437102) · [article](https://claude.com/resources/articles/building-always-on-agents)). The agent sleeps until a message, webhook or timer wakes it, runs one turn, saves state, and sleeps again. It has 5 parts: Inbox (dedup and retry), Timer, Memory, Permissions, and Outbox. The best idea is the deferred tool call: when a step needs human approval, it saves the session and frees the worker instead of blocking. The poster reads it next to DeepSeek's Router-plus-Workers Super-agent. [DeepSeek Super-agent](../../agentic-systems/2026-11-02-deepseek-super-agent-and-fresh-context-reviewers.md)

This is cost routing by moment, not by query. The "JEV" plug in the post is promo. [Advisor routing](../../ai-routing/2026-10-04-claude-code-advisor-routing-by-moment.md)
