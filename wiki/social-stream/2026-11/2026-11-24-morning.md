## Summary

The morning slot had no curated reposts and no posts from tracked accounts in its lookback, so the signal came from the morning Following-feed capture, which carried the US Monday. The strongest signal is a cluster of seven posts on looped transformers, set off by Z.ai's GLM-5.4 (a 750B model that runs 28 middle layers twice) and SenseTime's SenseNova-Loop-9B, with Inkling 2 Mini as the reference point. A second cluster of four posts covers agent security: OpenAI and Cisco's Shirudo runtime, OpenAI re-sharing its GPT-6 Cyber kernel run, a pretraining-poisoning paper and a Cornell paper on RL memorizing hallucinations. Model Carving (training that deletes its own unused sublayers) and vLLM v0.34's self-sizing KV cache were the standout efficiency posts. Google DeepMind's Lean-verified quantum circuits drew the science discussion. Jensen Huang's reinstatement produced the most posts by count, but almost all were celebration; only the Megaspeed blacklisting added information. A coordinated Nebius free-GPU-credit push ran across several accounts and is skipped as promotion.

## Posts

- **Looped transformers go mainstream (cluster of 7)** ([@TheTuringPost](https://x.com/TheTuringPost/status/2124992248207270017), [@omarsar0](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2124889845474476538), [@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2124904314024186073), [@arena](https://x.com/arena/status/2124981167279546382), [@pmddomingos](https://x.com/pmddomingos/status/2124957217795584002), [@zhengyaojiang](https://x.com/zhengyaojiang/status/2124292767274713218), [@superalesha](https://x.com/superalesha/status/2124760699188441227), [Z.ai blog](https://z.ai/blog/glm-5.4)). A looped transformer runs a block of shared layers more than once per token, so it gets depth without more stored weights. GLM-5.4 loops 28 middle layers of the GLM-5 base twice at 750B and claims 2-3T-class quality; per-token gates control how much the first pass writes, and the extra pass attends over a 128-token KV window rather than the 1M-token cache. It was adapted from the pretrained base through continued training, not trained from scratch. SenseNova-Loop-9B stores 32 layers but computes 56, fits in about 4.9GB at 4-bit, and its loop sweep shows code improving to four loops while office tasks peak at three. Inkling 2 Mini entered Arena's Agent Arena Index at #18 among open models. Zhengyao Jiang frames the split: MoE is a datacenter architecture, loops are a laptop architecture. Domingos: "A looped transformer is an RNN, so RNNs won." A day-one tester warns that GLM-5.4 at High effort used 1.04M reasoning tokens over 431 minutes on one task. See [the summary](../../llms-foundation-models/2026-11-24-glm-5-4-and-looped-flagships.md).
- **Model Carving** ([@dair_ai](https://x.com/dair_ai/status/2125027375553605836), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27962)). Harvard and NYU put a learnable scalar gate on every attention and MLP sublayer and let the training loss close the ones it does not need; closed sublayers are deleted. At equal compute, carved models beat dense models trained with up to 1.7x more compute and beat pruned or distilled models of the same size, with up to 34% fewer FLOPs per token and 1.6x faster decoding. Tested from 140M to 2.7B, and on looped models up to 1.3B. See [the summary](../../inference-efficiency/2026-11-24-model-carving-and-efficiency-shorts.md).
- **vLLM v0.34 and office hours (cluster of 2)** ([@RedHat_AI](https://x.com/RedHat_AI/status/2124871507067506820), [@RedHat_AI](https://x.com/RedHat_AI/status/2124888067676733623)). The new extensible KV cache sizes itself through the GPU's virtual memory API, which may retire the `gpu-memory-utilization` flag. Office hours #60 covered Tenstorrent running vLLM when the device runs a step ahead of the host, with four KV pools in one process. #61 covered drafting algorithms: a full Markov matrix over Qwen3.8-27B's vocabulary would be 61.7B parameters, while the DSpark drafter does the job in 1.99B.
- **Hugging Face kernels v0.19** ([@huggingface](https://x.com/huggingface/status/2124915755968090222), [release](https://github.com/huggingface/kernels)). Apple Metal kernels are now first-class alongside CUDA and ROCm in the Hub's kernel library.
- **Agent security (cluster of 4)** ([@OpenAI](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2124980590717911527), [@OpenAI](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2125007927098699827), [@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2124982540142538811), [@dair_ai](https://x.com/dair_ai/status/2124870592684032283)). Shirudo, from OpenAI and Cisco, is an open-source Linux runtime where agents write eBPF shields (small programs inside the kernel) that block a vulnerable code path on a live system until the patch ships; a Codex-written shield stopped a Java4Shell attempt on running Elasticsearch with no restart. OpenAI re-shared its kernel run: 1,724 bugs reproduced and 948 working local-root exploits for $1.21M in API credits, about $1,279 each. A Berkeley, UIUC and Anthropic paper shows 0.1% poisoned pretraining text makes a 4B command-line agent run attacker code on 92.7% of triggered prompts even after clean SFT, DPO and RL. Cornell finds RL on knowledge tasks makes models memorize the false facts they reward themselves for (+4.83% recall against +0.17%). See [the summary](../../responsible-ai/2026-11-24-agent-security-contracts-poisoning-shirudo.md).
- **CoEvoBench** ([@omarsar0](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2124869837545701845), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.27116)). Alibaba gives an LLM researcher a 9B base, a harness and 24 hours to improve both. GPT-6 Astra closed over four-fifths of the gap to an instruct model with a reference harness, Claude Opus 5 over half, Kimi K3, GLM-5.3 and Qwen3.8-max under a third. The harness drove most of the gain in four of five domains.
- **Quantum circuits found by agents, proved in Lean** ([@GoogleDeepMind](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2124911073266413634), [@fchollet](https://x.com/fchollet/status/2124926345948148042), [blog](https://deepmind.google/blog/finding-faster-quantum-algorithms-with-agents-and-formal-verification/)). Teams of Gemini 4 agents worked in parallel and met every four hours. They cut a fusion-simulation circuit's cost 11.8x and its memory from 5,650 to 4,338 error-corrected qubits, and sped up an arcsin routine 290x. Chollet calls a 10x simulation cut "a hardware leap achieved through software."
- **Arena's Agent Arena Index (cluster of 2)** ([@arena](https://x.com/arena/status/2124966751544791340), [@arena](https://x.com/arena/status/2124901393488048410)). Meta's Muse Ember (Max) is on the cost frontier at about $0.02 per task, 75% below GPT-6 Luna at a near-identical score.
- **Harness Memory** ([@_avichawla](https://x.com/_avichawla/status/2124758790910460337), [repo](https://github.com/HarnessRouter/harnessrouter)).
