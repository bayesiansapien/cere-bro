## Summary

The morning slot had no reposts and no AI-handle tweets, so this synthesis reads the morning capture of the Following feed, which covers the US Monday afternoon and evening. The strongest signal is Anthropic's Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 launch, a cluster of six posts covering benchmarks, Arena rankings, GitHub Copilot availability, the hardware-design demo and the safety split between the two models. Second is a cluster of five posts on Meta's Muse Ember, a small model priced for agent grunt work at a third of Haiku 5.5's cost per task. The best technical items are two papers on agents optimizing code. UC Berkeley's Evolving Agent Teams wrote a GPU decode kernel 1.2x faster than FlashInfer, and Microsoft's BOXBOX lets an agent rewrite its own harness mid-run. OpenAI announced Control Room, a central kill switch for research workloads. The Nvidia affair kept trending, now about Megaspeed's chip diversion and a short-seller's revenue claim, but it carries no technical artifacts. An NVIDIA-led interpretability paper on "intrusion heads" is the quiet high-signal find.

- **Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 (cluster of 6)** ([@AnthropicAI](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2119867707546071125), [@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2119870108223033374), [@arena](https://x.com/arena/status/2119941204003770690), [@github](https://x.com/github/status/2119926269982961833), [@VaibhavSisinty](https://x.com/VaibhavSisinty/status/2119868080650371308), [@rohanpaul_ai electronics](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2119904921139270003), [announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2)). - **Meta Muse Ember (cluster of 5)** ([@shengjia_zhao](https://x.com/shengjia_zhao/status/2119859580981407871), [@AIatMeta](https://x.com/AIatMeta/status/2119853595453702580), [@alexandr_wang](https://x.com/alexandr_wang/status/2119853374158032950), [@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2119893550456619398), [@VaibhavSisinty](https://x.com/VaibhavSisinty/status/2119857586342420665), [blog](https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-ember)). Meta's smallest model is built for tool calls, coding subtasks, routing, extraction and long-document parsing. On the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index it scores 33.9 at $0.116 per task, where Haiku 5.5 needs $0.248 per task to reach 34.3, and it beats every GPT-6 Luna setting. Pricing is $0.10 per million input tokens and $0.50 per million output. Alexandr Wang says to pair it with the larger Muse Wonder ("wonder can plan, ember can handle the volume") and revealed the anonymous "crouton-alpha" was an early Ember that processed over 5T tokens in testing. Wiki: [Muse Ember, AgentBaton, CPU-ARC](../../ai-routing/2026-11-10-muse-ember-agentbaton-cpu-arc.md).
- **Evolving Agent Teams** ([@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2119903588155830773), [arXiv 2610.36848](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36848)). Coding agents plateau on expert kernels: Claude Code stalled at 0.51x FlashInfer on the MLA decode kernel (attention for DeepSeek-style latent attention). A supervisor that diagnoses each plateau and rebuilds the team with new roles (profiler, debugger, knowledge integrator), plus a shared lessons file system, reached 0.74x at matched tokens, against 0.31x for a fixed four-role team. With more budget it wrote a kernel 1.2x faster than production FlashInfer, winning 36 of 47 workloads. Wiki: [kernels summary](../../hardware/2026-11-10-evolving-agent-teams-and-relative-position-kernels.md).
- **BOXBOX harness self-improvement** ([@dair_ai](https://x.com/dair_ai/status/2119886501421167042), [arXiv 2611.09218](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.09218)). Microsoft and colleagues let an agent decide when to revise its own harness (the code and prompts around the model) during a long run. A kernel hot-reloads the new harness while keeping the full execution context, so nothing restarts. On PerfRace, a new benchmark for long software performance tuning, it reaches 1.70x speedup in four hours against 1.54x for the best offline or frozen baseline, at lower model cost. Wiki: [harness runtime summary](../../agentic-systems/2026-11-10-boxbox-shepherd-harness-runtime.md).
- **OpenAI Control Room** ([@OpenAI](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2119913172039868579), [post](https://openai.com/index/control-room)). A central system that tracks every research inference workload, including agentic sampling used for training, and can stop them quickly when a safety or security concern comes up. It is the infrastructure-level counterpart to the per-tool-call classifiers Anthropic described for its Fable 5.2 agent swarm.
- **Intrusion heads** ([@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2120013219066753159)). An NVIDIA-led paper finds irrelevant context (even "Computers are useful") hurts by weakening the model's use of the question, not by pushing it toward a wrong answer. A small set of attention heads does this. Masking about 10 of them recovered 57% of the lost accuracy across four tasks on 7B models, with no training. An oracle fix that needs a matching clean run recovered 97.6%. Useful for anyone serving open models over noisy retrieved context.
- **Anthropic refusals on optimization work** ([@omarsar0](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2119895303201657333)). Elvis Saravia reports that even Opus 5.5 refuses kernel and LLM-optimization tasks most of the time, and argues open models are the only route for that work. This sits in direct tension with the Evolving Agent Teams result and Anthropic's statement that it curbed Fable 5.2's ability to speed up its own development.
- **Strong-to-weak OPD vs GRPO** ([@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2119222845347889485), [arXiv 2611.08453](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08453)). Resurfaced from yesterday. On-policy distillation from an outside teacher loses to RL per training FLOP, but distilling from the student's own RL checkpoint recovers its accuracy with 50x fewer FLOPs.
