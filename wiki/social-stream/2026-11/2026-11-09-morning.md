- **Distillation compute (cluster of 2)** ([@SergioPaniego](https://x.com/SergioPaniego/status/2119452382694408579), [@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2119222842332271059), [arXiv 2611.08453](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.08453)). Hugging Face TRL now supports multi-teacher on-policy distillation (MOPD), which merges several specialist teachers into one student trained on its own outputs. Salesforce's paper explains when this pays: distilling from an outside teacher loses to GRPO (group-based RL) per FLOP, but distilling from the student's own RL checkpoint is 50x cheaper, and merging per-domain RL experts costs only 5-8% extra compute.
