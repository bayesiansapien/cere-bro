- **Kilo Code measures 10,643 AI code reviews across 13 models** (cluster of 3) ([@kilocode](https://nitter.net/kilocode/status/2084286218435543358#m) · [full study](https://kilo.ai/articles/open-weight-models-code-review)) [evening]. This is the explainer to hand anyone asking how these models can be huge to store and still cheap to serve.
