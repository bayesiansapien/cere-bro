- **Hassabis steps back from running Google DeepMind; Jeff Dean leaves Google to found Discovery Loop** ([@ns123abc](https://x.com/ns123abc/status/2085048040952369477), [@ns123abc](https://x.com/ns123abc/status/2085047002786627644)) (cluster of 2). Hassabis moves to Chair of Google DeepMind and Chief Scientist of Alphabet, shifting his own focus to AGI, scientific discovery and disease through Isomorphic Labs, with the quote "I feel AGI is close at hand." Four minutes later, Jeff Dean's departure after 27 years, taking Sanjay Ghemawat, Quoc Le and Oriol Vinyals with him into a public benefit corporation whose stated mission is to automate the experimental loop in machine learning, science and engineering, with Google investing and providing compute. Losing the co-author of MapReduce, the co-author of seq2seq and the co-author of AlphaStar in one announcement is not a normal reorg, and the fact that Google is funding the spinout rather than fighting it says they would rather own a stake in automated research than run it in-house.

- **Anthropic confirms an in-house custom silicon team, with Samsung in talks on 2nm** ([@ns123abc](https://x.com/ns123abc/status/2084996304099291210), [@ns123abc](https://x.com/ns123abc/status/2084997589032751120), [@ns123abc](https://x.com/ns123abc/status/2085024640187892177), [Business Insider](https://www.businessinsider.com/anthropic-in-house-silicon-chip-team-claude-2026-8)) (cluster of 3). Confirmed on the record via a job listing and a company statement: Anthropic is hiring silicon engineers at $320k to $485k to co-design hardware and models together for Claude. The earlier scoop being re-upped here adds that the target is an inference chip and that Samsung, already an investor in Anthropic's Series H, is in early talks for its 2nm process plus advanced packaging. Read this against today's digest finding that [Google structured roughly $200B of Anthropic chip lease contracts off its own balance sheet](../../daily-digest/2026-08/2026-08-05.md) with Broadcom, Apollo and Blackstone. A tenant that is quietly designing its own accelerator is a different credit risk than one locked into TPUs for a decade, and it is the same vertical-integration move [OpenAI made with Broadcom](../../ai-industry/2026-05-10-broadcom-openai-microsoft-chip.md).

- **ByteDance bans employees from distilling US frontier models** ([@ns123abc](https://x.com/ns123abc/status/2085007601553576177)). The reported reasoning is entirely political rather than technical: distilling US models would attract Washington's attention and put TikTok at risk again, so "we should be willing to sacrifice some short-term gains for longer-term goals." The post notes that Anthropic has accused DeepSeek, Moonshot, MiniMax, Z.ai and Alibaba of distilling Claude, and pointedly not ByteDance. If true this is the first case of distillation policy being set by regulatory exposure rather than by capability economics, which is worth adding to the [knowledge distillation](../../inference-efficiency/knowledge-distillation.md) picture, where the assumption has been that everyone distills whatever they can reach.

- **Musk commits SpaceX to NVIDIA exclusively, calls Vera Rubin the best architecture** ([@ns123abc](https://x.com/ns123abc/status/2085013201897332906)). Two lines, but they close a loop from this morning's orbital-compute announcement and from the wiki's [Vera Rubin gigascale ramp](../../hardware/2026-07-22-nvidia-vera-rubin-gigascale-ramp.md) coverage. - **Diffusion Transformer worked by hand in 14 steps** ([@ProfTomYeh](https://x.com/ProfTomYeh/status/2085006702043099354)). A manual walkthrough of DiT, the architecture that replaces the U-Net in a diffusion model with a transformer, using a toy example: one training video, the prompt "sora is sky", diffusion step t equals 3. The framing is the useful part. OpenAI shut Sora down this year, but DiT is what every current video generation model is built on, so the brand died and the mechanism did not. Best item in the slot for anyone who wants the mechanism rather than the benchmark.
