- **The 4.6 recipe read: mid-train on your own last checkpoint, then SFT on its traces** ([@eliebakouch](https://x.com/eliebakouch/status/2087567163783614976)) [evening]. Bakouch attributes 4.6's test-time compute curve to more mid and pre-training on the Grok 4.5 checkpoint plus newer supervised fine-tuning stages built from Grok 4.5 traces with base-model filtering. His conclusion that the SFT checkpoint quality dominates is the self-distillation lineage this wiki tracks in [knowledge distillation](../../inference-efficiency/knowledge-distillation.md), where the previous generation of your own model is the cheapest good teacher available.
