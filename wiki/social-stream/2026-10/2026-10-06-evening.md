## Summary

This is a thin slot: two curated reposts, both from @ContentCase and both resurfacing June material, not new news. The real signal is **RL-Kernel**, an open-source GPU kernel library for RL post-training (GRPO and PPO). It is built on FlashInfer and ships custom sampling, prefix-shared attention and TMA kernels. The authors claim up to 163x speedups on hot training components, which matters because rollout and loss computation dominate the cost of RL post-training. The wiki already covered it in June, so treat this as a reminder, not a fresh release. The second post is an opinion piece on RL interview questions and PhD-versus-industry careers. It is career content, not research.

## Posts

- **RL-Kernel: GPU kernels for GRPO/PPO post-training** ([@ContentCase](https://x.com/ContentCase/status/2063554266325819575) · [repo](https://github.com/RL-Align/RL-Kernel) · [Zhihu write-up](https://zhuanlan.zhihu.com/p/2045985049229760399)). This is a FlashInfer-integrated kernel library that targets the RL training loop rather than inference. It aims for bitwise-matching numerics between training and inference engines, and claims up to 163x speedups plus lower memory on high-frequency components. The 163x is a per-op figure, not end-to-end. See the [June summary](../../inference-efficiency/2026-06-08-rl-kernel-grpo-ppo-kernels.md) and the [RL for LLMs concept page](../../llms-foundation-models/rl-for-llms.md).
