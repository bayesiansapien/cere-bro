## Summary

Only the morning slot carried signal; the evening slot was empty because the US conversation had barely started at capture time. The day's strongest cluster is agent harnesses and who makes each decision inside them, anchored by JevAgentHarness: a 4B decision model takes an agent's routine tool and argument choices when confident, cutting cost 14.2% with no changed outcomes across 500 tasks. Google's open-sourced Harness², a free harness-engineering course and Harvard/MIT's market-based post-training, where agents bid for each step, round out that theme. A smaller agent-training cluster (NVIDIA midtraining, CoPE for GRPO, Salesforce ideation) is solid but incremental. The single-slot standout for hardware is an unsourced claim that B300 rental prices rose over 20% in a month while neocloud stocks fell. Much of the high-engagement feed was "reverse-engineer any app" hype and is noise.
