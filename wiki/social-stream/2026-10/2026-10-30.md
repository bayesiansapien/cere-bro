## Summary

All of today's signal came in the morning slot; there were no night, afternoon or evening syntheses, so this is the Following feed's read of the late US Thursday. The strongest cluster is agent cost: NVIDIA's AgentBaton shows one well-timed handoff from a capable model to a cheap one cuts API cost 65.9%, Agent Arena splits cost per task into price versus token count, and prefix-aware routing cut p90 first-token latency from 92.5 s to 0.54 s by sending requests to the GPU already holding their KV cache. Distillation turned adversarial, with Meta FAIR's WM-Basin detecting distilled students through an API and reports that Kimi K3.1 Pro and DeepSeek V4.1-Flash drew on GPT-6 Astra. Mistral Large 4's open weights led the launch chatter, and OpenAI and Anthropic both published their model-monitoring systems; Anthropic's self-assessment trick, which reuses the already-cached conversation, is the standout single item. The self-improving-agents cluster was half promise and half deflation, and the compute-finance items (Anthropic as a third of SpaceX revenue, Intel's CPU:GPU ratio doubling) were real signal. The noise was the usual repost bait and prompt packs. See the [daily digest](../../daily-digest/2026-10/2026-10-30.md).
