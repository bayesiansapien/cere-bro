- **LoGRA: RL in less memory than LoRA** ([@omarsar0](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2109771098120450299), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06647)). NVIDIA compresses each weight gradient with a random rank-256 projection as microbatches accumulate, so the full gradient is never stored, and rebuilds the update from two small matrices. A predicted-KL controller scales each step. 7.2 GiB vs 13.2 GiB for rank-256 LoRA at 1.5B with equal Pass@4; 45.7% less memory at 7B; a 27B model trains on one 8-GPU node. Wiki: [efficiency shorts](../../inference-efficiency/2026-10-13-efficiency-shorts.md).

- **QwenGyre: idle GPUs during agent RL** ([@VaibhavSisinty](https://x.com/VaibhavSisinty/status/2109711951735746788), arXiv 2609.33848). Agent rollouts of 700K+ tokens leave GPUs waiting on tools. An elastic scheduler lends them to other training and reclaims them; shared trajectory prefixes are not recomputed; partial progress after timeouts is scored. Up to 1.85x faster than colocated training.

- **DUET: co-evolve the grader** ([@dair_ai](https://x.com/dair_ai/status/2109781219231801406), [DAIR](https://academy.dair.ai/papers/duet-co-evolving-solver-and-grader-agents-2610.04087)). Alternate updates to a solver agent and its LLM grader. Finance Agent Benchmark 96.5% vs GEPA 94.4%; GDPval grader agreement 68.4% to 78.5%. Updating the grader with the solver frozen barely helps.

- **Harness learning, transferable** ([@Xudong07452910](https://x.com/Xudong07452910/status/2109849107913486779), [arXiv 2609.35738](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.35738)). A 4B "proposer" trained with RL to revise a solver's harness (model calls, tools, information flow) beats a 35B teacher on 21 unseen Reasoning Gym tasks (0.62 vs 0.56 average revision score) and keeps improving over 10 rounds at test time. Covered in the 10-04 and 10-12 digests; this is a Chinese-language explainer.
