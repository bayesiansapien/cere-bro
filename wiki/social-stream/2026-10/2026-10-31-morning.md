## Summary

## Posts

- **Self-rebuilding harness, frozen model** (cluster of 3) ([@N01ennn](https://x.com/N01ennn/status/2116233378215411919), [@vasuman](https://x.com/vasuman/status/2116308503338066116), [@dair_ai repost of @omarsar0](https://x.com/dair_ai/status/2116222747701571993)). The first post describes a loop it attributes to Google researchers. The agent runs a task with its current harness. A Harness Editor proposes general and domain-specific edits to eight parts: system prompt, guardrails, memory, skills, plugins, tool guidance, scripts and subagents. Each edit is probed on the same task. A Harness Composer merges what worked. An Experience Reflector writes lessons into a persistent improvement harness for the next task, with no ground-truth labels. No paper or repo link was captured, so this is a claim, not an artifact. It matches the self-evolving-harness pattern in [agent-harness-engineering](../../agentic-systems/agent-harness-engineering.md) and, read with the same day's [rTaste result](../../agentic-systems/2026-10-31-rtaste-and-agent-judgment-influence.md) (agents rank their own ideas barely better than picking the first), it inherits the open question of who grades the edits. The second post argues enterprise AI pilots rarely reach the P&L because teams model the documented SOP instead of how work is actually done. The third reposts advice to invest in orchestration and measurement so the model writes better code faster.
- **"Graph engineering" at Anthropic** ([@halvenruns](https://x.com/halvenruns/status/2116255352438329556)). Claims an Anthropic research lead said most engineers run swarms of 300+ self-improving agents and are moving to graphs. Hype framing, no source. Skip.
