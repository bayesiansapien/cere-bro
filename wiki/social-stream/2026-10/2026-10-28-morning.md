- **Google's TPU goes merchant (cluster of 5)** ([@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2115228163408314757), [@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2115180665335611452), [@StockSavvyShay](https://x.com/StockSavvyShay/status/2115228202759234047), [@dylan522p](https://x.com/dylan522p/status/2115246475391471817), [The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/google-tpu-moment)). Oracle will be the first hyperscaler outside Google to run TPUs in its own data centers, deploying from calendar 2027. Oracle supplies power, cooling and networking, and links TPU clusters to its databases so enterprises can fine-tune and serve next to their records. Google claims 12.6 PFLOPS of FP4 per 8th-generation chip, over 97% productive time on large training runs, and up to 80% better performance per dollar for low-latency serving. Its open TorchTPU stack reportedly runs PyTorch, vLLM and SGLang without rewrites, which is the real switching-cost story. Bloomberg separately reports OpenAI plans to buy TPUs; its chip lead Richard Ho worked on TPUs at Google. Dylan Patel notes SemiAnalysis clients saw the OpenAI move earlier. See the [digest's TPU Deep Dive](../../daily-digest/2026-10/2026-10-28.md) and [summary](../../hardware/2026-10-28-tpus-leave-google-memory-sold-out.md).

- **Where the checker goes in a coding-agent harness** ([@dair_ai via @omarsar0](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2115107760857583971), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34577)). Alibaba and Cornell compare 17 verification designs across seven frontier coding models: initial instructions, mid-run reminders, completion gates, and an independent critic. Instructions and reminders help a little. Gates help more but can blow up cost. The critic gives the best accuracy per dollar: +5.9 to 9.5 points on Terminal-Bench at 1.13-1.25x cost. [Summary](../../agentic-systems/2026-10-28-harness-verification-and-tracetrial.md).

- **"RAG is so back": the memory tax** ([@omarsar0](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2115282418915795420), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34073)). Many dedicated agent-memory systems score below plain retrieval over the raw conversation history on LongMemEval, PersonaMem and MINT-Eval, and cost more. The paper calls the loss a memory tax: rewriting history into memory drops or distorts facts. Retrieving from raw history alongside memory and reranking both cuts it. Second paper in two days making this point. [Summary](../../agentic-systems/2026-10-28-memory-tax-and-cheap-checks.md).

- **A 187x cheaper RAG judge that approves too much** ([@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2115216308631749030), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20634)). A label-only verifier matches GPT-6 Astra's accuracy within 0.6 points on 495 human-labelled RAG claims at 187x lower cost, but approves 23.2% of unsupported claims against Astra's 14.1%. A confidence-gated cascade saved about as much money as routing the same share at random. [Summary](../../ai-routing/2026-10-28-small-decision-models-as-routers.md).

- **MANDATE: how delegated agent work goes wrong** ([@dair_ai](https://x.com/dair_ai/status/2115156635954090345), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34748)). Microsoft Research and Georgia Tech mined 163,487 Reddit posts about AI agents and surveyed 73 experienced users. The result is 23 failure categories in seven groups, each an agent that finishes the job in a way the user did not expect. The most severe failures were rated the hardest to notice. Agents that keep running and burning compute without progress are among the most reported and least studied.

- **Decision models do not make better rerankers** ([@omarsar0](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2115176716947833003), [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.32318)). Six Qwen backbones from 0.6B to 27B, ten TREC DL and BEIR collections, same BM25 top-100 candidates. A plain reranker fine-tune beats a decision fine-tune by 5 to 29 nDCG@10 points up to 9B; they tie at 27B. Up to 9B, decision fine-tuning adds nothing over asking the untuned model. The authors' advice: if you need a reranker, fine-tune a reranker.

- **Models helping build decision models** ([@TheTuringPost](https://x.com/TheTuringPost/status/2115253610535866590)). Three examples: Strands swaps Qwen3.5-2B's text head for a pointer head that scores given options, with other models generating and checking training questions; SII trains a fast readout to approximate a reasoning model's answer probabilities; and a coding agent caught a Nemotron labeling error, raising a Qwen student from 72.6% to 78.5%.

- **Jev Router: one MCP server, 10,000+ tools** ([@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2115219914013471089)).
