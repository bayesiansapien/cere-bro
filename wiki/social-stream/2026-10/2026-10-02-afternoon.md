- **ProVer: credit for the step that actually mattered** ([@omarsar0](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2105930871534690714) · [arXiv 2609.36178](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36178)). GRPO (group-relative policy optimization) gives every token in a trajectory the same advantage. ProVer has a judge compare successful and failed rollouts to find the pivotal segment. It then resamples continuations from just before and just after that segment, and uses the change in success rate as that segment's credit. The gain over GRPO is +9.9% relative at 2B and +7.1% at 4B, and it holds with a smaller judge. It is the third segment-credit method in a month, after [SLCA-GRPO](../../agentic-systems/2026-09-29-slca-grpo-segment-credit-tool-calling.md) and [DRACO](../../agentic-systems/2026-09-05-draco-rubric-credit-assignment.md).

- **FOCUS: drop the history your next decision doesn't need** ([@dair_ai](https://x.com/dair_ai/status/2105915729812050342) · [paper](https://academy.dair.ai/papers/focus-training-free-decision-preserving-context-compression-for-llm-agents-2609.37590)). This is Microsoft's test-time context compression for agents. It keeps only the past interactions that future decisions depend on. It needs no training, so it works in front of closed APIs. It cut peak context by up to 48% and raised success by up to 8.9 points over the full history. Less context scoring higher fits the [agentic context management](../../agentic-systems/2026-08-03-acm-agentic-context-management.md) thread.

- **ScienceBuddy: alternate prompt edits and weight updates** ([@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2105942613756924055) · [arXiv 2609.17523](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17523)). It turns user corrections into scored test tasks. It then alternates between rewriting prompts and skills and retraining the model. Together the two lifted a science agent from 42.2% to 73.3%. With a 4B model, prompt and skill edits alone went from 31.1% to 51.1%. This is harness and weights co-evolving; see [agent harness engineering](../../agentic-systems/agent-harness-engineering.md).
