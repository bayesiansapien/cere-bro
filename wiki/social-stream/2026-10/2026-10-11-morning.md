- **Decision models become a category (cluster of 5)** ([@Sumanth_077](https://x.com/Sumanth_077/status/2108912677586395324), [@satyanadella](https://x.com/satyanadella/status/2108627923888754862), [@Nandakishorm1](https://x.com/Nandakishorm1/status/2108836195639984284), [@crazyMLguy](https://x.com/crazyMLguy), [@cyrilXBT](https://x.com/cyrilXBT)). Microsoft-Decision-1 is Qwen3.5-9B post-trained to return one option plus a calibrated probability, priced at $0.042 per million input tokens with free output. AnyJev turns any open causal LLM into a decision model with no fine-tuning by reading the next-token scores over the allowed answers, and corrects option-order bias without labels. Vega (800M and 4B) maps the question to an energy landscape with one valley per answer and lets a small physics engine settle a ball; the valley is the answer, its depth the probability. An Unsloth notebook trains your own on 4 GB of VRAM. The router pitch ("one Jev call in front of your agent and every task finds its tier") is the cost case, but no launch reports AUROC. See the [routing summary](../../ai-routing/2026-10-11-decision-model-category-decision1-anyjev-vega.md).

- **Harness engineering (cluster of 4)** ([@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai), [@dair_ai](https://x.com/dair_ai), [@bibryam](https://x.com/bibryam), [@undefinedKi](https://x.com/undefinedKi); papers [2610.11655](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11655), [2610.11573](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11573)). Stanford's "Harness Evolution Hits a Ceiling" labels failed agent runs by their first failure signal: loops and stalls are fixed by evolving the harness, but plans that are delivered and simply poor are fixed only by training weights (a LoRA on evolved runs cut 9B content failures from a quarter to one in twenty). DAIR's pick MemoType proves one retrieval strategy has a precision ceiling on mixed memory stores and routes each memory and query by type, up to 16% better Recall@1. The X article "12 Agentic Harness Patterns From Claude Code" and a 17-author Huawei survey (model plus a six-part harness) circulated alongside. See the [harness ceiling summary](../../agentic-systems/2026-10-11-harness-ceiling-and-trained-orchestration.md).

- **AI-text detection breaks at model boundaries** ([@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai), [arXiv 2610.11599](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11599)). The paper paired 4,000 pre-ChatGPT PNAS abstracts with rewrites by 23 model versions from three vendors. Detectors trained only on a vendor's past versions, set to falsely flag 1% of human text, catch over 99% of rewrites just before the sharpest generation boundary and 3.8% just after it. Vocabulary drift between versions predicts where detection transfers.

- **Six parallelism strategies for inference** ([@_avichawla](https://x.com/_avichawla/status/2108861498252730759)). Data, tensor, pipeline, context, expert and hybrid parallelism, with the useful distinction that prefill context parallelism cuts time to first token while decode context parallelism splits stored KV history for capacity.
