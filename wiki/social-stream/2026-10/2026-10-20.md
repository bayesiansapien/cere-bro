## Summary

Only the morning slot ran today, and it had no curated reposts or saved posts, so the whole day comes from the Following feed covering the US Monday. The strongest cluster is the argument over a GPU glut versus a GPU shortage: Amazon has warehoused GPUs it cannot install, yet Nebius B200 spot prices reportedly tripled. The best standalone signal was inference efficiency: FlashMap's flow-map speculative drafter (up to 6.1x lossless), vLLM Speculators v0.9, and llm-d's work on tracking offloaded KV blocks. Agent papers were solid but secondary, while the Claude Code model-mixing threads were pure engagement bait. See the [daily digest](../../daily-digest/2026-10/2026-10-20.md).
