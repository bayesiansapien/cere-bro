- **Decision models take over routine agent choices** ([@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2112107486098473471) · [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21412)). Princeton + Google's JevAgentHarness hands each multiple-choice step (which tool next) to a fast decision model, gated by a confidence threshold fitted on the agent's own runs. AgentDecision-4B, trained on states the agent actually reaches, made agents 81 to 89% cheaper and up to 2.5x faster within 3 points of success; dropping the threshold cost 19 points. The cleanest routing result in weeks; see [LLM routing](../../ai-routing/llm-routing.md) and the [decision-model calibration debate](../../ai-routing/2026-09-21-jev-calibration-reckoning.md).
- **Jev as the arrows in a workflow graph (cluster of 2)** ([@imryven](https://x.com/imryven/status/2111919117082964122) · [@MaziyarPanahi](https://x.com/MaziyarPanahi/status/2111833867938333120) · [dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/MaziyarPanahi/Jev-RAG-Claim-Verification-130K)). Practitioner recipe: big models do the boxes, Jev answers every keep/drop and retry/stop arrow at about 100 ms and $0.042 per million tokens, so 1,000 triage calls cost 4 cents instead of $4. Panahi released 130K Jev-verified RAG claims. Same pattern as the Princeton paper, from the field side; the per-decision confidence cutoff is the part people skip.
- **Teams of coding agents: cheap planners, strong lead** ([@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2112048784457060471) · [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.17471)). Cohere finds workers that only research and plan match workers used at every stage, at half the cost. A GPT-5.6 Sol lead with 16 cheap Luna planners landed within 1.4 points of an all-Sol team at 70% lower cost, but cheap coders lowered reward. Extends the [agent-team cost thread](../../agentic-systems/2026-10-12-agent-team-costs-and-research-world-models.md).
- **Where security agents burn tokens** ([@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2112091251155825108) · [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.11602)). Across 200 vulnerability-hunting runs, 60.4% of tokens went to understanding code and reasoning about the trigger. Generic cost tricks kept success at lower cost in only 24.4% of comparisons; their AVRI tool, which returns related code together and caches input-to-sink links, cut cost 18 to 24% with no loss. Measure first, then cut.
- **Agent memory: grade the store, not the answer (cluster of 2)** ([@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2112061760626946307) · [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22209) · [@omarsar0](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2112061437996884152)). Amazon's FEB-30 knows the correct memory state after every document. On a Mem0-style system, stored-fact scores and QA scores differed by 16.8 points and disagreed on pass/fail 21% of the time; the right update action was chosen only 20.1% of the time. Omar Saravia's repost on latent semantic memory rides the same theme. See [agent memory](../../agentic-systems/agent-memory.md).

Trial cost and transfer are unproven. Fits [agent harness engineering](../../agentic-systems/agent-harness-engineering.md).
