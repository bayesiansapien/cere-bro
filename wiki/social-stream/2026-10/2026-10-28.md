## Summary

Compute supply ran through the whole day. Google's TPU going merchant showed up in both the morning and evening slots: Oracle will host TPUs from 2027, OpenAI plans to buy them, and Google claims up to 80% better performance per dollar for serving. It sat next to a memory squeeze (SK hynix at an 86% gross margin, with over half of 2027-28 capacity pre-sold) and GB300 rents near $14/hr. The best single post was the evening thread on DeepSeek's open-sourced DeepInfer, which serves KV cache hits from SSDs and gets a 128K-token hit to first token in 500ms instead of 13s. The morning's agent-evaluation cluster was the strongest research block: where to put the checker in a harness, the memory tax on agent memory, and cheap verifiers that approve too much, and it fits what the reader has been saving. Anthropic's IPO discourse, the "coding interview is dead" takes and the 50-agents virality were mostly noise. The afternoon slot was empty.
