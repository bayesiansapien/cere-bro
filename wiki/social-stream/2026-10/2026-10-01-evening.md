The clear lead this slot is Meta's Context Language Models (cluster of 3). The model edits its own context as a file, which cuts FLOPs and raises accuracy. It also ships a cache fix, because mid-context edits break prefix caching. The second thread is harness portability (cluster of 3): a multi-harness RL guide, Hugging Face's "train with RL inside any harness" release, and omarsar0 arguing that your own harness is what lets you swap models without breakage. - **Context Language Models: the model manages its own context (cluster of 3)** ([@omarsar0](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2105690460429996366) · [@dair_ai](https://x.com/dair_ai/status/2105691463795532091) · [@rohanpaul_ai RT](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2105685628482691326) · [paper page](https://academy.dair.ai/papers/context-language-models-2609.37725)). UW and Meta keep the live context as a file the model edits with Bash, so the model decides what to keep instead of harness compaction rules. Zero-shot it gets 11.4% higher accuracy with 21.5% fewer FLOPs on BrowseComp-Plus. Because mid-context edits break prefix caching, the paper adds Suffix Cache Reuse, which cuts server compute 35% versus stock SGLang. That is the KV-cache cost of self-editing context, and it is worth reading. See [Context Language Models](../../agentic-systems/2026-10-01-context-language-models.md).
