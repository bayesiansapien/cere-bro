## Posts

- **Decision models inside the harness (cluster of 5)** ([@dair_ai](https://x.com/dair_ai/status/2111496216567742892), [@omarsar0](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2111496448013725697), [@0x_rody](https://x.com/0x_rody/status/2111491014053511341), [@dr_cintas](https://x.com/dr_cintas/status/2111536299836354864), [@techNmak](https://x.com/techNmak/status/2111159344754745592); [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.21412)). JevAgentHarness (UIUC) sends each multiple-choice decision an agent makes, such as which tool to call or which argument to pass, to a fast decision model. It uses the answer only when confidence clears a threshold fitted on that agent's own past runs. With Gemini 3.5 Flash as the agent, the decision model made about a quarter of tool choices and cut cost 14.2% without changing any of 500 task outcomes. A fixed 0.5 threshold lost 21.5 points. Its open AgentDecision-4B, trained on states the agent reaches after its own mistakes, beat its teacher (91.5% vs 82.0% on shifted tasks) and made per-agent runs 81-89% cheaper. A practitioner post claims wiring Jev into Grok Bot cut his "fork" spend from about $110 to $4 a day (unverified). Google Research open-sourced [Harness²](https://github.com/google-research/harness2), a framework that refines the harness around a fixed model at both task and meta level, built from eight parts (system prompt, guardrails, memory, tool guidance, subagents, skills, scripts, plugins). Walking Labs published a [free course](https://github.com/walkinglabs/learn-harness-engineering) on harness engineering. See [JevAgentHarness summary](../../ai-routing/2026-10-18-jevagentharness-decision-model-delegation.md).

- **Agents that bid for each step** ([@snwiki238337](https://x.com/snwiki238337/status/2111339705774317898); [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22322)). A long Chinese-language thread on Harvard and MIT's market-based post-training (MPT). Each agent learns an action policy and a bidding policy, the highest bidder acts, and training credit is split by bid, so an agent that bids high and fails is penalized most. Trained swarms improve as more agents join at inference and degrade less when agents drop out. See [MPT summary](../../agentic-systems/2026-10-18-market-based-post-training-mpt.md).

- **Agent training recipes (cluster of 3)** ([@omarsar0](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2111579013072433395), [@haofeiyu44](https://x.com/haofeiyu44/status/2111158324984991902), [@dair_ai](https://x.com/dair_ai/status/2111452428294144180)). Salesforce trains an 8B model to write a set of research ideas in one pass, scoring each idea by quality times distance from earlier ones. It beats Claude Opus 4.8 and GPT-6 Astra on "creative utility" ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22324)). CoPE adds one loss to GRPO: on successful runs, the policy also predicts its next few actions, which gives up to 2x sample efficiency with no inference cost ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.20597)). NVIDIA's midtraining stage of 500B software-engineering tokens at 256K context lifts SWE-bench Multilingual from 55.8 to 62.8 on Nemotron 3.5 Lightning ([paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.22019)). See [recipes summary](../../agentic-systems/2026-10-18-agent-rl-recipes-midtraining-cope-ideation.md).
