- **CHIVE: interpretability tools do not beat the transcript** ([@Pyuyi2333](https://x.com/Pyuyi2333/status/2104411404581429328) · [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.16747)). An Anthropic paper asks whether an explanation of a model's behaviour helps predict what happens when you change the claimed cause. CHIVE automates the test: sample 30 outputs per real prompt, flag unusual behaviour, let an investigator agent make 5 to 15 prompt edits, and use the measured changes as ground truth. Activation oracles, natural-language autoencoders and sparse autoencoders (tools that split a model's internal activations into readable features) gave no uplift over reading the transcript, across several models and predictors. The investigations do work as training data: models trained to predict edit effects generalize to new cases. [Wiki summary](../../responsible-ai/2026-09-28-chive-counterfactual-explanations.md).

- **Agent research that removes structure (cluster of 4).** (1) Microsoft Research's Agensh ([@omarsar0](https://x.com/omarsar0/status/2104377054829613473) · [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26781)) coordinates parallel coding agents only through a shared workspace and message channel, with no orchestrator; 1 to 128 agents lifts the five hardest ProgramBench tasks from 19.31% to 28.78%, and 1,024 agents lift pandoc from 33.89% to 55.06% ([wiki](../../agentic-systems/2026-09-28-agensh-1024-agent-harness.md)). (2) SkillGym ([@dair_ai](https://x.com/dair_ai/status/2104339055194546595)) turns human-written SKILL.md files into 2,756 checked environments, fine-tunes on 8,364 passing runs, and lifts Qwen3.5-35B-A3B by 19.10 points on Terminal-Bench 2.1; the trained model without skills beats the base model with them ([wiki](../../agentic-systems/2026-09-28-skillgym-internalizing-skills.md)). (3) GraphMemix from MemoraX ([@TheTuringPost](https://x.com/TheTuringPost/status/2104371367793844299)) builds a query-aware "evidence forest" of linked memories when a question arrives, and reports +11.75 points over the strongest baseline on four multimodal memory benchmarks with Qwen3-VL-8B. (4) Synthetic Sciences' open-source OpenScience agent ([@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2104251882378272793)) solves 75.7% of Terminal-Bench-Science against 68.1% for Codex with GPT-6 Astra.

- **The buildout as credit risk (cluster of 3).** GPU loans rated BBB pay about 1.2 points more than ordinary loans of that grade, while data-center loans at BBB- or BB+ pay only about 0.2 more, because a grid connection and cooling plant outlive a chip generation; a debt-funded non-NVIDIA cluster may cost more to finance than it saves ([@rohanpaul_ai](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2104369639828681013)). A Brookings paper by a Columbia economist totals $10.3T of US AI infrastructure for 2025 to 2032, 3.6% of GDP a year, with over $1.3T of debt committed, much of it through private credit ([@alex_verem](https://x.com/alex_verem/status/2104269093256007884)). And token prices are collapsing while agents consume far more tokens per task ([@StockSavvyShay](https://x.com/StockSavvyShay/status/2104219617984164285)). [Wiki summary](../../hardware/2026-09-28-ai-buildout-financing-risk.md).
