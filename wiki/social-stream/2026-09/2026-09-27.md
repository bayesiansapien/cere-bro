## Summary

Only the morning slot carried posts. The afternoon slot was empty, which is normal for a US Sunday night, and no night or evening slot was written. The biggest cluster is the decision-model wave again, around fifteen posts on Jev and its clones. Only four carry numbers: jevgrep claims 40% lower coding-agent cost on SWE-bench, and RSI-Jev v2.0 is a 2B model an agent team improved in about 15 hours. The strongest single post is economic. An audited walk-through of MiniMax and Zhipu, the only listed pure inference companies, shows one reaching a 24.6% margin while the other lost 75% of its OpenRouter volume. PRIMESCIENTIST, which runs a research budget like a portfolio and needs half the attempts, and RSIAgent's broad-before-deep curriculum (56.5% to 74.5%) are the useful agent results. Most of the rest is hype. The [daily digest](../../daily-digest/2026-09/2026-09-27.md) has the deeper treatment.
