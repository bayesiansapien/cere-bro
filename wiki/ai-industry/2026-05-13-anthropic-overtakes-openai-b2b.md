# Anthropic overtakes OpenAI in B2B adoption for the first time (Ramp data)

**Date:** 2026-05-13
**Source:** [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-overtakes-openai-in-b2b-adoption-for-the-first-time-according-to-ramp-spending-data/) · companion [Claude for Small Business](https://the-decoder.com/anthropic-launches-claude-for-small-business-to-embed-ai-into-the-tools-you-forgot-you-pay-for/)
**Tier:** 2. AI industry, B2B adoption, deployment-services category

## TL;DR

For the first time on the Ramp AI Index, Anthropic leads OpenAI in B2B adoption: 34.4% of US companies using Claude vs 32.3% using OpenAI's products. Anthropic quadrupled its B2B footprint in one year. Same day, Anthropic launches Claude for Small Business, a package of 15 agentic workflows integrating with QuickBooks, PayPal, HubSpot, plus free training courses and a ten-city US workshop tour. The Decoder flags three factors that could erode Anthropic's lead quickly (cost pressure, OpenAI's Microsoft channel, enterprise switching friction), but the structural shift, Anthropic in front in enterprise for the first time, is the headline.

## Why it matters

Two threads in the wiki converge here.

1. **The capacity-binding-constraint thread**, ByteDance $30B (05-08), Broadcom-OpenAI-Microsoft (05-10), Anthropic-Colossus (05-08), Cerebras IPO (05-13). All four are bets on inference capacity. Anthropic's B2B lead is the demand-side signal that the bets are landing. Enterprise customers are routing budget away from OpenAI and toward Anthropic.
2. **The deployment-services thread** — Anthropic's "Claude for Small Business" launch with workshops and integrations mirrors OpenAI's Deployment Company subsidiary (covered 05-11) and Google's hiring of hundreds of customer engineers (today, The Decoder). Three labs in one week doubling down on the "implementation is the bottleneck" framing. Models alone are not enough; integration, training, support are the binding constraints for enterprise adoption.
