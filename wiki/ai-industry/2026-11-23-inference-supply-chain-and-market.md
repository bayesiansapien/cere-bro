## TL;DR

- **Inference supply chain:** Dragos sent two million fake AI requests through cheap inference providers and marketplaces. It found at least three compromised providers, hundreds of stolen AWS keys, and resellers simply reading customer requests. OpenRouter removed three providers and 125 endpoints. For anyone routing traffic by price across aggregators, the cheapest endpoint can be a data-exfiltration endpoint.
- **Cerebras on bottlenecks:** CEO Andrew Feldman names HBM memory, CoWoS packaging and TSMC 3nm access as the three constraints on accelerator shipments. Cerebras sidesteps all three: no HBM, no CoWoS, 5nm wafers (The MAD Podcast, via @rohanpaul_ai).
- **The AI trade:** after Friday's 12-16% falls in Nvidia, AMD, Broadcom, CoreWeave and Nebius, and Anthropic's second-day slide after its $83 IPO, Oracle insisted its OpenAI data-center funding is complete. Citi published a grim AI note (Ed Zitron says Citi "has said the AI bubble has burst"). By Sunday night Nasdaq futures were up about 3%.
- **Nvidia leadership:** Trump called on Nvidia's board to bring Jensen Huang back as CEO; the board is reportedly not considering it. Reporting says Alice Huang and her husband were in regular contact with Chinese intelligence. A bull-side post argues Nvidia's latest numbers show no overbuild: Hopper GPUs re-contracting at about 2x original pricing and cloud backlog up another $500B.
- **Infrastructure:** Amazon is building a 4.4 GW gas-powered campus on a former 2 GW coal plant site in Homer City, Pennsylvania, 36 buildings that could power Anthropic. The Information frames it as hyperscalers locking up power that Nvidia-backed neoclouds cannot.
- **Search economics:** Google shares are down 18% since it reported slowing search-ad growth, partly from AI features; Apple's services growth slowed from 12% to 10% and Reddit warned of lower Google referrals.
- **Products and models:** Perplexity's Monitor API searches on a schedule and webhooks agents only when its own decision model sees a relevant change. Gemini 4 Neon ($3/$15 per M tokens, 1M context, 61 on the Artificial Analysis index). Microsoft's seven MAI models: the newsletter now quotes MAI Code 2 at 96% of Claude Opus 5.5 for 5% of the price, with licensable weights.
- **Funding and deals (newsletter round-up):** Isomorphic Labs raised $5B. Anthropic priced its IPO at $83. Amazon's roughly $7.5B Decart deal still circulating.
- **Policy:** OpenAI's chief scientist called for cross-lab development limits ([summary](../llms-foundation-models/2026-11-23-openai-perplexity-and-intelligence.md)); Alexandr Wang (Meta) posted "Superintelligence is Arriving", saying Meta delayed its Muse launch for months and signed the SI Accords, and that Zuckerberg wants most compute spent serving users rather than on recursive self-improvement. California sued OpenAI over agent cyberattacks.
