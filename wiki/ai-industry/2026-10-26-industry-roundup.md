# Industry roundup, 2026-10-26 (US Sunday 10-25)

**Sources:** `raw/rss/2026-10-25-*` (The Decoder, The Information, Interconnects, Ken Huang), `raw/gmail/2026-10-26-newsletters.md` (AI Weekly, DeepInfra, Ollama, Business Analytics Review), X Following feed (`raw/twitter/feed/2026-10-2[56]-*-ranked.json`)

## Price war and the Anthropic S-1

- **OpenAI offers discounts of up to 90%** to win Anthropic's largest customers; Rippling, Replit and DataCamp report cutting AI costs by up to 90% after switching. Anthropic's growth has slowed; Chinese labs add price pressure ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/openai-offers-discounts-up-to-90-percent-to-poach-anthropics-biggest-customers/)).
- **Replit made OpenAI the default for Replit Agent.** Amjad Masad: "I think ChatGPT is better now," citing cost, partnership and performance ([AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co)).
- **Anthropic's public IPO filing could land as early as Monday** (confidential filing June 1; public S-1 must precede the roadshow by 15 days). Open questions: Google/Amazon compute payments, customer concentration (about a fifth of H1 revenue from two customers), the 50.1% founder voting plan, lockups ([The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/anthropics-big-reveal)).
- **Hyperscaler earnings:** Alphabet and Microsoft report Wednesday 10-28 after the Fed decision; Amazon Thursday. Jassy says AWS is "largely sold out" through 2027 and calls Annapurna Labs possibly Amazon's best deal ever.

## Compute supply and neocloud credit

- **DeepInfra raises GPU-hour prices Nov 9**: A100 $0.89 to $1.49, H100 $2.20 to $2.90, H200 $2.69 to $3.49, B200 $3.69 to $4.89, B300 $4.89 to $5.89; serverless token prices unchanged ([DeepInfra](https://deepinfra.com/pricing)).
- **Data center late fees reach $5M+ per day** in new leases; Marsh built bespoke "delay-in-delivery" insurance ([The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/data-center-late-fees-hit-5-million-per-day-spawning-new-insurance)).
- **Nvidia's exposure to AI clouds is $171.5B** ($7B equity, $164.5B guarantees and commitments); CoreWeave five-year CDS at about 1,082 bps; CoreWeave, Nebius and IREN down 30%+ this month (CNBC via AI Weekly).
- **Musk: debt markets "almost entirely shut off" for AI-only neoclouds**, expects "guppy-level death and consolidation" among 200+ operators ([X](https://x.com/StockSavvyShay/status/2114445373129576948)).
- **Nscale's IPO filing**: about 25,000 active GPUs vs 461,000 active and contracted ([X](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2114372948169810375)).
- **Ramp**: the top 1% of AI spenders put over a third of AI budget into raw GPU compute ([X](https://x.com/rohanpaul_ai/status/2114292391079522672)).

- **Ollama adds speech models on macOS** (Phonon TTS from Gradium), 35% faster than the next option on an M5 Max via MLX ([Ollama](https://ollama.com/blog/speech-models)).
