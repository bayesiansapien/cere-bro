# Claude's Explosive Market Share Surge (April 2026)

**Date:** 2026-04-17  
**Source:** [The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/chatgpt-bleeds-market-share-as-claude-posts-explosive-monthly-growth/)

## Summary

Claude doubled its market share in a single month, jumping past Deepseek and Grok. ChatGPT still leads overall but is losing ground fast. Google Gemini now captures a quarter of all AI traffic — making it the second-largest player.

## Key Datapoints

- Claude: doubled market share month-over-month (April 2026)
- Google Gemini: ~25% of AI traffic
- ChatGPT: still #1 but declining
- Deepseek and Grok: passed by Claude's surge

## What's Driving Claude's Growth

The Claude Opus 4.7 release combined with Claude Design (conversational prototyping tool) and Anthropic's coding focus appear to be driving developer and enterprise adoption. The same week, Anthropic's Mythos model (withheld from public release due to cybersecurity risk) attracted White House attention — raising Anthropic's government and defense profile.

## Industry Significance

Market share data for AI assistants is historically noisy and platform-dependent. But a doubling in a single month is a signal, not noise. If it holds, it represents a structural shift in the consumer AI market — the ChatGPT brand advantage that OpenAI has held since 2022 is genuinely eroding. The timing coincides with OpenAI's internal turbulence (exec departures, shareholder concerns about IPO).
