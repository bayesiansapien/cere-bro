# Earnings eve, IPO week, and the remote-access rule (2026-11-16 US day)

**Sources:** [The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/nvidias-board-come-clean-huangs-resignation) · [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co) · [AI Breakfast](https://aibreakfast.beehiiv.com) · [Algorithmic Bridge](https://www.thealgorithmicbridge.com/p/how-nvidias-crisis-could-end-the) · [Marcus on AI](https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/game-over-man) · [Pragmatic Engineer](https://newsletter.pragmaticengineer.com/p/software-engineering-after-code-becomes) · raw: `raw/gmail/2026-11-17-newsletters.md`, `raw/rss/2026-11-1[67]-*.md`

## TL;DR

The US Monday before Nvidia's Q3 report (Tuesday, guidance about $108B, Colette Kress's first call as interim CEO) and Anthropic's IPO (pricing Wednesday, Nasdaq debut Thursday as ANT, up to $64.4B on 825M shares). The Information's Martin Peers says the board must explain why it pressured Huang to resign, as a "bring back Jensen" campaign grows and Trump publicly backs Huang. Commerce's BIS released the long-awaited remote-access rule restricting Chinese firms from reaching controlled chips through overseas (mostly Southeast Asian) data centers; MiniMax fell 18% and Z.ai 13% in Hong Kong. Anthropic's filing lists $900.9B in compute commitments through 2045; Dario Amodei calls compute "the deepest business dilemma" and buys "on the low side," topping up on the spot market. JPMorgan expects a record $2.1T of US investment-grade issuance in 2027, about $450B of it AI-related. The Fed's financial-stability survey: 87% of market contacts name AI as a salient risk, up from 50% in spring.

## Arguments worth tracking

- **Alberto Romero (Algorithmic Bridge):** Huang's Nvidia was supplier, financier, political advocate and protector of an ecosystem that threatened its biggest customers at once. Without him, AI economics may get more competitive while control concentrates: the "Bank of Nvidia" ends, Chinese open models become the default, Google gains.
- **Gary Marcus:** Oracle down about 61% since his September "peak bubble" call; Nvidia's financing role ends under Kress (per the WSJ); LLM labs still lack a moat; he questions whether Anthropic's IPO can price well during an AI-stock rout.
- **Satya Nadella (Pragmatic Engineer):** Copilot fell behind Claude Code because the IDE "faded away" in six months; "own the intelligence" by decoupling harness, memory and model; "hybrid intelligence" routes between unmetered local models and paid cloud ones; decision models are "the stored procedure of the intelligence era."
- **The Information:** Claude for Microsoft 365 weekly users are under one-tenth of paid Copilot in Office per internal Microsoft data; Microsoft will debut AI-cost-reduction features at Ignite this week.
