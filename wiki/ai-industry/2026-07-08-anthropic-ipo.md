# Anthropic IPO: 3Q26 Profit Over $1B

**Source:** SemiAnalysis Tokenomics | **Date:** 2026-07-08

## TL;DR

Anthropic confidentially filed for IPO on June 1, 2026. SemiAnalysis's Tokenomics model estimates 3Q26 profit exceeding $1B, with Anthropic and OpenAI combining for approximately $100B in annualized recurring revenue. Anthropic is the clear leader in profitable B2B AI monetization, driven primarily by Claude Code's adoption in software development. SemiAnalysis sees Anthropic as a base-case $6T company if execution continues.

## Key Points

- **Confidential IPO filing on June 1, 2026.** No public numbers disclosed yet, but SemiAnalysis built financials from the bottom up by SKU, tier, and customer type. A recent WSJ article confirmed the accuracy of their Tokenomics work.
- **Claude Code is the revenue engine.** Anthropic captured the B2B market through Claude Code, which took the software development world by storm. Pricing power, gross margins, business model, and profitability are all reasons for Anthropic to IPO first.
- **OpenAI IPO pushed to 2027.** OpenAI's delay gives Anthropic a first-mover advantage in public markets. The contrast: Anthropic is profitable and focused; OpenAI is burning cash and unfocused.
- **Two Chinese AI labs already IPO'd this year.** Zhipu and Minimax went public, but Anthropic would be the first AI lab of this scale.
- **Anthropic's lead is structural.** They can invest profits into new models that extend their lead and monetization over closed and open-source competitors. SemiAnalysis: "Anthropic has the ability to truly make OpenAI dance."

## Why This Matters

This is the first hard evidence that AI labs can be profitable at scale, not just revenue-generating. The narrative shift from "AI labs are money pits" to "AI labs are cash machines" has profound implications for the entire AI value chain: hardware vendors, cloud providers, and the broader AI buildout. If Anthropic's S-1 confirms the $1B+ quarterly profit, the public market re-rates the entire AI sector.
