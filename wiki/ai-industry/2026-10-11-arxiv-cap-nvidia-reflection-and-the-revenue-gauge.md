# arXiv caps submissions, Nvidia eyes Reflection, and OpenAI's run rate becomes the market's AI gauge

**Sources:** The Decoder (RSS), The Information (RSS), AI Weekly Espresso (Gmail), X Following feed for the US day 2026-10-10.
**Raw:** `raw/rss/2026-10-1[01]-*.md`, `raw/gmail/2026-10-11-newsletters.md` (private), `raw/twitter/feed/2026-10-1[01]-*-ranked.json` (private)

## TL;DR

A quiet Saturday in research and a busy one in structure. The preprint server, the chip vendor and the stock market each changed how they treat AI output.

- **arXiv caps submissions at two per person per month** from October 2026. Monthly submissions doubled in two years and cs.AI grew more than sixfold; arXiv blames a small set of authors flooding low-quality papers past volunteer moderators. Direct consequence for this wiki: the HF/Kurate/DAIR intake gets thinner and, likely, better.
- **Nvidia in early talks to acquire or further fund Reflection AI** (FT via X). Reflection is a $25B open-weight lab (Nvidia already invested about $800M); an acqui-hire structure like the Groq deal would avoid full antitrust review. Reflection has about $7.3B of compute committed through SpaceX and Nebius. The strategic read: Nvidia would own an open model layer that runs best on CUDA.
- **OpenAI's re-adjusted revenue figure moved markets.** The Nasdaq 100 fell 1.4% (worst day in seven weeks) and the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index 3.4%, even as OpenAI told investors run-rate revenue grew 77% in Q3 and enterprise 107%. OpenAI's run rate now works as the public gauge of AI demand.
- **Google's "Carbon".** Business Insider reports a stronger Gemini 4 variant that one employee compared to Opus 5.5 at coding; new modes are appearing in the Gemini app and AI Studio ahead of a broad Gemini 4 launch. Rumor.
- **Odyssey-3** (14B) generates interactive worlds from text in real time and is a free public research preview; claimed physics-benchmark wins have methodological limits.
- **Nadella's "super intelligence" post** frames frontier models as insider risks ("assume the model is compromised"); The Decoder reads it as a business attack on OpenAI and Anthropic as they become the primary interface.
- **Mathematicians react to OpenAI's 700+ AI-generated manuscripts**: over 100 responses ranging from fascination to grief; Fields Medalist Hugo Duminil-Copin: "I am paralysed."
- **Home compute hoarding** (The Information): techies build basement GPU rigs (one seven-GPU build under $6,000 plus $10-20K of electrical and HVAC work) because "I want to own, I don't want to rent."
- **Also:** Manus parent Butterfly Effect raised $500M+ (Boyu, IDG, Tencent); Anthropic's usage policy from Nov 12 bars sustained abuse of Claude and targets election deception; UK PM Burnham pledged a startup non-compete ban; a Berkeley study (1,222 participants, COLM) found 10 minutes of ChatGPT use reduced persistence on a hard task; Tomek Korbak said OpenAI's safety leadership told him they no longer trust him and removed his access.
