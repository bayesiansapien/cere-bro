# The AI selloff week: ANT gives back its pop, Nvidia's SEC probe, SoftBank eyed SK Hynix

**Sources:** [Last Week in AI #348](https://lastweekin.ai/p/last-week-in-ai-348-ipo-correction) · [The Information: greenshoe slide](https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/anthropic-shares-slide-banks-exercise-greenshoe) · [The Information: SoftBank/SK Hynix](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/softbanks-masayoshi-son-explored-1-trillion-moonshot-buyout-sk-hynix) · [SpaceX debt](https://www.theinformation.com/briefings/spacex-closes-32-billion-debt-deal-steep-yields) · [Nvidia/Inception](https://the-decoder.com/nvidia-bets-at-least-500-million-on-inceptions-diffusion-models-to-strengthen-its-open-source-nemotron-lineup/) · [Marcus on AI](https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/breaking-anthropic-stock-is-crashing) · raw: `raw/rss/2026-11-2*`, `raw/gmail/2026-11-21-newsletters.md` (private)

## What happened
- **Markets:** on Friday 11-20 the Nasdaq fell 5.28% and erased its 2026 gain; the S&P 500 fell 3.7%; both are in correction (more than 10% below record). Nvidia fell 13% after Reuters reported SEC enforcement staff are examining potential accounting issues; AMD fell 13%, Micron almost 14%, CoreWeave and Nebius more than 10%. Semiconductor ETF SMH had its worst week in 25 years.
- **Anthropic (ANT):** the IPO closed with the full underwriter option (1.127B shares at $83, about $93.5B, the largest on record). Shares fell about 10% on day two to around $87 after Morgan Stanley's greenshoe exercise, close to the $83 offer.
- **Nvidia governance:** following Jensen Huang's 11-12 resignation, California's AG subpoenaed Nvidia over possible China chip diversion; Huang and the general counsel testify to House committees on 12-01 and 12-02. Q3 revenue was $115.84B with $130B guidance and no China data-center revenue assumed.
- **Memory:** SoftBank's Masayoshi Son approached Wall Street about financing a roughly $1T buyout of SK Hynix; SoftBank says it is no longer under active consideration.
- **Compute finance:** SpaceX closed $32B of debt at yields above 8% to fund about $47.3B of Nvidia Rubin GPUs, leased via an Apollo-managed trust for about five years, with deliveries from January.
- **Deals:** Nvidia is close to buying diffusion-LM startup Inception for at least $500M (Mercury 3 claims over 1,000 tokens/s). CrowdStrike is in talks to buy Zscaler ($32B). Palo Alto Networks to buy Applied Compute for about $4.5B in stock. Isomorphic Labs raised a $5B Series C.
- **OpenAI financials (WSJ):** Q3 revenue about $10B, operating loss $10.9B, adjusted margin -109% (from -150%); enterprise now the largest revenue source.
