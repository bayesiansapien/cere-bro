# Industry roundup, 2026-10-13: Reflection, research taste, and the capital wall

**Sources:** AI Breakfast, AI Weekly Espresso, Marcus on AI (Gmail); Import AI 476, The Information, The Decoder (RSS); X Following feed. Raw: `raw/gmail/2026-10-13-newsletters.md`, `raw/rss/2026-10-1[23]-*.md`, `raw/twitter/feed/*-ranked.json`.

## Key items

- **Nvidia may buy or acquihire Reflection AI** (FT via [Reuters/The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/10/11/nvidia-in-talks-to-invest-further-in-reflection-ai-or-buy-it-ft-reports)). Nvidia already put in $800M; Reflection raised at a $25B pre-money valuation in April. An acquihire (hire staff, license tech) would skip antitrust review. Reflection's Beam is a 501B MoE (23B active) pitched on token efficiency, pretrained on 6,144 GB300 NVL72 GPUs for four weeks; Import AI notes its scores trail Chinese open models (GLM, Kimi, DeepSeek) ([Import AI 476](https://importai.substack.com/p/import-ai-476-creativity-and-rsi)).
- **Research taste is doubling every 3 months** (Import AI on P-Zero's TasteVal). Frontier models' experimental research taste (designing experiments and interpreting results across 8 AI-development tasks) doubled every 3.0 months since Dec 2025 (95% CI 1.7-5.0). Opus 5.5 beats a 24-expert human baseline with a 2.30x compute multiplier at ~1/30 the cost per attempt. Jack Clark moves his full-RSI estimate from 2028 to 2027.
- **Gary Marcus: "the frontier labs' WeWork moment?"** ([Marcus on AI](https://garymarcus.substack.com/p/could-this-be-the-frontier-labs-wework)). Tokenmaxxing ended, Chinese open weights forced price wars, OpenAI revised revenue forecasts down ~$20B, agent liability is rising ("Large Liability Modules"), and the Third Circuit held AI training on copyrighted material is not fair use. He questions trillion-dollar IPO valuations; Anthropic's S-1 is still not public.
- **VCs securing compute for portfolio startups** ([The Information](https://www.theinformation.com/articles/andreessen-horowitz-general-catalyst-race-secure-chips-startups-need)). a16z (20,000-GPU cluster since 2024) is in talks to expand via two backed data center companies; General Catalyst is building its own data center.
- **Bain's Bridge Data Centres** seeks to upsize a Malaysia loan from $2.8B to ~$6.6B, among the largest Asian data center debt deals (The Information/Bloomberg).
- **Agent CPU and inference economics:** UBS sees Cerebras revenue ~4x to $3.3B in 2027 and ~$23B by 2030 with ~$25B contracted backlog; Perplexity says revenue is growing 5x YoY on agent demand and sovereign funds want it run on their own hardware (X feed).
- **Policy:** the White House restored the UK AI Security Institute's early model access ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/white-house-reverses-course-and-restores-uk-ai-safety-institutes-access-to-frontier-models/)); CAISSI senior officials are being moved to report directly to the White House (The Information); Nadella's "assume a model is compromised" post (The Verge via AI Weekly).
- **Infrastructure risk:** Ukrainian drones shut Yandex's Vladimir data center, the third Yandex AI compute site hit in four days ([Moscow Times](https://www.themoscowtimes.com/2026/10/11/russias-yandex-says-data-center-in-vladimir-shut-down-after-drone-attack-a93942)). China plans $3.9T of grid buildout through 2049 to feed AI data centers (BloombergNEF).
- **Science:** Google DeepMind's AlphaProof Nexus solved dozens of open math problems, including nine Erdős problems, with results peer reviewed in *Science* ([The Decoder](https://the-decoder.com/google-deepminds-alphaproof-nexus-solved-dozens-of-open-math-problems-and-actually-got-peer-reviewed/)).
- **Microsoft** reorganized Azure leadership around AI (The Information), and its Office chief recounts the 2025 decision to pay for Anthropic models inside Copilot.
