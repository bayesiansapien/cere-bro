### Routing between open and closed becomes the pitch, with numbers

- Kilo published deployment data: open weights now carry 79% of its coding workload.
- Their line: closed models win the hardest 20%, open wins cost, privacy, control.
- Separate test: Kimi K3 plus Grok 4.5 built the same database as Opus 5.
- Cost was $1.27 versus $31.71. Scores 93 versus 98, identical crash tests.
- Gap came from tests, docs and hygiene, not correctness.
