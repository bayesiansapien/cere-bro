## Routing, KV cache, compression, GPU

### RL post-training gets a kernel layer

- RL-Kernel open-sourced: GRPO/PPO kernels, up to 163x on hot components.
- Built on FlashInfer with prefix-shared attention and Hopper TMA copies.
- Companion: OpenPipe ART brings GRPO to multi-step agent training.
- The post-training loop is getting its FlashAttention moment, late but fast.
