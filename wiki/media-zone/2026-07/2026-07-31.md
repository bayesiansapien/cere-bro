### Kilo publishes the number everyone suspected

- Kimi K3 plus Grok 4.5 scored 93/100 against Opus 5's 98/100.
- Cost: $1.27 versus $31.71. Identical crash-test results.
- The five-point gap was tests, docs and hygiene, never correctness.
- Cause named: Opus 5 defaulted to a 150-step build/test/fix loop, the budget pair went one-shot.
- Kilo's own telemetry: open weights now carry 79% of its coding workload.
