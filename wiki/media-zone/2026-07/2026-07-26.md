### Don't let the validator see the answer first

- July's AI Engineer talk argued validators must not inherit authority from generators.
- Today's judge paper is that argument measured: candidate-first gives 0.719 false positives.
- Commit-first drops it to 0.012, same judge, only the ordering changed.
- Conference talk and arXiv paper reached the same primitive independently, weeks apart.
