## Routing, KV cache, compression, GPU

### Context economics: the 20x cache gap nobody puts on a model card

- Nemotron Cascade 2 holds 262K context with a KV cache under 2 GB.
- Devstral Small 2, dense and comparable, needs roughly 40 GB for the same.
- Kilo tested 9 models from an 8 GB GPU to dual 3090s.
- Inverts the advice: a 30B with a small cache outfits a 14B dense model.
- Nobody publishes cache-per-token, so buyers cannot see the deciding number.
