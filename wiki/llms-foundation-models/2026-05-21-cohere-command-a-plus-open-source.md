# Cohere Command A+ released as open-source under Apache 2.0

**Source:** The Decoder (Maximilian Schreiner), 2026-05-21.
**Link:** [The Decoder coverage](https://the-decoder.com/cohere-open-sources-its-strongest-model-yet/)

## TL;DR

Cohere released Command A+ as open-source weights under Apache 2.0. It is the company's most powerful language model to date. The Decoder coverage is short on technical detail, so this page is a placeholder pending Cohere's full model card and benchmark disclosures.

## Why this matters

Command A+ joins a growing list of frontier-class open-weight models released in May 2026, including the [open-model wave through May 21 documented in the 2026-05-17 page](../ai-industry/2026-05-17-open-artifacts-21-may-open-model-wave-caisi.md) and Tencent's [Hy-MT2 translation family (1.8B / 7B / 30B-A3B) released the same day on r/LocalLLaMA](../inference-efficiency/2026-05-21-tencent-hy-mt2-translation-quantization.md). Cohere's Apache 2.0 license is the most permissive of the May-2026 cohort. The release reinforces the AI Snake Oil position that frontier capability is "at most a few months" away from open weights, which is load-bearing for the Kapoor argument that nonproliferation alone is brittle.
