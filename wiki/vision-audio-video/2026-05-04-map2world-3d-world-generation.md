## TL;DR

Generates 3D worlds from user-defined segment maps of arbitrary shape and scale, contrasting with prior grid-layout-constrained methods. A detail-enhancer network adds fine-grained details without breaking global coherence. Improves user-controllability, scale consistency, and content coherence over baselines.
