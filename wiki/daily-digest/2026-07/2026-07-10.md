## Deep Dives

### Vidu S1: Real-Time Interactive Video Generation

> Video generation has been an offline, wait-and-render process. Vidu S1 pushes toward real-time interaction, where the model responds to input as the video is being generated.

**Source:** HuggingFace Daily Papers (108 upvotes, top of 10 Jul list)
**Links:** [HuggingFace](https://huggingface.co/papers)

**What is it about?**
Vidu S1 is a real-time interactive video generation model. The headline capability is latency: rather than submitting a prompt and waiting for a full render, the model generates video at a rate that allows interaction, where the user can steer the output as it is being produced. This is the same latency threshold that Wan-Streamer (July 7 digest) was chasing, from a different lab.

**Key takeaways**
- Real-time interactive video generation, topped the HuggingFace daily list at 108 upvotes.
- The interactive-latency angle is the same problem Wan-Streamer v0.2 (July 7) attacked from the audio-visual side.
- Two real-time video models in one week is an early pattern: video generation is crossing the latency threshold that makes it interactive rather than batch.

**Industrial implication**
Real-time video generation is the precondition for a class of products that do not exist yet: interactive video assistants, live generative avatars, and real-time scene editing. The wiki's attention hierarchy puts video generation at Tier 3 (light coverage unless it touches efficiency), but the latency angle is genuinely an efficiency story: getting video generation to interactive speed is a systems and inference-optimization problem, not just a quality problem.
